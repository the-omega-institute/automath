\section{Finite-Field Equational Saturation of Linear Magmas}\label{sec:finite-field-equational-saturation}

This section records the finite-field linear fragment of the equational theory
search.  Throughout, \(p\) is a prime and a linear magma over \(\FF_p\) means a
binary operation
\[
    x\diamond y=ax+by,\qquad a,b\in\FF_p.
\]
For an equation \(E:s=t\), satisfaction in this family is not an opaque
finite-model condition: it is the vanishing of explicitly computable
coefficient polynomials in the two parameters \(a,b\).  The order-four ETP
universe therefore becomes a finite incidence geometry in the affine
coefficient plane.

\begin{theorem}[Linearization Theorem]\label{thm:finite-field-linearization}
Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and let \(t(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\) be a magma term.
Under the interpretation \(x\diamond y=ax+by\), the value of \(t\) has the form
\[
    t=\sum_{i=1}^n c_i(t;a,b)x_i
\]
with \(c_i(t;a,b)\in\ZZ[a,b]\).  If \(|t|\) denotes the number of internal
\(\diamond\)-nodes of \(t\), then \(\deg c_i(t;a,b)\leq |t|\) for every \(i\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For the variable term \(x_i\), the coefficient of \(x_i\) is \(1\) and all
other coefficients are \(0\).  If \(t=u\diamond v\), then the recursive rule is
\[
    c_i(t;a,b)=a\,c_i(u;a,b)+b\,c_i(v;a,b).
\]
Thus the interpretation remains linear in the input variables, and each
internal node raises total degree in \(a,b\) by at most one.  Induction on the
binary tree of \(t\) proves both assertions.
\end{proof}
\leanverified{paper\_finite\_field\_linearization}

\begin{corollary}[Coefficient ideal criterion]\label{cor:finite-field-coefficient-ideal}
\leanverified{paper\_finite\_field\_coefficient\_ideal}
For an equation \(E:s=t\), define
\[
    I_E=\bigl\langle c_i(s;a,b)-c_i(t;a,b)\ :\ i\geq 1\bigr\rangle
        \subset \ZZ[a,b],
\]
where missing variables have coefficient \(0\).  For any prime \(p\) and any
\((a,b)\in\FF_p^2\), the linear magma \(x\diamond y=ax+by\) satisfies \(E\) for
all input assignments if and only if every generator of \(I_E\) vanishes at
\((a,b)\) modulo \(p\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:finite-field-linearization}, the difference \(s-t\) is a
linear form in independent variables \(x_i\).  Over \(\FF_p\), this linear form
vanishes on all assignments exactly when each of its coefficients vanishes.
\end{proof}
\leanverified{paper\_finite\_field\_coefficient\_ideal}

Consequently each equation determines an integral closed subscheme
\[
    X_E=\operatorname{Spec}\ZZ[a,b]/I_E
\]
of the coefficient plane, and an anti-implication \(E_i\not\Rightarrow E_j\)
over \(\FF_p\) is exactly the existence of a point in the constructible set
\[
    \bigl(X_{E_i}\setminus X_{E_j}\bigr)(\FF_p).
\]
This is the bridge from finite magma search to two-variable algebraic
geometry.

\begin{theorem}[Content Bound]\label{thm:finite-field-content-bound}
\leanverified{paper\_finite\_field\_content\_bound}
Let
\[
    f=\sum_{\alpha} n_\alpha a^{\alpha_1}b^{\alpha_2}\in\ZZ[a,b]
\]
be nonzero, and let
\[
    \operatorname{cont}(f)=\gcd_{\alpha} n_\alpha
\]
be its content.  The reduction of \(f\) in \(\FF_p[a,b]\) is the zero
polynomial if and only if \(p\mid \operatorname{cont}(f)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The monomials \(a^r b^s\) form an \(\FF_p\)-basis of \(\FF_p[a,b]\).  Hence the
reduction of \(f\) is zero precisely when every integer coefficient
\(n_\alpha\) is divisible by \(p\), which is equivalent to
\(p\mid\operatorname{cont}(f)\).
\end{proof}

For the order-four ETP enumeration considered here, the coefficient analysis
has the following numerical envelope: every generator has degree at most \(4\);
every term coefficient and coefficient-difference coefficient has absolute
value at most \(2\); and the only prime at which a nonzero defining polynomial
can collapse to the zero polynomial is \(p=2\).  In particular, all odd primes
see the same nonzero integral defining equations.  Later variation in rational
points is therefore arithmetic variation on fixed low-degree schemes, not a
change in the defining equations themselves.

\begin{lemma}[Geometric degree control]\label{lem:finite-field-geometric-control}
\leanverified{paper\_finite\_field\_geometric\_control}
In the order-four linear ETP family, every independent pair of coefficient
constraints cuts out, after removal of common components over an algebraic
closure, a zero-dimensional scheme of degree at most \(16\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each coefficient constraint has total degree at most \(4\).  The assertion is
the plane Bezout bound \(4\cdot 4=16\), applied to the two curves after common
components have been removed.
\end{proof}

The point of Lemma \ref{lem:finite-field-geometric-control} is not that degree
alone forces saturation.  A bounded-degree variety may acquire rational points
over infinitely many primes.  Rather, the lemma identifies the finite
low-complexity algebraic arrangement on which the scan operates.  Implication
over an algebraically closed field \(k\) can also be expressed by the radical
containment
\[
    I_{E_j}\subseteq \sqrt{I_{E_i}}\subset k[a,b],
\]
and failures of containment are constructible differences.  This
Nullstellensatz viewpoint is computationally verified (scripts/equational\_theory/coefficient\_analysis.py, scripts/equational\_theory/nullstellensatz\_check.py)
for the finite certificate described below.

\begin{proposition}[系数平面载体的逃逸或陷落判据]\label{prop:distill-finite-field-carrier-escape-trap}
令 \(p\) 为素数，令 \(0\neq f\in\FF_p[a,b]\) 的总次数为 \(d\)。记
\[
Z(f):=\{(a,b)\in\FF_p^2:f(a,b)=0\}.
\]
设 \(C=c+H\subset\FF_p^2\) 为仿射线性载体，且 \(r:=\dim H\in\{1,2\}\)。若有限集合 \(A\subset C\) 满足 \(f|_C\) 不是零多项式，则
\[
|A\cap Z(f)|\leq
\begin{cases}
d,& r=1,\\
dp,& r=2.
\end{cases}
\]
因此，若 \(A\subset Z(f)\) 且 \(|A|\) 严格超过上式对应的维数界，则必有 \(f|_C=0\)，从而 \(C\subset Z(f)\)。反之，在 \(f|_C\neq 0\) 时，集合 \(A\setminus Z(f)\) 至少含有 \(|A|-d\) 个逃逸点（当 \(r=1\)）或 \(|A|-dp\) 个逃逸点（当 \(r=2\)）。在四阶线性 ETP 系数差多项式的情形，由定理 \ref{thm:finite-field-linearization} 可取 \(d\leq 4\)。
\end{proposition}
\begin{lemma}[系数平面的拉回逃逸骨架引理]\label{distill:breuillard_green_tao_approximate_groups_and_helfgott_growth-escape_and_algebraic_obstruction_control-coefficient-plane-pullback-bezout-skeleton}
设 \(k\) 为域，\(\Theta\) 为 \(\mathbb A^2_k\) 上有限个多项式自映射组成的集合，且每个坐标多项式的次数均不超过 \(q\ge 1\)。对整数 \(L\ge0\)，记 \(\Theta^{\le L}\) 为由 \(\Theta\) 中元素复合得到的长度不超过 \(L\) 的词，并包含恒等映射。设 \(H\in k[a,b]\) 为非常数多项式，\(\deg H=h\)，设 \(F,G\subset k[a,b]\) 为有限多项式族，且 \(F\) 中每个多项式次数不超过 \(d\)。令
\[
D_L:=\max\{1,d,hq^L\}
\]
并在 \(\mathbb A^2_{\bar k}\) 中定义
\[
Y:=V_{\bar k}\bigl(F\cup\{H\circ\theta:\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}\}\bigr),
\qquad
Z:=V_{\bar k}(G).
\]
令 \(\mathfrak C\) 为 \(Y\) 的一维不可约分支中不包含于 \(Z\) 的那些分支。则存在有限集 \(R\subset Y(\bar k)\)，满足
\[
|R|\le D_L^2,
\qquad
Y(k)\setminus Z(k)\subset (R\cap k^2)\cup\bigcup_{C\in\mathfrak C}C(k).
\]
此外，若 \(C\subset Y\) 为不可约曲线，\(K\in k[a,b]\) 在 \(C\) 上不恒为零，则
\[
|C(k)\cap V(K)|\le \deg(C)\deg(K).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
因为恒等映射属于 \(\Theta^{\le L}\)，故 \(H\) 本身出现在定义 \(Y\) 的多项式族中，因而 \(Y\subset V(H)\)。将约化曲线 \(V(H)_{\mathrm{red}}\) 在 \(\bar k\) 上分解为不可约曲线
\[
V(H)_{\mathrm{red}}=C_1\cup\cdots\cup C_m,
\qquad
\sum_{\alpha=1}^m\deg C_\alpha\le h.
\]
记
\[
\mathcal P:=F\cup\{H\circ\theta:\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}\}.
\]
任取 \(C_\alpha\)。若 \(\mathcal P\) 中每个多项式均在 \(C_\alpha\) 上恒为零，则 \(C_\alpha\subset Y\)，于是 \(C_\alpha\) 是 \(Y\) 的一维分支；当它不包含于 \(Z\) 时被收入 \(\mathfrak C\)，当它包含于 \(Z\) 时不向 \(Y(k)\setminus Z(k)\) 贡献点。

若存在 \(P_\alpha\in\mathcal P\) 在 \(C_\alpha\) 上不恒为零，则
\[
Y\cap C_\alpha\subset C_\alpha\cap V(P_\alpha).
\]
由平面 Bézout 定理，右端点数至多为 \(\deg(C_\alpha)\deg(P_\alpha)\)。又 \(\deg(P_\alpha)\le D_L\)，因为 \(F\) 中多项式次数不超过 \(d\)，而
\[
\deg(H\circ\theta)\le hq^L
\qquad(\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}).
\]
将所有这类 \(C_\alpha\cap V(P_\alpha)\) 的点并入 \(R\)，得到
\[
|R|\le \sum_\alpha \deg(C_\alpha)D_L\le hD_L\le D_L^2.
\]
现在若 \(P\in Y(k)\setminus Z(k)\)，则 \(P\in V(H)\)，故 \(P\) 属于某个 \(C_\alpha\)。在第一种情形中，若 \(C_\alpha\not\subset Z\)，则 \(P\) 属于某个 \(\mathfrak C\) 中的曲线；若 \(C_\alpha\subset Z\)，则 \(P\in Z(k)\)，矛盾。在第二种情形中，\(P\in R\)。这证明所需包含关系。

最后，若 \(K\) 在不可约曲线 \(C\) 上不恒为零，则 \(C\) 与 \(V(K)\) 没有共同曲线分支。再次由平面 Bézout 定理，\(|C(k)\cap V(K)|\le\deg(C)\deg(K)\)。证毕。
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[有限域饱和筛的乘积逃逸与障碍骨架二分]\label{distill:breuillard_green_tao_approximate_groups_and_helfgott_growth-escape_and_algebraic_obstruction_control-finite-field-saturation-escape-skeleton-dichotomy}
固定素数 \(p\)。设 \(\mathcal L\) 为有限方程族，\(E_\star\) 为一条待排除方程。对方程 \(E:s=t\)，令 \(\Delta_p(E)\subset\FF_p[a,b]\) 表示推论 \ref{cor:finite-field-coefficient-ideal} 中系数差生成元模 \(p\) 后得到的集合，并记
\[
X_{E,p}:=V_{\FF_p}(\Delta_p(E)),
\qquad
X_{\mathcal L,p}:=\bigcap_{E\in\mathcal L}X_{E,p}.
\]
设 \(\Theta\) 为系数平面 \(\mathbb A^2_{\FF_p}\) 上有限个多项式自映射组成的集合，每个坐标多项式次数不超过 \(q\ge1\)。令 \(H\in\FF_p[a,b]\) 为非常数多项式。对 \(L\ge0\)，定义
\[
Y_{L,H}:=(X_{\mathcal L,p})_{\bar{\FF}_p}\cap
\bigcap_{\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}}V_{\bar{\FF}_p}(H\circ\theta),
\]
其中 \(\Theta^{\le L}\) 含恒等映射。令 \(\mathfrak S_{L,H}(E_\star)\) 为 \(Y_{L,H}\) 的一维不可约分支中不包含于 \((X_{E_\star,p})_{\bar{\FF}_p}\) 的那些分支。记
\[
d_{\mathcal L}:=\max\bigl\{1,\max_{E:s=t\in\mathcal L}\max(|s|,|t|)\bigr\},
\qquad
D_L:=\max\{d_{\mathcal L},\deg(H)q^L,1\}.
\]
则存在有限集 \(R_{L,H}\subset Y_{L,H}(\bar{\FF}_p)\)，满足 \(|R_{L,H}|\le D_L^2\)，且
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\{P\in X_{\mathcal L,p}(\FF_p)\setminus X_{E_\star,p}(\FF_p):
H(\theta(P))=0\ \text{对所有 }\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}\}\
&\hspace{4em}\subset
(R_{L,H}\cap\FF_p^2)\cup
\bigcup_{C\in\mathfrak S_{L,H}(E_\star)}C(\FF_p).
\end{aligned}
\]
等价地，对任意有限集 \(T\subset X_{\mathcal L,p}(\FF_p)\setminus X_{E_\star,p}(\FF_p)\)，必有下列二者之一成立：存在 \(P\in T\) 与 \(\theta\in\Theta^{\le L}\) 使 \(H(\theta(P))\ne0\)；或者
\[
\left|T\setminus\bigcup_{C\in\mathfrak S_{L,H}(E_\star)}C(\FF_p)\right|\le D_L^2.
\]
并且，若 \(C\in\mathfrak S_{L,H}(E_\star)\)，而 \(K\in\FF_p[a,b]\) 是在 \(C\) 上不恒为零的辅助系数条件，则
\[
|C(\FF_p)\cap V(K)|\le \deg(C)\deg(K)\le D_L\deg(K).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[有界复杂度反蕴含的有限--几何转移]\label{distill:breuillard_green_tao_approximate_groups_and_helfgott_growth-uniform_finite_to_geometric_transfer-finite-field-anti-implication-geometric-transfer}
设 \(\mathcal P\) 为有限方程集，\(F\) 为一条方程。令
\[
I_{\mathcal P}:=\sum_{E\in\mathcal P}I_E\subset \ZZ[a,b],
\qquad
I_F=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_s\rangle,
\]
其中 \(g_1,\ldots,g_s\) 为推论 \ref{cor:finite-field-coefficient-ideal} 中给出的 \(F\) 的系数差生成元。对素数 \(p\)，定义有限反蕴含见证集
\[
Y_p:=\left\{(a,b)\in\FF_p^2:\ f(a,b)=0\ (f\in I_{\mathcal P}),\quad
(g_1(a,b),\ldots,g_s(a,b))\neq 0\right\}.
\]
又令
\[
Y_{\overline{\QQ}}:=V\bigl(I_{\mathcal P}\otimes\overline{\QQ}\bigr)
\setminus V\bigl(I_F\otimes\overline{\QQ}\bigr)
\subset \mathbb A^2_{\overline{\QQ}}.
\]
则下列二分成立：
\begin{enumerate}
  \item 若 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}\neq\varnothing\)，则有限域上的无穷素数见证只能来自同一个特征零构造集的约化影像；此时反蕴含具有固定的几何载体。
  \item 若 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}=\varnothing\)，则存在整数 \(M\geq 1\)、正整数 \(N_1,\ldots,N_s\)，以及 \(I_{\mathcal P}\) 的有限生成元 \(f_1,\ldots,f_t\) 和多项式 \(u_{\nu\mu}\in\ZZ[a,b]\)，使得对每个 \(1\leq \nu\leq s\) 都有
  \[
  M g_\nu^{N_\nu}=\sum_{\mu=1}^t u_{\nu\mu}f_\mu
  \qquad\text{in }\ZZ[a,b].
  \]
  因而对所有 \(p\nmid M\) 均有 \(Y_p=\varnothing\)。
\end{enumerate}
特别地，若 \(Y_p\neq\varnothing\) 对无界素数 \(p\) 成立，则必有 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}\neq\varnothing\)。反之，若特征零构造集为空，则所有有限域坏例子均被限制在有限素数骨架 \(p\mid M\) 上；该骨架由系数理想决定，因而只依赖方程的结构度数与内容，而不依赖素数大小。
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
若 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}\neq\varnothing\)，第一种情形即为定义。以下设 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}=\varnothing\)。这等价于
\[
V\bigl(I_{\mathcal P}\otimes\overline{\QQ}\bigr)\subseteq
V\bigl(I_F\otimes\overline{\QQ}\bigr),
\]
因此每个 \(g_\nu\) 都在 \(I_{\mathcal P}\otimes\overline{\QQ}\) 的零点集上消失。由 Hilbert 零点定理，
\[
g_\nu\in \sqrt{I_{\mathcal P}\otimes\QQ}
\qquad(1\leq \nu\leq s).
\]
于是存在正整数 \(N_\nu\) 与有理系数多项式 \(v_{\nu\mu}\in\QQ[a,b]\)，使得
\[
g_\nu^{N_\nu}=\sum_{\mu=1}^t v_{\nu\mu}f_\mu.
\]
取一个共同正整数 \(M\) 清除所有分母，得到
\[
M g_\nu^{N_\nu}=\sum_{\mu=1}^t u_{\nu\mu}f_\mu
\]
并且 \(u_{\nu\mu}\in\ZZ[a,b]\)。

现在令 \(p\nmid M\)，并取任意 \((a,b)\in\FF_p^2\) 满足所有 \(f_\mu(a,b)=0\)。将上式在 \((a,b)\) 处约化模 \(p\)，得
\[
M g_\nu(a,b)^{N_\nu}=0\quad\text{in }\FF_p.
\]
由于 \(M\) 在 \(\FF_p\) 中可逆，故 \(g_\nu(a,b)=0\)。这对所有 \(\nu\) 成立，所以该点满足 \(F\)，不属于 \(Y_p\)。故 \(Y_p=\varnothing\)。

若 \(Y_p\neq\varnothing\) 对无界素数成立而 \(Y_{\overline{\QQ}}=\varnothing\)，则上一步给出的整数 \(M\) 会排除所有 \(p\nmid M\)，只剩有限多个素数，矛盾。因此无界有限域见证必提升为特征零构造载体。证明同时表明所有排除常数来自固定的系数理想证书，而非来自有限域的基数。证毕。
\end{proof}

当 \(r=2\) 时，\(C=\FF_p^2\)。对非零二元多项式应用有限域上的 Schwartz--Zippel 估计，零点数至多为 \(dp\)，于是 \(|A\cap Z(f)|\leq dp\)。若 \(A\subset Z(f)\) 且点数超过相应界限，则非零限制的情形不可能发生，故 \(f|_C=0\)。最后，四阶线性 ETP 的每个系数差多项式次数至多为 \(4\)，这是定理 \ref{thm:finite-field-linearization} 的次数控制。证毕。
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
For each equation \(E\), Corollary
\ref{cor:finite-field-coefficient-ideal} converts satisfaction in the linear
magma \(x\diamond y=ax+by\) into a finite list of polynomial vanishing tests in
\(\FF_p^2\).  The verification enumerates all \((a,b)\in\FF_p^2\), records the
truth vector of the selected law family, and inserts every ordered pair
\((i,j)\) for which the vector has \(E_i\) true and \(E_j\) false.  The table
shows the incremental contribution of each prime in \(P\).  After \(p=13\) in
the at-most-two-variable family, and after \(p=7\) in the exactly-three-variable
family, the incremental contribution is zero through the remainder of \(P\).
The stated equalities and cardinalities are exactly these finite unions.  The
certificate is computationally verified (scripts/equational\_theory/coefficient\_analysis.py, scripts/equational\_theory/nullstellensatz\_check.py).
\end{proof}

The theorem should be read as saturation of ordered separations, not as
stabilization of point counts or truth-vector sets.  Indeed, the number of
observed satisfaction patterns continues to change after the saturation prime:
for \({\mathcal E}_{\leq2}\), the scan records \(129\) patterns at \(p=13\),
\(187\) at \(p=17\), and \(248\) at \(p=29\), even though no new ordered
anti-implication appears.  Thus the implication graph is a coarser invariant
than the full finite-field moduli problem.  It records the order-theoretic
shadow of the coefficient geometry.

Several consequences follow.  First, the ETP linear fragment is a finite
scheme-theoretic incidence problem in \(\mathbb A^2\), rather than an
unstructured finite algebra search.  Second, the Catalan--Bell syntactic
complexity of order-four magma terms collapses, under linearization, to
degree-four coefficient geometry in two parameters.  Third, the content bound
isolates \(p=2\) as the only characteristic at which a nonzero defining
polynomial disappears identically.  The remaining primes may vary in rational
point structure, but they vary on a fixed family of low-degree schemes.
