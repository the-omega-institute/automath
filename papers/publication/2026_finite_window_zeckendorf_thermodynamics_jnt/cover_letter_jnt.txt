Dear Editors of the Journal of Number Theory,

We submit "Finite-Window Zeckendorf Thermodynamics" for consideration as a
research article, together with its separately compiled supplement.

The article studies the fibers obtained by folding binary Fibonacci sums
into the legal Zeckendorf window. Its first contribution is an exact finite
correspondence: an affine residue permutation identifies each fiber with a
finite coefficient spectrum and gives a pointwise partition-difference
formula, while a two-layer interval identity identifies the complete fiber
multiset with the classical Fibonacci partition function.

The thermodynamic contribution concerns the freezing corner. The all-real
pressure is the published golden-ratio Bernoulli-convolution spectrum under
an affine normalization, and the manuscript labels it as imported. Starting
from that input and the exact finite correspondence, we prove local spectral
stabilization, critical finite-window Gibbs and coexistence laws, and a full
large deviation principle. The pressure corner leaves a continuum of
nonexposed slopes outside the standard Gartner--Ellis lower bound; an
injective generator-orbit filling construction supplies those missing local
lower bounds. A marked version gives the joint generator-cost and
multiplicity rate function with its affine coexistence face.

The extremal formulas for the ordinary Fibonacci partition function are also
published inputs. They are retained only to record their exact finite-window
transfer and the complete maximizing-residue list. The abstract,
introduction, and conclusion distinguish these recoveries and transfers from
the finite-window results.

A companion manuscript, "Brocot Condensation and Critical Fibonacci
Renewal," contains the continued-fraction condensation theorem and sharp
critical tail. The present article is independently readable and does not
use that total-variation theorem.

The manuscript and supplement have not been published and are not under
consideration elsewhere. The authors have no conflicts of interest to
declare.
