\begin{abstract}
We study the multiplicity spectrum of the finite map obtained by folding
binary Fibonacci sums into the legal Zeckendorf window.  The fibers admit
two exact descriptions.  An affine residue permutation identifies them
pointwise with a finite Fibonacci coefficient spectrum and yields a
partition-difference formula; an interval identity identifies their complete
multiset with the classical Fibonacci partition function on two consecutive
layers.  These identities transfer the known extremal classification while
also giving exact local spectral stabilization.

The exponential pressure is the published \(L^q\)-spectrum of the
golden-ratio Bernoulli convolution in an affine normalization and is used as
an input, not claimed as an independent contribution.  At its freezing
corner we prove finite-window critical Gibbs and coexistence laws.  The
corner leaves a whole interval of slopes outside the black-box lower bound
of the G\"artner--Ellis theorem.  An injective generator-orbit filling
construction supplies every missing slope and completes the full large
deviation principle.  Retaining generator cost gives a joint
cost--multiplicity large deviation principle whose coexistence region is an
affine face of the rate surface.
\end{abstract}
