Abstract:
We study cyclic recodings of greedy Pisot numeration systems in which
ordered-language ranks are extended to the full digit cube, reduced modulo
the number of canonical words, and overlapped along consecutive windows. For
every fixed strictly increasing Pisot linear numeration system U with u_0=1,
canonical greedy digit set, and canonical normal words, we prove that there is
an effectively computable C_U such that injectivity at aperture m implies
future-only inverse length at most C_U m. No Condition F or zero-preservation
hypothesis is required. The proof uses an explicit finite carry interval,
algebraic separation, and contraction in the Pisot conjugates rather than an
exponential state count. The order is sharp in one fixed binary cubic system:
for the leading root theta of x^3-2x^2+x-1, every m at least 4 is injective and
the exact inverse length is 2 floor(m/2)-1, with penultimate obstruction set
exactly {+/- E_m} and the next set empty. We also classify the overlap
threshold for every quadratic Pisot base, identify exact Fischer covers and
Markov orders, and give an exact arithmetic pair-graph quotient for
nonintegral simple-Parry bases. The results concern this cyclic language-rank
recoding, not numerical beta-normalization in general.

Novelty statement:
To the authors' knowledge, this is the first aperture-linear inverse-depth
bound for cyclic language-rank recodings over fixed Pisot numeration systems.
