\section{Concluding remarks}\label{sec:discussion}

The Parry-rank construction of
Definition~\ref{def:quadratic-parry-fold} closes the arbitrary-quadratic
interface: Theorem~\ref{thm:full-quadratic-pisot-threshold} proves that every
quadratic Pisot base has nontrivial threshold two or three.  The maximum is
three, attained exactly by the roots of $x^2-ax-a$ ($a\ge1$) and
$x^2-ax+1$ ($a\ge3$).  Hence golden uniqueness is a theorem only inside the
norm-$-1$ metallic-unit subfamily; globally the golden mean is the least
extremizer.  The family-wide block identity is
\[
|\mathcal L_n(Y_{\beta,m})|
=(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{n+m-1}\qquad(n\ge m\ge m_*(\beta)).
\]

This extension uses the greedy language and a cyclic reduction of its ordered
rank.  It is not numerical $\beta$-value normalization and does not identify
published Pisot normalization transducers with a common boundary closure.
Signed or redundant digit alphabets, non-greedy transformations, higher
degree non-simple-Parry Pisot bases and general Pisot substitutions remain
explicit open interfaces as stated in
Remark~\ref{rem:quadratic-open-interface}.  For nonintegral simple-Parry
Pisot bases, Theorem~\ref{thm:simple-parry-causal-collision} gives an exact
finite collision-graph algorithm for the causal inverse length without
asserting a uniform aperture threshold.  In particular, it remains open
whether $\ell_{\rm cau}(\beta,m)$ is uniformly bounded over nonintegral cubic
simple-Parry Pisot bases and their injective apertures.

There is also a narrower unresolved interface already in the quadratic
negative-conjugate chamber.  At aperture three the exact causal length is two;
for $m\ge4$ we prove only
$2\le\ell_{\rm cau}(\beta,m)\le m$.  Closing the candidate two-output bound
requires the parameter-uniform carry classification stated in
Remark~\ref{rem:negative-two-window-open-interface}; finite enumeration is not
used as a substitute for that argument.

The higher-degree calculation also marks the precise limit of the equalities
proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:quadratic-optimal-causal-inverse}.  In the
simple-Parry companion convention the least bounded-multiple degree is merely
the Parry order.  Corollary~\ref{cor:pbonacci-unbounded-separation} separates
it unboundedly above the causal length, while
Proposition~\ref{prop:cubic-reverse-separation} gives the opposite strict
inequality.  Hence neither polynomial degree nor the sign of an individual
conjugate replaces the bounded sliding-congruence depth.

We have proved the sharp overlap-threshold function throughout the metallic
quadratic Pisot-unit family $\beta_A=(A+\sqrt{A^2+4})/2$.  With the
nontrivial aperture restricted to $m\ge2$,
Theorem~\ref{thm:metallic-threshold-classification} gives
$m_*(\beta_1)=3$ and $m_*(\beta_A)=2$ for every $A\ge2$.  Thus the golden
mean is the unique metallic member with threshold three.

The stronger finite-block statement is
Theorem~\ref{thm:metallic-family-block-bijection}: at every aperture above
threshold and every $n\ge m$, the language count is exactly
$|\mathcal L_n(Y_{A,m})|=(A+1)^{n+m-1}$.  Its residue-difference proof closes
the small apertures $m=2,3$ separately and uses the budget inequality
$Q_{m-2}>2A$ for every $m\ge4$.  Decoded finite blocks then glue to a
finite-memory conjugacy onto an SFT.

The failure at $m=2$ is equally rigid. Theorem~\ref{thm:m2-branch-locus} identifies the unique exceptional orbit pair, Corollary~\ref{cor:m2-defect-formulas} computes the entropy and Kullback--Leibler defect concentrated there, and Corollary~\ref{cor:m2-mod-zero} shows that the map is invertible modulo null sets away from the branch.

Theorem~\ref{thm:metallic-entropy-zeta-extremality} gives an invariant form of
the same extremality.  All image shifts have entropy $\log(A+1)$, so entropy
alone does not expose the exceptional branch.  The two-window zeta defect is
$1-z$ at $A=1$ and $1$ for every $A\ge2$; the missing periodic orbit is
exactly the identification of the two constant golden sequences.

Theorem~\ref{thm:fischer-cover-stabilized-image} proves that the overlap
presentation is the right Fischer cover of $Y_m$ for every $m\ge3$.  Thus
$2^{m-1}$ states are required among essential right-resolving presentations
of this labeled shift in its stabilized output alphabet.  The number is not a
topological-conjugacy invariant: $Y_m$ is conjugate to the binary full shift,
whose usual Fischer cover has one state.

The fixed-window entropy decomposition, Blackwell equivalence, pressure
transport, second-moment recurrence, discrepancy bounds, and source-law
motivation are collected in the supplementary material.  They are standard
or auxiliary consequences and are not used in the decoder, separation, or
Fischer-cover proofs.  The metallic construction remains the
boundary-modified norm-$-1$ recurrence model of
Definition~\ref{def:metallic-window-data}, while the full theorem uses the
cyclic language-rank recoding of Definition~\ref{def:quadratic-parry-fold}.
