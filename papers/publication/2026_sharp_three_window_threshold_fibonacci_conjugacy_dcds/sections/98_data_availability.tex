\section*{Data and code availability}

No external data sets were used.  The proofs in the paper are analytic; the
finite computations supplied with the source are regression tests and are not
certificates of any parameter-uniform theorem.

The accompanying source archive contains
\path{artifacts/verify_metallic_threshold.py},
\path{artifacts/verify_quadratic_pisot_threshold.py},
\path{artifacts/test_quadratic_pisot_threshold.py},
\path{artifacts/verify_simple_parry_causal.py},
\path{artifacts/test_verify_simple_parry_causal.py}, and saved text output.
The quadratic rank battery tests lengths
$1\le m\le5$ for the negative chamber
$1\le a\le6$, $1\le b\le a$, and for the positive chamber
$3\le a\le8$, $1\le b\le a-2$.  The block-language battery tests
$m=n\in\{2,3,4\}$ on the same 42 parameter pairs.  Additional tests use
$n=m+1$ for six boundary/interior representatives through $m=4$, and periodic
counts through period six for four extremal representatives.
The extended local-structure battery checks all 15 dual chamber pairs with
$3\le q\le7$ at apertures two and three, the exact causal lengths and
finite-block onset through $m=5$ for four representatives, and the critical
periodic-fiber histogram through period six for four extremal representatives.
The simple-Parry battery checks the Parry recurrences and consecutive colex
ranks on five finite Parry words through length six, compares Toeplitz bad
blocks with collision-graph path counts in three systems, exhausts the
$p$-bonacci causal calculation for $3\le p\le8$, and enumerates all bad
blocks through four outputs for the cubic word $101$ at aperture four.
The metallic
script tests the main cases $1\le A\le4$, $2\le m\le5$, together with
$A\in\{5,6\}$, $2\le m\le4$; its extended block tests use $A=1$,
$3\le m\le5$, together with $2\le A\le4$, $2\le m\le4$.  It also checks
the golden block-bijection identity for $3\le m\le6$ at $n=m,m+1$ and
periodic counts through period six for $1\le A\le4$ at $m=2$.

These internal paths identify files in the review bundle only; they are not
presented as persistent URLs.  A versioned public archival release and its DOI
have not yet been assigned and must be supplied before final publication.
