\begin{abstract}
We define a cyclic rank recoding attached to the greedy language of every
quadratic Pisot base $\beta$.  At length $m$, the legal words are ordered
colexicographically, their language ranks are extended to the full digit
cube, and those integer ranks are reduced modulo the number $Q_m(\beta)$ of
legal words.  This is a congruence of ordered-language ranks.  It is not
numerical $\beta$-value normalization in $\mathbb Z[\beta]$, and no arithmetic
equality between an input word and its recoding is asserted.  Every quadratic
Pisot minimal polynomial is either
$x^2-ax-b$ with $a\ge b\ge1$ or $x^2-ax+b$ with
$a\ge3$ and $1\le b\le a-2$.  For the associated sliding rank recoding, let
$m_*(\beta)$ be the least nontrivial aperture $m\ge2$ at which the map is a
topological conjugacy from the full digit shift onto its finite-type image.
We prove the full threshold classification
\[
m_*(\beta)=3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{minpoly}(\beta)\in
\{x^2-ax-a:a\ge1\}\cup\{x^2-ax+1:a\ge3\},
\]
and $m_*(\beta)=2$ otherwise.  Thus three is the global maximum; the golden
mean is its least, but not its unique, extremizer.  Above threshold the
stronger identity
$|\mathcal L_n(Y_{\beta,m})|=(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{n+m-1}$ holds for
every $n\ge m$, and the inverse has memory zero and finite anticipation.  The former metallic-unit
classification, the sharp Fibonacci decoder, branch-locus formulas,
dynamical-zeta defect and Fischer-cover theorem are recovered as special
cases.  We further prove that aperture two pairs the positive- and
negative-conjugate parameter chambers by an exact rank-fold duality, with
first separation at aperture three.  The optimal causal inverse uses exactly
three output symbols throughout the positive-conjugate chamber and exactly
two in the negative-conjugate chamber at aperture three; at larger negative
apertures the parameter-uniform two-output assertion remains open.  At every
invertible aperture the first injective finite-block length and the exact
Markov step are both $m$, and the natural
$(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{m-1}$-state overlap graph is the right Fischer
cover.  The result is expressly limited to this newly defined cyclic
language-rank recoding; standard $\beta$-normalization transducers, redundant
alphabets, and non-greedy representations are outside its scope.
For nonintegral simple-Parry Pisot bases of higher degree, we prove an exact
finite collision-graph formula for the optimal causal inverse length.  The
$p$-bonacci family has causal length two at aperture $p+1$ although its least
bounded companion-polynomial multiple has degree $p$, while the cubic base
with polynomial $z^3-z^2-1$ has the reverse inequality $4>3$.  Thus even the
proved quadratic equalities between these quantities admit no universal
extension.
\end{abstract}
