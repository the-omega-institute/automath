\begin{abstract}
We define a canonical cyclic finite-window fold for every quadratic Pisot
base $\beta$ by colexicographically ranking the reversed greedy
$\beta$-language determined by Parry's boundary word, extending that rank to
the full digit cube, and reducing it modulo the number $Q_m(\beta)$ of legal
words.  Every quadratic Pisot minimal polynomial is either
$x^2-ax-b$ with $a\ge b\ge1$ or $x^2-ax+b$ with
$a\ge3$ and $1\le b\le a-2$.  For the associated sliding-window fold, let
$m_*(\beta)$ be the least nontrivial aperture $m\ge2$ at which the map is a
topological conjugacy from the full digit shift onto its finite-type image.
We prove the full threshold classification
\[
m_*(\beta)=3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{minpoly}(\beta)\in
\{x^2-ax-a:a\ge1\}\cup\{x^2-ax+1:a\ge3\},
\]
and $m_*(\beta)=2$ otherwise.  Thus three is the global maximum; the golden
mean is its least, but not its unique, extremizer.  Above threshold the
stronger identity
$|\mathcal L_n(Y_{\beta,m})|=(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{n+m-1}$ holds for
every $n\ge m$, and the inverse has finite memory.  The former metallic-unit
classification, the sharp Fibonacci decoder, branch-locus formulas,
dynamical-zeta defect and Fischer-cover theorem are recovered as special
cases.  The result is expressly limited to the cyclic Parry-rank fold; other
redundant alphabets and non-greedy normalizations remain open interfaces.
\end{abstract}
