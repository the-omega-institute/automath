\begin{abstract}
For the metallic quadratic Pisot units
$\beta_A=(A+\sqrt{A^2+4})/2$, $A\ge1$, we classify the sharp nontrivial
aperture at which cyclic finite-window Ostrowski normalization becomes a
topological conjugacy onto its finite-type image.  The alphabet is
$\{0,\ldots,A\}$, the legal words satisfy
$x_k=A\Rightarrow x_{k-1}=0$, and normalization preserves the valuation with
weights $Q_0=1$, $Q_1=A+1$, $Q_{j+2}=AQ_{j+1}+Q_j$ modulo $Q_m$.
Writing $m_*(\beta_A)$ for the least aperture $m\ge2$ with conjugacy, we
prove the complete threshold function
\[
m_*(\beta_A)=3\quad(A=1),\qquad m_*(\beta_A)=2\quad(A\ge2).
\]
Thus the golden mean is the unique metallic base with threshold three.  The
proof establishes the stronger block-bijection identity
$|\mathcal L_n(Y_{A,m})|=(A+1)^{n+m-1}$ for every $n\ge m$ above threshold
and supplies a finite-memory inverse.  At the unique excluded pair
$(A,m)=(1,2)$, the only nontrivial fiber is
$\{0^{\mathbb Z},1^{\mathbb Z}\}$; consequently the two-window zeta function
is $(1-z)/(1-2z)$ rather than the full-shift value $1/(1-2z)$, although the
entropy remains $\log2$.  The sharp Fibonacci three-window decoder,
branch-locus formulas and right Fischer-cover theorem are retained as the
golden special case and its structural consequences.
\end{abstract}
