\begin{abstract}
We study the symbolic dynamics of cyclic recodings of greedy Pisot
numeration systems.  The canonical rank of a legal length-$m$ word is
extended to the full digit cube, reduced modulo the number $u_m$ of legal
words, and overlapped along consecutive windows.  This is a congruence of
integer language ranks, not numerical $\beta$-normalization in
$\mathbb Z[\beta]$.

Our first main result is uniform over every fixed strictly increasing Pisot
linear numeration system.  If $U=(u_j)$ has $u_0=1$, its canonical greedy
digit set, and its canonical normal words, then there is an effectively
computable $C_U<\infty$ such that
\[
\Phi_{U,m}\text{ injective}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\ell_{\rm cau}(U,m)\le C_Um.
\]
No zero-preservation or Condition~F hypothesis is required.  The proof bounds
all window carries in an explicit finite interval and uses the contracting
Pisot conjugates to collapse adjacent carries; it does not count the
exponentially many difference states.  To the authors' knowledge, this is the
first aperture-linear inverse-depth bound for cyclic language-rank recodings
over fixed Pisot numeration systems.

The same contraction has a formulation independent of this code.  For every
fixed Pisot recurrence $U$ and every bounded integer coefficient alphabet
$[-D,D]$, the finite overlap graph of bounded $U$-representations of zero has
an effective dichotomy: either a first-coordinate-nonzero state reaches a
cycle, or every path from such a state has length $O_{U,D}(m)$.  The order is
sharp for a fixed cubic recurrence with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$.  This
statement uses neither a legal representation language nor a factor code.

The order is sharp in one fixed binary cubic system.  If $\theta$ is the
leading root of $x^3-2x^2+x-1$, then every $m\ge4$ is injective and
\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(\theta,m)=2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1.
\]
The penultimate collision set is exactly $\{\pm E_m\}$ and the next set is
empty.  Hence no theorem valid for every fixed system in the preceding class
can replace $O_U(m)$ by $o(m)$; this does not claim numerical optimality of
$C_U$ or a universal coefficient.

We also classify all quadratic Pisot bases.  The least nontrivial aperture at
which the sliding recoding is a conjugacy onto its finite-type image is three
exactly for the roots of $x^2-ax-a$ and $x^2-ax+1$, and is two otherwise.
At and above threshold, finite-block injectivity begins at block length $m$,
the natural overlap graph is the right Fischer cover, and the image is
exactly $m$-step, not $(m-1)$-step.  A coordinatewise-difference quotient of
the classical pair graph computes the exact future-only inverse length for every
nonintegral simple-Parry base.  Applied to Bassino's cubic family, it gives
injective apertures with exact inverse lengths tending to infinity.

All claims concern this cyclic language-rank recoding.  They are not claims
about numerical $\beta$-normalization in general, arbitrary
$\beta$-transformations, or all Pisot symbolic dynamics.
\end{abstract}
