\begin{abstract}
We study the symbolic dynamics of cyclic recodings of greedy Pisot
numeration systems.  The canonical rank of a legal length-$m$ word is
extended to the full digit cube, reduced modulo the number $u_m$ of legal
words, and overlapped along consecutive windows.  This is a congruence of
integer language ranks, not numerical $\beta$-normalization in
$\mathbb Z[\beta]$.

Our first main result is uniform over standard Pisot numeration systems.  If
a fixed strictly increasing system $U=(u_j)$ preserves zeros, equivalently
its leading root satisfies Condition~F, there is an effectively computable
constant $C_U$ such that every injective aperture-$m$ recoding satisfies
\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(U,m)\le C_Um.
\]
The constant is computed from the recurrence, canonical digit set, and
bounded-zero normalization automaton.  The proof uses the contracting Pisot
conjugates to collapse adjacent window carries; it is not a count of the
exponentially many difference states.  The order is sharp.  For the fixed
binary cubic system whose leading root $\theta$ satisfies
$\theta^3-2\theta^2+\theta-1=0$, every $m\ge4$ is injective and
\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(\theta,m)=2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1.
\]
We classify the two signed terminal collision words attaining this depth.

We also give a full quadratic Pisot classification: the least nontrivial
aperture at which the sliding recoding is a conjugacy onto its finite-type
image is
\[
m_*(\beta)=3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{minpoly}(\beta)\in
\{x^2-ax-a:a\ge1\}\cup\{x^2-ax+1:a\ge3\},
\]
and $m_*(\beta)=2$ otherwise.  At and above threshold, finite-block
injectivity begins exactly at block length $m$, the natural overlap graph is
the right Fischer cover with $(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{m-1}$ states, and the
image is exactly $m$-step, not $(m-1)$-step, in the fixed output alphabet.
In particular,
$|\mathcal L_N(Y_{\beta,m})|=(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{N+m-1}$ for $N\ge m$.

As a second cubic calculation, for every $n\ge4$, let
$\beta_n\in(n,n+1)$ be the root of
$x^3-(n+2)x^2+2nx-n$ and set $m_n=n-1$.  Then $\beta_n$ is a nonintegral
cubic simple-Parry Pisot number, the aperture-$m_n$ recoding is injective,
and
\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(\beta_n,m_n)=n-1,
\]
so the optimal inverse anticipation is $n-2$.  This is Bassino's cubic
simple-Parry family; the new assertion is the exact depth of its
rank-modulo-$Q_m$ sliding recoding.

The common mechanism is an arithmetic quotient of the classical pair/fiber graph
for one-dimensional local codes: input pairs collapse to difference states
in $[-\lfloor\beta\rfloor,\lfloor\beta\rfloor]^{m-1}$.  In the simple-Parry
setting this quotient computes the exact future-only inverse length by a
longest-path formula, strengthens invertibility to a memory-zero inverse via
the unique predecessor of the zero state, and gives explicit bounded
periodic failure certificates.  Its aperture-two trichotomy and the
quadratic/cubic exact depths are the map-specific arithmetic conclusions.
\end{abstract}
