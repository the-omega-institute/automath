\begin{abstract}
We study a cyclic recoding of greedy $\beta$-languages: legal words of length
$m$ are ordered colexicographically, the resulting integer rank is extended
to the full digit cube and reduced modulo the number $Q_m(\beta)$ of legal
words, and consecutive recoded windows are overlapped.  This is a congruence
of ordered-language ranks, not numerical $\beta$-normalization in
$\mathbb Z[\beta]$.  For every quadratic Pisot base, we determine the least
nontrivial aperture at which the sliding recoding is a conjugacy onto its
finite-type image:
\[
m_*(\beta)=3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{minpoly}(\beta)\in
\{x^2-ax-a:a\ge1\}\cup\{x^2-ax+1:a\ge3\},
\]
and $m_*(\beta)=2$ otherwise.  Above threshold the inverse has memory zero
and finite anticipation, and
$|\mathcal L_N(Y_{\beta,m})|=(\lfloor\beta\rfloor+1)^{N+m-1}$ for every
$N\ge m$.

For arbitrary nonintegral simple-Parry bases, without a Pisot hypothesis, we
identify the exact
finite collision graph governing the optimal causal inverse length
$\ell_{\rm cau}$.  Its finiteness is equivalent to injectivity, while every
failure has a bounded periodic certificate.  The principal higher-degree
result shows that causal depth is already unbounded in degree three.  For
every $n\ge4$, let $\beta_n\in(n,n+1)$ be the root of
$x^3-(n+2)x^2+2nx-n$ and set $m_n=n-1$.  Then $\beta_n$ is a nonintegral
cubic simple-Parry Pisot number, the aperture-$m_n$ recoding is injective,
and
\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(\beta_n,m_n)=n-1.
\]
The proof uses a parameter-uniform two-window carry exclusion and an exact
classification of the two terminal collision-path lengths.  The cubic
expansion data are credited to Bassino; the new assertion is the
rank-modulo-$Q_m$ sliding collision depth.
\end{abstract}
