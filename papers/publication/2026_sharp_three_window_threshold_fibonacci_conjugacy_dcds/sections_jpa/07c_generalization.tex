\section{Beyond Fibonacci: a brief outlook}\label{sec:generalization}

The local arithmetic developed in Sections~\ref{sec:stabilization}--\ref{sec:entropy-ledger} is not tied to Fibonacci numeration. What is specific to the present paper is the sharp sequence-level threshold.

\begin{proposition}[Local numeration template]\label{prop:gen-fiber}
For the local numeration template and its Fourier fiber formula, see
\cite[Proposition~\textnormal{``Local numeration template''}]{MaZhang2026Nonlinearity}.
\end{proposition}

Ostrowski and metallic systems provide the next natural class of examples \cite{Ostrowski1922,Berthe2001Ostrowski,AlloucheShallit2003}. For the golden mean they reduce to the Fibonacci model. For metallic parameter $a\ge2$, the local stabilization still makes sense, and Theorem~\ref{thm:metallic-two-window-rigidity} shows that the smallest nontrivial window is already dynamically rigid.

What is still missing beyond this constant-type boundary result is a sequence-level decoder for arbitrary Ostrowski systems and arbitrary apertures. The Fibonacci threshold proof uses an explicit base-case inversion together with a degree-one overlap argument; no comparable uniform decoder is asserted here for general Ostrowski systems. For that reason Section~\ref{sec:metallic-cover} is restricted to the metallic two-window problem.
