\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit our manuscript entitled

\medskip
\centerline{\textbf{Metallic threshold classification and golden extremality}}
\centerline{\textbf{for finite-window Ostrowski folds}}
\medskip

\noindent for consideration in \textit{Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A} (DCDS-A).

The paper proves the sharp overlap-threshold function for the constant-type metallic quadratic Pisot units $\beta_A=(A+\sqrt{A^2+4})/2$, $A\ge1$, using the canonical cyclic finite-window Ostrowski fold. The main contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
\item A complete threshold classification: the nontrivial threshold is three for the golden parameter $A=1$ and two for every $A\ge2$. Above threshold, the length-$n$ image language has exactly $(A+1)^{n+m-1}$ words for every $n\ge m$, and the sliding fold is a finite-memory conjugacy onto an SFT.

\item A complete classification of the unique exceptional pair $(A,m)=(1,2)$: injectivity fails on exactly one branch orbit, with exact conditional entropy and Kullback--Leibler defect formulas. Its zeta function is $(1-z)/(1-2z)$, whereas every conjugate case has the corresponding full-shift zeta function.

\item Above threshold, the conjugacy transports invariant measures, continuous potentials, pressure, and equilibrium states exactly. Finite-range Hamiltonians and block-level information geometry are preserved by the local inverse.

\item In the Sturmian benchmark, quantitative discrepancy bounds control stabilized block laws and the recovery of finite-range observables, with explicit error terms involving the star discrepancy and boundary-layer mass.
\end{itemize}

We believe the manuscript fits the scope of \textit{Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A} (DCDS-A) because it is a structural symbolic-dynamics paper with explicit arithmetic content. The main theorem determines a family-wide threshold function, proves finite-memory conjugacy onto subshifts of finite type, and detects the unique golden obstruction by a dynamical-zeta invariant. The explicit residue decoder, branch-locus classification, and golden Fischer-cover minimality do not follow from general sofic-shift frameworks alone.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
