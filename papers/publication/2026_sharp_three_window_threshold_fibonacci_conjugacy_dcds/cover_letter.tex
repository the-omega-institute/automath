\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit our manuscript entitled

\medskip
\centerline{\textbf{Overlap thresholds and unbounded future-only inverse depth}}
\centerline{\textbf{for cyclic ranks of Pisot $\beta$-languages}}
\medskip

\noindent for consideration in \textit{Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A} (DCDS-A).

The paper studies a cyclic ordered-language-rank recoding of greedy
$\beta$-languages as a finite-window factor code.  Its main results concern
the exact finite-type onset, canonical right-resolving presentations, Markov
order, and optimal memory-zero inverse anticipation.  The recoding is
expressly distinguished from numerical $\beta$-normalization in
$\mathbb Z[\beta]$.  The three headline contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
\item A complete two-chamber classification for all quadratic Pisot bases:
the threshold is three exactly for $x^2-ax-a$ and $x^2-ax+1$, and is two
otherwise.  The result also determines the exact finite-block onset,
identifies the natural overlap presentation as the right Fischer cover, and
proves that the image is $m$-step but not $(m-1)$-step at aperture $m$.

\item For nonintegral simple-Parry bases, an exact arithmetic compression of
the classical pair-graph decision.  Coordinatewise differences give a finite
quotient that retains every collision, computes the future-only inverse
length by a longest-path formula, and yields the exact aperture-two
trichotomy.  Pair graphs, fiber products, directed-cycle injectivity tests,
and finite inverse-window decisions are classical; the contribution here is
their exact quotient and arithmetic evaluation for cyclic Parry ranks.

\item An exact unboundedness theorem on Bassino's cubic simple-Parry Pisot
family.  If $\beta_n\in(n,n+1)$ is the root of
$x^3-(n+2)x^2+2nx-n$ and $m_n=n-1$, then the aperture-$m_n$ map is injective
and its optimal future-only inverse length is exactly $n-1$.  The proof gives
parameter-uniform carry estimates and a complete terminal-path exhaustion.
\end{itemize}

We believe the manuscript fits the scope of \textit{Discrete and Continuous
Dynamical Systems - Series A} because it is a structural symbolic-dynamics
paper about finite-type classification, follower separation, Fischer covers,
Markov order, and directional decoding for factor codes of $\beta$-shifts.
The ordered-language construction is placed within abstract numeration and
Bertrand systems, and the collision decision is placed within the classical
pair-graph and cellular-automata tradition.  The exact quadratic thresholds,
simple-Parry arithmetic quotient, aperture dichotomy, and Bassino-family
future-decoding depths are specific to the map studied here.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
