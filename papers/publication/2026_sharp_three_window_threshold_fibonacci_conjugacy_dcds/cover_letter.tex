\begin{letter}{Editorial Board\\Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A (DCDS-A)}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit our manuscript entitled

\medskip
\centerline{\textbf{Overlap-injectivity thresholds for cyclic ranks}}
\centerline{\textbf{of quadratic Pisot $\beta$-languages}}
\medskip

\noindent for consideration in \textit{Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A} (DCDS-A).

The paper defines a cyclic ordered-language-rank recoding for every quadratic
Pisot $\beta$-language and proves its sharp overlap-injectivity threshold.  The
recoding is expressly distinguished from numerical $\beta$-normalization in
$\mathbb Z[\beta]$.  The main contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
\item A complete two-chamber classification for all quadratic Pisot bases:
the threshold is three exactly for $x^2-ax-a$ and $x^2-ax+1$, and is two
otherwise.  Above threshold the finite-block maps are injective and the
sliding recoding has a memory-zero future inverse.

\item A complete classification of the unique exceptional pair $(A,m)=(1,2)$: injectivity fails on exactly one branch orbit, with exact conditional entropy and Kullback--Leibler defect formulas. Its zeta function is $(1-z)/(1-2z)$, whereas every conjugate case has the corresponding full-shift zeta function.

\item A coupled interval-rank induction for the positive-conjugate language, a
nearest-multiple separation lemma for both recurrence signs, and an explicit
two-output decoder at every nonextremal two-window parameter.

\item Four complete nonmetallic examples and a Fischer-cover identification
for the presented Fibonacci output shift.  Routine entropy, pressure,
Blackwell-equivalence, and discrepancy consequences are separated into
supplementary material.
\end{itemize}

We believe the manuscript fits the scope of \textit{Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series A} (DCDS-A) because it is a structural symbolic-dynamics paper with explicit finite-rank arithmetic.  The ordered-language construction is compared directly with abstract numeration and Bertrand systems, while the overlap threshold, finite-block decoder, and concrete Fischer-cover identification are map-specific results.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests.

\closing{Sincerely,}
