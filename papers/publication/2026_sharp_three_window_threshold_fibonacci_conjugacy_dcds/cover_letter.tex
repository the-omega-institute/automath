\begin{letter}{Editors\\Transactions of the American Mathematical Society}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit our manuscript entitled

\medskip
\centerline{\textbf{Overlap thresholds and unbounded future-only inverse depth}}
\centerline{\textbf{for cyclic ranks of Pisot $\beta$-languages}}
\medskip

\noindent for consideration in \textit{Transactions of the American
Mathematical Society}.

The paper studies a cyclic ordered-language-rank recoding of greedy
numeration systems as a finite-window factor code.  The map reduces integer
language ranks modulo the number of canonical words and is not numerical
$\beta$-normalization in $\mathbb Z[\beta]$.  Its principal theorem is a
fixed-system structural estimate over a recognized Pisot numeration class:
for every strictly increasing Pisot linear numeration system $U$ with
$u_0=1$, canonical greedy digits, and canonical normal words, there is an
effectively computable $C_U<\infty$ such that
\[
 \Phi_{U,m}\text{ injective}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \ell_{\rm cau}(U,m)\le C_Um.
\]
No Condition~F or zero-preservation hypothesis is required.  The proof uses
an explicit finite carry interval, separation in the dominant embedding, and
contraction in every Pisot conjugate direction, followed by finitely many
exact small-aperture graph searches.

The contraction also yields a code-independent theorem for every fixed
coefficient bound $D$: in the finite overlap graph of bounded
$U$-representations of zero, absence of a reachable cycle forces every
nonzero path to have fewer than $C(U,D)m$ edges.  This statement does not use
the legal representation language, cyclic rank map, or a factor code, and
its linear order is sharp for a fixed cubic recurrence over
$\{-1,0,1\}$.

The growth order is sharp within one fixed binary cubic system.  For the
leading root $\theta$ of $x^3-2x^2+x-1$, every aperture $m\ge4$ is injective,
the optimal future-only inverse length is
$2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$, the penultimate obstruction set is exactly
$\{\pm E_m\}$, and the next obstruction set is empty.  Thus no theorem valid
for every fixed system in the general class can replace $O_U(m)$ by $o(m)$.
This does not claim numerical optimality of the computed constant or a
universal leading coefficient.

The manuscript also gives a complete two-chamber threshold classification
for all quadratic Pisot bases, identifies the resulting overlap
presentations as right Fischer covers with exact Markov order, and develops
an exact arithmetic quotient of the classical pair graph for nonintegral
simple-Parry bases.  The latter yields a complementary cubic family with
unbounded exact future-only inverse depth.

To the authors' knowledge, this is the first aperture-linear inverse-depth
bound for cyclic language-rank recodings over fixed Pisot numeration systems.
The claim is deliberately limited to this prescribed recoding: the paper does
not claim a theorem about numerical $\beta$-normalization in general,
arbitrary $\beta$-transformations, or all Pisot symbolic dynamics.  The
manuscript gives a concentrated comparison with finite Pisot normalization,
bounded-zero automata, bounded-delay synchronization, Ashley's
state-count-linear decoder bounds, and classical pair/fiber graphs.

We believe the combination of an effective class-wide theorem, fixed-system
sharpness, and exact arithmetic classifications is appropriate for
\textit{Transactions}.  The paper's map is specialized, and the letter does
not claim otherwise; the contribution is the new quantitative mechanism and
its sharp realization across a classical numeration class.
