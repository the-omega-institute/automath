3. Single highest-value change
Resolve the incidence obstruction completely for arbitrary constant k-forms on a box.
The paper should give, beyond coordinate monomials, the exact optimal coefficient-sup norm, characterize all extremal boundary traces, and prove the corresponding stability theorem for general orientation support. The obstruction should become the content of a classification theorem rather than the boundary of the paper.
That would turn the work from an elementary saturation calculation in a separable case into a genuine finite-dimensional geometry of the exterior derivative. I would raise the Results in Mathematics acceptance probability by roughly 20 percentage points, from 22% to about 42%.
4. Weakest load-bearing step
The vulnerable result is the box boundary-readout stability theorem, specifically the passage from the total Stokes deficit to quantitative control on each face.
A hostile referee will ask exactly what norm is used on the trace. The flux argument supports integrated L1 control because the global deficit decomposes into nonnegative facewise slacks. It does not support pointwise, L∞, or generally Lp control for p>1: a trace can have an order-one defect on a sufficiently small boundary set while contributing only a small flux deficit.
Without the theorem’s exact statement and proof, I cannot determine whether the manuscript stays at the valid L1 endpoint. On the formulation suggested by your description, I classify this as repairable, not fatal. If the theorem claims a stronger trace topology without additional regularity or compactness assumptions, it is false in that form and would become fatal to the advertised “quantitative readout.”

Its stated scope specifically welcomes original work from any mathematical area centered on inequalities and also accommodates focused notes with interesting results.
