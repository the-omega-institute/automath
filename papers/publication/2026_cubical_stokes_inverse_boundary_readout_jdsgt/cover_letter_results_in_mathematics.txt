Dear Editors,

We submit "Sharp coefficient bounds for the centered homotopy operator and
quantitative boundary readout on boxes" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. The
paper stands on its sharp box readout theorem: for a box R it identifies the
optimal coefficient-L-infinity value, gives an explicit affine minimizer,
and proves a linear stability estimate showing that the gap from the sharp
interior norm quantitatively controls the boundary trace. In particular,
exact minimizers have the canonical trace on every codimension-one face.

The global cubical theorem is supporting rather than the novelty claim. Under
the stated coherent-orientation and exterior-sink hypotheses, its compatible
profile is max-flow/min-cut, complementary slackness, finite-dimensional LP
duality, and Hoffman's circulation criterion translated to the cubical
setting. We make no claim to a new global patching principle. The combination
of a sharp variational inequality with a carefully delimited cubical
application fits the broad pure-and-applied scope of Results in Mathematics.

Reproducibility: artifacts/ contains scripts checking the cubical patching
calculation and the necessity of the corrected network hypotheses.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
