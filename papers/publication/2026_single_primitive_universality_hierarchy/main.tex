\begin{abstract}
We separate three meanings of universality for one binary primitive.  Level
\(L0\) is expression universality: the cited Odrzywo{\l}ek
arXiv:2603.21852v2 record for \(\eml(x,y)=e^x-\operatorname{Log}y\) is used only
as a representative-existence theorem for its displayed \(\mathcal B_{36}\)
calculator basis.  Level \(L1\) is protocol universality: Zeckendorf normal
forms, valuation transport through \(\NN\), and Church iteration give an internal
computable protocol for \(+\) and \(\times\) on \(\NN\).  The hierarchy is strict
in the following submission-facing sense.  Any finite EML triple whose
\(\pi\)-row, sine row, and punctured absolute-value row are semantically verified
transports Richardson's
identity-to-zero theorem and forbids a computable semantic normalizer or
equality-reflecting compiler for the translated fragment; the paper proves the
punctured absolute-value row internally by \(G(M(u,u))=|u|\) for \(u\ne0\).  The
particular printed strings \(U_{47}\) and \(U_{111}\) instantiate this clause
only under the optional analytic hypothesis \((H_{\mathcal W})\), or under a
separate proof of those two exact real-domain identities for those exact printed
strings; otherwise they are merely source-locked syntax.  The endpoint-root
string \(U_{126}\) is not part of the punctured obstruction.  The intrinsic
fold-height formula below has the same explicit dependency boundary: the ordinary
positive-index Fibonacci interval maximum is imported from
Koc\'abov\'a--Mas\'akov\'a--Pelantov\'a, while the shifted half-interval and
\(\Fold_m\)-fibre conversions are proved here.  Independently,
multiplication on \(\NN_{\ge1}\) cannot be faithfully read from the ambient
product on one free monogenic orbit, except on the trivial monoid or a single
cyclic multiplicative ray.  Level \(L2\) adds finite-fibre certificates over the
fixed Zeckendorf normal-form sets: the unmodified fold has intrinsic fixed-degree
moment transfer and exact \(q=\infty\) height
\(M_{2s-1}=F_{s+1}\), \(M_{2s}=2F_s\), while computable finite-fibre covers give
a separate finite-state-transfer budget obstruction.  Thus the unconditional core result is a verified-triple relative
\(L0/L1/L2\) hierarchy; the printed \(U_{47}\)/\(U_{111}\) branch is a
conditional specialization, not a premise of the core theorem.  It is not a term-by-term proof of the external
calculator table, an online Zeckendorf arithmetic paper, a global rigidity
classification of single-primitive protocols, a full intrinsic fibre
thermodynamics, or an \(L3\) capacity theory.
\end{abstract}
