\begin{abstract}
We isolate what a one-binary-operation representation theorem does, and does
not, supply.  For the operation \(\eml(x,y)=e^x-\operatorname{Log}y\), the
Odrzywo{\l}ek arXiv:2603.21852v2 record is cited only as an \(L0\)
representative-existence result for the displayed \(\mathcal B_{36}\)
calculator basis; the individual representatives are not reproved here.  The main
positive protocol is smaller and effective: Zeckendorf normal forms, valuation
transport through \(\NN\), and the classical Church-iteration identities give
compiled normal-form operations for \(+\) and \(\times\) on \(\NN\).  The two
  primary obstructions are then separated from this positive protocol.  First,
Richardson undecidability gives a relative obstruction: it forbids a computable
semantic normalizer on every translated Richardson fragment obtained from
supplied finite EML witnesses for \(\pi\), sine, and punctured absolute value
after those three real-domain identities have been proved; the paper proves the
absolute-value row internally by
\(G(M(u,u))=|u|\) for \(u\ne0\).  This uses Richardson's punctured
absolute-value hypothesis and therefore does not require, and in the same
principal-branch semantics cannot have, a pure EML endpoint value for
\(\sqrt0\).  The endpoint-root string \(U_{126}\) is not part of the
punctured obstruction.  Second,
multiplication on \(\NN_{\ge1}\) cannot be injectively read from the ambient
product on one free monogenic orbit; all such same-level readouts reduce to a
prime-weight exponent skeleton, and the faithful restrictions are exactly a
single cyclic multiplicative ray (besides the trivial monoid).  The
  finite-fiber moment material is a subordinate certificate layer:
  the unmodified \(\Fold_m\) moments have intrinsic fixed finite-state transfer
  degree by degree, with a sharp second-moment recurrence, and the intrinsic
  fibre height has the exact law \(M_{2s-1}=F_{s+1}\), \(M_{2s}=2F_s\), yielding
  the zero-temperature entropy endpoint \((1/2)\log\varphi\).  Equivalently, the
  intrinsic fixed-degree moments and the intrinsic height are exponentially
  budgeted with a sharp height slope, whereas the additional \(L2\) strictness is
  exactly the finite-state transfer budget obstruction for computable
  finite-fiber covers over the fixed Zeckendorf normal-form sets, not an
  intrinsic finite-state failure theorem about the original \(\Fold_m\) fibers.
  A dependency-cut theorem records the resulting
  self-contained core: deleting the externally imported \(\mathcal B_{36}\)
  existence assertion and the optional \((H_{\mathcal W})\) printed-string
  assumption leaves the verified-triple relative punctured Richardson
  obstruction, the Zeckendorf
  protocol, the one-orbit obstruction, and the cover-relative \(L2\) obstruction
  intact, while removing the calculator-basis headline and any concrete printed
  \(U_{47}\)/\(U_{111}\) Richardson specialization unless those two analytic
  identities are separately supplied.
\end{abstract}
