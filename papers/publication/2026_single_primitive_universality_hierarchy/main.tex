\begin{abstract}
For \(m\ge1\), let \(\Fold_m:\{0,1\}^{m+1}\to X_m\) be reduction modulo
\(F_{m+2}\) followed by greedy Zeckendorf normalization, with
\(F_1=1,F_2=2\).  We prove the following fixed-degree statement: for every
fixed integer \(q\ge0\), there is an effectively constructible integer matrix
\(T_q\), independent of \(m\), whose matrix coefficient is the collision
moment \(S_q(m)=\sum_{x\in X_m}|\Fold_m^{-1}(x)|^q\).  The key point is
\emph{losslessness}: the bounded signed-Fibonacci carry automaton deletes no
accepting path.  After trimming, its weighted graph is primitive for every
\(q\ge1\), giving a Perron asymptotic and a fixed-degree pressure.  For \(q=2\)
we derive the exact recurrence
\(S_2(m)=2S_2(m-1)+2S_2(m-2)-2S_2(m-3)\) for \(m\ge4\), with initial
values \(6,14,36\).  We also prove the exact maximal fibre heights
\(M_{2s-1}=F_{s+1}\) and \(M_{2s}=2F_s\).  As a concise consequence, the bivariate
moment series is not rational: fixed-\(q\) transfer is uniform in resolution,
but the transfer dimension and matrix cannot be chosen as one rational law
uniform in the moment degree.  The only external enumerative input is the
ordinary Fibonacci interval-maximum formula of Kocábová--Masáková--Pelantová;
its conversion to the shifted fold interval is proved here.
\end{abstract}
