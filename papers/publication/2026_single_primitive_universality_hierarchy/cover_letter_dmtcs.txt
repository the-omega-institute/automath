Dear Editors,

We submit the short note "Carry Automata, Primitive Transfer, and Fibre
Heights for Zeckendorf Folds" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. For each fixed
moment degree, the note gives a resolution-independent carry automaton for
the Zeckendorf fold and proves that its bounded signed-Fibonacci carry cube
is lossless. In degree two it obtains the exact recurrence
S_2(m) = 2S_2(m-1) + 2S_2(m-2) - 2S_2(m-3) and the pairing identity
S_2(m) = T_2(m) + 2S_2(m-2).

The claim is deliberately limited. Losslessness is the key correctness
verification, not a new general method; the contribution is the explicit,
economical automaton for these residue-fold moments rather than the discovery
that some finite automaton exists. The ordinary-integer interval-sum
recurrence is due in substance to Chow and Jones and is cited as such; it is
not claimed here. This scope and the note's automata-and-combinatorics focus
motivate submission to DMTCS as a short note.

Reproducibility: the scripts in artifacts/ verify the fold moments, the
pairing identity, and the comparison with the ordinary recurrence literature.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
