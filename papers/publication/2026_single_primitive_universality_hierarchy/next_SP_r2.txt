You are refereeing the attached 10-page paper for Discrete Mathematics & Theoretical Computer
Science. This manuscript is the surviving core of an 89-page one that you rejected outright: you
said the advertised L0/L1/L2 hierarchy was not a separation theorem, that repairing it would mean

(1) IS THE LOSSLESSNESS STEP REALLY THE SUBSTANTIVE ONE? You singled it out last time: that the
bounded signed-Fibonacci carry automaton deletes no accepting path, and that this is not implied by
a growth estimate. The paper now leans its whole novelty claim on that. Read the proof and tell me
whether it is a genuine argument or a routine verification dressed up. If a competent reader in
automatic sequences would call it standard, say so plainly; the contribution collapses to a
computation if you do, and I would rather know now.

I have independently confirmed the exact statements: S_2 = 6, 14, 36 then
2 S_2(m-1) + 2 S_2(m-2) - 2 S_2(m-3), no violations to m = 18; and M_{2s-1} = F_{s+1},
M_{2s} = 2 F_s through s = 7. Do not spend the round on arithmetic. Spend it on whether the

(2) THE LITERATURE COMPARISON. The introduction contrasts our S_2 with the ordinary-integer
interval sum U(m) = sum over n < F_{m+2} of R(n)^2, where R counts representations by DISTINCT
Fibonacci numbers. U has minimal characteristic polynomial (X-1)(X+1)(X^3-2X^2-2X+2) while S_2
satisfies the bare cubic, and the paper claims the pairing of the two residue representatives is
what removes the parity factor. I verified the polynomials myself. I want two things from you:
(a) is that ordinary-integer result already in Chow-Jones-Slattery, Stockmeyer or
Bicknell-Johnson, and if so must it be cited as known rather than presented as computed here;
(b) is the "pairing removes the parity factor" explanation actually proved in the paper, or only
observed?

(3) VENUE AND ODDS. Is DMTCS right for a 10-page paper of this kind? Name the strongest venue where
acceptance is genuinely plausible, give a rough numeric probability, and say why you are not naming
the next one up. If 10 pages of this material is a note rather than an article, say which journal
takes notes of this kind.
