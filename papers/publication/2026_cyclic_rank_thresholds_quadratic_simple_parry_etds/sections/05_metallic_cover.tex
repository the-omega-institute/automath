\subsection{The metallic specialization}
\label{sec:metallic-cover}

Fix $A\ge1$ and let $\beta_A$ be the positive root of $x^2-Ax-1$.  Define
\[
Q_0^{(A)}=1,\qquad Q_1^{(A)}=A+1,
\qquad Q_{j+2}^{(A)}=AQ_{j+1}^{(A)}+Q_j^{(A)}.
\]
On $\mathcal A_A=\{0,\ldots,A\}$, the legal words are
\[
X_m^{(A)}=\{x\in\mathcal A_A^m:
x_k=A\Rightarrow x_{k-1}=0\text{ for }2\le k\le m\},
\]
with colexicographic rank
\[
\Rank_{A,m}(x)=\sum_{k=1}^m x_kQ_{k-1}^{(A)}.
\]
These formulas are the negative-chamber specialization $(a,b)=(A,1)$ of
Definition~\ref{def:quadratic-parry-fold} and
\eqref{eq:qminus}.  They also have the boundary-modified constant-type
interpretation
\[
Q_j^{(A)}=q_j+q_{j-1},
\]
where $q_{j+1}=Aq_j+q_{j-1}$ are the convergent denominators for
$[0;A,A,\ldots]$.  The weights are not the standard Ostrowski weights
$q_j$, and the lowest digit $A$ remains allowed; the model uses the
constant-type local constraint followed by cyclic rank reduction
\cite{HieronymiTerry2018}.

For $A=1$, $Q_j^{(1)}=F_{j+2}$, the legal language is the Fibonacci language,
and the recoding is exactly the map of Sections~\ref{sec:stabilization}--
\ref{sec:stab:branch-and-conjugacy}.  Thus the negative-chamber case $b=1$
in the dual parameterization is already represented by this construction,
while the full quadratic theorem supplies its threshold classification.

\begin{corollary}[Metallic thresholds as a quadratic specialization]
\label{cor:metallic-specialization}
For the preceding rank data, excluding the trivial aperture $m=1$,
\[
\{m\ge2:\Phi_{A,m}\text{ is a topological conjugacy onto }Y_{A,m}\}
=
\begin{cases}
\{3,4,5,\ldots\},&A=1,\\
\{2,3,4,\ldots\},&A\ge2.
\end{cases}
\]
For every aperture in this set and every $n\ge m$, the finite-block map is a
bijection onto its image and
\[
|\mathcal L_n(Y_{A,m})|=(A+1)^{n+m-1}.
\]
Its memory-zero inverse has anticipation at most $m-1$.  The natural overlap
graph is the right Fischer cover in the fixed stabilized alphabet, finite
block injectivity begins exactly at length $m$, and the image is exactly an
$m$-step SFT.  Hence the golden mean is the unique metallic parameter whose
nontrivial threshold is three.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The polynomial $x^2-Ax-1$ is precisely the negative quadratic chamber with
$(a,b)=(A,1)$.  The threshold formula is therefore the specialization of
Theorem~\ref{thm:full-quadratic-pisot-threshold}: it is exceptional exactly
when $A=1$.  The exact finite-block onset, inverse, Fischer-cover, and Markov
order statements follow from
Theorem~\ref{thm:quadratic-exact-local-order}.  The language count is the
cardinality of the full raw block alphabet once the finite-block map is
injective.  In the golden case these identifications agree with
Lemma~\ref{lem:m3-decoder}, Theorem~\ref{thm:block-invertibility}, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:m2-branch-locus}; no separate metallic decoder or
Fischer-cover proof is required.
\end{proof}
