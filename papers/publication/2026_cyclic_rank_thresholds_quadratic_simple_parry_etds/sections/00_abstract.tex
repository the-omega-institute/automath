\begin{abstract}
We determine exact overlap thresholds and local inverse structure for cyclic
language-rank recodings of quadratic and simple-Parry beta-languages.  A
legal length-$m$ word is identified with its colexicographic positional rank;
the same rank is evaluated on the full digit cube, reduced modulo the number
of legal words, returned to the legal language, and overlapped along the
shift.  This is cyclic reduction of integer language ranks, not numerical
$\beta$-normalization.

The Fibonacci model has sharp nontrivial threshold three.  We give its
explicit three-window decoder, low-window counterexamples, and complete
two-window branch locus.  For every quadratic Pisot base, the least
nontrivial aperture is three exactly for the roots of
$x^2-ax-a$ and $x^2-ax+1$, and is two otherwise.  We also determine chamber
duality, future-only inverse bounds, the critical two-fixed-point normal
form, exact finite-block onset, the canonical right Fischer cover, and exact
Markov order in the stabilized alphabet.  The metallic family becomes a
transparent specialization of this classification.

For every nonintegral simple-Parry base, a coordinatewise-difference quotient
of the classical pair graph preserves every collision and gives an exact
longest-path formula for future-only inverse depth.  We derive the complete
aperture-two trichotomy without a Pisot hypothesis.  Applied to Bassino's
cubic simple-Parry Pisot family, the quotient gives injective apertures
$m_n=n-1$ with exact future-only inverse length $n-1$, hence unbounded depth
across the family.
\end{abstract}
