\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Cyclic language-rank recodings of quadratic Pisot shifts have threshold two
except on the negative diagonal $x^2-ax-a$ and the positive norm-one family
$x^2-ax+1$, where the threshold is three.  At every aperture at least three,
their future-only inverse length is two for negative conjugate and three for
positive conjugate.  The same recurrence argument identifies the critical
two-fixed-point fiber, finite-block onset, Fischer cover, and Markov order.

For general simple-Parry languages, coordinatewise difference gives a finite
arithmetic quotient of the pair graph without losing collisions.  Directed
path length is exactly future-only inverse depth, and reachable cycles decide
noninjectivity.  Bassino's cubic family has injective apertures with depth
$n-1$, showing that no uniform bound exists across cubic systems.

Thus the quadratic chamber classification is rigid, whereas fixed algebraic
degree three already permits unbounded future-only depth across systems.
