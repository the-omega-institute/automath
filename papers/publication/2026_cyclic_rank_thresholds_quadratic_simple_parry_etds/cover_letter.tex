\begin{letter}{Editors\\Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our manuscript ``Cyclic rank recodings of quadratic and
simple-Parry beta-languages: overlap thresholds and future-only inverse depth''
for publication in \emph{Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems}.

The paper classifies the exact nontrivial overlap threshold for every
quadratic Pisot beta-language and determines chamber duality, exact
future-only inverse depth, critical fibers, exact finite-block onset, the canonical
right Fischer cover, and exact Markov order in the stabilized alphabet.  For
arbitrary nonintegral simple-Parry bases it proves an exact
coordinate-difference quotient of the classical pair graph and a complete
aperture-two trichotomy.  Bassino's cubic family then gives injective
apertures with exact inverse lengths tending to infinity.

The related fixed-system bounded-zero transient theorem is treated in our
companion manuscript ``Linear overlap transients for bounded zero
representations in Pisot numeration: sharp inverse depth for cyclic rank
recodings.''  The submitted article includes only the technical appendices
needed for its arithmetic proofs.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
been approved by both authors.  The authors declare no competing interests.

\closing{Sincerely,\\Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang}
