\section{Displayed algebraic certificates}\label{app:reproducibility-note}

The exact algebraic certificates used in this article are the displayed identities listed below.
Each item is either a determinant or pseudo-remainder criterion printed in the text, or a coefficient comparison whose inputs and outputs are explicitly displayed.
When cited by a theorem or proposition, these identities are ordinary algebraic inputs to the proof.
Where a resultant or discriminant is invoked, the relevant Sylvester matrix, Bareiss pivots, expanded polynomial, or primitive factorization is printed before use.
Where a sign count is invoked, the primitive Sturm sequence, its sign-controlling leading coefficients, the denominator exclusions, and its sign-variation table are printed before use.
Where endpoint constants are invoked, the branch ansatz, coefficient equations, and resulting constants are printed before use.
Consequently the algebraic verification of the theorem chain is internal to the manuscript.

As optional supplementary verification, an exact-arithmetic checker may be run from the paper directory:
\[
\texttt{python scripts/check\_algebraic\_certificates.py}.
\]
It recomputes the printed resultants, Sturm and pseudo-remainder data, positive-axis sign table, and endpoint branch/amplitude coefficient equations displayed in the appendices.
The article does not rely on the checker as an oracle: the proof record is the collection of displayed formulas, determinant inputs, sign tables, and coefficient comparisons in the manuscript.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Appendix~\ref{app:disc} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_\lambda(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi)\), the Bareiss pivots, the expanded polynomial \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}, and its factorization.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination} gives the two input polynomials for the cubic branch-resultant computation, the corresponding \(6\times6\) Sylvester determinant, and its primitive output \(-64(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32)\).
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-criterion} uses only direct endpoint substitutions, the discriminant factorization already certified in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, and the opposite-modulus resultant \(\Res_x(\Pi(x,y),\Pi(-x,y))=16y^3(y-1)^2(y+1)\).  The determinant reduction \(\Pi(-x,y)-\Pi(x,y)=2x(x^2-1)\), multiplicativity step, and primitive output are displayed in \eqref{eq:opposite-modulus-resultant}.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_t(N,D)\), its Bareiss pivots, the resultant \(y^3(y-1)^3(y+1)^6\), the exceptional cancellations at \(y=-1,0,1\), and the primitive Sturm pseudo-remainders and variation table used on the positive-axis cells.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching} gives the endpoint branch and amplitude computations from the local equation \eqref{eq:endpoint-recursive-polynomial}, the residue formula, the coefficient comparisons for \(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\), and the compact-uniform remainder estimate \(R_m(-u^2/m^2)=O_K(m^{-2})\); these displayed identities are imported into Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-cosine-hypotheses}, Remark~\ref{rem:endpoint-zero-law-data-package}, and Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-zero-quantization}.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:projective-infinity} gives the projective-infinity homogeneous balance \(\Pi_\infty=(\lambda^2-y)^2\) and the coefficient comparison in \eqref{eq:infinity-expansion} leading to the displayed large-\(y\) branch and logarithmic-slope expansions.  These identities are included as an exact consistency calculation for the opposite end of the positive branch.
\end{enumerate}
The logical dependencies for the theorems are the labelled results and the displayed exact algebraic identities in the preceding sections and appendices.

\paragraph{Printed-certificate dependency summary.}
The main certificate dependencies are local and displayed.
Proposition~\ref{prop:minimal-order} uses the printed Sylvester determinant and factorization of \(\Res_t(N,D)\), together with the exceptional cancellations at \(y=-1,0,1\).
Theorem~\ref{thm:elliptic-reduction}, Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination}, and Corollary~\ref{cor:disc-factor} use the displayed substitution, resultant matrices, Bareiss pivots, expanded polynomials, and factorizations in Section~\ref{sec:genus-one} and Appendix~\ref{app:disc}.
Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-criterion} uses the discriminant determinant, endpoint substitutions \eqref{eq:positive-axis-resultants}, opposite-modulus resultant \eqref{eq:opposite-modulus-resultant}, and the primitive Sturm chain and variation table of Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-axis-sturm-chain}.
The endpoint zero theorem and its preparatory results use the branch expansions, amplitude identities, coefficient comparisons, coordinate transfer, and uniform remainder estimates displayed in Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching} and Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-coordinate-transfer}.
Lemma~\ref{lem:projective-infinity-consistency} is the coefficient comparison \eqref{eq:infinity-expansion} in Appendix~\ref{app:projective-infinity}.
Thus every certificate-dependent proof reads from a displayed formula whose algebraic input and output are printed in the article.
