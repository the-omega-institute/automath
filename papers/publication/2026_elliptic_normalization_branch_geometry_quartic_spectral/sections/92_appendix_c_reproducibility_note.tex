\section{Supplementary reproducibility note}\label{app:reproducibility-note}

The proof-bearing computations in this article are the explicit algebraic identities and estimates displayed in the text: the Sylvester determinant in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, the numerator--denominator resultant in Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies}, and the endpoint expansion package in Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching}.
Section~\ref{sec:verification} records only independent numerical checks of these conclusions.

For reproducibility, the checks reconstruct \(Z_m(y)\) from the recurrence \eqref{eq:recurrence}, evaluate the four roots of \eqref{eq:spectral-poly} near the real branch values, remove the algebraically forced factor \((y+1)^2\) from Proposition~\ref{prop:double-zero}, and compare the first negative zeros with Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-zero-quantization}.
All logical dependencies needed for the theorems are contained in the labelled results and displayed identities of the article itself.
