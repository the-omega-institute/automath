\section{Computational verification checks}\label{app:reproducibility-note}

The proof-bearing computations in this article are the displayed algebraic identities listed below.
Each item is either a determinant/pseudo-remainder certificate printed in the text or a coefficient comparison whose inputs and outputs are explicitly displayed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Appendix~\ref{app:disc} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_\lambda(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi)\), the Bareiss pivots, the expanded polynomial \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}, and its factorization.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination} gives the two input polynomials for the cubic branch-resultant computation, the corresponding \(6\times6\) Sylvester determinant, and its primitive output \(-64(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32)\).
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-certificate} uses only direct endpoint substitutions, the discriminant factorization already certified in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, and the opposite-modulus resultant \(\Res_x(\Pi(x,y),\Pi(-x,y))=16y^3(y-1)^2(y+1)\).  The determinant reduction \(\Pi(-x,y)-\Pi(x,y)=2x(x^2-1)\), multiplicativity step, and primitive output are displayed in \eqref{eq:opposite-modulus-resultant}.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_t(N,D)\), its Bareiss pivots, the resultant \(y^3(y-1)^3(y+1)^6\), the exceptional cancellations at \(y=-1,0,1\), and the primitive Sturm pseudo-remainders used on the positive-axis cells.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching} gives the endpoint branch and amplitude computations from the local equation, the residue formula, the coefficient comparisons for \(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\), and the compact-uniform remainder estimates; these displayed identities are the inputs to Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-cosine-hypotheses} and Lemma~\ref{lem:two-branch-oscillation-law}.
\end{enumerate}
The optional script \texttt{scripts/generate\_validation\_figure.py} is not used as a proof source; it only reproduces numerical plots from the already displayed exact formulae.
The following independent checks compare the exact statements with direct recurrence evaluation and root computation.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The real-axis ordering of the moduli of the four roots of \(\Pi(\lambda,y)=0\) agrees with Theorem~\ref{thm:ep-classification} and Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-certificate}: the branch value \(y=0\) is the only maximal-modulus collision on the positive side, while \(y=1\) and \(y=\ySub\) are non-dominant collisions.
\item The first negative zeros obtained from the recurrence for \(m=80,120,200\) have scaled values \(-m^2y_{m,k}\), for fixed \(k\), matching the leading constants \(\pi^2(2k-1)^2/2\) and the two-term correction in Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-zero-quantization}.
\item The factor \((y+1)^2\) from Proposition~\ref{prop:double-zero} appears in the tested recurrence polynomials for \(m\ge2\), while no corresponding forced zero appears at the non-dominant branch value \(y=1\).
\end{enumerate}
These checks are finite numerical verifications of the displayed exact formulas; the logical dependencies for the theorems are the labelled results and algebraic identities in the preceding sections and appendices.
