\section{Displayed algebraic certificates}\label{app:reproducibility-note}

The exact algebraic certificates used in this article are the displayed identities listed below.
Each item is either a determinant or pseudo-remainder criterion printed in the text, or a coefficient comparison whose inputs and outputs are explicitly displayed.  When cited by a theorem or proposition, these identities are ordinary algebraic inputs to the proof.
The accompanying repository also contains the independent exact check
\[
\texttt{scripts/check\_algebraic\_certificates.py},
\]
which recomputes the determinant, resultant, opposite-modulus, and Sturm pseudo-remainder identities over \(\QQ[y]\) from the printed formulas.
Its checked output labels are exactly the displayed expanded discriminant, discriminant factorization, cubic branch resultant, minimal-order resultant, opposite-modulus resultant, three Euclidean Sturm remainders, three primitive pseudo-remainders, the three positive-axis sign-variation rows, and the auxiliary denominator-cell check.
The endpoint branch and amplitude coefficients are audited separately by the printed coefficient comparisons in Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching}, because their proof uses the local ansatz and initial values \(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\) rather than a single resultant.
The script is a synchronization check for the displayed certificates, not a hidden replacement for the algebra written in the manuscript.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Appendix~\ref{app:disc} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_\lambda(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi)\), the Bareiss pivots, the expanded polynomial \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}, and its factorization.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination} gives the two input polynomials for the cubic branch-resultant computation, the corresponding \(6\times6\) Sylvester determinant, and its primitive output \(-64(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32)\).
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-criterion} uses only direct endpoint substitutions, the discriminant factorization already certified in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, and the opposite-modulus resultant \(\Res_x(\Pi(x,y),\Pi(-x,y))=16y^3(y-1)^2(y+1)\).  The determinant reduction \(\Pi(-x,y)-\Pi(x,y)=2x(x^2-1)\), multiplicativity step, and primitive output are displayed in \eqref{eq:opposite-modulus-resultant}.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies} gives the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_t(N,D)\), its Bareiss pivots, the resultant \(y^3(y-1)^3(y+1)^6\), the exceptional cancellations at \(y=-1,0,1\), and the primitive Sturm pseudo-remainders and variation table used on the positive-axis cells.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching} gives the endpoint branch and amplitude computations from the local equation, the residue formula, the coefficient comparisons for \(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\), and the compact-uniform remainder estimates; these displayed identities are the inputs to Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-cosine-hypotheses} and Lemma~\ref{lem:two-branch-oscillation-law}.
\item Appendix~\ref{app:projective-infinity} gives the projective-infinity homogeneous balance \(\Pi_\infty=(\lambda^2-y)^2\) and the coefficient comparison in \eqref{eq:infinity-expansion} leading to the displayed large-\(y\) branch and logarithmic-slope expansions.  These identities are included as an exact consistency calculation for the opposite end of the positive branch.
\end{enumerate}
The logical dependencies for the theorems are the labelled results and the displayed exact algebraic identities in the preceding sections and appendices.

\paragraph{Printed-certificate dependency ledger.}
Every theorem, proposition, lemma, or corollary in the main proof chain that uses an exact computed certificate depends only on one of the displayed algebraic identities listed below, and not on the verification script.
For each named certificate, the manuscript prints both the algebraic inputs and the algebraic output used in the proof: the relevant Sylvester or pseudo-remainder inputs are displayed before the determinant/resultant value is invoked, the Sturm signs are read from the printed leading coefficients and final constant term, and the endpoint coefficient computations display the local ansatz, the coefficient equations, and the resulting constants.
The dependency ledger is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:minimal-order} uses the displayed numerator \(N(t,y)\), denominator \(D(t,y)\), the \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_t(N,D)\), its Bareiss pivots, and the printed factorization
\[
\Res_t(N,D)=y^3(y-1)^3(y+1)^6
\]
in Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies}.  The proof uses only the vanishing locus of this displayed resultant and the separately printed exceptional cancellations at \(y=-1,0,1\).
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:elliptic-reduction} uses the displayed normalization substitution \(\eta=y-\lambda^2\), \(Y=2y+1-2\lambda^2\), the ramification polynomial \eqref{eq:ramification-poly}, and the cubic branch-resultant identity \eqref{eq:cubic-branch-elimination-identity}.  The inputs to that resultant are exactly the two polynomials printed in Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination}.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination} uses the two displayed input polynomials, its printed \(6\times6\) Sylvester determinant, and the primitive output
\[
-64(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32).
\]
No later argument uses an unprinted specialization of this elimination.
\item Corollary~\ref{cor:disc-factor} uses the displayed \(7\times7\) Sylvester determinant for \(\Res_\lambda(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi)\), the printed Bareiss pivots, the expanded polynomial \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}, and the displayed factorization in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}.
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-criterion} and Theorem~\ref{thm:canonical-branch-dominance} use the discriminant determinant from Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, the endpoint substitutions \eqref{eq:positive-axis-resultants}, the opposite-modulus resultant \eqref{eq:opposite-modulus-resultant}, and the primitive Sturm pseudo-remainders and variation table of Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-axis-sturm-chain}.  The Sturm input polynomial, derivative, pseudo-remainders, excluded denominator factors, and sign-variation table are all printed before they are used to count roots on the positive-axis cells.
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-cosine-hypotheses}, Lemma~\ref{lem:two-branch-oscillation-law}, Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-coordinate-transfer}, and Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-zero-quantization} use the endpoint coefficient comparisons and compact-uniform remainder estimates printed in Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-coefficient-computation} and Proposition~\ref{prop:endpoint-expansion}.  The displayed inputs are the branch ansatz in the coordinate \(y=s^2\), the residue formula, the exact coefficient comparisons for \(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\), and the two noncolliding-branch estimates leading to \(R_m(-u^2/m^2)=O_K(m^{-2})\).
\item Lemma~\ref{lem:projective-infinity-consistency} uses the homogeneous balance and coefficient comparison \eqref{eq:infinity-expansion} printed in Appendix~\ref{app:projective-infinity}.
\end{enumerate}
Inspecting the cited proofs gives the advertised dependency separation.
Proposition~\ref{prop:minimal-order} invokes only the vanishing locus of \(\Res_t(N,D)\), whose determinant, pivots, and factorization are printed in Appendix~\ref{app:degeneracies}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:elliptic-reduction}, Lemma~\ref{lem:cubic-branch-elimination}, and Corollary~\ref{cor:disc-factor} cite only the displayed substitution, resultant matrices, Bareiss pivots, expanded polynomials, and coefficient factorizations recorded in Section~\ref{sec:genus-one} and Appendix~\ref{app:disc}.
Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-axis-dominance-criterion} states its certificate explicitly before its proof: the discriminant determinant, endpoint substitutions, opposite-modulus resultant, and the primitive Sturm chain of Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-axis-sturm-chain}; Theorem~\ref{thm:canonical-branch-dominance} uses that lemma as its positive-axis algebraic input.
The endpoint zero theorem and its preparatory results use the branch expansions, amplitude identities, coefficient comparisons, coordinate transfer, and uniform remainder estimates displayed in Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching} and Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-coordinate-transfer}.
Finally, Lemma~\ref{lem:projective-infinity-consistency} is itself a coefficient comparison in Appendix~\ref{app:projective-infinity}.
Thus every certificate-facing proof reads from a printed formula whose input and output have already been displayed in the manuscript.
The script named above recomputes these printed identities from their displayed inputs, but no implication in any listed proof uses the script as a mathematical premise.
