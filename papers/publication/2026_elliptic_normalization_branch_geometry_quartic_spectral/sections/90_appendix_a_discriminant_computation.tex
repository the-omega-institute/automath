\section{Discriminant computation}\label{app:disc}

Recall
\[
\Pi(\lambda,y)=\lambda^4-\lambda^3-(2y+1)\lambda^2+\lambda+y(y+1).
\]
Its derivative with respect to \(\lambda\) is
\[
\partial_\lambda \Pi(\lambda,y)=4\lambda^3-3\lambda^2-2(2y+1)\lambda+1.
\]

For a monic polynomial \(P(\lambda)\) of degree \(n\), the discriminant satisfies
\[
\Disc_\lambda P = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\Res_\lambda(P,P').
\]
Here \(n=4\) so \((-1)^{n(n-1)/2}=(-1)^6=1\), and thus
\[
\Disc_\lambda \Pi(\lambda,y)=\Res_\lambda\bigl(\Pi(\lambda,y),\partial_\lambda \Pi(\lambda,y)\bigr).
\]

The resultant is the determinant of the following \(7\times 7\) Sylvester matrix, formed from the coefficient rows of \(\Pi\) and \(\partial_\lambda\Pi\):
\[
\Res_\lambda\bigl(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi\bigr)
=
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)&0&0\\
0&1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)&0\\
0&0&1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)\\
4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0&0&0\\
0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0&0\\
0&0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0\\
0&0&0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
A direct expansion of this determinant gives the audit-ready polynomial
\begin{equation}\label{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}
\Res_\lambda\bigl(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi\bigr)
=
-256y^5-155y^4+246y^3+133y^2+32y.
\end{equation}
Factoring \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial} yields the complete factorization
\[
\Disc_{\lambda}\Pi(\lambda,y)=-y(y-1)\bigl(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32\bigr),
\]
which is the claim in Corollary~\ref{cor:disc-factor}.
