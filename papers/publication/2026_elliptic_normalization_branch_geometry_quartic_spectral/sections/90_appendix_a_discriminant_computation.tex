\section{Discriminant computation}\label{app:disc}

This appendix records the exact discriminant identity used in Corollary~\ref{cor:disc-factor} and Proposition~\ref{prop:positive-axis-dominance-package}.  The determinant, its expanded form, and its factorization are displayed below, so the proof does not require any external certificate file.
Recall
\[
\Pi(\lambda,y)=\lambda^4-\lambda^3-(2y+1)\lambda^2+\lambda+y(y+1),
\qquad
\partial_\lambda\Pi=4\lambda^3-3\lambda^2-2(2y+1)\lambda+1.
\]
Since \(\Pi\) is monic of degree four,
\[
\Disc_\lambda\Pi=\Res_\lambda(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi).
\]
The Sylvester determinant is
\begin{center}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
$\displaystyle
\Res_\lambda\bigl(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi\bigr)
=
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)&0&0\\
0&1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)&0\\
0&0&1&-1&-(2y+1)&1&y(y+1)\\
4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0&0&0\\
0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0&0\\
0&0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1&0\\
0&0&0&4&-3&-2(2y+1)&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$%
}
\end{center}
Exact expansion gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial}
\Res_\lambda\bigl(\Pi,\partial_\lambda\Pi\bigr)
=
-256y^5-155y^4+246y^3+133y^2+32y.
\end{equation}
Factoring \eqref{eq:disc-expanded-polynomial} yields
\[
\Disc_{\lambda}\Pi(\lambda,y)=-y(y-1)\bigl(256y^3+411y^2+165y+32\bigr).
\]
This is the only discriminant input imported elsewhere in the paper.
