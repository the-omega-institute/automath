\section{Computational reproducibility policy}\label{app:pseudocode}

This appendix records the editorial policy for computations accompanying the article.
All results used in the proof chain are the labelled algebraic and analytic statements in Sections~\ref{sec:genus-one}--\ref{sec:boundary-zeros}, the discriminant computation in Appendix~\ref{app:disc}, and the endpoint expansion package in Appendix~\ref{app:endpoint-branching}.
The numerical checks reported in Section~\ref{sec:verification} are independent consistency checks and are not assumptions in any theorem or proof.

For reproducibility, the computations consist of recurrence construction from \eqref{eq:recurrence}, high-precision evaluation of the four roots of \eqref{eq:spectral-poly} near the real branch values, symbolic removal of the forced factor \((y+1)^2\) from Proposition~\ref{prop:double-zero}, and comparison of the nearest negative zeros with Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint-zero-quantization}.
The accompanying code and optional plotting instructions are maintained as supplementary material rather than as part of the article source.
