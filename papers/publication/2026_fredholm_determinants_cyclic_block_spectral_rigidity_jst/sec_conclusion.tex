\section{Closing problems}\label{sec:conclusion}

The preceding sections prove the exact trace-class cyclic divisor
threshold, the normal classification, the trace-norm minimizer
classification in the full determinant fiber, the Schur-entry stability
estimate for near-minimizers, and the sharp Schatten
\(\mathcal S_p\) regularized determinant analogue. One natural remaining
problem is to classify determinant fibers modulo trace-class
quasinilpotent stabilization. For summable cyclic data \(\mathcal D\),
write
\[
  \mathcal F(\mathcal D)
  :=
  \{T\in\sone:\det(I-zT)=D_{\mathcal D}(z)\text{ as a germ at }0\}.
\]
By \Cref{cor:nonnormal-fiber-unbounded}, every
\(L_{\mathcal D}\oplus Q\) with \(Q\in\sone\) quasinilpotent belongs to
\(\mathcal F(\mathcal D)\). Determine the quotient of
\(\mathcal F(\mathcal D)\) under the stabilization relation
\[
  \begin{aligned}
    T\sim_{\mathrm q}T'
    \iff{}& \exists\,Q,Q'\in\sone\text{ quasinilpotent such that} \\
    & T\oplus Q
    \text{ and }
    T'\oplus Q'
    \text{ are unitarily equivalent.}
  \end{aligned}
\]
Equivalently, decide whether every stabilized determinant-fiber class is
represented by the canonical normal cyclic model, or whether nontrivial
nonnormal invariants remain after quasinilpotent stabilization.
