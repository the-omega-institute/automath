\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The main result identifies the trace-class Fredholm determinant fiber of a
prescribed cyclic divisor.  The exact threshold
\[
  \sum_{j\in J}m_jn_j\abs{\beta_j}^{1/n_j}<\infty
\]
is precisely the condition under which the normal cyclic-block model exists.
At this threshold the determinant collapses the cyclic data to the intrinsic
non-zero spectral multiplicity function \(M_{\mathcal D}\).  Normal
realizations are classified by this collapsed multiset up to zero summands, and
the same model is the sharp trace-norm minimizer in the full trace-class
determinant fiber.  Equality in the trace-norm bound is rigid: it forces
unitary equivalence to the normal cyclic model, again modulo a zero summand.

The remaining results quantify and organize the consequences of this rigidity.
After a Ringrose triangularization has been chosen, near-minimal trace norm
pinches fixed Schur entries; global infinite-dimensional distance estimates
require the stated Schur-tail and determinant-tail hypotheses.  For finite
collapsed support, the fixed-dimensional matrix fibers have the classical
Weyl--Horn singular-value body determined by the zero-padded collapsed
eigenvalue vector, yielding the corresponding symmetric-gauge minimization and
fixed-dimensional Schatten-budget ranges.

The finite non-zero skeleton records the residual algebraic nonnormal freedom
away from the spectral point zero, while the trace-class quasinilpotent zero
sector remains determinant-invisible and is not classified.  Similarly, the
Schatten extension is a finite-budget localization theorem for prescribed
finite-subset regularized cyclic products \(P_F^{(\lceil p\rceil)}\): for
\(1<p<\infty\), the relevant budget is
\[
  \sum_{j\in J}m_jn_j\abs{\beta_j}^{p/n_j}<\infty,
\]
with regularized determinant \(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\), and the minimization and
equality statements hold within the localized class where product realization
and determinant equality with the cyclic model coincide.  Thus the paper
does not construct a cross-dimensional Horn inverse system or an unrestricted
natural-domain \(\det_r\)-fiber classification.  It shows instead that, for the
cyclic zero divisors considered here, the Fredholm determinant fixes the
collapsed non-zero spectrum, the sharp trace budget, and the normal minimizer
class.
