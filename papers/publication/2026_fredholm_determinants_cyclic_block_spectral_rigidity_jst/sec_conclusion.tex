\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The preceding sections separate three layers of the same Fredholm
determinant problem. The cyclic divisor first imposes the exact
trace-class threshold
\[
  \sum_{j\in J}m_jn_j\abs{\beta_j}^{1/n_j}<\infty
\]
and, in the normal category, determines the cyclic-block model up to
unitary equivalence and zero summands. In the full trace-class category,
the same determinant fixes the sharp trace-norm infimum and the full
minimizer class, while the Schur-entry estimate records a quantitative
near-minimizer constraint in a Ringrose triangularisation. For finite
ambient dimension, this becomes an optimal square-root estimate for the
trace-norm distance to the unitary orbit of minimizers, in the sense that
the exponent \(1/2\) cannot be improved uniformly. Away from zero, the
determinant fixes only algebraic multiplicities; the possible finite
Jordan profiles are exactly the algebraic partitions of those
multiplicities.
These profiles can be approached arbitrarily closely at the sharp
trace-norm budget, but a nontrivial profile cannot attain the budget.

The zero spectral point has a different role. Trace-class quasinilpotents
do not change the Fredholm determinant and make every determinant fiber
trace-norm unbounded, but they also do not erase the non-zero root-space
geometry: by
\Cref{thm:quasinilpotent-stabilization-nonzero-invariants}, stabilization
by trace-class quasinilpotents preserves the unitary equivalence class of
each non-zero generalized eigenspace restriction. The stable-skeleton
formulation in \Cref{thm:stable-nonzero-skeleton-classification} records
the resulting stable unitary invariant of the whole non-zero root-space
collection.
When the determinant-forced non-zero support is finite,
\Cref{thm:finite-support-quasinilpotent-quotient} turns this into a
formal algebraic quotient statement: after splitting off the Riesz root spaces at the
prescribed non-zero eigenvalues, the remaining summand is trace-class and
quasinilpotent, and the quotient by that zero sector is exactly the
product of finite-dimensional nilpotent \(GL\)-orbit classes. Thus the
determinant fiber is rigid at the level of the collapsed non-zero
spectral multiset, flexible at the level of allowed nonnormal Jordan
profiles, and still retains those profiles under quasinilpotent
stabilization at zero. The finite-support quotient also has a variational
boundary and an explicit closure order:
\Cref{thm:finite-support-quotient-closure-order} identifies the stratum
closures with the product dominance order on the nilpotent Jordan
partitions, while \Cref{thm:nonnormal-quotient-degeneration} shows that a
nonnormal quotient class has the same sharp trace-norm infimum as the
normal class, but every sequence approaching that infimum must pass
through similarities with condition numbers tending to infinity. Hence
the normal minimizer is not simply another representative of a nonnormal
class; it is the unique closed quotient stratum and the boundary limit of
the remaining strata in an unbounded similarity gauge.

The tail-controlled trace-norm theorem is the infinite-dimensional
stability statement proved here: distance to the normal minimizer class is
controlled only when the Schur compression tail and the
determinant-forced diagonal tail are controlled. Without such a tail
hypothesis, the paper asserts fixed-entry Schur stability and the
finite-dimensional trace estimate, not a global infinite-dimensional
orbit-distance theorem.

The Schatten theorem gives the corresponding sharp cyclic-block threshold
for every \(1<p<\infty\),
\[
  \sum_{j\in J}m_jn_j\abs{\beta_j}^{p/n_j}<\infty,
\]
together with the explicit regularized determinant factors for
\(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\). This is the exact \(\mathcal S_p\)-membership
condition for the cyclic model and the sharp lower bound inside the
prescribed regularized determinant fiber; it is not a classification of
when an arbitrary \(\mathcal S_{\lceil p\rceil}\)-operator has a
regularized determinant. The companion Schatten minimizer theorem shows
that, once the prescribed cyclic divisor is fixed, in the full regularized
determinant fiber, equality in the sharp \(\mathcal S_p\)-budget again
forces normality and unitary equivalence to the cyclic model up to a zero
summand, and near equality forces the fixed Schur off-diagonal entries to
be small. In finite dimension,
\Cref{thm:finite-dimensional-schatten-stability} upgrades this entrywise
constraint to a finite-dimensional bounded-set compactness theorem:
within a fixed finite-dimensional regularized determinant fiber and an
\(\mathcal S_p\)-bounded set, \(\mathcal S_p\)-budget defect tending to
zero forces \(\mathcal S_p\)-distance to the whole minimizer orbit to
tend to zero. This extends the cyclic-block realization picture
beyond trace class, while preserving the same collapsed spectral
interpretation and the same variational rigidity of the prescribed
non-zero divisor.
