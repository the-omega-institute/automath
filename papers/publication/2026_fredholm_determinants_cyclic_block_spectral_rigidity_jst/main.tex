\begin{abstract}
We study how standard Fredholm determinant zero-divisor theory and
Weyl--Ky Fan equality cases look when the zero divisor is packaged by
prescribed cyclic factors.  For cyclic data
\(\mathcal D=\{(n_j,\beta_j,m_j)\}_{j\in J}\), \(\beta_j\ne0\), the exact
trace-class threshold for the positive-exponent determinant
\[
  D_{\mathcal D}(z)=\prod_j(1-\beta_jz^{n_j})^{m_j},
\]
is
\[
  \sum_j m_jn_j|\beta_j|^{1/n_j}<\infty .
\]
The cyclic packaging gives a canonical block model, but the determinant itself
remembers precisely only the collapsed non-zero
spectral multiset
\[
  M_{\mathcal D}(\lambda)=
  \sum_{\{j:\lambda^{n_j}=\beta_j\}}m_j,
\]
and the normal cyclic-block model is unique only after passing to this
collapsed multiset and adjoining zero summands.  The genuinely new point is
the cyclic-divisor bookkeeping together with the equality-case Schur pinching
which identifies the sharp trace-norm minimizer in the full nonnormal
trace-class determinant fiber: equality forces unitary equivalence to the
normal cyclic model up to a zero summand.  As consequences of classical
determinant, Weyl--Fan, Horn, Ringrose, and nilpotent-orbit facts in this
packaging, we record chosen-Schur stability criteria whose infinite-dimensional
form is conditional on an externally supplied Schur-tail modulus and is not an
orbit-distance theorem, fixed-dimensional Weyl--Horn singular-value
consequences, finite non-zero skeleton results, and a prescribed
finite-subset \(\mathcal S_p\) extension for
\(\det_r\), \(r=\lceil p\rceil\), with sharp budget
\(\sum_jm_jn_j|\beta_j|^{p/n_j}<\infty\) and equality rigidity within the
resulting finite-budget class, where product realization and determinant
equality with the cyclic model coincide.  The natural-domain \(\det_r\) problem beyond
this prescribed budget, cross-dimensional Horn stabilization, and the
determinant-invisible quasinilpotent zero sector are treated only as boundary
obstructions or non-assertions, not as global classification theorems.
\end{abstract}
