\begin{abstract}
We study Fredholm determinant fibers of trace-class operators associated with
prescribed cyclic divisors.  For cyclic data
\(\mathcal D=\{(n_j,\beta_j,m_j)\}_{j\in J}\), \(\beta_j\ne0\), the exact
trace-class threshold for the positive-exponent determinant
\[
  D_{\mathcal D}(z)=\prod_j(1-\beta_jz^{n_j})^{m_j},
\]
is
\[
  \sum_j m_jn_j|\beta_j|^{1/n_j}<\infty .
\]
At this threshold the determinant remembers precisely the collapsed non-zero
spectral multiset
\[
  M_{\mathcal D}(\lambda)=
  \sum_{\{j:\lambda^{n_j}=\beta_j\}}m_j,
\]
and the canonical normal cyclic-block model is the unique normal realisation
up to zero summands and this collapsed multiset.  This is the normal
classification.  The full nonnormal determinant fiber has a different kind of
rigidity: among all trace-class operators with determinant \(D_{\mathcal D}\),
the cyclic model is the sharp trace-norm minimizer, and equality forces
unitary equivalence to the normal cyclic model up to a zero summand; outside
equality, quasinilpotent zero-sector directions are not classified.  Around
this rigidity theorem we prove Schur-gauge stability criteria,
finite-dimensional Weyl--Horn consequences, a finite non-zero nonnormal
skeleton, and a controlled prescribed-budget \(\mathcal S_p\) regularized
determinant extension.  The latter gives the sharp \(\mathcal S_p\)-threshold
\(\sum_jm_jn_j|\beta_j|^{p/n_j}<\infty\) and equality rigidity inside the
prescribed cyclic-product budget fiber, without claiming a classification on
the full natural \(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\) domain.
\end{abstract}
