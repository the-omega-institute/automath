\title[Cyclic-Block Trace-Class Realisations]{Cyclic-block trace-class realisations\\
for prescribed cyclic zero divisors}

\author{Haobo Ma}

\date{}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We study the Fredholm determinant realisation problem for prescribed
cyclic zero divisors.  For cyclic data
\(\mathcal D=\{(n_j,\beta_j,m_j)\}_{j\in J}\), with \(\beta_j\ne0\), the
determinant convention is fixed at the outset:
\[
  D_{\mathcal D}(z)=\prod_j(1-\beta_jz^{n_j})^{m_j}
\]
is the positive-exponent Fredholm determinant, while the reciprocal Euler
product means only \(D_{\mathcal D}^{-1}\) on a chosen zero-free
neighbourhood.  The exact trace-class threshold is
\[
  \sum_j m_jn_j|\beta_j|^{1/n_j}<\infty .
\]
At this threshold the normal realisation is the cyclic-block direct sum
\(\bigoplus_j C(n_j,\alpha_j)\otimes I_{m_j}\),
\(\alpha_j^{n_j}=\beta_j\), and the determinant classifies normal
realisations only through the collapsed non-zero spectral multiset
\(M_{\mathcal D}(\lambda)=\sum_{\lambda^{n_j}=\beta_j}m_j\), not through a
chosen Euler-factor packaging.

The same collapsed multiset governs the full trace-class determinant
fiber.  The cyclic model is the sharp trace-norm minimizer among all
trace-class operators with determinant \(D_{\mathcal D}\), and equality
forces unitary equivalence to the normal cyclic model up to a zero
summand.  The proof yields quantitative Schur-entry stability; with
explicit tail control it gives an infinite-dimensional distance estimate,
and in finite dimension the square-root exponent in the distance estimate
is sharp.  Nonnormal fibers remain large: finite Jordan profiles at
prescribed non-zero eigenvalues are classified algebraically by partitions
of the collapsed multiplicities, while the stable unitary skeleton retains
the full unitary equivalence class of the finite-dimensional nilpotent
root-space restrictions. Trace-class quasinilpotent stabilization at zero
does not change the determinant or erase these non-zero root-space
invariants.

For every \(1<p<\infty\) we also prove the exact \(\mathcal S_p\)
membership threshold
\(\sum_jm_jn_j|\beta_j|^{p/n_j}<\infty\) for the cyclic model and the
corresponding explicit regularized determinant formula for
\(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\). This \(p\)-budget is a Schatten-membership and
minimization threshold, not a classification of the larger natural
\(\mathcal S_{\lceil p\rceil}\)-domain of the determinant.  In each
regularized determinant fiber with that prescribed cyclic divisor,
equality in the sharp \(\mathcal S_p\)-budget again forces the same normal
cyclic model up to zero summands, with a finite-dimensional bounded-set
stability statement near the minimizer orbit.
\end{abstract}
