\begin{abstract}
We study Fredholm determinant realisation for prescribed cyclic zero
divisors.  For cyclic data
\(\mathcal D=\{(n_j,\beta_j,m_j)\}_{j\in J}\), \(\beta_j\ne0\), we use the
positive-exponent convention
\[
  D_{\mathcal D}(z)=\prod_j(1-\beta_jz^{n_j})^{m_j},
\]
while the reciprocal Euler product denotes only \(D_{\mathcal D}^{-1}\) on a
chosen zero-free neighbourhood.  The exact trace-class threshold is
\[
  \sum_j m_jn_j|\beta_j|^{1/n_j}<\infty .
\]
At this threshold the canonical normal realisation is the cyclic-block sum
\(\bigoplus_j C(n_j,\alpha_j)\otimes I_{m_j}\),
\(\alpha_j^{n_j}=\beta_j\), and normal realisations are classified by the
collapsed non-zero spectral multiset
\(M_{\mathcal D}(\lambda)=\sum_{\lambda^{n_j}=\beta_j}m_j\), not by a chosen
Euler-factor presentation.

The same collapsed multiset governs the trace-class determinant fiber.  The
cyclic model is the sharp trace-norm minimizer among all trace-class
operators with determinant \(D_{\mathcal D}\), and equality forces unitary
equivalence to the normal cyclic model up to a zero summand.  Near equality
gives fixed-Schur-entry stability and a chosen-Schur-basis tail criterion for
orbit-distance convergence after a Ringrose triangularisation has been chosen;
no basis-free infinite-dimensional orbit-distance modulus is asserted from
trace defect alone.  Nonnormal fibers
remain large: finite non-zero root-space skeletons are classified by Jordan
profiles, while arbitrary trace-class quasinilpotent zero summands do not
change the determinant.  The finite-support quotient statements are algebraic
statements on this finite non-zero skeleton, not quotient-topology statements
for the whole trace-class fiber.

As secondary extensions, for every \(1<p<\infty\) we prove the exact
\(\mathcal S_p\)-membership threshold
\(\sum_jm_jn_j|\beta_j|^{p/n_j}<\infty\), the associated
\(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\) formula, and equality rigidity in the prescribed
cyclic-product \(\mathcal S_p\)-budget fiber, together with
finite-dimensional bounded-set stability; this is not a classification of the
whole natural \(\det_{\lceil p\rceil}\) domain.
\end{abstract}
