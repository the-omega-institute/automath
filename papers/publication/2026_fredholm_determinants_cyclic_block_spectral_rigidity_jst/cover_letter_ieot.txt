Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript "Trace-class Fredholm determinant fibers and spectral rigidity for cyclic divisors" for consideration in Integral Equations and Operator Theory.

The paper studies Fredholm determinant zero-divisor data packaged by prescribed cyclic factors. It identifies the exact trace-class budget for realizing the finite-subset cyclic products, constructs the canonical normal cyclic model, and proves sharp minimality and equality rigidity in the full trace-class determinant fiber. The manuscript also records the corresponding prescribed-budget Schatten analogue for regularized determinants and the fixed-dimensional Weyl--Horn singular-value consequences of the collapsed non-zero spectral data.

We believe the manuscript is appropriate for IEOT because its core concerns trace-class and Schatten-ideal operator theory, Fredholm and regularized determinants, spectral rigidity, and operator-ideal minimization. The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The authors declare no competing interests and received no specific funding for this work.
