The R2 submission source is revised_main_flattened.tex. It is self-contained
and includes its 22-entry bibliography inline, so it has no companion .bbl or
.bib dependency. It builds with latexmk -xelatex.

The revised_ prefix prevents Editorial Manager from carrying forward
same-named files from the previous submission when it builds the
author-approval PDF.

The source tree holds a single copy of the manuscript. Earlier modular and
mirrored copies of the same text were removed; they remain in the repository
history.
