\textbf{Ms.\ Ref.\ No.: JNTH-D-26-00330R1}\\
\textbf{A quartic cover of \(37\mathrm{a}1\) and its regular \(S_4\)-closure}

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent
Dear Editors,

\vspace{0.8em}

\noindent
We are grateful to the editor and the referee for the careful second-round report, and we
submit herewith the revised version of the above manuscript. The three minor revisions and
the three points of presentation and language raised by the referee have all been addressed;
a point-by-point reply is given in the accompanying response letter.

\vspace{0.8em}

\noindent
Two of the requested clarifications turned out to expose errors, and we would rather state
them plainly here than leave them to be discovered in the response letter. While preparing
the justification of the ramified fibre counts that the referee asked for, we found that the
point-count formula in the Prym-trace proposition was itself incorrect: the indices \(12\)
and \(6\) count geometric points, and are attained rationally only under conditions we had
not imposed. We have corrected the formula, and nine of the fifteen tabulated values of
\(a_1(Q,p)\) change as a consequence. In the proposition on component groups, the stated
Tamagawa numbers are correct, but the proof rested on an incorrect general principle; that
argument has been replaced by one using the thickness of the node. Neither correction
affects Theorem~4.8, the isogeny decomposition, or any other structural result of the paper.

\vspace{0.8em}

\noindent
Two corollaries were added in Section~6, so the numbered items after Proposition~6.1 are
renumbered. In particular, the referee's Proposition~6.10 and Proposition~6.22 are now
Proposition~6.11 and Proposition~6.24. The response letter gives both numbers on first
mention so that the report and the revised manuscript can be read side by side.

\vspace{0.8em}

\noindent
The response letter also records the remaining corrections, which are matters of statement
and attribution rather than of results.

\vspace{1.5em}

\noindent
With kind regards,
