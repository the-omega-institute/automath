Revision notes / comments to editor

We thank the editor and referee for the careful report. The three minor revisions and three presentation comments have been addressed, as detailed in the point-by-point response in response_to_referee_r2.pdf.

While preparing the requested clarification of the ramified fibre counts, we found that the point-count formula in the Prym-trace proposition was incorrect; we have corrected it, and nine of the fifteen entries in the trace table have changed. The Tamagawa numbers are unchanged, but the component-group argument has been rewritten. Neither correction affects the isogeny decomposition or any structural result.

Two corollaries were added in Section 6, so the items after Proposition 6.1 are renumbered: the referee's Proposition 6.10 and Proposition 6.22 are now Proposition 6.11 and Proposition 6.24. The response letter gives both numbers on first mention, and records the smaller copy-level corrections, which are not enumerated here.
