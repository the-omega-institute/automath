Revision notes / comments to editor

We thank the editor and referee for the careful report. The revised manuscript reframes the S4-Prym decomposition as an application of known fourfold-cover Prym theory, especially Lange--Rodriguez, and emphasizes the new contribution as the explicit arithmetic analysis of this concrete cover of 37a1 and its branch cubic.

The revision adds a known-vs-new table, a quotient/factor table, a direct comparison with Lange--Rodriguez before Theorem 4.8, a clarification of the limited status of the Prym threefold Q, and a computational verification inventory with exact script/file names. We also revised the language, clarified that no full conductor or full endomorphism algebra is claimed where not proved, and reordered/updated the bibliography.

A detailed point-by-point response is included in response_to_referee.pdf.
