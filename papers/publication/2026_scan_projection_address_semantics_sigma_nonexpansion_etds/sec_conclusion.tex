\section{Scope and limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}

For an irreducible periodic survivor graph, the R\'enyi exponent remains
the familiar pressure difference
\[
  h_{s,H}=sP_{Y_H}(\varphi)-P_{Y_H}(s\varphi),
\]
but the precise power sum has a depth-phase prefactor.  The collision
law therefore has to be stated either in the intrinsic form
\(\binom{N_m}{k}S_k^H(m)\to\tau\), or phase by phase when the sample
size is normalized only by pressure.  The period-two example proves
that this distinction is genuine.

The extension closes under two explicit hypotheses.  Irreducibility is
used to obtain positive cyclic Perron limits.  Branching is used to make
the entrywise-power spectral inequality strict.  If branching is
removed, the safe graph is a deterministic cycle, every R\'enyi gap
vanishes, and the exponential birthday regime disappears.  This is the
structural boundary of the theorem.

The first-entry Bayes calculation is secondary.  Its exact path sum is
valid for every integer \(q\geq2\) and every nonempty proper
\(R\subsetneq\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\).  The stronger assertion that the
Bayes error is uniformly comparable with survivor mass additionally
requires attainable hit times on both sides of the residue decision for
every safe state and every current phase.  Under that condition, the
periodic survivor phase and observation phase combine into amplitudes
indexed modulo \(\operatorname{lcm}(d,q)\).

No H\"older-potential extension beyond one-step Markov equilibrium laws
is claimed here.  For a general Gibbs source, pressure estimates from
bounded distortion give exponential rates, but the collision theorem
requires precise power-sum prefactors and a strict higher-moment gap for
the actual cylinder probabilities.  Establishing those operator
statements would be a separate result; they are not assumed or presented
as completed steps in this paper.

\section*{Reproducibility}
The period-two constants and numerical comparisons are generated by the
scripts in \path{artifacts/}.  The verified script
\path{artifacts/reproduce_period_two_constants.py} derives the exact
phase-dependent and phase-blind constants; \path{artifacts/test_reproduce_period_two_constants.py}
runs the corresponding regression test; and
\path{artifacts/verify_period_two_example.py} checks the controls, the two
constants, and their differing means.  These finite checks corroborate, but
do not replace, the pressure and survivor-graph arguments or the stated
scope limitations.
