Dear Editors,

We submit "Phase-Resolved Collision Laws for Periodic Survivors in Open
Markov Shifts" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. The main result is the dependence
of the critical collision law on the survivor phase. In the explicit
period-two example, under the paper's critical scaling, the collision count
converges to Poisson means (953/5618) alpha^2 and
[267/(676 sqrt(5))] alpha^2 in the two depth classes. Both constants are
derived directly from the transition data, so the phase-free primitive
survivor law does not extend unchanged to periodic survivors.

The work is finite-state. It does not claim the Holder/Gibbs extension, which
is not part of this paper, and the Poisson approximation mechanism is the
classical Chen--Stein method; the contribution is the phase-resolved Perron
asymptotic and the resulting distinct critical means. Stochastics and
Dynamics is the appropriate venue because the paper lies at the interface of
open symbolic dynamics, periodic Markov structure, and finite-state collision
probability.

Reproducibility: artifacts/reproduce_period_two_constants.py reconstructs
both phase constants from the printed transition data, with a regression test
in artifacts/test_reproduce_period_two_constants.py.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
