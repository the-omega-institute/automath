\begin{remark}[Status of the secondary spectral pattern]
\label{rem:secondary-spectral-pattern}
Table~\ref{tab:secondary-spectral-q9-17} records high-precision approximations
to the dominant root \(\lambda_q\), the outer negative real root
\(\lambda_q^-\), and the largest remaining modulus \(\Theta_q\) for
\(q=9,\dots,17\).  Throughout the displayed window the numerical fingerprints
are consistent with a unique negative real secondary root satisfying
\[
  \Theta_q<|\lambda_q^-|<\lambda_q.
\]
This table is not a symbolic root-isolation certificate.  Consequently it is
not used to prove any eventual alternating law for \(S_q(m)\) in the
arithmetic-window range.  If the ordering is later upgraded to exact isolating
intervals for all roots of the certified \(P_q\), then the usual
linear-recurrence expansion would become available.  At present, however, the
entries remain descriptive fingerprints and are kept outside the formal theorem
chain.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[What remains open]
\label{rem:q16q17}
The exact certificate records above close the full symmetric-group statement for
\(q=16\) and \(q=17\).
The next arithmetic target is to extend squareclass independence and linear
disjointness beyond the block \(q=12,13,14,15\).  On the spectral side, the
table above strongly suggests a persistent negative second root throughout the
resonance window, but proving any alternating secondary term beyond the
quadratic case requires a separate symbolic root-isolation certificate and is
not part of the present theorem chain.
\end{remark}
