\section{Galois groups in the arithmetic window}
\label{sec:chebotarev}

By \Cref{thm:all-q-transfer}, the dominant root of every collision recurrence is
the same partition constant \(\lambda_q\) that governs Sanna's Fibonacci
partition power sums.  The arithmetic window \(q=9,\dots,17\) studies the
arithmetic of these identified constants through a separate exact
computer-assisted certificate layer.  Its proof interface is a deterministic
exact-arithmetic verifier applied to the hash-pinned paper-local certificate
file of \Cref{prop:pinned-normalizer-certificate-table}.  The coefficient table
is not used as evidence for a recurrence.  For each \(q\),
Appendix~\ref{app:certification} first constructs the exact
histogram realization
\[
  S_q(m)=\widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^m\widetilde v_q,
  \qquad N_q=\dim\widetilde A_q,
\]
from the finite transducer of Appendix~\ref{app:transducer}.  The integer
\(N_q\) is an a priori order bound, known before any proposed row polynomial is
tested.  A row polynomial
\[
  P_q(X)=X^{d_q}-c_{q,1}X^{d_q-1}-\cdots-c_{q,d_q}
\]
is accepted only after substitution into this exact kernel.

Here is the complete recurrence contract used below.  In the finite Krylov
space
\[
  W_q=\operatorname{span}_{\QQ}
  \{\widetilde A_q^j\widetilde v_q:j\ge0\},
\]
define the offset-two unobservable subspace
\[
  Z_q=
  \{w\in W_q:\widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^t w=0
    \text{ for all }t\ge2\}.
\]
By Cayley--Hamilton, this infinite condition is equivalent to the finite
\(N_q\)-row test
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:section7-primary-kernel-residual}
  R_qw=0,\qquad
  R_q=
  \begin{bmatrix}
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^2\\
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^3\\
  \vdots\\
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^{N_q+1}
  \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Thus the exact residual identity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:section7-residual-certificate}
  R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0
\end{equation}
is precisely the assertion
\[
  P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q\in Z_q.
\]
Consequently
\[
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^t
  P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0
  \qquad(t\ge2),
\]
and, after writing \(m=t+d_q\),
\[
  S_q(m)=\sum_{i=1}^{d_q}c_{q,i}S_q(m-i)
  \qquad(m\ge d_q+2).
\]
This is the all-tail recurrence theorem.  The finite block in
\eqref{eq:section7-residual-certificate} is not a sample interval in the scalar
variable \(m\); it is the finite construction of \(Z_q\) inside the exact matrix
kernel, with length fixed by the pre-existing dimension \(N_q\).

The observable-quotient part of the certificate is separate.  With
\[
  K_q=[\widetilde v_q,\dots,\widetilde A_q^{N_q-1}\widetilde v_q],
  \qquad
  B_q=[\widetilde v_q,\dots,\widetilde A_q^{d_q-1}\widetilde v_q],
\]
the exact rank identities
\[
  \rk(R_qK_q)=\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q
\]
say that \(W_q/Z_q\) has dimension \(d_q\), that the first \(d_q\) Krylov
classes form a cyclic basis, and that the same polynomial \(P_q\) is the
characteristic polynomial of the induced endomorphism on the observable
quotient.  The recurrence proof needs only the residual identity; the rank
identities are used for minimality and Hankel-principalization.  With this
contract in place, the arithmetic profile proved below is exact: integral Hankel
relations are generated by the certified recurrence polynomial, irreducibility
and full symmetric Galois groups are certified by explicit mod-\(p\)
factorizations, and the splitting fields for \(q=12,13,14,15\) are linearly
disjoint.  The high-precision root fingerprints in
Appendix~\ref{app:certification} are descriptive data and are not invoked in any
proof in this section.

\begin{theorem}[Hash-pinned arithmetic-window certificate records]
\label{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates}
For \(q=9,\dots,17\), the arithmetic-window layer is a computer-assisted exact
algebraic layer with the following verification protocol.  The submission
includes the hash-pinned certificate file and verifier script of
\Cref{prop:pinned-normalizer-certificate-table}; running
\[
  \texttt{python certificates/verify\_arithmetic\_window.py}
\]
on that file performs only exact integer, rational, Smith-form, resultant, and
finite-field arithmetic.  Acceptance of that protocol is the certificate
record used in this section.

The printed TeX tables carry the human-readable part of the proof obligations:
the row polynomials \(P_q\), the tail offsets \(m\ge d_q+2\), the residual
normalization statement, the displayed Smith invariant factors, the listed
finite-field degree patterns, and the Legendre-symbol squareclass matrix.  The
submission-included artifact carries the full exact witnesses behind those
summaries: the reconstructed transition table, the residual products
\(R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q\), the quotient-rank witnesses, the
finite moment windows and Hankel nullmode witnesses, the unimodular Smith
matrices, the monic finite-field factors with Bezout identities, and the
discriminant residues with Euler-criterion witnesses.  The tables identify the
claims; the artifact supplies the exact certificates checked by the verifier.

Let \(P_q\) denote the row polynomial printed in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17}.  First, the exact-kernel recurrence
record for \(P_q\) is the integer residual identity
\[
  R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0
\]
and the observable-quotient rank identities
\[
  \rk(R_qK_q)=\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q,
\]
as recorded in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrence-cert-normalization}; this is the theorem-level
exact-kernel record of Appendix~\ref{app:certification}.  Second, the archived
supplement pinned in \Cref{prop:pinned-normalizer-certificate-table} contains
the exact finite moment-window records of
\Cref{prop:exact-moment-window-supplement}: the values \(S_q(m)\) for
\(0\le m\le 2n_q\), the residual block \(P_q(E)S_q(j)=0\) for
\(2\le j\le n_q+1\), the Hankel matrix \(H_q\), its exact rank \(d_q\), and
the annihilation records for the printed nullmode basis.  Third, the
Smith-normal-form records of Table~\ref{tab:appendix-principalization-q9-17}
are exact unimodular reduction certificates over \(\ZZ\), with the matrices
\(U_q,V_q\) supplied in the same artifact.  Fourth, the
finite-field factorization records of
Table~\ref{tab:galois-certificates-q9-17} are exact squarefree factorizations
in the indicated rings \(\FF_p[\lambda]\), with monic factors and Bezout gcd
witnesses supplied in the artifact.  Fifth, for
\(q=12,13,14,15\), the Legendre-symbol records of
Table~\ref{tab:disc-squareclass-q12-15} are exact evaluations of the
discriminants of the printed polynomials, with the discriminants, residues, and
Euler-criterion witnesses supplied in the artifact.

The exact-kernel recurrence record is not a finite scalar audit in the variable
\(m\).  It is the exact-kernel identity above inside the histogram realization
of Appendix~\ref{app:certification}.  The supplementary moment-window records
are separate: they are the exact finite consequences used for the Hankel and
Smith arguments.  All arithmetic-window results below are computer-assisted in
exactly this sense and use these records only through the soundness lemmas in
this section and Appendix~\ref{app:certification};
Theorems~A--F and the entropy ledger do not use this certificate theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This proof is the exact verification protocol specified in the statement.  Each
record is a finite algebraic identity in an explicitly specified ring; the
tables in the manuscript are digests of the exact records pinned by
\Cref{prop:pinned-normalizer-certificate-table}, and the included script checks
the corresponding full witness data.
For the recurrence records, the exact-kernel meaning is the one fixed in
Appendix~\ref{app:certification}: the record is the residual identity
\(R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0\) together with the rank identities
\(\rk(R_qK_q)=\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q\).  Their soundness is
\Cref{prop:observable-quotient-derivation,prop:kernel-derived-recurrences}.

The local supplementary checker handles the finite records.  By
\Cref{prop:exact-moment-window-supplement}, it computes the exact moment window
\(S_q(m)\) for \(0\le m\le 2n_q\), forms the Hankel block \(H_q\), checks the
recorded residual block \(P_q(E)S_q(j)=0\) for \(2\le j\le n_q+1\), and
verifies that the recorded nullmode basis is annihilated by
\(H_q^{\mathsf T}\) with \(\rk H_q=d_q\).  The Smith records then consist of
explicit unimodular row-and-column reductions over
\(\ZZ\); Table~\ref{tab:appendix-principalization-q9-17} prints the resulting
invariant factors, while the artifact supplies \(U_q,V_q\).  The finite-field
records consist of monic factors in the indicated \(\FF_p[\lambda]\);
Table~\ref{tab:galois-certificates-q9-17} prints their degree pattern.  They
are verified by reducing \(P_q\) modulo the indicated primes, multiplying those
monic factors, and checking the recorded Bezout identity for
\(\gcd(P_q\bmod p,P_q'\bmod p)=1\).  The discriminant records are verified by
the resultant formula for \(\Disc(P_q)\) followed by the recorded
Euler-criterion residues at the four displayed primes.  Thus every line is an
exact equality or nonvanishing certificate over \(\ZZ\), \(\QQ\), or a stated
finite field.  The all-tail
recurrence consequence is not part of the local moment-window check; it follows
from the exact-kernel part through Cayley--Hamilton via
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification,prop:kernel-derived-recurrences}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[How the certificate records are checked]
\label{rem:certificate-record-checking}
Each line of Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} is a finite algebra
identity in a specified ring.  For the recurrence part, the manuscript uses the
exact-kernel record summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrence-cert-normalization}; the soundness theorem
turning that record into an all-tail recurrence is
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification}.  The local supplement handles the
finite visible consequences: by
\Cref{prop:exact-moment-window-supplement}, the verifier computes the exact
moment window, forms the Hankel block \(H_q\), checks the displayed residual
block \(P_q(E)S_q(j)=0\) on the needed finite range, and verifies that the
recorded nullmode basis is annihilated by \(H_q^{\mathsf T}\) with exact rank
\(d_q\).  These are the entries summarized later by the Hankel and Smith
tables.

For the Galois data, the checker reduces the printed integer polynomial
\(P_q\) modulo the indicated prime and verifies the recorded monic
factorization and Bezout identity with \(P_q'\) in \(\FF_p[\lambda]\).  For the
squareclass data it computes \(\Disc(P_q)\) by the resultant formula and checks
the recorded Euler-criterion residues modulo the primes in
Table~\ref{tab:disc-squareclass-q12-15}.  Thus the certificate records are
finite exact identities over \(\ZZ\), \(\QQ\), or the indicated finite fields.
No all-tail recurrence is inferred from a finite list of scalar moments.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Section-wide convention]
\label{rem:arithmetic-window-convention}
Every theorem, proposition, and corollary in the rest of
Section~\ref{sec:chebotarev} uses the exact certificate records of
Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} only through the soundness
statements made explicit below.  The logical content in the body is the
derivation from those finite identities to all-tail recurrences, Hankel
principalization, Galois groups, and Chebotarev densities.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Certificate dependency skeleton]
\label{prop:arithmetic-window-dependency-skeleton}
The arithmetic-window arguments below factor through the following directed
finite certificate skeleton:
\[
\begin{array}{c}
\text{pinned normalizer table}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{exact histogram kernel }
(\widetilde A_q,\widetilde u_q,\widetilde v_q)\\
\downarrow\\
R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\rk(R_qK_q)=\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q\\
\downarrow\\
\text{all-tail recurrence and observable quotient}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{Hankel, Smith, Galois, discriminant, and Chebotarev conclusions.}
\end{array}
\]
In particular, no theorem in Section~\ref{sec:chebotarev} infers an all-tail
recurrence from a finite scalar moment window or from the displayed coefficient
row alone.  The finite scalar window is used only after the exact-kernel
recurrence has been certified, and only for Hankel and Smith-form consequences.
Theorems~A--F and the entropy ledger do not use this certificate skeleton.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The top arrow is \Cref{prop:auditable-kernel-reconstruction}: the archived
finite normalizer table reconstructs the one-copy augmented automaton and hence
the histogram kernel of \Cref{def:exact-histogram-kernel}.  The second arrow is
the residual and rank part of
\Cref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates}, recorded in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrence-cert-normalization}.  The residual identity
is the finite \(N_q\)-row matrix equality
\[
  R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0,
\]
where \(N_q=\dim\widetilde A_q\) is fixed before the polynomial row is tested.
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification,prop:kernel-derived-recurrences}
turn this exact kernel equality, by Cayley--Hamilton, into the all-tail
recurrence for \(S_q(m)\).  The rank identities are interpreted by
\Cref{lem:cyclic-observable-quotient-certificate} and
\Cref{prop:observable-quotient-derivation} as the observable quotient
statement.

The remaining consequences use only the finite exact records named in
\Cref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates}.  Hankel principalization and the
Smith-form statements use the certified recurrence together with the finite
moment-window supplement and the recorded unimodular reductions.  The
irreducibility and Galois statements use the printed polynomials and the
finite-field factorizations, whose soundness is
\Cref{lem:finite-field-certificate-soundness}.  Linear disjointness and product
Chebotarev use, in addition, the discriminant squareclass records certified by
\Cref{lem:discriminant-squareclass-certificate}.  None of these implications
feeds back into the proofs of Theorems~A--F or the entropy ledger, whose inputs
are fixed earlier in the manuscript.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Finite residual propagation in the exact kernel]
\label{lem:section7-finite-residual-propagation}
Let \(A\in M_N(\ZZ)\), \(u,v\in\ZZ^N\), and
\[
  P(X)=X^d-c_1X^{d-1}-\cdots-c_d\in\ZZ[X].
\]
Set \(s_m=u^{\mathsf T}A^mv\) and
\[
  e_m:=s_m-\sum_{i=1}^d c_i s_{m-i}
  =u^{\mathsf T}A^{m-d}P(A)v
  \qquad(m\ge d).
\]
If
\[
  e_{d+h}=e_{d+h+1}=\cdots=e_{d+h+N-1}=0
\]
for some \(h\ge0\), then \(e_m=0\) for every \(m\ge d+h\).
Thus an \(N\)-term residual block is an all-tail proof once the scalar sequence
has been realized by the \(N\times N\) matrix \(A\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The residual sequence \((e_m)_{m\ge d}\) is itself a scalar matrix coefficient of
\(A\).  If
\[
  \chi_A(X)=X^N+a_1X^{N-1}+\cdots+a_N,
\]
then Cayley--Hamilton gives
\[
  e_{m+N}+a_1e_{m+N-1}+\cdots+a_Ne_m=0
  \qquad(m\ge d).
\]
The displayed block is a zero state for this order-\(N\) recurrence, and forward
induction gives \(e_m=0\) for all \(m\ge d+h\).
\end{proof}
