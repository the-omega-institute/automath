\(A=\widetilde A_q\), \(u=\widetilde u_q\),
\(v=\widetilde v_q\), and \(N=N_q\).  Form the finite Krylov matrix
\[
  K_q=\bigl[v\ Av\ \cdots\ A^{N-1}v\bigr]
\]
and the finite observability matrix
\[
  R_q=
  \begin{bmatrix}
  u^{\mathsf T}A^2\\
  u^{\mathsf T}A^3\\
  \vdots\\
  u^{\mathsf T}A^{N+1}
  \end{bmatrix},
  \qquad
  B_q=\bigl[v\ Av\ \cdots\ A^{d_q-1}v\bigr].
\]
The equality \(R_qP_q(A)v=0\) is the primary recurrence certificate.  By
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification}, it gives
\[
  u^{\mathsf T}A^tP_q(A)v=0\qquad(t\ge2),
\]
and hence the all-tail recurrence.  The rank identities have a separate role:
they identify the observable quotient on which \(P_q\) is the characteristic
polynomial.
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification} also gives the precise quotient
implication: the row space of \(R_q\) detects exactly the quotient
\(W(A,v)/Z_2(A,u,v)\), the equality \(\rk(R_qK_q)=d_q\) gives quotient
dimension \(d_q\), the equality \(\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q\) makes
\[
  \overline v,\overline{Av},\dots,\overline{A^{d_q-1}v}
\]
a cyclic basis, and the residual equality supplies the companion relation.
Therefore multiplication by \(A\) on the observable quotient has characteristic
polynomial \(P_q\).
The paper uses these equalities as theorem-level exact-kernel hypotheses.  The
local supplementary artifact pinned in
\Cref{prop:pinned-normalizer-certificate-table} does not attempt to rebuild the
enormous matrix \(\widetilde A_q\).  Instead, it stores the exact finite
consequences needed later for the Hankel and Smith arguments: for each
\(q=9,\dots,17\), the field
\(\texttt{exact\_moment\_window\_records}\) records the exact integers
\(S_q(m)\) for \(0\le m\le 2n_q\), the residual block
\(P_q(E)S_q(j)=0\) for \(2\le j\le n_q+1\), the finite Hankel matrix
\(H_q=[S_q(i+j+2)]_{0\le i,j\le n_q-1}\), its exact rank \(d_q\), and the
annihilation of the recorded nullmode basis by \(H_q^{\mathsf T}\).  Thus the
all-tail recurrence is justified by the exact-kernel theorems of this appendix,
while the finite Hankel-window arguments are independently reproducible from the
archived supplement.

\begin{theorem}[Soundness of the recurrence certificate records]
\label{prop:observable-quotient-derivation}
For every \(q=9,\dots,17\), the recurrence and observable-quotient records
specified in Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} imply that the
exact residual verifier
\(\mathsf W_q(P_q)\) accepts the row polynomial \(P_q\) in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17}; explicitly,
\[
  R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0.
\]
The additional row-reduction records imply
\(\mathsf V_q(P_q)=\mathrm{accept}\).  Equivalently, \(P_q\) is the
characteristic polynomial of multiplication by \(\widetilde A_q\) on
\[
  \mathcal O_2(\widetilde A_q,\widetilde u_q,\widetilde v_q).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each displayed \(q\), the recurrence record of
Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} is, by definition, a finite
certificate record in the sense of
\Cref{def:finite-recurrence-certificate-record} for the printed polynomial
\(P_q\).  Therefore \Cref{lem:finite-certificate-record-soundness} applies
directly.  Its residual part is exactly the acceptance
\(\mathsf W_q(P_q)=\mathrm{accept}\), which is the equality
\[
  R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0,
\]
and its observable-quotient part is exactly the acceptance
\(\mathsf V_q(P_q)=\mathrm{accept}\), namely
\[
  \rk(R_qK_q)=\rk(R_qB_q)=d_q.
\]
The first implication gives the all-tail recurrence.  The second implication,
together with the same residual identity, identifies the observable quotient and
its characteristic polynomial.  No scalar moment list is used in this theorem:
the entire implication runs through the exact-kernel framework fixed earlier in
the appendix.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exact finite moment-window supplement]
\label{prop:exact-moment-window-supplement}
For every \(q\in\{9,\dots,17\}\), let
\[
  n_q:=2\left\lfloor\frac{q}{2}\right\rfloor+1,
  \qquad
  H_q:=\bigl[S_q(i+j+2)\bigr]_{0\le i,j\le n_q-1}.
\]
The supplementary verifier
\[
\texttt{certificates/verify\_arithmetic\_window.py}
\]
computes the exact integers \(S_q(m)\) for \(0\le m\le 2n_q\) directly from
the finite-window definition, forms the matrix \(H_q\), and checks the
following finite identities recorded in the field
\[
\texttt{exact\_moment\_window\_records}
\]
of the pinned arithmetic-window certificate file:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the residual block \(P_q(E)S_q(j)=0\) for \(2\le j\le n_q+1\);
\item \(\rk H_q=d_q\), witnessed by a nonzero leading \(d_q\times d_q\) minor;
\item every recorded nullmode basis vector \(\alpha\) satisfies
\(H_q^{\mathsf T}\alpha=0\).
\end{enumerate}
Consequently the finite Hankel-window and Smith-normal-form arguments in the
arithmetic window are independently reproducible from the archived supplement,
without reconstructing the full histogram kernel \(\widetilde A_q\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For fixed \(m\), let \(c_m(s)\) be the number of words
\(\omega\in\Omega_m\) with
\[
  N_m(\omega)=\sum_{j=1}^m \omega_jF_{j+1}=s.
\]
The values \(c_m(s)\) satisfy the exact subset-sum recursion
\[
  c_{m+1}(s)=c_m(s)+c_m(s-F_{m+2}),
\]
with the usual convention that \(c_m(t)=0\) for \(t<0\).  Thus the verifier
computes all \(c_m(s)\) exactly for \(m\le 34\).  Since
\[
  \sum_{j=1}^m F_{j+1}=F_{m+3}-2<2F_{m+2},
\]
every value of \(N_m(\omega)\) is either \(r\) or \(r+F_{m+2}\) for a unique
residue \(0\le r<F_{m+2}\).  Therefore the fiber multiplicity at residue \(r\)
is exactly
\[
  d_m(r)=c_m(r)+c_m(r+F_{m+2}),
\]
and hence
\[
  S_q(m)=\sum_{r=0}^{F_{m+2}-1} d_m(r)^q.
\]
This gives exact integers \(S_q(m)\) for the full range needed in the
arithmetic window, since \(2n_q\le 34\) for \(q\le 17\).

With these exact integers in hand, the verifier forms the displayed Hankel
matrix \(H_q\) and checks the nullmode equalities by direct matrix
multiplication over \(\ZZ\).  It also computes the rank over \(\QQ\) and checks
that the leading \(d_q\times d_q\) minor is nonzero, which gives
\(\rk H_q=d_q\).  Finally, it evaluates the residual block
\(P_q(E)S_q(j)\) for \(2\le j\le n_q+1\) directly from the same exact moments.
These are precisely the finite records needed later for the Hankel-window and
Smith computations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Kernel residual certificate gives the all-tail recurrence]
\label{prop:quotient-residual-equivalence}
For the exact histogram kernels, the certificate records of
Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} give the residual identity in
\Cref{tab:appendix-recurrence-cert-normalization}, namely
\[
  P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q\in
  Z_2(\widetilde A_q,\widetilde u_q,\widetilde v_q).
\]
Consequently it implies the recurrence in the stated validity range.  The
finite row set in \Cref{tab:appendix-residual-order-certificates} appears only
because Cayley--Hamilton gives a finite construction of \(Z_2\); it is not a
finite scalar sample check of the moment sequence.
The row-reduction rank identities are not needed for this implication.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(q\) and abbreviate
\((A,u,v)=(\widetilde A_q,\widetilde u_q,\widetilde v_q)\).  The exact
residual identity gives
\[
  u^{\mathsf T}A^tP_q(A)v=0\qquad(t=2,\dots,N_q+1).
\]
By \Cref{lem:cayley-hamilton-recurrence-certificate}, these finitely many
equalities are equivalent to \(P_q(A)v\in Z_2(A,u,v)\).  Hence
\[
  u^{\mathsf T}A^tP_q(A)v=0\qquad(t\ge2).
\]
Putting \(m=t+d_q\), using \(m_0(q)=d_q+2\), and rewriting
\(S_q(m)=u^{\mathsf T}A^mv\) gives the recurrence for \(S_q(m)\) throughout
the displayed tail.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kernel derivation of the arithmetic-window recurrences]
\label{prop:kernel-derived-recurrences}
For each \(q=9,\dots,17\), the coefficient row of
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17}, together with the exact residual
certificate of Theorem~\ref{thm:arithmetic-window-certificates} recorded in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrence-cert-normalization}, gives a
recurrence satisfied by \(S_q(m)\) for every integer \(m\) in the displayed
validity range.  Equivalently,
the exact kernel identity, for every \(t\ge2\),
\[
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^t
  P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0
\]
holds for every \(q=9,\dots,17\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(q\in\{9,\dots,17\}\), and take the exact histogram realization
\[
  S_q(m)=\widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^m\widetilde v_q
\]
from \Cref{lem:exact-histogram-realization}.  Let \(d_q\) and \(m_0(q)\) be
the data in Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17}, and write its
coefficient row as \((c_{q,1},\dots,c_{q,d_q})\).  Set
\[
  P_q(X):=
  X^{d_q}
  -c_{q,1}X^{d_q-1}
  -\cdots
  -c_{q,d_q}.
\]

By Theorem~\ref{prop:observable-quotient-derivation}, the residual certificate
\(\mathsf W_q(P_q)=\mathrm{accept}\) holds as an exact identity in
\(\widetilde A_q\).  Applying
\Cref{thm:apriori-exact-kernel-certification} gives the kernel identity for
every \(t\ge2\),
\[
  \widetilde u_q^{\mathsf T}\widetilde A_q^t
  P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0.
\]
Every row of Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17} has
\(m_0(q)=d_q+2\).  Put \(m=t+d_q\).  The displayed identity becomes
\[
  S_q(m)=\sum_{i=1}^{d_q}c_{q,i}S_q(m-i)
  \qquad(m\ge d_q+2).
\]
This proves the recurrence in the displayed validity range.  The proof has used
the coefficient row only after the exact residual verifier has accepted it in the
kernel.

\Cref{prop:quotient-residual-equivalence} gives the same proof in
unobservable-subspace language: the identity
\(R_qP_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q=0\) is exactly the finite construction
of the inclusion
\(P_q(\widetilde A_q)\widetilde v_q\in Z_2\), and membership in \(Z_2\) gives
the whole tail.  This theorem is therefore the proof of the recurrence rows in
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-recurrences-q9-17}.  No floating-point root data, and no
extrapolation from finitely many scalar values of \(S_q(m)\), enters the proof.
\end{proof}
