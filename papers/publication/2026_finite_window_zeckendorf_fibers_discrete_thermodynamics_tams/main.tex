\begin{abstract}
Let \(R(n)\) be the number of partitions of \(n\) into distinct Fibonacci
numbers, with the two unit terms identified.  Sidorov--Vershik's finite
representation frequencies are the same subset-sum coefficients after the
shift from their convention \(F^{\rm SV}_j=F_{j+1}\).  Their normalized
frequencies approximate the Erd\H{o}s measure.  Consequently the layer moment
pressure is the affine normalization
\(P(t)=t\log2-\tau_\mu(t)\log\varphi\) of the \(L^q\)-spectrum of the
golden-ratio Bernoulli convolution.  Lau--Ngai determined the positive-\(q\)
spectrum, while Feng--Olivier and Feng determined the all-real spectrum and
its negative first-order phase transition.  We record the exact dictionary
and recover those results in Fibonacci-partition coordinates.  The arithmetic
  refinements identify the critical parameter by
  \(\zeta(\sigma_0-1)/\zeta(\sigma_0)=2\).  A one-large-partial-quotient
  theorem proves the local denominator-layer law
  \(b_{2d+1}(s)\sim2\{\zeta(s-1)/\zeta(s)\}^2d^{-s}\) for every fixed
  \(s>2\).  At criticality its explicit context constant is \(b_C=8\); the
  resulting stable-renewal expansion gives the first noninteger correction of
  order \(m^{3-\sigma_0}\) to the one-layer and finite-window partition sums.
  The critical Gibbs law converges to a uniform mixture of generating-layer
  fractions.  Under the
 uniform law on \(\mathcal I_m\), \(m^{-1}\log R(n)\) satisfies a full large
 deviation principle; the rate is affine on the coexistence interval.  Jointly
 with normalized generating cost, it satisfies a two-dimensional large
 deviation principle whose rate surface has an explicit affine coexistence
face.  The inputs specific to these refinements are a weighted renewal identity
for Weinstein's free generators and a parity-free orbit-filling argument for
the nonexposed slopes.

A finite-window Zeckendorf fold provides an exact finite model for the same
spectrum: its fiber list is the list of \(R\) on two consecutive layers.
An affine permutation also identifies each fiber with a finite Fibonacci
subset-sum coefficient and a partition-function difference.  This yields the
 exact largest fibers
 \(D_{2k}=F_{k+2}\) for \(k\ge0\), \(D_{2k+1}=2F_{k+1}\) for \(k\ge1\), and
 \(D_m=D_{m-2}+D_{m-4}\) for \(m\ge6\), including every maximizing residue.  The two-layer
model has the same pressure and large-deviation rate, an exact frozen
Dirichlet limit, and a critical finite-size constant \(4/\mu_C\).  On every
fixed finite prime support we retain an exact rational coefficient generating
function; no directional coefficient asymptotic is asserted.
\end{abstract}
