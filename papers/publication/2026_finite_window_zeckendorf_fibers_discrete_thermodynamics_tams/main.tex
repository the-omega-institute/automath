\begin{abstract}
Fix \(s>2\), and weight the canonical regular continued fractions
\([0;a_1,\ldots,a_r]\) with \(a_r\ge2\) and
\(a_1+\cdots+a_r=n\) proportionally to the inverse \(s\)-th power of their
denominators.  We prove a total-variation condensation theorem: with
probability tending to one there is a unique partial quotient larger than
\(n/2\), and the words on its left and right converge jointly to independent
finite words whose masses are proportional to their inverse-continuant
weights.  Thus the largest partial quotient is \(n-O_{\mathbb P}(1)\).  We
give the exact limiting law of its defect, the joint total-variation limit of
its location and the numbers of partial quotients on both sides, and the
asymptotic factorization of the sampled denominator.

The normalization is the fixed-digit-sum continuant sum studied by
Dushistova after the Farey-tree work of Moshchevitin--Zhigljavsky.  We prove
\(Z_n(s)\sim2\{\zeta(s-1)/\zeta(s)\}^2n^{-s}\) without a tail hypothesis.
Under the exact translation \(n=d+1\), this is the termwise identity
\(b_{2d+1}(s)=\sigma_{s/2}(d+1)\).  We also localize the loss of the
restriction \(u>1\) in Dushistova's Lemma~7: the empty left context is
included in a doubled convolution and counted twice, so the printed leading
coefficient \(R_s+2R_s^2\) has excess \(R_s\) and becomes \(2R_s^2\).

As downstream consequences, at the arithmetic critical point
\(\zeta(\sigma_0-1)/\zeta(\sigma_0)=2\), the condensation estimate supplies
the regularly varying cost law and the constant \(b_C=8\).  A weighted
renewal identity for Weinstein's free generators then gives a stable law and
the noninteger \(m^{3-\sigma_0}\) correction to one-layer Fibonacci
partition sums.  We also prove full one- and two-dimensional large deviation
principles, including affine coexistence faces, and transfer them to an exact
finite-window Zeckendorf model.  The pressure and its negative-temperature
transition are the published \(L^q\)-spectrum of the golden-ratio Bernoulli
convolution in an affine normalization; they are recovered here, not claimed
as new.
\end{abstract}
