\begin{abstract}
We determine the largest fibers of finite-window Zeckendorf stabilization
without a recurrence assumption.  If \(D_m=\max_x d_m(x)\), then
\(D_0=D_1=1\), \(D_{2k}=F_{k+2}\) for \(k\ge0\), and
\(D_{2k+1}=2F_{k+1}\) for \(k\ge1\); hence
\(D_m=D_{m-2}+D_{m-4}\) for every \(m\ge6\).  All maximizing residues and their
degeneracies are classified explicitly.  The proof identifies the fiber list
with ordinary Fibonacci-partition values on two exact intervals.  After an
affine reindexing, the same multiplicities are Fibonacci-lag discrete
derivatives of the classical partition function.  The collision moments
\(S_q(m)=\sum_x d_m(x)^q\) satisfy
\(S_q(m)\asymp_q\lambda_q^m\), and a finite-state kernel identifies each
\(\lambda_q\), \(q\ge2\), as an algebraic-integer Perron root.  The integer
pressure is convex and yields canonical thickness bands and the
zero-temperature endpoint \(\frac12\log\varphi\).  We also isolate the exact
remaining interface for a full large deviation principle: conditional on
existence of a real-analytic, strictly convex real-tilt pressure, the
G\"artner--Ellis theorem gives a full LDP with an analytic strictly convex rate
function on the interior of its effective interval, and its upper endpoint is
\(\frac12\log\varphi\).
In the arithmetic window $q=9,\dots,17$, explicit exact-kernel residual
certificates give recurrence polynomials that are irreducible over
$\QQ$ with full symmetric Galois groups $S_{d_q}$; for
\(q\in\{12,13,14,15\}\) the
splitting fields are linearly disjoint, giving the product Chebotarev density
$1/20449$ for simultaneous irreducibility.
\end{abstract}
