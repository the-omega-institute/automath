\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The finite-window fiber spectrum admits two exact descriptions.  The affine
permutation \(\pi_m\) identifies each multiplicity with a coefficient of a
finite Fibonacci subset-sum polynomial and with a Fibonacci-lag difference of
the indexed partition function.  The interval identity identifies the same
fiber list with ordinary partition values on two consecutive Fibonacci
layers.  The latter correspondence transfers the published extremal
classification, after an explicit convention change, and yields
\[
 D_{2k}=F_{k+2},\qquad D_{2k+1}=2F_{k+1},\qquad
 D_m=D_{m-2}+D_{m-4}\quad(m\ge6),
\]
together with the complete maximizing-residue list.

For positive integer orders, the adjacent-window sandwich transfers Sanna's
partition power-sum constants to the finite-window moments.  Sanna's endpoint
law \(\lambda_q^{1/q}\to\varphi^{1/2}\) also follows here from the maximum-norm
squeeze; the separate consequence of that squeeze is the diagonal limit for
\(q_m\to\infty\), and indeed for arbitrary real \(t_m\to\infty\).  The
quadratic moment has a direct cubic recurrence that does not use finite-state
normalization.

For negative real tilts, Weinstein's orbit counts give the frozen half-line
\[
 P(t)=0\qquad(t\le-\sigma_0),
 \qquad
 \frac{\zeta(\sigma_0-1)}{\zeta(\sigma_0)}=2.
\]
The uniform layer-count lemma treats the two unit conventions, the levels
\(1\) and \(2\), shared endpoints, and early layers explicitly.  Analytic
perturbation theory then rules out a representation of all real pressure
limits by the positive simple isolated eigenvalue of an analytic compact
operator family satisfying the stated local gap condition.
At the unnormalized scale, however, the local spectrum stabilizes exactly and
\[
 S_{-s}(m)\longrightarrow
 \frac4{2-\zeta(s-1)/\zeta(s)}-2
 \qquad(s>\sigma_0).
\]
This function has a simple pole at \(\sigma_0\), produces an explicit
total-variation Gibbs limit, and yields the Tauberian law
\(\sum_{k\le x}\Psi(k)\sim
x^{\sigma_0}/(\sigma_0\kappa)\).  A separate Jensen lower bound proves
positive lower exponential growth for \(t>-\beta_*\), where
\(\beta_*=2.2705594539\ldots\).  The weighted free-generator renewal equation,
together with the published Stern--Brocot denominator pressure, proves
existence for every real tilt and gives
\[
 \{t:P(t)>0\}=(-\sigma_0,\infty).
\]
On this positive phase, \(P\) is real analytic and strictly convex.  At the
freezing point its left derivative is zero and its right derivative is a
finite positive continued-fraction moment ratio, so the transition is not
\(C^1\).

The derivative jump prevents the G\"artner--Ellis theorem alone from treating
the intervening slopes.  A finite-window orbit-filling construction supplies
their matching local lower bounds and proves a full large deviation principle;
its good rate function is affine on the coexistence interval.  The marked
critical renewal measure further resolves the conditional geometry inside
this interval.  In a fixed band centered at \(\alpha\), the log multiplicity
selects the upper edge \(\alpha+\varepsilon\), and the generating-layer ratio
converges to \((\alpha+\varepsilon)/v_c\).  This yields the iterated limit
\(\alpha/v_c\), remains valid for shrinking windows with
\(\liminf m\varepsilon_m>0\), and cannot in general have exponential
concentration because the letters \(1/q\) produce a polynomial upper tail.
No
 transducer-based collision kernel, algebraicity statement for \(\lambda_q\),
arithmetic-window
recurrence polynomial, exact-kernel certificate, Galois group, or Chebotarev
density is claimed in this paper.
