Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration the manuscript "Arithmetic Criticality and
Large Deviations for Fibonacci Partitions and Finite-Window Fibers."

The paper studies the classical Fibonacci partition function R(n) and an exact
finite-window Zeckendorf fold. Its number-theoretic contributions are an
arithmetic evaluation of the critical parameter by
zeta(sigma_0-1)/zeta(sigma_0)=2, a low-temperature denominator-layer theorem
with an explicit one-large-partial-quotient context sum, and the resulting
stable second-order critical laws beyond the leading constants 2/mu_C and
4/mu_C. It also proves uniform coexistence laws, finite-layer large deviations
at the nonexposed slopes, and exact finite-window fiber identities.

The pressure framework is attributed directly to the existing theory of the
Erdos measure and the golden-ratio Bernoulli convolution. Sidorov and Vershik's
finite representation frequencies use F_j^(SV)=F_{j+1} in our convention and
are exactly the raw subset-sum coefficients. Hu studied local dimensions,
Lau and Ngai determined the positive-q L^q-spectrum, and Feng--Olivier and
Feng determined the all-real spectrum and its negative first-order phase
transition. In our convention the normalization is

    P(t) = t log 2 - tau_mu(t) log phi.

Accordingly, the manuscript records the all-real pressure and frozen branch as
recovery and consistency results. It does not claim them as new. A
theorem-by-theorem table in the introduction separates every imported result,
normalization, and surviving contribution.

The finite identities are stated with their exact domains. The affine
fiber--partition correspondence holds for m >= 1. In the maximizing-residue
formulas, I_{2k+1} is defined for k >= 5, while I_{2k} and J_{2k} are defined
for k >= 7. The manuscript also retains an exact rational generating-function
interface on each fixed finite prime support, but asserts no directional
coefficient or quenched-velocity asymptotic.

The main article is in amsart format. A separately compiled supplement contains
conditional arithmetic and integer-moment consequences. All external inputs,
including the extremal classification of Kocabova--Masakova--Pelantova,
Weinstein's free-generator decomposition, Stern--Brocot denominator pressure,
and standard renewal, Tauberian, and large-deviation theorems, are identified
at their points of use.

Thank you for considering this submission.

Sincerely,
The Authors
