Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration the manuscript "Fibonacci Partition
Thermodynamics and Finite-Window Zeckendorf Fibers."

The paper studies the classical Fibonacci partition function R(n) on one
standard Fibonacci layer. Its principal result determines the moment pressure
for every real tilt. The pressure freezes at the exact threshold determined
by zeta(sigma_0-1)/zeta(sigma_0)=2, is analytic and strictly convex above the
threshold, and has a first-order corner there. At criticality the partition
sum has an explicit linear asymptotic and the Gibbs law has a uniform
coexistence limit. Under the uniform one-layer law, m^{-1} log R(n) satisfies
an unconditional full large deviation principle, including the nonexposed
slopes at the corner.

A finite-window Zeckendorf fold provides an exact finite model: its complete
fiber list is the list of R on two consecutive layers. An affine permutation
also identifies individual fibers with finite Fibonacci subset-sum
coefficients and partition-function differences. After a precise convention
translation, the known extremal classification gives every largest fiber and
maximizing residue. The frozen phase further rules out a globally analytic
compact-operator representation under stated spectral hypotheses.

The proof separates its published inputs, namely Weinstein's free-generator
decomposition and Stern--Brocot denominator pressure, from the new weighted
renewal, critical finite-size, and parity-free orbit-filling arguments.

The manuscript does not assert a finite-state normalization transducer,
collision kernel, recurrence-polynomial certificate, Galois-group result, or
Chebotarev theorem. Those claims and their computational artifacts have been
withdrawn.

The manuscript is in amsart format and is self-contained apart from
the cited results that are transferred with explicit convention dictionaries.

Thank you for considering this submission.

Sincerely,
The Authors
