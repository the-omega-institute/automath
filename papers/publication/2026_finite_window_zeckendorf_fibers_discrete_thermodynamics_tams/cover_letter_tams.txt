Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration the manuscript "Finite-Window Zeckendorf
Fibers: Affine Partition Correspondence and Negative-Temperature Freezing."

The paper studies the fibers of a finite-window map that reduces a binary
Fibonacci sum modulo the next Fibonacci number and selects the corresponding
legal visible word. Its principal result is an explicit affine permutation
identifying individual fiber multiplicities with coefficients of a finite
Fibonacci subset-sum polynomial and with Fibonacci-lag differences of the
classical partition function. A complementary interval identity identifies
the complete fiber list with ordinary partition values on two consecutive
Fibonacci layers. After a precise translation of Fibonacci conventions, this
transfers the known extremal classification and gives all largest fibers and
maximizing residues.

The second principal result is a negative-temperature freezing theorem. Using
Weinstein's orbit counts, with the low levels, endpoints, and early layers
treated explicitly, the paper proves that the real-tilt pressure vanishes for
all t at or below the exact threshold determined by
zeta(sigma_0-1)/zeta(sigma_0)=2. A precise analytic-perturbation argument then
rules out a globally analytic compact-operator representation under stated
spectral hypotheses. The full real-tilt large deviation principle is stated
only as a conditional corollary; its residual pressure hypothesis remains
open.

Supporting results transfer Sanna's integer partition power-sum constants to
the finite-window moments, give a direct cubic recurrence for the quadratic
moment, and isolate a diagonal high-tilt consequence. Sanna's previously
published endpoint law is explicitly credited as prior work.

The manuscript does not assert a finite-state normalization transducer,
collision kernel, recurrence-polynomial certificate, Galois-group result, or
Chebotarev theorem. Those claims and their computational artifacts have been
withdrawn.

The manuscript is 30 pages in amsart format and is self-contained apart from
the cited results that are transferred with explicit convention dictionaries.

Thank you for considering this submission.

Sincerely,
The Authors
