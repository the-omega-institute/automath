Dear Editors,

We submit "Context-free rigidity in recurrent numeration: prime support and
a Cobham theorem" for consideration as an Original Research Article in
Monatshefte fuer Mathematik.

The article studies arithmetic rigidity for canonical languages in
eventually recurrent numeration systems. One branch combines weak
multiple-context-free pumping with the affine recurrence action, adic
topology, and recurrence arithmetic to classify bounded prime support.
The second branch combines the paired-loop classification of slender
context-free languages with dominant-root transfer and Mignotte's theorem
to prove a two-system Cobham finiteness theorem.

Online Resource 1 is a reproducibility archive containing the deterministic
Python verifier, unit tests, archived outputs, the literature record,
checksums, and reproduction instructions. The mathematical proofs are
self-contained and do not depend on computational sampling.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests. No
research datasets were generated or analysed.
