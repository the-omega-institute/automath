\begin{abstract}
This document collects the independent finite-state part of the project:
the detailed case analysis for deterministic automata and fixed-base prime
slices, the Zeckendorf growth and regular-language consequences, and the
classical analytic comparisons cited there. None is used in the recurrent
multiple-context-free theorem chain of the article. The ordinary-base
fixed-DFA bounds are compared explicitly with Shen's qualitative obstruction
and Dubbe's growing-state automaticity bound.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Dependency and submission scope}

The supplement is submitted as a separately compiled companion. Its
dependency graph is as follows. The deterministic transition-matrix
expansions in the first section imply the ordinary-base fixed-DFA slice
bounds and provide the finite-budget input for the Zeckendorf slice bounds.
The Zeckendorf regular-immunity argument instead uses its own unimodular
block-action lemma and is independent of those density estimates. The final
analytic comparisons are independent of both automata branches. The article
repeats the short Zeckendorf block-action lemma needed for its motivational
context-free theorem, so no theorem in the article cites a supplement number.
