Dear Editors,

We submit "Prime support and multiple-context-free languages in recurrent
numeration" for consideration as an Original Research Article in Monatshefte
fuer Mathematik.

The article studies prime-support obstructions for canonical languages in
eventually recurrent numeration systems. Its main results combine weak
multiple-context-free pumping with the affine recurrence action, adic
topology, and recurrence arithmetic. The manuscript is mathematically
self-contained; none of its theorem proofs depends on a numbered result in
the Supplementary Information.

The submission includes two referee-facing online resources. Online Resource
1 is the separately compiled Supplementary Information PDF containing the
independent finite-state, Zeckendorf, and analytic comparisons described in
the article. Online Resource 2 is the reproducibility archive containing the
Python verifier, its unit tests, archived verifier and test outputs, the
literature audit, checksums, and reproduction instructions. We ask that both
online resources be made available to the referees with the main manuscript.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests. No
research datasets were generated or analysed.
