\subsection{Stationary likelihood reduction and the two-state proof}
\label{subsec:stationary-renewal-lan-proof-main}

We use the following renewal lemma with a finite-dimensional parameter
\(q\).  Its uniform formulation is included because replacing a deterministic
number of Palm gaps by the stationary record is essential here.

\begin{lemma}[Compact-uniform stationary renewal LAN]
\label{lem:compact-uniform-stationary-renewal-lan-main}
Let \(\mathcal Q_0\) be compact.  Suppose that, on a fixed one-sided
neighbourhood of \(\mathcal Q_0\), the integer-valued Palm gap masses
\(g_q(k)>0\) satisfy the following bounds for some \(r<1\):
\[
 \sup_q g_q(k)\le C(1+k)^m r^k,
 \qquad
 \sup_q|\partial_q^a\log g_q(k)|\le C_a(1+k)^{m_a},
 \quad 1\le |a|\le3.
\tag{SL1}
\]
Assume the same type of polynomial bound for the first two derivatives of
\(\log S_q(k)\), where \(S_q(k)=\sum_{j\ge k}g_q(j)\), and put
\(\mu_q=\sum_{k\ge0}S_q(k)\).  If
\[
 I(q)=\mu_q^{-1}\sum_{k\ge0}g_q(k)
 \dot\ell_q(k)\dot\ell_q(k)^\top,
 \qquad \dot\ell_q=\nabla_q\log g_q,
\tag{SL2}
\]
is positive definite on \(\mathcal Q_0\), then the stationary binary renewal
record is LAN, uniformly over \(q\in\mathcal Q_0\) and bounded admissible
local vectors \(h\):
\[
 \log\frac{dP_{q+h/\sqrt N}^{(N)}}{dP_q^{(N)}}
 =h^\top\Delta_{N,q}-\frac12h^\top I(q)h+o_{P_q}(1),
 \qquad
 \Delta_{N,q}\Rightarrow\mathcal N\{0,I(q)\}.
\tag{SL3}
\]
The convergence of the central sequence and the remainder are compact-uniform.
The conclusion remains valid when some coordinates of \(h\) are restricted
to a closed convex cone.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let the clicks in \([0,N-1]\) be
\(0\le C_1<\cdots<C_J<N\), set
\[
 A=C_1,\qquad R=N-1-C_J,
 \qquad G_j=C_{j+1}-C_j-1\quad(1\le j<J).
\]
For \(J\ge1\), stationary Palm inversion gives the exact record probability
\[
 P_q^{(N)}(A_0,\ldots,A_{N-1})
 =\frac1{\mu_q}S_q(A)
   \prod_{j=1}^{J-1}g_q(G_j)S_q(R).
\tag{SL4}
\]
Here \(A\) is the forward recurrence (residual life) from the left boundary
and \(R\) is the backward recurrence (age) at the right boundary.  Thus
\(S_q(A)/\mu_q\) is the equilibrium forward-recurrence mass of the first
click, the displayed gaps are complete Palm cycles, and after the last click
the absence of another click contributes \(S_q(R)\).  Formula
\textup{(SL4)} is exact only on \(J\ge1\).  The all-zero record has a separate
probability, bounded by \(CN^{m+1}r^N\), uniformly in \(q\), by summing over
the equilibrium cycle containing the whole window.

\Cref{lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main}, applied with
\(K_N=J-1\), gives uniformly in \(q\),
\[
 J/N\longrightarrow1/\mu_q,\qquad
 J=N/\mu_q+O_{P_q}(\sqrt N),\qquad
 \max\{A,R,G_1,\ldots,G_{J-1}\}=O_{P_q}(\log N).
\tag{SL5}
\]
including for every bounded local triangular array.  Taylor's theorem for
\(\log\mu_q\), the polynomial log-tail derivative bound, and
\textup{(SL5)} imply, for bounded \(h\),
\[
 \log\frac{\mu_q}{\mu_{q+h/\sqrt N}}
 +\log\frac{S_{q+h/\sqrt N}(A)}{S_q(A)}
 +\log\frac{S_{q+h/\sqrt N}(R)}{S_q(R)}=o_{P_q}(1)
\tag{SL6}
\]
uniformly.  Thus the increments of the logarithms of the two endpoint factors
and \(\mu_q^{-1}\), rather than those factors themselves, are negligible in
the root-\(N\) local likelihood ratio.

Taylor expansion of the complete-gap part of \textup{(SL4)} yields
\[
 \frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_{j=1}^{J-1}h^\top\dot\ell_q(G_j)
 +\frac1{2N}\sum_{j=1}^{J-1}
 h^\top\ddot\ell_q(G_j)h+o_{P_q}(1).
\tag{SL7}
\]
The third-order remainder is uniform by the last assertion of
\Cref{lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main}: after the \(N^{-3/2}\)
Taylor factor, the stopped sum of the common polynomial envelope is
\(o_P(1)\).
Differentiating \(\sum_kg_q(k)=1\), justified by \textup{(SL1)}, gives
\[
 \EE_q\dot\ell_q(G)=0,\qquad
 \EE_q\ddot\ell_q(G)=-\EE_q\{
 \dot\ell_q(G)\dot\ell_q(G)^\top\}.
\tag{SL8}
\]
The stopped Hessian LLN \textup{(U8)} and \textup{(SL8)} turn the second
term in \textup{(SL7)} into \(-h^\top I(q)h/2+o_P(1)\).  The bounded-
Lipschitz stopped-score CLT \textup{(U7)} gives
\[
 \Delta_{N,q}:=N^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^{J-1}\dot\ell_q(G_j)
 \Rightarrow\mathcal N\{0,I(q)\}.
\tag{SL9}
\]
The cited stopped-renewal lemma proves these assertions jointly and uniformly,
including under the local triangular arrays.  Combining
\textup{(SL4)}--\textup{(SL9)} proves \textup{(SL3)}.  Restricting local
coordinates to a cone changes only the parameter set of the limit experiment.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of \Cref{thm:stationary-two-state-collision-main}]
Let \(W=X_1+X_2\), where the two independent exponential holding times have
rates \(c+d\) and \(c-d\).  Its Laplace transform and the transform of its
continuous survival function are
\[
 \phi_{c,\delta}(s)
 =\frac{c^2-\delta}{(s+c)^2-\delta},
 \qquad
 H_{c,\delta}(s)=\frac{1-\phi_{c,\delta}(s)}s.
\tag{T21}
\]
Differentiation at \(\delta=0\) gives exactly \textup{(SC5)} and
\[
 \partial_cH_{c,0}(s)=-\frac{2c}{(s+c)^3}.
\tag{T22}
\]
Inverse Laplace transformation and sampling at \(t=k\Delta\tau\) show that
\(\partial_\delta S_{c,0}(k)\) has a nonzero cubic coefficient at
\(e^{-c\Delta\tau k}\), whereas \(\partial_cS_{c,0}(k)\) has degree two.
Taking first differences preserves linear independence.  Since every
\(g_{c,0}(k)>0\), a linear combination of the two scores can vanish in
\(L^2(g_{c,0})\) only if the corresponding linear combination of the mass
derivatives, hence of the tail derivatives, vanishes at every \(k\).  The
distinct polynomial degrees then force both coefficients to be zero.  This
proves pointwise positive definiteness in \textup{(SC3)}.

On \([\varepsilon,M]\), partial-fraction inversion gives common absolute
bounds
\[
 |\partial_c^a\partial_\delta^b S_{c,\delta}(k)|
 \le C_{a,b}(1+k)^{m_{a,b}}e^{-\varepsilon\Delta\tau k/2}
\tag{T23}
\]
on a fixed neighbourhood with \(c\pm\sqrt\delta>0\).  The relative log-mass
and log-tail bounds do not follow from \textup{(T23)} alone; they follow from
the conditional complete-data argument in
\Cref{lem:uniform-sampled-bin-tail-score-main}, specialized to \(n=2\).
Continuity of \(I_2(c)\), dominated by that lemma, and compactness now give
\textup{(SC6)}.  Applying
\Cref{lem:compact-uniform-stationary-renewal-lan-main} with
\(q=(c,\delta)\) proves \textup{(SC4)} and the half-plane limit.

For completeness, the efficient central sequence for the collision
coordinate is
\[
 \Delta_{\delta\cdot c,N}
 =\Delta_{\delta,N}-I_{\delta c}I_{cc}^{-1}\Delta_{c,N},
 \qquad \Var(\Delta_{\delta\cdot c,N})\longrightarrow J_2(c).
\tag{T24}
\]
Replacing \(c\) by the root-\(N\) null recurrence fit and the information
entries by their empirical versions changes the standardized statistic by
\(o_P(1)\), by \Cref{lem:efficient-score-plugin-main}.  Le Cam's third lemma
gives mean \(v\sqrt{J_2(c)}\) under the local alternative.  The
Neyman--Pearson lemma in the residual one-dimensional Gaussian shift gives
\textup{(SC8)} and its envelope; the one-sided likelihood-ratio statistic is
the square of the positive part of the same standardized score.  This is the
standard Gaussian-cone conclusion
\cite{Chernoff1954LikelihoodRatio,SelfLiang1987NonstandardLRT,
vanDerVaart1998AsymptoticStatistics}.

We next give an explicit rate-attaining estimator.  From the complete gaps in
the stationary record define
\[
 \widehat S_k=K_N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}\mathbf1_{\{G_j\ge k\}},
 \qquad 0\le k\le3,
\tag{T25}
\]
with an arbitrary fixed value on \(K_N=0\).  The endpoint deletion and the
stopped i.i.d. CLT give
\(\max_{k\le3}|\widehat S_k-S_k|=O_P(N^{-1/2})\) uniformly.  The order-two
tail recurrence is
\[
 S_{k+2}-A S_{k+1}+B S_k=0,\qquad
 A=z_1+z_2,\quad B=z_1z_2,\quad z_i=e^{-\theta_i\Delta\tau}.
\tag{T26}
\]
Its leading Hankel matrix is nonsingular at the double pole by
\Cref{cor:nstate-serial-all-collision-identifiability-main}; compactness makes
the inverse uniform.  Solving the empirical version of \textup{(T26)} gives
\((\widehat A,\widehat B)-(A,B)=O_P(N^{-1/2})\).

In a neighbourhood of \(A=2z,B=z^2\), the functions
\[
 c=-\frac1{2\Delta\tau}\log B,\qquad
 \delta=\frac1{(\Delta\tau)^2}
 \left[\operatorname{arcosh}\left(\frac{A}{2\sqrt B}\right)\right]^2
\tag{T27}
\]
are real analytic after continuing the square of \(\operatorname{arcosh}\)
through one.  Let \(\widetilde\delta_N\) be this analytic continuation and
put \(\widehat\delta_N=(\widetilde\delta_N)^+\).  Then
\[
 \widehat c_N-c_N=O_P(N^{-1/2}),\qquad
 \widehat\delta_N-\delta_N=O_P(N^{-1/2})
\tag{T28}
\]
uniformly on bounded collision alternatives.  Because
\(|\sqrt{x^+}-\sqrt{y}|\le\sqrt{|x-y|}\) for \(y\ge0\), the pair
\(\{\!\{\widehat c_N\pm\sqrt{\widehat\delta_N}\}\!\}\) satisfies
\textup{(SC9)}.

Finally apply \Cref{lem:two-point-threshold-rate-main} with \(n=2\).  It gives
both the disjoint-ball reduction and the strictly positive Gaussian testing
lower bound, and therefore proves \textup{(SC10)}.  This completes the
two-state result.
\end{proof}
