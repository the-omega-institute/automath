=\PP(\mathcal A_N^{\rm aux}\cap\mathcal E_N^c)
\le \PP(\mathcal E_N^c).
\]
The convergence follows from the assumed vanishing withholding probability.
The fixed-event hypothesis is essential: the displayed inequality is not a
post-selection theorem for an auxiliary event chosen after seeing the record.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Retained-cycle CLT reduction for adjacent-gap rewards]\label{lem:retained-cycle-covariance-adjacent-gap-main}
Work under the retained stationary renewal representation in
\Cref{ass:finite-record-master-main}, with i.i.d. gaps \(G_j\), cycle lengths
\(L_j=G_j+1\), \(\mu=\EE L_0\in(0,\infty)\), and an exponential gap moment.
For the three inclusion coordinates put
\[
U_j=
\bigl(1,\mathbf1_{\{G_j=0\}},
\mathbf1_{\{G_j=1\}}+\mathbf1_{\{G_j=0,G_{j+1}=0\}}\bigr)^\top,
\qquad
Y_j=U_j-\Rinc L_j=f(G_j,G_{j+1}),
\]
where \(\Rinc=(r_0,r_1,r_2)^\top\) and
\(r_m=\PP(A_0=A_m=1)\).  Then \(\EE Y_j=0\),
\(\EE\|Y_j\|^4<\infty\), and \((Y_j)\) is \(1\)-dependent.  Define
\[
T_1^{(r)}
=\Var(Y_0)+\operatorname{Cov}(Y_0,Y_1)+\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_0),
\qquad
\Sigma_r=T_1^{(r)}/\mu .
\]
Let
\[
S_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}L_j,\qquad
K_N=\max\{k:S_k<N\},
\]
with the harmless auxiliary value \(K_N=0\) if no retained click is present.
Let \(J_N\) be the common complete-cycle, terminal-look-ahead-retained index
set used by \(\Rinch_N^{\mathrm{cyc}}\) and Algorithm~1.  Then the
endpoint and terminal-look-ahead deletion has the explicit negligible scale
\begin{equation}\label{eq:retained-cycle-endpoint-deletion-main}
\left\|
\sum_{j\in J_N}Y_j-\sum_{j=0}^{K_N-1}Y_j
\right\|=O_{\PP}(\log N),
\qquad
\sqrt N(\Rinch_N^{\mathrm{cyc}}-\Rinc)
=\frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_{j=0}^{K_N-1}Y_j+o_{\PP}(1).
\end{equation}
Consequently
\[
\sqrt N(\Rinch_N^{\mathrm{cyc}}-\Rinc)
\Rightarrow \mathcal N(0,\Sigma_r).
\]

The retained covariance estimator for this same adjacent-gap reward, including
the exact \(J_N\), \(M_N\), separator, multiplier \(K_N/N\), empirical batch
mean, and plug-in centering by \(\Rinch_N^{\mathrm{cyc}}\), is the object
in \Cref{prop:exact-inclusion-separated-batch-covariance-main}.  In
particular, for the paper's protocol \(b_N=\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor\), its
auxiliary total-space completion obeys
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_r\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/3}).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The formula for \(U_j\) is the adjacent-gap version of the three inclusion
rewards: a Palm cycle contributes one left-end click, the lag-one coordinate
is \(\mathbf1_{\{G_j=0\}}\), and the lag-two coordinate is
\(\mathbf1_{\{G_j=1\}}+\mathbf1_{\{G_j=0,G_{j+1}=0\}}\).  Palm inversion gives
\(\EE U_j=\Rinc\,\EE L_j\), hence \(\EE Y_j=0\).  Since \(Y_j=f(G_j,G_{j+1})\)
and the gaps are i.i.d., the sequence is \(1\)-dependent; the exponential gap
tail gives the fourth moment and the maximal-cycle bound
\(\max_{j\le K_N+2}L_j=O_{\PP}(\log N)\).  Deleting the fixed initial,
terminal, and terminal-look-ahead cycles can therefore change the retained
cycle sum by only \(O_{\PP}(\log N)\), proving the first estimate in
\eqref{eq:retained-cycle-endpoint-deletion-main}.  The second estimate follows
from the same endpoint bound after dividing by \(\sqrt N\), and the displayed
CLT is the ordinary \(1\)-dependent CLT for the cycle rewards combined with
the renewal random-index replacement \(K_N/N\to1/\mu\).

The covariance statement is exactly
\Cref{prop:exact-inclusion-separated-batch-covariance-main} with
\(b_N=\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Pre-diagonal quotient delta method]\label{prop:finite-record-pre-diagonal-quotient-rule-main}
Let \(\Rinch_N\) be any three-coordinate finite-record estimator.  On the
population inverse domain
\[
r_0>0,\qquad \Delta_{\mathrm{inv}}(\Rinc):=r_1-r_0^2\ne0,
\]
define
\[
a(\Rinc)=\frac{r_1}{r_0},\qquad
u_2(\Rinc)=\frac{r_2}{r_0},\qquad
\lambda(\Rinc)=\frac{u_2(\Rinc)-r_0}{a(\Rinc)-r_0},
\]
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finite-inverse-delta-map-main}
\Phi(\Rinc)=
\bigl(1-a(\Rinc)+\lambda(\Rinc),\,
r_0(1-\lambda(\Rinc))-a(\Rinc)+\lambda(\Rinc)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Then \(\Phi\) is \(C^1\) on a neighbourhood of every point in this domain, and
its Jacobian is the one displayed in
\eqref{eq:lambda-gradient-main}--\eqref{eq:phi-jacobian-main}.  If
\[
\sqrt N(\Rinch_N-\Rinc)\Rightarrow \mathcal N(0,\Sigma_r),
\]
then
\[
\sqrt N\{\Phi(\Rinch_N)-\Phi(\Rinc)\}
\Rightarrow
\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_{\sigma}),\qquad
\Sigma_{\sigma}=D\Phi(\Rinc)\Sigma_rD\Phi(\Rinc)^\top .
\]
Moreover, if a reported covariance estimator has an auxiliary total-space
completion \(\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}\) satisfying
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_r\|_{\operatorname{op}}=o_{\PP}(t_N),
\qquad t_N\downarrow0,\qquad N^{-1/2}=o(t_N),
\]
then the plug-in sandwich
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{\rm tot}
=\mathbf1_{\mathcal E_N}
D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}D\Phi(\Rinch_N)^\top
+\mathbf1_{\mathcal E_N^c}0_{2\times2}
\]
satisfies
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_{\sigma}\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=o_{\PP}(t_N).
\]
If, in addition, the constrained D-MAP population has
\[
\Phi(\Rinc)=\sigma=(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)=(p+s,ps),
\qquad
\Delta_\sigma:=\sigma_1^2-4\sigma_2=(p-s)^2>0,
\]
then the two unordered roots of
\[
z^2-\sigma_1z+\sigma_2=0
\]
are locally \(C^1\) functions of \(\sigma=\Phi(\Rinc)\).  The unordered
survival-factor set \(\{p,s\}\) is therefore identified locally from
\(\Phi(\Rinc)\).  For known design interval \(\Delta\tau\), the unordered
numerical rate set follows by applying
\[
z\longmapsto -\frac{\log z}{\Delta\tau}
\]
to the two roots.  Without known \(\Delta\tau\), the same roots identify only
the dimensionless products \(\{-\log p,-\log s\}
=\{\Gamma\Delta\tau,\kappa_{\mathrm r}\Delta\tau\}\).  No diagonal
projection, exchange-diagonal confidence, or diagonal no-label conclusion is
asserted in this proposition.

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The only possible poles of the displayed rational formula are \(r_0=0\) and
\(\Delta_{\mathrm{inv}}(\Rinc)=r_1-r_0^2=0\).  On the stated domain these poles are absent in a small
neighbourhood of \(\Rinc\), so \(\Phi\) is \(C^1\), with the already computed
derivative \eqref{eq:lambda-gradient-main}--\eqref{eq:phi-jacobian-main}.  The
ordinary multivariate delta method gives the Gaussian limit.

The covariance statement is the same continuous-mapping argument at the
Jacobian level.  The CLT gives \(\Rinch_N-\Rinc=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/2})\).  Since
\(D\Phi\) is \(C^1\) on the retained neighbourhood,
\[
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)-D\Phi(\Rinc)\|_{\operatorname{op}}=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/2}),
\qquad
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}=O_{\PP}(1).
\]
Submultiplicativity and the triangle inequality give
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left\|
D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}D\Phi(\Rinch_N)^\top
-D\Phi(\Rinc)\Sigma_rD\Phi(\Rinc)^\top
\right\|_{\operatorname{op}}\\
&\le
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}^2
\|\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_r\|_{\operatorname{op}}\\
&\quad+
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)-D\Phi(\Rinc)\|_{\operatorname{op}}\,
\|\Sigma_r\|_{\operatorname{op}}\,
\{\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}+\|D\Phi(\Rinc)\|_{\operatorname{op}}\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first term is \(o_{\PP}(t_N)\); the second is \(O_{\PP}(N^{-1/2})\), hence
\(o_{\PP}(t_N)\) by the stated threshold scale.

If \(\Delta_\sigma>0\), the discriminant is nonzero and the implicit-function
theorem applied to \(z^2-\sigma_1z+\sigma_2\) gives two local \(C^1\) root
charts; taking the set of their values gives the unordered root map, independent
of a branch labelling.  The logarithm map is \(C^1\) on compact subintervals of
\((0,1)\), so, for known design interval \(\Delta\tau\), the unordered
numerical rate set is the image of this unordered root set under
\(z\mapsto-\log z/\Delta\tau\).  Without known \(\Delta\tau\), the same root
set gives only the unordered dimensionless products.

\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Diagonal raw quotient CLT from the retained-record CLT and diagonal covariance formula]\label{lem:dmap-diagonal-raw-quotient-clt-main}
Assume the finite-record master assumption
\Cref{ass:finite-record-master-main}, specifically
\ref{fr:law-main}--\ref{fr:projection-main}.  Fix a constrained D-MAP
exchange-diagonal population point \(p=s=z\), \(0<z<1\), put
\(\theta=-\log z\), and write \(\sigma_z=(2z,z^2)\).  Let \(\Rinc(z)\) be the
corresponding inclusion-coordinate vector in
\Cref{prop:computable-diagonal-covariance-main}.  Then the quotient denominator
has the explicit diagonal value
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diagonal-quotient-denominator-main}
\Delta_{\mathrm{inv}}(z):=r_1(z)-r_0(z)^2
=-\frac{\theta^2z^2(1-z)^2}{(1-z+\theta z)^2}<0 .
\end{equation}
Consequently the diagonal point is not a singular point of the quotient map
\[
\Phi(\Rinc)=\left(1-\frac{r_1}{r_0}+\lambda,\;
r_0(1-\lambda)-\frac{r_1}{r_0}+\lambda\right),
\qquad
\lambda=\frac{r_2-r_0^2}{r_1-r_0^2}.
\]
The map \(\Phi\) is \(C^1\) on a neighbourhood of \(\Rinc(z)\), and \(D\Phi\) is
locally bounded there.  Hence, if Theorem~C gives
\[
\sqrt N(\Rinch_N-\Rinc(z))\Rightarrow
\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_r(z)),
\]
then the raw diagonal symmetric-coordinate estimator
\[
\widehat\sigma_N^{\rm raw}:=\Phi(\Rinch_N)
\]
satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diagonal-raw-quotient-clt-main}
\sqrt N\{\Phi(\Rinch_N)-\Phi(\Rinc(z))\}
\Rightarrow
\mathcal N\!\left(0,\,
D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\,\Sigma_r(z)\,D\Phi(\Rinc(z))^\top\right).
\end{equation}
For the constrained D-MAP population, \(\Phi(\Rinc(z))=\sigma_z\).

Moreover, for any reported inclusion covariance estimator whose auxiliary
total-space completion satisfies
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_r(z)\|_{\operatorname{op}}=o_{\PP}(t_N),
\qquad t_N\downarrow0,\qquad N^{-1/2}=o(t_N),
\]
the reported plug-in sandwich on \(\mathcal E_N\),
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}
=D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}D\Phi(\Rinch_N)^\top,\qquad
\Sigma_\sigma(z)=D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\Sigma_r(z)D\Phi(\Rinc(z))^\top,
\]
has auxiliary total-space completion
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{\rm tot}
:=\mathbf 1_{\mathcal E_N}\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}
+\mathbf 1_{\mathcal E_N^c}0_{2\times2}
\]
satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diagonal-sandwich-consistency-main}
\|\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_\sigma(z)\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=o_{\PP}(t_N).
\end{equation}
In particular, with the separated-batch estimator from
\Cref{thm:finite-record-covariance-main} and \(b_N=\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor\),
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_\sigma(z)\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/3})=o_{\PP}(N^{-1/4}).
\]
Thus the raw diagonal CLT and the covariance consistency needed for the
exchange-diagonal projection theorem are consequences of Theorem~C,
\Cref{prop:finite-record-pre-diagonal-quotient-rule-main}, and the separated-batch
covariance theorem, not separate diagonal assumptions and not consequences of
Theorem~D or Theorem~E.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{prop:computable-diagonal-covariance-main} gives the diagonal coordinates
\(\Rinc(z)\).  Substituting the equal-rate dictionary with
\(\theta=-\log z\) into \(\Delta_{\mathrm{inv}}=r_1-r_0^2\) gives
\eqref{eq:diagonal-quotient-denominator-main}.  Since \(0<z<1\),
\(\theta>0\), \(z>0\), \(1-z>0\), and
\[
1-z+\theta z>0,
\]
the denominator in \eqref{eq:diagonal-quotient-denominator-main} is nonzero
and \(D(z)<0\).  Also \(r_0(z)>0\).  Therefore the rational formula defining
\(\Phi\) has no pole on a small neighbourhood of \(\Rinc(z)\), and the derivative
displayed in \eqref{eq:lambda-gradient-main}--\eqref{eq:phi-jacobian-main} is
finite and locally bounded there.

Theorem~C,
\Cref{thm:finite-record-inference-audit-main}, gives the displayed inclusion
CLT for the complete-cycle estimator \(\Rinch_N=\Rinch_N^{\mathrm{cyc}}\)
on the retained reporting branch.  The ordinary \(C^1\) delta method applied
to \(\Phi\), equivalently the diagonal specialization of the pre-diagonal
quotient rule \Cref{prop:finite-record-pre-diagonal-quotient-rule-main}, gives
\eqref{eq:diagonal-raw-quotient-clt-main}.  The population quotient-inverse
identity gives \(\Phi(\Rinc(z))=(p+s,ps)=(2z,z^2)=\sigma_z\).

It remains to check that the sandwich covariance consistency is inherited from
the inclusion-coordinate covariance estimator.  The CLT implies
\(\Rinch_N-\Rinc(z)=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/2})\), hence
\(D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\to D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\) in probability; local boundedness gives
\[
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}=O_{\PP}(1),\qquad
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)-D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\|_{\operatorname{op}}=o_{\PP}(1).
\]
The regenerative covariance \(\Sigma_r(z)\) is a fixed finite matrix.  Using
the triangle inequality and submultiplicativity,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left\|
D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}D\Phi(\Rinch_N)^\top
-D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\Sigma_r(z)D\Phi(\Rinc(z))^\top
\right\|_{\operatorname{op}}\\
&\le
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}^2
\|\widehat\Sigma_{r,N}^{\rm tot}-\Sigma_r(z)\|_{\operatorname{op}}\\
&\quad+
\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)-D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\|_{\operatorname{op}}\,
\|\Sigma_r(z)\|_{\operatorname{op}}\,
\{\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)\|_{\operatorname{op}}
+\|D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\|_{\operatorname{op}}\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first term is \(o_{\PP}(t_N)\) by the assumed inclusion covariance rate.
For the second term, the concrete retained-rank choices in
\Cref{ass:finite-record-master-main} use \(t_N=N^{-1/4}\), and the displayed
hypothesis \(N^{-1/2}=o(t_N)\) is the only rate fact needed here.  The CLT gives
\(\|D\Phi(\Rinch_N)-D\Phi(\Rinc(z))\|_{\operatorname{op}}=O_{\PP}(N^{-1/2})\)
because \(D\Phi\) is \(C^1\) on the retained neighbourhood.  Thus the second
term is \(o_{\PP}(t_N)\), proving
\eqref{eq:diagonal-sandwich-consistency-main}.  With
\Cref{thm:finite-record-covariance-main} and
\(b_N=\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor\), the inclusion covariance rate is
\(O_{\PP}(N^{-1/3})\), so the displayed \(O_{\PP}(N^{-1/3})=o_{\PP}(N^{-1/4})\)
specialization follows.  The finite covariance formula is exactly
\eqref{eq:dmap-diagonal-sigma-finite-main} on \(\mathcal E_N\).  On
\(\mathcal E_N^c\) the auxiliary zero completion is used only in the preceding
probability bound, while the operational protocol reports no covariance matrix.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Local-projection expansion used in Theorem~E]\label{lem:local-projection-expansion-before-theorem-e-main}
For fixed \(z\in(0,1)\) and every \(M<\infty\), there is a constant
\(C_M<\infty\), depending only on \(z\) and \(M\), such that
\[
\sup_{\|h\|\le M}
\left\|
\sqrt N\{\Pi_F^{\rm tb}(\sigma_z+h/\sqrt N)-\sigma_z\}
-\Pi_{H_z}h
\right\|
\le C_MN^{-1/2},
\]
where \(\sigma_z=(2z,z^2)\), \(H_z=\{h:h_2\le zh_1\}\), and
\(F=\{(x+y,xy):0\le x,y\le1\}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
This is the uniform expansion
\eqref{eq:explicit-local-projection-expansion-main}.  The argument is local:
near \(\sigma_z\), \(F\) is the hypograph
\[
F=\{(\sigma_1,\sigma_2):\sigma_2\le \sigma_1^2/4\}.
\]
For \(\sigma=\sigma_z+h/\sqrt N\), feasibility is
\[
h_2\le zh_1+\frac{h_1^2}{4\sqrt N}.
\]
If this holds, the local projection is \(\sigma\), and the only discrepancy
from \(\Pi_{H_z}h\) occurs in the \(O(N^{-1/2})\) boundary strip where
\(0<h_2-zh_1\le h_1^2/(4\sqrt N)\).  If feasibility fails, write the projected
boundary point as
\[
s_1=2z+\frac{k_1}{\sqrt N},\qquad
s_2=z^2+\frac{zk_1}{\sqrt N}+\frac{k_1^2}{4N}.
\]
The normal equation to the parabola gives, uniformly for \(\|h\|\le M\),
\[
k
=h-\frac{h_2-zh_1}{1+z^2}(-z,1)+O_M(N^{-1/2}).
\]
Taking the positive part across the feasible and infeasible split gives
\[
k=\Pi_{H_z}h+O_M(N^{-1/2}),
\]
which is the displayed bound.  \Cref{lem:exchange-diagonal-local-reach-before-main}
also verifies that the local projection is the tie-broken projection onto the
full compact set \(F\) in a sufficiently small neighbourhood of \(\sigma_z\).
\end{proof}

The full-rank certificate makes \(\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)\) invertible.  Put
\[
T:=X^\top\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)^{-1}X,
\]
and let \(c_{1-\alpha}\) be the lower \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile of \(T\).  With
\[
E_N:=\mathcal E_N
\]
the retained-record gate, for each fixed \(0<\alpha<1\),
\[
\PP_{\sigma_0}\{E_N\cap[
X_N^\top\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)^{-1}X_N\le c_{1-\alpha}]\}
\longrightarrow 1-\alpha .
\]
If, in addition, a retained covariance estimator satisfies
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}-\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=o_{\PP}(t_N),
\]
with retained-rank pseudoinverse
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{+}
=\sum_{\widehat\lambda_{i,N}>t_N}
\widehat\lambda_{i,N}^{-1}
\widehat v_{i,N}\widehat v_{i,N}^{\top},
\]
and, under the auxiliary Gaussian conditional law given the retained record,
\[
Z_N^\ast\sim\mathcal N(0,\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}),\qquad
T_N^\ast=
(\Pi_{H_{z_0}}Z_N^\ast)^\top
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{+}(\Pi_{H_{z_0}}Z_N^\ast),
\]
then the conditional law of \(T_N^\ast\) converges in probability to the law
of \(T\).  This auxiliary convergence is a limit statement for the ideal
projected-Gaussian calibration law; it is not a realized finite comparison.
This is the only diagonal inference theorem in the main text.  Its confidence
language is conditional on \Cref{ass:model-inference-contract-main}, on the
fixed candidate \(z_0=1/2\), and on the pre-specified reporting gate \(E_N\).
It is an ideal fixed-null asymptotic statement, not a simultaneous confidence
procedure over \(0<z<1\), not valid after data-dependent diagonal selection,
and not an executed finite-record accept/reject report.  The printed
\(z_0=1/2\) branch certificate supplies the population covariance branch
needed by the theorem; it is not a diagnostic pass, a model certificate, or a
critical-value transcript for a realized record.  The theorem does not supply
record-specific intervals \([Q_N]\) or \([L,U]\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The raw quotient CLT in the theorem statement and the local projection

a moving, selected, or simultaneous family of diagonal points.

The printed branch certificate gives full rank, so

\[
\PP_{\sigma_0}\{
X_N^\top\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)^{-1}X_N\le c_{1-\alpha}\}\to1-\alpha .

\Cref{lem:finite-record-reporting-gate-main}.  Intersecting the preceding

For the optional auxiliary calibration-law convergence, Weyl's inequality and
\[
\|\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}-\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)\|_{\operatorname{op}}
=o_{\PP}(t_N),
\]

probability tending to one.  The printed branch certificate gives full rank,
so on that event
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}^{+}\to \Sigma_\sigma(z_0)^{-1}
\qquad\text{in probability}.
\]
The conditional projected-Gaussian lemma
\Cref{lem:projected-gaussian-bootstrap-halfspace-main}, applied with the
fixed half-space \(H_{z_0}\), \(\widehat\Sigma_N=\widehat\Sigma_{\sigma,N}\),
and \(\Sigma=\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)\), gives weak convergence in probability of
the conditional law of \(T_N^\ast\) to the law of \(T\).  Since the certificate
establishes \(\operatorname{rank}\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)=2\),
\Cref{thm:projected-gaussian-continuity-degeneracy-main} gives continuity of
the limiting distribution at its lower quantiles.  This proves the stated
auxiliary convergence and deliberately stops before any record-specific

\end{proof}
