\emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability}

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, \emph{``Stationary double-pole inference for
sampled generalized-Erlang renewal records''}, as an Original Paper in
\emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability}.

The manuscript determines the stationary-record limit experiment when two
rates collide in a sampled generalized-Erlang, equivalently serial discrete
phase-type, renewal process.  For fixed serial order and one isolated double
collision, it proves uniform local asymptotic normality in the squared
separation coordinate, the efficient one-sided testing envelope, and matching
upper and lower \(N^{-1/4}\) minimax recovery of the unordered rate multiset.
The exact likelihood retains the equilibrium endpoint cycles and random
renewal stopping.

The model-specific nondegeneracy step separates pole orders after sampling:
the collision derivative has a nonzero cubic polynomial factor at the repeated
exponential base, the centre derivative has lower degree, and each separated
rate has its own distinguishing base.  A recurrence estimator from the first
\(2n\) empirical complete-gap tails supplies the matching upper bound.  The
paper first proves the complete stationary experiment for the present
two-state sampled counter and then proves the fixed-order serial theorem.

The two-state Palm law, weak-equivalence cone intersection, and fixed-setting
record tools remain as complements.  The serial population inverse is
expressly a finite-coordinate specialization of He--Zhang
generalized-Erlang/Coxian theory.  The three-inclusion quotient inverse remains
a candidate original result pending complete reduction against canonical
DMAP2 coordinates, not an established priority claim.  The supplement
identifies Bickel--Kwon's tangent and additive projection as antecedents and
keeps its deterministic-product split calculation distinct from the new
stationary-record theorem.

We selected \emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability} because
the article's organizing contribution is methodological probability for
constrained D-MAP and phase-type models, supported by exact algebraic and
computational certificates.  Its comparison section relates each formula to
Ram\'irez-Cobo--Lillo weak equivalence, M\'esz\'aros--Telek canonical DMAP2
coordinates, He--Zhang generalized-Erlang/Coxian theory, and standard
minimal-realization results.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration
elsewhere.  All authors have approved the submission.  The authors declare no
competing interests.

Thank you for your consideration.
