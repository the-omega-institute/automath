Editor-in-Chief\\
\emph{Stochastic Processes and their Applications}

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, \emph{``Stationary double-pole inference for
sampled generalized-Erlang renewal records''}, as an Original Paper in
\emph{Stochastic Processes and their Applications}.

The paper first proves two-sided local asymptotic equivalence in Le Cam
distance between a stationary binary lattice-renewal indicator on a
deterministic window and an undershot fixed-size sample of i.i.d. Palm
interarrivals.  The proof gives explicit randomizations in both directions,
uniformly removes endpoint censoring and random cycle count, and transfers
bounded-loss risks and local asymptotic minimax conclusions.  The exact
stationary likelihood is stated on records containing at least one renewal,
with the equilibrium endpoint cycles retained and the all-zero atom handled
separately.

The principal application determines the stationary-record limit experiment
when two rates collide in a sampled generalized-Erlang, equivalently serial
discrete phase-type, renewal process.  For fixed serial order and one isolated
double collision, the identifiable collision coordinate is the squared
half-separation.  We prove compact-uniform LAN, a residualized efficient
one-sided score test, and matching upper and lower \(N^{-1/4}\) recovery of
the unordered two-rate multiset.

The model-specific nondegeneracy step separates pole orders after sampling:
the collision derivative has a nonzero cubic polynomial factor at the repeated
exponential base, the centre derivative has lower degree, and each separated
rate has its own distinguishing base.  A recurrence estimator from the first
\(2n\) empirical complete-gap tails supplies the matching upper bound.  The
paper first determines the complete stationary-record local limit experiment
for the present two-state sampled counter, including random renewal stopping
and both endpoint cycles, and then proves the fixed-order serial theorem.

Zhao--Nagaraja give the closest information-level predecessor.  Their
continuous-time window-censored renewal analysis gives the endpoint and
complete-gap likelihood and the limiting Fisher-information rate.  We cite
that result explicitly and distinguish it from the present two-sided
deficiency theorem, its parameter-free local randomizations, and its
decision-theoretic and semiparametric transport consequences.

The two-state Palm law, weak-equivalence cone intersection, and fixed-setting
record tools remain as complements.  The serial population inverse is
expressly a finite-coordinate specialization of He--Zhang
generalized-Erlang/Coxian theory.  The three-inclusion quotient inverse remains
a candidate original result pending complete reduction against canonical
DMAP2 coordinates, not an established priority claim.  The supplement
identifies Bickel--Kwon's tangent and additive projection as antecedents and
keeps its deterministic-product split calculation distinct from the new
stationary-record theorem.

We selected \emph{Stochastic Processes and their Applications} because the
manuscript treats renewal observation, stationary censoring, D-MAP
realization, phase-type structure, and singular inference as one stochastic-
process problem.  We have retained that full architecture.  We do not claim
that the article has been recast as a statistics-only paper around equivalence,
LAN, testing, and minimax recovery; the representation and diagnostic package
remains part of the main article.  The comparison section relates the retained
structural formulas to Ram\'irez-Cobo--Lillo weak equivalence,
M\'esz\'aros--Telek canonical DMAP2 coordinates, He--Zhang generalized-
Erlang/Coxian theory, and standard minimal-realization results.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration
elsewhere.  All authors have approved the submission.  The authors declare no
competing interests.

Thank you for your consideration.
