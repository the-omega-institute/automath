\emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability}

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, \emph{``Renewal laws, quotient inversion, and
Markovian fibres for a killed-reset D-MAP''}, as an Original Paper in
\emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability}.

The manuscript asks what the stationary binary output of a constrained
discrete-time Markovian arrival process determines when each marked transition
resets the hidden chain to a fixed state.  This places the problem directly at
the intersection of D-MAP weak equivalence, renewal theory, phase-type
representation, and inference for dependent event records.  A sampled counter
provides one concrete realization of the kernel, but the analysis and the
claims concern the applied-probability model rather than a separate physical
system.

The main results derive the Palm gap and complete finite-word laws, give an
explicit intersection of a two-state weak-equivalence class with the
killed-reset Markov cone, and characterize the resulting closed fibre arc.
For higher-state kernels, the paper isolates the minimal interior stratum on
which the positivity-preserving similarity orbit has dimension \((n-1)^2\).
The serial phase-type statement is expressly a finite-coordinate sampled-tail
specialization of He--Zhang generalized-Erlang and Coxian theory, including
repeated poles.  The three-inclusion quotient inverse is presented as a
candidate original result pending complete reduction against canonical DMAP2
coordinates, not as an established priority claim.

The statistical results retained in the article are tied directly to the
finite-dimensional visible law: a fixed-setting inclusion central limit
theorem, a complete-visible-law specification test, a root-free physical-image
test, and a quotient delta method.  Sorted-root and log-rate limits are stated
as consequences of that delta method.  The abstract known-marginal Markov
tangent calculation, moving-dimensional Gaussian coupling, exact
complete-Palm-gap split experiment, and pointwise projection example are
separated into a supplement.  In particular, the supplement identifies
Bickel--Kwon's tangent and additive projection as antecedents and states only
the additional killed-reset restrictions proved here.

We selected \emph{Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability} because
the article's organizing contribution is methodological probability for
constrained D-MAP and phase-type models, supported by exact algebraic and
computational certificates.  Its comparison section relates each formula to
Ram\'irez-Cobo--Lillo weak equivalence, M\'esz\'aros--Telek canonical DMAP2
coordinates, He--Zhang generalized-Erlang/Coxian theory, and standard
minimal-realization results.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration
elsewhere.  All authors have approved the submission.  The authors declare no
competing interests.

Thank you for your consideration.
