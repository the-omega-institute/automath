Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, \emph{``Classical sampled-counter records: renewal principal-minor encoding, finite-record diagnostics, and conditional response calibration''}, for publication as a Research Article in \emph{Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical}.

The paper shows that the full stationary binary record of a stipulated sampled classical counter contains record-level thresholds that rate thermometry alone would miss. Its attempt intensity is a classical parameter until a separate conditional closure identifies it with a supplied stationary Unruh--DeWitt excitation-rate profile. Dead time and finite recovery are intrinsic features of counting detectors, and exploiting their full stochastic output goes beyond summarizing the detector by its mean transition rate. The mathematical core of the work---symbolic dynamics, renewal-process theory, spectral analysis, exact finite-dependence criteria, and fixed-record asymptotics---is presented as a self-contained theorem chain suited to \emph{Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical}.

The present manuscript is a self-contained treatment of the sampled-record law and its narrow fixed-response calibration interface. Geometry-facing quotient, near-horizon, fixed-radius inverse, and Schwarzschild calculations are retained only as appendix-level conditional corollaries or supplementary bookkeeping, not as the main theorem narrative.

The principal results are the following.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In the ideal dead-time model, the sampled record is a Markov chain on the golden-mean shift, and the Parry benchmark is realized at a unique detector threshold.
\item A general stationary-renewal lemma gives the renewal principal-minor encoding from the renewal masses \(u_n=\PP(A_n=1\mid A_0=1)\). In the sampled one-recovery-opportunity boundary-reset model, this specializes to a closed Toeplitz finite-principal-minor representative with \(u_n=\rho+(a-\rho)\lambda_{\mathrm{hid}}^{n-1}\). These matrices are finite principal-minor representatives supplied by the already constructed renewal law; finite admissibility comes from those renewal probabilities and inclusion--exclusion, not from separate nonsymmetric-kernel conditions.
\item This renewal principal-minor encoding and its boundary-reset specialization yield the complete finite-dimensional law: finite-window count generating functions are finite determinants of the Toeplitz representative, and arbitrary prescribed one/zero words are recovered by finite inclusion-exclusion.
\item At the memory-transition threshold $\Gamma=\Gamma_c$, the Toeplitz transfer parameter collapses to zero. The critical record is simultaneously full-support renewal, exact $1$-dependent, represented by the collapsed Toeplitz finite-principal-minor matrix and an equivalent one-dependent Hessenberg representative, strictly non-finite-order Markov, and neither Bernoulli nor Parry.
\item Away from the critical package, the record theorem chain proves soft approach to the ideal grammar, suppressed adjacent-click conditional probability for the sampled renewal record, strictly increasing spectral density on $[0,\pi]$, a sub-Poisson asymptotic Fano factor, and fixed-record diagnostics for the covariance selector.
\item After the record thresholds and finite-dimensional laws are proved, a fixed calibrated stationary response profile turns the two record-selected rates into response loci subject to independent range and regularity checks; KMS detailed balance fixes only the one-gap thermal ratio and does not determine those loci. The homogeneous, near-horizon, fixed-radius inverse, and Schwarzschild consequences are retained only as appendix-level conditional corollaries after their extra hypotheses are stated.
\end{enumerate}

The main article contains the complete sampled-record theorem proofs and the
fixed-response locus theorem. The Supplementary Material records auxiliary
calculations, finite-counting checks, coordinate expansions, numerical
bookkeeping, and a deterministic reproduction recipe for solving
$\Gamma_c(\kappa_{\mathrm r},\Delta\tau)$ and regenerating the figures and
worked table directly from the displayed formulas.

The work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. We have no competing interests to declare.

Thank you for your consideration.
