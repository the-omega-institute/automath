\maketitle

This supplement contains secondary inference layers supporting the article:
the stopped separated-batch covariance argument, the sampled-counter section
of the known-marginal Markov interaction tangent, the moving-dimensional
Helmert coupling boundary, the deterministic-product symmetric split
experiment that precedes the stationary Theorems~F--G, and a pointwise
projection calculation.  Article labels are imported with
\texttt{xr-hyper}.  Computational checks are regression and formula sanity
checks only; none is used in place of an asymptotic remainder bound.

\section{Reproducibility scope}

The algebraic regression scripts are
\path{artifacts/verify_A8_results.py} and
\path{artifacts/verify_nstate_identifiability.py}; their unit-test
wrappers are the correspondingly named \path{test_*.py} files.  The
fixed-point interval certificate is replayed by
\path{certificates/diagonal_branch_certificate.py} with option
\texttt{--test}.  These checks
cover finite algebra and interval arithmetic.  They do not establish the
uniform limits in the preceding sections.
