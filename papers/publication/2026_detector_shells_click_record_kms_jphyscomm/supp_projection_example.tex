\begin{proposition}[Pointwise exchange-diagonal projection example]\label{thm:e-fixed-candidate-exchange-diagonal-main}
Fix
\[
z_0=\frac12,\qquad \sigma_0=(1,1/4),\qquad
H_{z_0}=\{h\in\mathbb R^2:h_2\le z_0h_1\},
\]
and let \(F=\{(\sigma_1,\sigma_2):\sigma_2\le\sigma_1^2/4\}\).  Suppose the
true population satisfies \(\sigma=\sigma_0\), the printed interval
certificate proves
\(\lambda_{\min}\{\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)\}>0.037\), and
\[
\sqrt N(\widehat\sigma_N^{\rm raw}-\sigma_0)
\Rightarrow Z\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_\sigma(z_0)).
\]
Let \(\Pi_F\) be the locally unique Euclidean projection, with any fixed
measurable tie rule outside the local neighbourhood, and set
\[
\widehat\sigma_N=\Pi_F(\widehat\sigma_N^{\rm raw}),\qquad
X_N=\sqrt N(\widehat\sigma_N-\sigma_0).
\]
Then
\[
X_N\Rightarrow \Pi_{H_{z_0}}Z .
\]
This proposition is only a pointwise projection example at the preselected point
\(z_0=1/2\).  It defines no statistic for testing, no simulated or limiting
critical value, no confidence region, and no feasible or oracle coverage
claim.  Selection of \(z_0\) from the data and uniformity over \(0<z<1\) are
outside its scope.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Near \(\sigma_0\), the boundary of \(F\) is the smooth graph
\(\sigma_2=\sigma_1^2/4\), whose tangent cone at \(\sigma_0\) is
\(H_{z_0}\).  The standard first-order metric-projection expansion at a smooth
inequality boundary is
\[
\sqrt N\{\Pi_F(\sigma_0+N^{-1/2}h_N)-\sigma_0\}
=\Pi_{H_{z_0}}h_N+o(1)
\]
for every bounded convergent sequence \(h_N\)
\cite{Shapiro1987MetricProjection}.  Tightness of the raw quotient CLT permits
the expansion on events with probability tending to one.  Slutsky's theorem
and continuity of projection onto the closed half-space give the asserted
limit.  The certificate establishes only that the covariance in this limit is
nonsingular; it is not used to calibrate a procedure.
\end{proof}
