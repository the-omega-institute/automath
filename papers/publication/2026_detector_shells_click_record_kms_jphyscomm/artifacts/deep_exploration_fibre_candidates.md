## Selected result

The first candidate maximizes reach times value and directly completes the
paper's physical quotient story. For
\(K=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}p&0\\b&s\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), every
reset-preserving similarity is generated by
\(M_q=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}q&1-q\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
Writing \(q_*=\Gamma/\kappa_{\mathrm r}\), the transformed upper-right entry
is \(b(1-q)(q-q_*)/q\). The lower-left entry forces \(q>0\), so Markov
nonnegativity is equivalent to \(q\) lying between \(1\) and \(q_*\). The two
endpoints are exactly the original and rate-swapped physical kernels; strict
interior points are positive nontriangular kernels. At equal rates \(q_*=1\),
the full two-state fibre collapses to one point.
