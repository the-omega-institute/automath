\section{Optional External Scalar Calibration}\label{sec:kms-shells}

\begin{corollary}[Inverse images of the two record-selected constants]
\label{cor:conditional-calibration-bridge-main}\label{thm:response-locus-main}\label{ass:udw-rate-calibration-main}
Let \(U\) be a \(C^1\) manifold and let
\(R_\Omega:U\to(0,\infty)\) be an externally supplied positive \(C^1\)
calibration.  If the D-MAP coordinate \(\Gamma\) is externally assigned by
\(\Gamma=R_\Omega(x)\), then the ideal Parry record condition and the
constrained-D-MAP exact-\(1\)-dependence condition correspond to the inverse images
\[
R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma_\varphi),
\qquad
R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma_{\mathrm{mem}}),
\]
where
\[
\gamma_\varphi=\frac{\log\varphi}{\Delta\tau},
\qquad
\gamma_{\mathrm{mem}}=
\Gamma_{\mathrm{crit}}(\kappa_{\mathrm r},\Delta\tau).
\]
For any \(\gamma>0\), \(R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma)\) is nonempty exactly when
\(\gamma\in R_\Omega(U)\); on any component where \(dR_\Omega\ne0\), it is a
\(C^1\) hypersurface.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Nonclaim.}\label{rem:external-calibration-only-main}
The function \(R_\Omega\) is an input, not an estimand of one D-MAP record
and not a theorem derived from KMS, Unruh, detector, thermodynamic, or
continuous-time measurement structure in this article.  The corollary only
composes the record-selected scalar constants with that input map.  It has no
reverse direction: membership in one of the displayed inverse images does not
derive the killed-leakage kernels, the constrained D-MAP convention, the
renewal law, the quotient inverse, or any finite-record CLT.  For the
constrained-D-MAP exact-\(1\)-dependence target, naming a separated branch still
requires the external branch-map convention of
\Cref{conv:branch-map-labelled-rates-main}; without that map the visible
record identifies only the unordered survival-factor pair, with the diagonal
common-rate exception described in \Cref{lem:diagonal-common-rate-main}.
