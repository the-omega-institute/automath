\section{Short Parry Comparator}\label{sec:kms-shells}

\begin{remark}[Parry comparator and external calibration only]
\label{def:thermality-notions-main}
\label{prop:ideal-main}
\label{cor:conditional-calibration-bridge-main}
\label{thm:response-locus-main}
\label{ass:udw-rate-calibration-main}
This appendix is only a compact comparator, not part of the D-MAP theorem
package.  For the ideal one-step blanking comparison law
\[
P_\beta=
\begin{pmatrix}
p_\beta&1-p_\beta\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad p_\beta=e^{-\Gamma\Delta\tau},
\]
the standard golden-mean Parry calculation gives
\[
p_\beta=\varphi^{-1}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Gamma\Delta\tau=\log\varphi
\]
as the maximum-entropy benchmark on the golden-mean shift
\cite{Parry1964,LindMarcus1995}.  This comparison law is not the
sampled-boundary-reset D-MAP law used in Theorems~A--E.  If an external
positive \(C^1\) calibration \(R_\Omega:U\to(0,\infty)\) is supplied and a
rate is assigned by \(\Gamma=R_\Omega(x)\), the two scalar targets mentioned
in the paper correspond only to the inverse images
\[
R_\Omega^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\log\varphi}{\Delta\tau}\right),
\qquad
R_\Omega^{-1}\!\left(\Gamma_{\mathrm{crit}}
(\kappa_{\mathrm r},\Delta\tau)\right),
\]
with the usual regular-level-set interpretation where \(dR_\Omega\ne0\).
This is a response-side composition of externally supplied data with
record-selected constants.  It gives no inference from one visible D-MAP
record to \(R_\Omega\), \(\Gamma_+\), KMS temperature, lapse, coupling,
spectral weight, thermodynamic parameters, or ordered labelled rates.
\end{remark}
