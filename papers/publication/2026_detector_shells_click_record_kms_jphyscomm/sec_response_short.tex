\section{Optional Benchmark and External Scalar Calibration}\label{sec:kms-shells}

\begin{remark}[Thermality criteria and the record comparator]\label{def:thermality-notions-main}
This appendix separates comparison terminology from the D-MAP record theorem.
Detailed-balance thermality is the rate-ratio condition
\(\Gamma_+/\Gamma_-=e^{-\beta\Omega}\), while Gibbs thermalization is
convergence of a reduced detector state to \(e^{-\beta H_{\mathrm{det}}}/Z\).
Neither notion is used to derive the labelled kernels in
\Cref{ass:constrained-dmap-record-main}; those kernels have already been
derived from the classical sampled-counter protocol in
\Cref{lem:classical-counter-kernel-construction-main}.  The appendix therefore
has only two roles: it records the symbolic-dynamics Parry comparator, and it
states how an externally supplied scalar response profile may be composed with
the record-selected rates.  The Parry measure is the maximum-entropy law on
the golden-mean shift under \(11\notin\operatorname{supp}\mu\)
\cite{Parry1964,LindMarcus1995}.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Standard Parry comparator for the stipulated ideal blanking law]\label{prop:ideal-main}
For the ideal one-step blanking comparison law with transition matrix
\[
P_\beta=
\begin{pmatrix}
p_\beta&1-p_\beta\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad p_\beta=e^{-\Gamma\Delta\tau},
\]
the binary output is the Parry law on the golden-mean shift iff
\begin{equation}\label{eq:parry-threshold-main}
p_\beta=\varphi^{-1}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Gamma\Delta\tau=\log\varphi .
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The standard golden-mean Parry calculation gives
\(P^{\mathrm P}(0,0)=\varphi^{-1}\) and \(P^{\mathrm P}(1,0)=1\)
\cite{Parry1964,LindMarcus1995}.  The stipulated blanking law has the
same support and the same forced transition from \(1\), so equality is
equivalent to \(p_\beta=\varphi^{-1}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Inverse images of the two record-selected constants]
\label{cor:conditional-calibration-bridge-main}\label{thm:response-locus-main}\label{ass:udw-rate-calibration-main}
Let \(U\) be a \(C^1\) manifold and let
\(R_\Omega:U\to(0,\infty)\) be an externally supplied positive \(C^1\)
calibration.  If the D-MAP coordinate \(\Gamma\) is externally assigned by
\(\Gamma=R_\Omega(x)\), then the ideal Parry record condition and the
constrained-D-MAP exact-\(1\)-dependence condition correspond to the inverse images
\[
R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma_\varphi),
\qquad
R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma_{\mathrm{mem}}),
\]
where
\[
\gamma_\varphi=\frac{\log\varphi}{\Delta\tau},
\qquad
\gamma_{\mathrm{mem}}=
\Gamma_{\mathrm{crit}}(\kappa_{\mathrm r},\Delta\tau).
\]
For any \(\gamma>0\), \(R_\Omega^{-1}(\gamma)\) is nonempty exactly when
\(\gamma\in R_\Omega(U)\); on any component where \(dR_\Omega\ne0\), it is a
\(C^1\) hypersurface.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{External-calibration scope.}\label{rem:external-calibration-only-main}
The function \(R_\Omega\) is an external input, not an estimand of one D-MAP
record.  This appendix is therefore only the response-side composition allowed
by the model contract in
\hyperref[par:model-contract-main]{the model-contract paragraph}: it maps the
two record-selected scalar constants through a supplied \(R_\Omega\).  It has
no reverse direction from a response level set back to the sampled-counter
derivation of \(T_0,T_1\), the renewal law, the quotient inverse, or the
finite-record CLT.  Labelled-rate wording in the exact-\(1\)-dependence target
follows the inference scope in
\hyperref[par:inference-scope-main]{the inference-scope paragraph}.  With
known \(\Delta\tau\), one visible stationary record can identify only the
unordered rate set \(\{\Gamma,\kappa_{\mathrm r}\}\) inside the sampled-counter
model; it cannot identify \(R_\Omega\), \(\Gamma_+\), KMS temperature, lapse,
coupling, or spectral weight.
