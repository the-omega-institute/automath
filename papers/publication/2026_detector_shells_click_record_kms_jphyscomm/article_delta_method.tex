\begin{paperthm}[Fixed-setting retained-record quotient delta method]\label{thm:finite-record-quotient-delta-main}
Assume the hypotheses of Theorem~C and suppose the population inverse lies in
the stable quotient chart
\[
r_0>0,\qquad \Delta_{\rm inv}=r_1-r_0^2\ne0 .
\]
For the quotient map \(\Phi\) in Theorem~B,
\[
\sqrt N\{\Phi(\Rinch_N^{\rm cyc})-\Phi(\Rinc)\}
\Rightarrow
\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_\sigma),
\qquad
\Sigma_\sigma=D\Phi(\Rinc)\Sigma_rD\Phi(\Rinc)^\top .
\]
For the constrained D-MAP population, \(\Phi(\Rinc)=(p+s,ps)\).  If
\[
\Delta_\sigma=\Phi_1(\Rinc)^2-4\Phi_2(\Rinc)=(p-s)^2>0,
\]
let \(z_-<z_+\) be the sorted roots and let
\(\widehat z_{-,N}<\widehat z_{+,N}\) be the correspondingly sorted roots
formed from \(\Phi(\Rinch_N^{\rm cyc})\).  This ordering is visible and
requires no external physical label.  With
\[
J_{\rm root}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{z_-}{z_--z_+}&-\dfrac1{z_--z_+}\\[6pt]
\dfrac{z_+}{z_+-z_-}&-\dfrac1{z_+-z_-}
\end{pmatrix},
\]
the label-free root CLT is
\[
\sqrt N
\begin{pmatrix}\widehat z_{-,N}-z_-\\ \widehat z_{+,N}-z_+\end{pmatrix}
\Rightarrow
\mathcal N\!\left(0,J_{\rm root}\Sigma_\sigma J_{\rm root}^{\top}\right).
\]
For known design interval \(\Delta\tau\), define the root-ordered rates
\[
\vartheta=
\begin{pmatrix}-\log z_-/\Delta\tau\\-\log z_+/\Delta\tau\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\widehat\vartheta_N=
\begin{pmatrix}-\log\widehat z_{-,N}/\Delta\tau\\
-\log\widehat z_{+,N}/\Delta\tau\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Then
\[
\sqrt N(\widehat\vartheta_N-\vartheta)
\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,J_{\rm rate}\Sigma_\sigma J_{\rm rate}^{\top}),
\qquad
J_{\rm rate}=
\begin{pmatrix}-(\Delta\tau z_-)^{-1}&0\\
0&-(\Delta\tau z_+)^{-1}\end{pmatrix}J_{\rm root}.
\]
Thus the roots identify the unordered survival-factor set \(\{p,s\}\) and
the displayed root ordering gives a canonical, label-free version of the
unordered numerical rate set
\[
\left\{-\frac{\log p}{\Delta\tau},-\frac{\log s}{\Delta\tau}\right\}.
\]
If \(\Delta\tau\) is not supplied as a known design interval, the theorem
identifies only the dimensionless unordered product set
\[
\{-\log p,-\log s\}
=\{\Gamma\Delta\tau,\kappa_{\mathrm r}\Delta\tau\},
\]
not numerical rates per unit time.
Only the assignment of the two numerical entries to the physical names
\(\Gamma\) and \(\kappa_{\mathrm r}\) requires the side-data branch
convention.
\end{paperthm}
