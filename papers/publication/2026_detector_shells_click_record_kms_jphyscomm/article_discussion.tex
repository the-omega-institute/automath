\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

The deterministic marked reset turns the visible output of the constrained
D-MAP into a renewal process.  This is the source of both the explicit record
law and the low-dimensional quotient inverse.  It is also the source of the
model's fragility: a marked transition into the ready state removes the fixed
Palm restart and invalidates the renewal product used by the inverse and the
retained-cycle calculations.

The visible law determines the unordered survival pair, not the hidden labels.
The two-state fibre result makes that distinction concrete.  Away from the
exchange diagonal, weakly equivalent stochastic realizations in the
killed-reset cone form a closed arc with the two triangular rate orderings as
endpoints.  On the diagonal the arc collapses.  In higher state dimension the
\((n-1)^2\) orbit calculation applies to the minimal interior stratum and is
not a statement about the global dimension of every visible fibre.

The lettered inferential statements are deliberately downstream of the
population algebra.  Theorem~C estimates the three inclusion coordinates;
Theorem~D checks the complete visible law under its stated null and alternative
classes; Theorem~E transports Theorem~C through the stable quotient chart of
Theorem~B.  Sorted roots and log-rates are ordinary delta-method consequences.
The physical-image test is a separate finite-dimensional diagnostic.  The
abstract semiparametric, moving-dimensional coupling, complete-Palm-gap split,
and projection calculations are supplementary.

\subsection{Sampled-counter interpretation}

Continuous dead-time and exponential-recovery detector models motivate the
two-state parametrization
\cite{YuFessler2000,SarbaziSafariHaas2018LongDeadTime,
KrauseWalenta2025SPADRecovery}.  They do not provide the theorem object here:
continuous within-bin recovery after a click can give a marked transition into
the ready state, whereas the present D-MAP latches every click in the dead
state until the boundary.  The sampled counter is therefore an interpretation
of the constrained kernel, not an independently analysed physical system.
