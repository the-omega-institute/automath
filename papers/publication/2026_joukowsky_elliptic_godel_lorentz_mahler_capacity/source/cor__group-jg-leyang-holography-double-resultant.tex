\begin{corollary}[Holographic identity under the Lee--Yang unit-circle hypothesis]\label{cor:group-jg-leyang-holographic-identity}
In the setting of Theorem \ref{thm:group-jg-pullback-factorization}, assume that \(K\subseteq\CC\), that every zero \(z_j\) of \(P\) satisfies \(|z_j|=1\), and that \(r\in\RR_{>0}\). Then for every \(z\in\CC^\times\),
\[
Q_r\!\bigl(J_r(z)\bigr)=a_n^{-1}r^n\,P(z)\,P\!\bigl(r^{-2}z^{-1}\bigr).
\]
Although the algebraic identity itself does not use the unit-circle hypothesis, we retain that assumption here because it is needed in the geometric corollaries below.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:group-jg-pullback-factorization},
\[
r^n z^n\,Q_r\!\bigl(J_r(z)\bigr)=P(z)\,P^{\vee}(r^2 z).
\]
Since \(P^{\vee}(u)=a_n^{-1}u^nP(1/u)\), substituting \(u=r^2 z\) gives
\[
P^{\vee}(r^2 z)=a_n^{-1}r^{2n}z^n P(r^{-2}z^{-1}).
\]
Cancelling the common factor \(z^n\) yields the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Double-resultant elimination and concentric spectral shells]\label{cor:group-jg-leyang-double-resultant}
Retain the hypotheses of Corollary \ref{cor:group-jg-leyang-holographic-identity}, and define
\[
S_r(z):=\operatorname{Res}_w\!\bigl(Q_r(w),\,r z^2-w z+r^{-1}\bigr)\in K(r)[z].
\]
Then
\[
S_r(z)=(-1)^n a_n^{-1} r^{n}\,P(z)\,\Bigl[z^{n}P\!\bigl(r^{-2}z^{-1}\bigr)\Bigr].
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathrm{Zero}(S_r)
=\{z_1,\dots,z_n\}\cup\{r^{-2}z_1^{-1},\dots,r^{-2}z_n^{-1}\},
\]
where the two clusters lie on the circles \(|z|=1\) and \(|z|=r^{-2}\), respectively.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Because \(r z^2-w z+r^{-1}\) is linear in \(w\), the linear specialization formula for the resultant gives
\[
\operatorname{Res}_w\!\bigl(Q_r(w),\,r z^2-w z+r^{-1}\bigr)
=(-z)^n\,Q_r\!\left(\frac{r z^2+r^{-1}}{z}\right)
=(-z)^n Q_r\!\bigl(J_r(z)\bigr).
\]
Substituting Corollary \ref{cor:group-jg-leyang-holographic-identity} yields
\[
S_r(z)=(-z)^n a_n^{-1}r^n P(z)P(r^{-2}z^{-1})
=(-1)^n a_n^{-1}r^{n}P(z)\,\Bigl[z^{n}P\!\bigl(r^{-2}z^{-1}\bigr)\Bigr],
\]
which is the required factorization. The description of the zero set follows immediately from the two factors.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Noiseless full-polynomial Lee--Yang reconstruction data]\label{def:group-jg-noiseless-leyang-reconstruction-data}
A noiseless one-scale Lee--Yang reconstruction datum is a pair \((r,Q)\) with the following exact information.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item The scale \(r\in\RR\) is known exactly and satisfies \(r>1\).
  \item The polynomial \(Q\in\CC[w]\) is given by its full coefficient list, not by samples, truncations, moments, or noisy approximations.
  \item The datum is certified to lie in the monic Lee--Yang transport class: there exists a monic polynomial \(P\in\CC[u]\), all of whose zeros lie on \(\TT\), such that \(Q=Q_r\) for \(P\) under Definition \ref{def:reciprocal-transport-normalization}.
\end{enumerate}
The degree of the hidden Lee--Yang polynomial is part of the recovered information, not an external input: it is
\[
n=\deg_w Q,
\]
because the leading coefficient of \(Q_r\) is \(a_0\ne0\).  Exact shell selection means the following algebraic operation.  Form
\[
S_{r,Q}(z):=\operatorname{Res}_w\!\bigl(Q(w),\,r z^2-w z+r^{-1}\bigr),
\]
factor it over its exact splitting field with multiplicities, and select the sub-divisor supported on \(|z|=1\).  Equivalently, after choosing any exact real \(\rho\) with \(r^{-2}<\rho<1\), select the degree-\(n\) sub-divisor supported in \(|z|>\rho\).  This definition excludes partial coefficient data, numerical thresholding, and stability assertions under perturbation.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Exact reconstruction interface and shell-factor selection]\label{prop:group-jg-exact-reconstruction-interface}
Fix a noiseless one-scale Lee--Yang reconstruction datum \((r,Q)\) in the sense of Definition \ref{def:group-jg-noiseless-leyang-reconstruction-data}, and let \(P\) be the certified monic Lee--Yang source polynomial.  Put \(n=\deg_w Q\) and define
\[
S_{r,Q}(z):=\operatorname{Res}_w\!\bigl(Q(w),\,r z^2-w z+r^{-1}\bigr).
\]
The reconstruction interface is the following exact algebraic procedure.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
  \item Recover \(n\) as \(\deg_w Q\).
  \item Compute the resultant \(S_{r,Q}\) as a polynomial in \(z\).  Equivalently, because the second input is linear in \(w\), compute \(S_{r,Q}(z)=(-z)^n Q(J_r(z))\) and expand.
  \item Factor \(S_{r,Q}\) over the exact splitting field, keeping multiplicities in its zero divisor.
  \item Select precisely the degree-\(n\) sub-divisor supported on \(|z|=1\), equivalently the degree-\(n\) sub-divisor in \(|z|>\rho\) for any exact \(\rho\) with \(r^{-2}<\rho<1\), and define \(P_{\mathrm{rec}}\) to be the unique monic polynomial whose zero divisor is that selected sub-divisor.
\end{enumerate}
Then \(P_{\mathrm{rec}}=P\).  In particular, repeated Lee--Yang zeros are reconstructed with their original multiplicities, and the normalization ambiguity is removed by the final monic normalization.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Since \(P\) is monic, the double-resultant identity of Corollary \ref{cor:group-jg-leyang-double-resultant} gives
\[
S_{r,Q}(z)=(-1)^n r^n P(z)\,\Bigl[z^nP\!\bigl(r^{-2}z^{-1}\bigr)\Bigr].
\]
Since \(Q=Q_r\) has leading coefficient \(a_0\ne0\), its degree is the degree \(n\) of \(P\), proving the degree-recovery step.  If \(\zeta\) is a zero of \(P\) of multiplicity \(m\), then \(|\zeta|=1\) and the first factor contributes exactly multiplicity \(m\) at \(z=\zeta\).  The corresponding zero of the second factor is \(r^{-2}\zeta^{-1}\), again with multiplicity \(m\), and it lies on the circle \(|z|=r^{-2}<1\).  Thus the two factors of \(S_{r,Q}\) have disjoint radial supports: one shell is \(|z|=1\), and the other is \(|z|=r^{-2}\).  Selection of the unit-circle sub-divisor is therefore well-defined and preserves all multiplicities, even when several zeros of \(P\) coincide.  The annular selection with \(r^{-2}<\rho<1\) is equivalent because the outer shell is precisely \(|z|=1\) and the inner shell is precisely \(|z|=r^{-2}\).

The monic polynomial with a prescribed finite zero divisor is unique, namely \(\prod_\zeta (z-\zeta)^{m_\zeta}\).  Applying this to the selected unit-circle divisor yields exactly the original monic polynomial \(P\).  The computation uses the exact value of \(r\), the full polynomial \(Q_r\), and exact factorization or equivalent exact zero-divisor data; no numerical thresholding or partial observation enters the argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact one-scale Lee--Yang inverse theorem]\label{thm:group-jg-leyang-single-scale-reconstruction}
Fix an exact real number \(r>1\).  Let \(\mathcal L\) be the class of all monic polynomials \(P\in\CC[z]\) of positive degree with every zero on \(\TT\), counted with multiplicity, and with nonzero constant term.  For \(P\in\mathcal L\), let \(Q_r\) be its transport polynomial from Definition \ref{def:reciprocal-transport-normalization}.  Then the one-scale transport map
\[
\mathcal T_r:\ P\longmapsto Q_r
\]
is injective on \(\mathcal L\).  More explicitly, the input consists exactly of the noiseless full-polynomial datum \((r,Q_r)\): the scale \(r\) is known exactly, every coefficient of \(Q_r\) is known exactly, and the datum is certified to lie in the monic Lee--Yang transport class.  The output is the unique source polynomial \(P\), recovered by the following finite algebraic procedure:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item set \(n=\deg_w Q_r\);
  \item form
  \[
  S_r(z)=\operatorname{Res}_w\!\bigl(Q_r(w),rz^2-wz+r^{-1}\bigr);
  \]
  \item take the degree-\(n\) zero sub-divisor of \(S_r\) supported on \(|z|=1\), equivalently supported in \(|z|>\rho\) for any exact \(\rho\) with \(r^{-2}<\rho<1\);
  \item return the monic polynomial with that zero divisor.
\end{enumerate}
This theorem asserts exact uniqueness and reconstruction only in the certified noiseless full-coefficient regime.  It excludes noisy observations, truncated coefficient lists, unknown scale, approximate shell separation, and stability under perturbation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each \(P\in\mathcal L\), the hypotheses of Corollary \ref{cor:group-jg-leyang-double-resultant} hold with \(K=\CC\), and monicity gives \(a_n=1\).  Hence
\[
S_r(z)=(-1)^n r^n\,P(z)\,\Bigl[z^nP\!\bigl(r^{-2}z^{-1}\bigr)\Bigr].
\]
When \(r>1\), every zero of the second factor lies on \(|z|=r^{-2}<1\), whereas the zeros of \(P\) lie on \(|z|=1\). The two spectral shells are therefore disjoint.  Also \(Q_r\) has degree \(n=\deg P\), because the leading coefficient in the transport polynomial is the nonzero constant term of the monic source polynomial.

The procedure listed in the theorem is exactly the procedure in Proposition \ref{prop:group-jg-exact-reconstruction-interface}; that proposition proves that the selected unit-circle zero divisor, with multiplicities, is the zero divisor of \(P\), and the monic polynomial with a prescribed zero divisor is unique.  Therefore the output of the procedure is the original source polynomial.  If \(\mathcal T_r(P_1)=\mathcal T_r(P_2)\), then the same input pair \((r,Q_r)\) gives the same resultant and hence the same selected unit-circle divisor; the reconstruction procedure returns both \(P_1\) and \(P_2\), so \(P_1=P_2\).  The final exclusions are precisely the hypotheses not used in the algebraic shell argument: the proof requires exact \(r\), the full polynomial \(Q_r\), certification of the Lee--Yang transport class, and exact factorization or equivalent exact zero-divisor data.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Annular shell projection certificate]\label{cor:group-jg-leyang-annular-shell-projection}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem \ref{thm:group-jg-leyang-single-scale-reconstruction}, let \(\rho\) be any real number satisfying
\[
r^{-2}<\rho<1.
\]
Then \(P\) is equivalently recovered as the unique monic degree-\(n\) factor of \(S_r\) whose zeros lie in the exterior annulus \(\{|z|>\rho\}\). The complementary monic factor has all its zeros in \(\{|z|<\rho\}\). Thus the one-scale reconstruction used here is an exact spectral-shell projection, not a partial-data or noisy inverse theorem.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Corollary \ref{cor:group-jg-leyang-double-resultant}, the zeros of \(S_r\) are the disjoint union
\[
\{z_1,
\dots,z_n\}\cup\{r^{-2}z_1^{-1},\dots,r^{-2}z_n^{-1}\}.
\]
The first set lies on \(|z|=1\), hence in \(|z|>\rho\). The second lies on \(|z|=r^{-2}\), hence in \(|z|<\rho\). Therefore exactly \(n\) zeros of \(S_r\), counted with multiplicity, lie in the exterior annulus \(|z|>\rho\), and they are precisely the zeros of \(P\). Since \(P\) is monic, these zeros determine the monic degree-\(n\) factor uniquely. The final sentence records the hypotheses used in the argument: the full polynomial \(Q_r\), the exact value of \(r\), and the exact shell separation are all part of the reconstruction input.
\end{proof}
