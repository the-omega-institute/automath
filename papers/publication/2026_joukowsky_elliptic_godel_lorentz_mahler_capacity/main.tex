\begin{abstract}
For \(J_r(z)=rz+r^{-1}z^{-1}\), the elliptic boundary problem has a
unique source maximizer when \(r>1\): normalized Haar measure on the
unit circle.  At the singular value \(r=1\), the ellipse collapses to
\([-2,2]\), the boundary map identifies reflected points, and the
equality class becomes an infinite-dimensional affine fiber.  We give
a complete description of that fiber as
\[
 \dd\eta=(1+h)\dd m_{\TT},\qquad
 h(\overline z)=-h(z),\qquad |h|\leq1.
\]
We then reopen the ellipse with \(r=e^s\).  For every member of the
collapsed fiber, the exact first-order deficit converges to
\[
 \lim_{s\downarrow0}
 \frac{s-I(J_{e^s\ast}\eta)}{2s}
 =\frac12\lVert h\rVert_{L^2(m_{\TT})}^2.
\]
Thus restoring injectivity selects Haar measure quantitatively from the
collapsed equality class.  The selection functional has sharp range
\([0,1/2]\).  A finite-Blaschke corollary identifies exactly which
covered members remain maximizers at every nondegenerate scale.
\end{abstract}
