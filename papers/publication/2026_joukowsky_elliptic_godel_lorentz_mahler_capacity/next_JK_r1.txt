You are refereeing the attached 90-page paper. In an earlier round it was rejected for a strong
journal, and the rejection quoted the paper's own novelty table back at it: every entry was rated LOW
or MEDIUM by the authors themselves. That is the situation. I am not asking you to re-derive the
rejection.

(1) IS THERE A PAPER INSIDE THIS ONE? Ninety pages that self-assess as LOW or MEDIUM novelty
throughout is usually one of two things: a monograph-style treatment whose value is coverage rather
than any single theorem, or a collection of exercises around a theme. Which is it? If there is a
single result that the authors have undersold -- something they rated MEDIUM that a referee would
rate higher -- name it by number and say why they were wrong about their own work. That is the most
useful thing you can tell me.

(2) IF THE VALUE IS COVERAGE, say so, and name the format that fits: an expository article, a survey,
a chapter, a lecture-note series. Those have real venues and real standards, and a 90-page
research-article submission to a strong journal is not the way to place them.

(3) WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN TO IT? Give me one of: extract a specific short paper (say which theorem and
how long), reformat as a survey (say where), split into two or more papers (say which), or withdraw.
Argue for exactly one. Include a rough numeric acceptance probability for whatever you recommend, and
say why you are not recommending the next option up.

I would rather be told this is not publishable as it stands than spend another round polishing it.
Do not summarize the paper back to me.
