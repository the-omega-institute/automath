The Editors
Complex Analysis and Operator Theory

Dear Editors,

We submit "Opening Selection at the Collapsed Joukowsky Ellipse" by Haobo Ma
and Wenlin Zhang. The main theorem is a source-space bifurcation for
J_r(z) = rz + r^{-1}z^{-1}: Haar measure is the unique source maximizer for
r > 1, while at r = 1 the equality set becomes an infinite-dimensional
affine fibre d eta = (1+h) d m_T with reflection-antisymmetric h and |h| <= 1.
When r = exp(s) is reopened, the exact normalized penalty is
[s - I(J_{exp(s)*} eta)]/(2s) -> (1/2)||h||_2^2, quantitatively selecting Haar
measure as injectivity is restored.

The paper does not claim the limiting arcsine measure, continuity of
logarithmic capacity, conformal degeneration itself, or the general fact
that non-injectivity permits multiple lifts. Those belong to the prior work
of Binder--Rojas--Yampolsky, Kalmykov--Kovalev, Warschawski, and Pommerenke;
the work of Levenberg--Wielonsky is also close in map and vocabulary. The
novelty claimed here is only the complete source-space bifurcation and its
exact normalized opening penalty. Complex Analysis and Operator Theory is a
good specialist fit because the argument joins logarithmic potential theory
with Fourier and harmonic analysis for a concrete complex-analytic family.

Reproducibility: artifacts/verify_collapsed_ellipse_selection.py checks the
collapsed equality fibre and the normalized opening-selection formula.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
