\begin{letter}{Editors\\Electronic Journal of Statistics}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our manuscript, ``Renewal-window equivalence and singular
inference at a sampled double pole,'' as an original research article for the
Electronic Journal of Statistics.

The paper proves a two-sided local asymptotic equivalence theorem between a
stationary lattice renewal window and an undershot fixed-size sample from its
Palm interarrival distribution.  Both deficiency directions are realized by
explicit parameter-free Markov kernels.  The result transfers bounded-loss
risks, identifies information per unit calendar time, and gives the
semiparametric efficiency conversion, including its qualification for fixed
constrained local experiments.

The application determines the singular local experiment at a sampled real
double pole in a serial generalized-Erlang renewal model.  A
pole-order argument proves score nondegeneracy, the local limit is a Gaussian
shift on \(\mathbb R^{n-1}\times[0,\infty)\), and a residualized score test
attains the one-sided power envelope.  A measurable recurrence estimator
attains \(N^{-1/4}\) recovery of the unordered colliding rates, matched by a
fixed-base two-point threshold lower rate.

The main article is 32 pages including references in the supplied
double-spaced referee format.  A separate 22-page supplement contains the
uniform derivative bounds, stopped-renewal score CLT and LLN, efficient-score
plug-in argument, an independent direct stationary-likelihood LAN proof,
detailed two-state calculations, and the finite-atlas estimator construction.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration
elsewhere.  Both authors have approved the submission.  We declare no
competing interests.

\closing{Sincerely,}
