# Literature check

Searches were run on 2026-08-17 in Crossref, OpenAlex, and Semantic Scholar
before the higher-multiplicity result was added.  The query families combined
the following terms:

- generalized Erlang, hypoexponential, convolution of exponentials, repeated
  rates, equal rates, and parameter estimation;
- multiple collision, colliding roots, singular LAN, local asymptotic
  normality, loss of identifiability, and Gaussian cone;
- phase-type distribution, multiple poles, confluent Prony, statistical
  estimation, and collision;
- stationary renewal, Palm interarrival, asymptotic equivalence, and
  nonparametric efficiency.

No returned record states the quadratic-dispersion collapse for an isolated
multiple pole in a sampled serial generalized-Erlang law, an efficient
multiple-collision power envelope for that experiment, or total-variation
indistinguishability of nonpermutation equal-dispersion cluster shapes.

The closest bodies of work were checked separately.

- Dacunha-Castelle and Gassiat, "Testing in locally conic models, and
  application to mixture models" (1997), and Liu and Shao, "Asymptotics for
  likelihood ratio tests under loss of identifiability" (2003), provide
  general testing frameworks.  They do not calculate the sampled
  generalized-Erlang tangent experiment or its surviving dispersion
  coordinate.
- Ho and Nguyen, "Singularity Structures and Impacts on Parameter Estimation
  in Finite Mixtures of Distributions" (2019), and the finite-mixture rate
  papers already cited in the article concern additive mixture models.  A
  serial convolution has a product Laplace transform and a different
  collision tangent.
- He and Zhang, "Coxian representations of generalized Erlang distributions"
  (2009), studies representations rather than local statistical experiments.
  Miller, "Estimation for renewal processes with unobservable gamma or Erlang
  interarrival times" (1997), concerns an observation scheme with
  unobservable interarrivals and does not treat colliding component rates.
- The confluent-Prony and super-resolution papers already cited analyze root
  recovery under different observation and noise models.  They do not yield
  the renewal-window testing envelope or the equal-dispersion
  total-variation obstruction.
