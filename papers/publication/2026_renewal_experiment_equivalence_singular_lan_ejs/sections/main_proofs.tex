\section{Proof of the limit-experiment theorem}
\label{sec:main-proofs-main}

The proof uses the following exact supplementary inputs.  Their proofs are
\Cref*{lem:uniform-sampled-bin-tail-score-main,lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main,lem:efficient-score-plugin-main}
of the supplementary material.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{DQM and envelope.}
For fixed \(n,\varepsilon,M,c_0\), and \(\Delta\tau>0\), there is a common
physical one-sided neighbourhood \(\mathcal Q^+\) of
\(\mathcal C_n(\varepsilon,M,c_0)\), in the coordinates
\(q=(\eta,\delta)\), on which \(c\pm\sqrt\delta>0\) and, for every
multi-index \(a\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sup_{q\in\mathcal Q^+}|\partial_q^a\log g_q(k)|
 &\le C_a(1+k)^{2|a|},&& 1\le|a|\le3,\\
 \sup_{q\in\mathcal Q^+}|\partial_q^a\log S_q(k)|
 &\le C_a(1+k)^{2|a|},&& 1\le|a|\le2.
\end{aligned}
\]
All parameter derivatives extend continuously to \(\delta=0\), and
\(g_q(k)\le C(1+k)^{n-1}r^k\) for a common \(r<1\).  Writing
\(e_q(a)=S_q(a)/\mu_q\), the families \(g_q\) and \(e_q\) are uniformly
differentiable in quadratic mean.  Uniformly over base points on the collision
stratum and bounded physical root-\(N\) local arrays \(q_N\),
\[
 H^2(g_{q_N},g_{q_0})=O(N^{-1}),\qquad
 H^2(e_{q_N},e_{q_0})=O(N^{-1}),\qquad
 |\mu_{q_N}-\mu_{q_0}|=O(N^{-1/2}).
\]

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Stopped-score CLT and information LLN.}
Let \(\mathcal Q\) be compact and let the integer Palm gaps \(G\) have full
support, a common exponential moment, and parameter-continuous masses.  Let a
stationary binary renewal record of length \(N\) contain \(K_N\) complete
gaps, with initial forward recurrence \(A_N\) and terminal backward recurrence
\(R_N\), and put \(D=G+1\), \(\mu_q=\EE_qD\).  Uniformly in
\(q\in\mathcal Q\), and for triangular arrays \(q_N\in\mathcal Q\),
\[
 K_N=N/\mu_{q_N}+O_{P_{q_N}}(\sqrt N),\qquad
 \max\{A_N,R_N,G_1,\ldots,G_{K_N}\}=O_{P_{q_N}}(\log N).
\]
If \(b_q(G)\in\RR^d\) is parameter-continuous, \(\EE_qb_q(G)=0\), and is
bounded by a common polynomial in \(G\), then
\[
 \sup_{q\in\mathcal Q}d_{\rm BL}\!\left(
 \mathcal L_q\!\left[N^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}b_q(G_j)\right],
 \mathcal N_d\!\left(0,\mu_q^{-1}\EE_q[b_qb_q^\top]\right)
 \right)\longrightarrow0.
\]
For every similarly dominated matrix reward \(H_q\),
\[
 N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}H_q(G_j)
 -\mu_q^{-1}\EE_qH_q(G)\longrightarrow0
\]
uniformly in probability.  If \(B_q\) has a common polynomial envelope, then
\[
 N^{-3/2}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}B_q(G_j)=o_P(1)
\]
uniformly.  These conclusions hold jointly and apply to the score, stopped
Hessian, and third-order likelihood remainder under bounded root-\(N\) local
triangular arrays.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Plug-in equicontinuity.}
On the canonically ordered collision stratum, write the one-cycle null scores
as \(s_\eta\) and \(s_\delta\), and define
\[
 \psi_\eta=s_\delta-I_{\delta\eta}I_{\eta\eta}^{-1}s_\eta,
 \qquad
 \Delta_{{\rm eff},N}(\eta)=N^{-1/2}
 \sum_{j=1}^{K_N}\psi_\eta(G_j).
\]
For every fixed \(L<\infty\),
\[
 \sup_\eta\sup_{\|\eta'-\eta\|\le L/\sqrt N}
 |\Delta_{{\rm eff},N}(\eta')-\Delta_{{\rm eff},N}(\eta)|
 =o_{P_{\eta,0}}(1).
\]
The conclusion also holds under bounded contiguous local alternatives.  The
empirical calendar information and its Schur complement, evaluated anywhere
in the same root-\(N\) ball, converge uniformly to \(I_n(\eta)\) and
\(J_n(\eta)\).  Hence any measurable root-\(N\) null fit may be inserted in
the standardized efficient central sequence with an \(o_P(1)\) change.

\begin{proof}[Proof of \Cref{thm:stationary-serial-double-pole-main}]
We first make the route from the stationary record to the local experiment
explicit.  The serial killed-reset construction gives a stationary renewal
indicator with
\begin{equation}
 S_{\eta,\delta}(k)=\PP_{\eta,\delta}\{W>k\Delta\tau\},\qquad
 g_{\eta,\delta}(k)=S_{\eta,\delta}(k)-S_{\eta,\delta}(k+1).
\label{eq:main-sampled-gap}
\end{equation}
The required uniform score bounds are supplied in the supplement.
Specifically, \Cref*{lem:uniform-sampled-bin-tail-score-main} of the supplementary material gives polynomial relative
derivative bounds through the orders required for DQM, together with a common
exponential envelope.  Along every bounded root-\(N\) local array, the DQM
expansion therefore gives
\begin{equation}
 H^2(g_{\eta_N,\delta_N},g_{\eta_0,0})=O(N^{-1}),
\label{eq:main-gap-hellinger}
\end{equation}
uniformly.  The equilibrium residual-life mass is
\(e_{\eta,\delta}(a)=S_{\eta,\delta}(a)/\mu_{\eta,\delta}\).  The same
tail-derivative bounds and dominated differentiation give
\begin{equation}
 H^2(e_{\eta_N,\delta_N},e_{\eta_0,0})=O(N^{-1}),\qquad
 |\mu_{\eta_N,\delta_N}-\mu_{\eta_0,0}|=O(N^{-1/2}),
\label{eq:main-equilibrium-hellinger}
\end{equation}
again uniformly.  Thus every hypothesis of
\Cref{thm:stationary-renewal-palm-equivalence-main} holds on these arrays.

For any fixed \(\gamma\in(1/2,1)\), that theorem identifies the stationary
window experiment, in Le Cam distance, with
\(m_N=\lfloor N/\mu_{\eta_0,0}-N^\gamma\rfloor\) independent Palm gaps.
The i.i.d. DQM expansion has one-gap information
\(\sum_k g\dot\ell\dot\ell^\top\).  Since
\(m_N/N\to1/\mu_{\eta_0,0}\), its calendar-time information is exactly
\(I_n(\eta_0)\) in \eqref{eq:main-calendar-information}.  The uniform i.i.d. LAN theorem and the
two-sided deficiency comparison therefore yield convergence of the local
stationary experiments to the Gaussian shift on
\(\RR^{n-1}\times[0,\infty)\).  Restriction to \(v\ge0\) is precisely the
fixed-cone qualification in \Cref{thm:stationary-renewal-palm-equivalence-main};
the information remains that of the ambient DQM chart, while decisions are
made in the constrained experiment.

This equivalence route proves the experiment convergence without replacing
Palm gaps by gaps conditioned to fit in the observation window.  For the
explicit stationary-record likelihood-ratio expansion in \eqref{eq:main-stationary-lan},
\Cref*{lem:compact-uniform-stationary-renewal-lan-main} of the supplementary material gives an independent
direct proof.  On a record with renewal sites
\(0\le C_1<\cdots<C_J<N\), that proof starts from the exact factorization
\begin{equation}
 \frac1{\mu_q}S_q(C_1)
 \prod_{j=1}^{J-1}g_q(C_{j+1}-C_j-1)S_q(N-1-C_J),
\label{eq:main-stationary-factorization}
\end{equation}
handles the all-zero record separately, and proves that increments of the
two endpoint log tails and \(-\log\mu_q\), rather than the factors
themselves, are \(o_P(1)\).  Its stopped-score central sequence is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{N,\eta_0}=N^{-1/2}
 \sum_{j=1}^{J-1}\dot\ell_{\eta_0,0}(C_{j+1}-C_j-1).
\label{eq:main-stopped-score}
\end{equation}
The stopped Hessian LLN and score CLT in
\Cref*{lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main} of the supplementary material give the quadratic term and the
uniform Gaussian limit in \eqref{eq:main-stationary-lan}.  Proposition
\ref{prop:sampled-double-pole-nondegeneracy-main} supplies the positive
definiteness needed in both routes, proving part~\textup{(A)}.

For part~\textup{(B)}, use the test constructed in
\eqref{eq:main-empirical-information}--\eqref{eq:main-residualized-score-test}.  The finite-atlas fit is Borel, and finite
sums, eigenvalues, and matrix inversion on the positive-definite gate preserve
measurability.  The off-gate convention therefore makes \(\phi_N\) total and
Borel on every binary record.

The population efficient central sequence is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{\delta\cdot\eta,N}
 =\Delta_{\delta,N}
 -I_{\delta\eta}I_{\eta\eta}^{-1}\Delta_{\eta,N},
\label{eq:main-efficient-central-sequence}
\end{equation}
with null variance \(J_n>0\).  Le Cam's third lemma gives mean \(vJ_n\)
under \((\eta_0+u/\sqrt N,v/\sqrt N)\); the nuisance shift cancels.
\Cref*{lem:efficient-score-plugin-main} of the supplementary material shows that the root-\(N\) null fit and
empirical information change the standardized sequence by \(o_P(1)\)
uniformly.  The stopped LLN and \eqref{eq:main-information-nondegeneracy} make \(\mathcal G_N\)
asymptotically inactive.  This proves \eqref{eq:main-power-envelope} and uniform local level.
The Neyman--Pearson lemma after nuisance residualization in the Gaussian shift
gives the asserted power envelope.

For part~\textup{(C)}, the full construction in
\Cref*{sec:estimator-atlas-supp} of the supplementary material proves \eqref{eq:main-regular-estimator-rate}.  Nonexpansive
projection and the square-root inequality following
\Cref{est:serial-collision-recurrence-main} yield \eqref{eq:main-recovery-rate}, while
uniform separation fixes the optimal matching with probability tending to
one.  Finally, at a fixed base hold \(\eta=\eta_0\) and compare \(v=0\) with
\(v=v_0\).  \Cref{lem:two-point-threshold-rate-main} gives their eventual
matching distance \(\sqrt{v_0}N^{-1/4}\) and the positive Gaussian lower
bound in \eqref{eq:main-threshold-lower-bound}.  The upper and lower statements together prove
attainability and pointwise local minimax optimality in rate.
\end{proof}
