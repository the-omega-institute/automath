\section{Proof of the limit-experiment theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof of \Cref{thm:stationary-serial-double-pole-main}]
We first make the route from the stationary record to the local experiment
explicit.  The serial killed-reset construction gives a stationary renewal
indicator with
\[
 S_{\eta,\delta}(k)=\PP_{\eta,\delta}\{W>k\Delta\tau\},\qquad
 g_{\eta,\delta}(k)=S_{\eta,\delta}(k)-S_{\eta,\delta}(k+1).
\tag{4.1}
\]
The required uniform score bounds are supplied in the supplement.
Specifically, \Cref{lem:uniform-sampled-bin-tail-score-main} gives polynomial relative
derivative bounds through the orders required for DQM, together with a common
exponential envelope.  Along every bounded root-\(N\) local array, the DQM
expansion therefore gives
\[
 H^2(g_{\eta_N,\delta_N},g_{\eta_0,0})=O(N^{-1}),
\tag{4.2}
\]
uniformly.  The equilibrium residual-life mass is
\(e_{\eta,\delta}(a)=S_{\eta,\delta}(a)/\mu_{\eta,\delta}\).  The same
tail-derivative bounds and dominated differentiation give
\[
 H^2(e_{\eta_N,\delta_N},e_{\eta_0,0})=O(N^{-1}),\qquad
 |\mu_{\eta_N,\delta_N}-\mu_{\eta_0,0}|=O(N^{-1/2}),
\tag{4.3}
\]
again uniformly.  Thus every hypothesis of
\Cref{thm:stationary-renewal-palm-equivalence-main} holds on these arrays.

For any fixed \(\gamma\in(1/2,1)\), that theorem identifies the stationary
window experiment, in Le Cam distance, with
\(m_N=\lfloor N/\mu_{\eta_0,0}-N^\gamma\rfloor\) independent Palm gaps.
The i.i.d. DQM expansion has one-gap information
\(\sum_k g\dot\ell\dot\ell^\top\).  Since
\(m_N/N\to1/\mu_{\eta_0,0}\), its calendar-time information is exactly
\(I_n(\eta_0)\) in \textup{(3.3)}.  The uniform i.i.d. LAN theorem and the
two-sided deficiency comparison therefore yield convergence of the local
stationary experiments to the Gaussian shift on
\(\RR^{n-1}\times[0,\infty)\).  Restriction to \(v\ge0\) is precisely the
fixed-cone qualification in \Cref{thm:stationary-renewal-palm-equivalence-main};
the information remains that of the ambient DQM chart, while decisions are
made in the constrained experiment.

This equivalence route proves the experiment convergence without replacing
Palm gaps by gaps conditioned to fit in the observation window.  For the
explicit stationary-record likelihood-ratio expansion in \textup{(3.10)},
\Cref{lem:compact-uniform-stationary-renewal-lan-main} gives an independent
direct proof.  On a record with renewal sites
\(0\le C_1<\cdots<C_J<N\), that proof starts from the exact factorization
\[
 \frac1{\mu_q}S_q(C_1)
 \prod_{j=1}^{J-1}g_q(C_{j+1}-C_j-1)S_q(N-1-C_J),
\tag{4.4}
\]
handles the all-zero record separately, and proves that increments of the
two endpoint log tails and \(-\log\mu_q\), rather than the factors
themselves, are \(o_P(1)\).  Its stopped-score central sequence is
\[
 \Delta_{N,\eta_0}=N^{-1/2}
 \sum_{j=1}^{J-1}\dot\ell_{\eta_0,0}(C_{j+1}-C_j-1).
\tag{4.5}
\]
The stopped Hessian LLN and score CLT in
\Cref{lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main} give the quadratic term and the
uniform Gaussian limit in \textup{(3.10)}.  Proposition
\ref{prop:sampled-double-pole-nondegeneracy-main} supplies the positive
definiteness needed in both routes, proving part~\textup{(A)}.

For part~\textup{(B)}, write the null one-cycle score as
\[
 s_{\eta,0}(k)=
 \begin{pmatrix}s_{\rm nuis}(k;\eta)\\s_\delta(k;\eta)\end{pmatrix}
\]
and choose, before observing the record, a compact null-coordinate
neighbourhood \(\mathcal E_0\) whose interior contains the stratum projection
and whose closure lies in the common score chart.  For \(\eta\in\mathcal E_0\)
put
\[
 I_N(\eta)=\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}
 s_{\eta,0}(G_j)s_{\eta,0}(G_j)^\top
\tag{4.6}
\]
and partition it as in \textup{(3.11)}.  Set \(I_N(\eta)=0\) outside
\(\mathcal E_0\), and define
\[
 J_N(\eta)=
 \begin{cases}
 I_{\delta\delta,N}-I_{\delta\eta,N}
 I_{\eta\eta,N}^{-1}I_{\eta\delta,N},
 &\eta\in\mathcal E_0,\ \lambda_{\min}(I_{\eta\eta,N})>0,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{4.7}
\]
Let \(\widehat\eta_N\) be the fit in
\Cref{est:serial-collision-recurrence-main} and
\(\mathcal G_N=\{\widehat\eta_N\in\mathcal E_0,
J_N(\widehat\eta_N)>0\}\).  On \(\mathcal G_N\), define
\[
 Z_N=\frac{N^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}
 \left[s_\delta(G_j;\widehat\eta_N)
 -I_{\delta\eta,N}(\widehat\eta_N)
 I_{\eta\eta,N}(\widehat\eta_N)^{-1}
 s_{\rm nuis}(G_j;\widehat\eta_N)\right]}
 {\sqrt{J_N(\widehat\eta_N)}}
\tag{4.8}
\]
and set \(\phi_N=\mathbf1_{\{Z_N>z_{1-\alpha}\}}\); off the gate set
\(\phi_N=0\).  The finite-atlas fit is Borel.  Finite sums, eigenvalues, and
matrix inversion on the positive-definite gate preserve measurability, so
\(\phi_N\) is total and Borel on every binary record.

The population efficient central sequence is
\[
 \Delta_{\delta\cdot\eta,N}
 =\Delta_{\delta,N}
 -I_{\delta\eta}I_{\eta\eta}^{-1}\Delta_{\eta,N},
\tag{4.9}
\]
with null variance \(J_n>0\).  Le Cam's third lemma gives mean \(vJ_n\)
under \((\eta_0+u/\sqrt N,v/\sqrt N)\); the nuisance shift cancels.
\Cref{lem:efficient-score-plugin-main} shows that the root-\(N\) null fit and
empirical information change the standardized sequence by \(o_P(1)\)
uniformly.  The stopped LLN and \textup{(3.5)} make \(\mathcal G_N\)
asymptotically inactive.  This proves \textup{(3.12)} and uniform local level.
The Neyman--Pearson lemma after nuisance residualization in the Gaussian shift
gives the asserted power envelope.

For part~\textup{(C)}, the full construction in
\Cref{sec:estimator-atlas-supp} proves \textup{(3.18)}.  Nonexpansive
projection and the square-root inequality following
\Cref{est:serial-collision-recurrence-main} yield \textup{(3.13)}, while
uniform separation fixes the optimal matching with probability tending to
one.  Finally, at a fixed base hold \(\eta=\eta_0\) and compare \(v=0\) with
\(v=v_0\).  \Cref{lem:two-point-threshold-rate-main} gives their eventual
matching distance \(\sqrt{v_0}N^{-1/4}\) and the positive Gaussian lower
bound in \textup{(3.15)}.  The upper and lower statements together prove
attainability and pointwise local minimax optimality in rate, with no claim of
an exact continuum minimax constant.
\end{proof}
