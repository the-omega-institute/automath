\subsection{Contour atlas, compatibility, and measurability}
\label{sec:estimator-atlas-supp}

\begin{proof}[Proof of \Cref{est:serial-collision-recurrence-main}]
Delete the initial and terminal incomplete cycles and write the observed
complete gaps as \(G_1,\ldots,G_{K_N}\).  Define
\[
 \widehat S_k=K_N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_N}\mathbf1_{\{G_j\ge k\}},
 \qquad 0\le k\le2n-1,
\tag{E1}
\]
with arbitrary fixed values on \(K_N=0\).  Apply
\Cref{lem:uniform-stopped-renewal-score-main} to the finite vector of
indicator rewards.  Its stopped CLT and count bound imply
\[
 \max_{0\le k\le2n-1}|\widehat S_k-S_k|=O_P(N^{-1/2})
\tag{E2}
\]
uniformly over the stratum and bounded local alternatives.

Write the monic recurrence polynomial as
\[
 \chi(x)=x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0
 =\prod_{i=1}^n(x-z_i),\qquad z_i=e^{-\theta_i\Delta\tau}.
\tag{E3}
\]
The equations
\[
 S_{k+n}+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_jS_{k+j}=0,
 \qquad0\le k<n,
\tag{E4}
\]
have coefficient matrix \((S_{i+j})_{0\le i,j<n}\).  It is nonsingular at
collisions by \Cref{prop:serial-tail-coordinates-main}.  Its determinant has
a positive minimum in absolute value on the compact stratum, and the same is
true on a sufficiently small common neighbourhood.  Define
\[
 \widehat H_N=(\widehat S_{i+j})_{0\le i,j<n},\qquad
 \widehat s_N=(\widehat S_{n+i})_{0\le i<n},
\]
fix deterministic \(a^\ast\in\RR^n\), and give the recurrence estimator the
total definition
\[
 \widehat a_N=
 \begin{cases}
 -\widehat H_N^{-1}\widehat s_N,
 &\lambda_{\min}(\widehat H_N^\top\widehat H_N)>N^{-1/2},\\
 a^\ast,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{E5}
\]
This is Borel measurable.  The uniform population singular-value bound,
\textup{(E2)}, and Weyl's inequality make the fallback probability tend to
zero uniformly.  The inverse perturbation identity then gives
\[
 \max_j|\widehat a_j-a_j|=O_P(N^{-1/2}).
\tag{E6}
\]

We next distinguish the cluster from the noncollision roots.  Rate separation
gives the common sampled-root distance
\(\rho_0=\Delta\tau e^{-M\Delta\tau}c_0>0\).  At each base polynomial choose
one circle enclosing its double root and \(n-2\) disjoint circles enclosing
its simple roots, with radii below \(\rho_0/4\).  On a coefficient
neighbourhood \(U\), the argument principle keeps the root counts equal to
two and one.  For a simple-root circle, the contour power sum
\[
 z_j(a)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\gamma_j}
 z\frac{\chi_a'(z)}{\chi_a(z)}\,dz
\tag{E7}
\]
is analytic.  On the double-root circle the first and second power sums
\(p_1,p_2\) are analytic, and
\[
 A=p_1,\qquad B=(p_1^2-p_2)/2
\tag{E8}
\]
are the elementary symmetric functions of the two-root cluster.  They remain
analytic when the roots split, and assign no label within the cluster.

Cover the compact collision stratum in coefficient space by finitely many
such neighbourhoods \(U_1,\ldots,U_L\).  Shrink them to open sets
\(V_\ell\) whose closures lie in \(U_\ell\) and still cover the stratum.
Define the chart index as the least \(\ell\) with
\(\widehat a\in V_\ell\).  Membership in finitely many open sets is Borel.
To check compatibility, observe that every simple contour has root count one.
At a collision polynomial it cannot contain the double root, whose count is
two; hence every valid two-root contour encloses the unique double cluster.
After shrinking the coefficient neighbourhoods, root continuity and uniform
cluster separation imply that valid cluster contours on an overlap enclose
the same two roots.  Their power sums and \((A,B)\) agree.  The remaining
simple-root multisets agree as well, and their vectors agree after numerical
ordering by \(-\log z/\Delta\tau\).  The least-index selection therefore
cannot change the estimand.  The finite maximum of the chart derivatives is
bounded.

For every deterministic \(C_N\to\infty\), \textup{(E6)} gives
\[
 \sup_q\PP_q\{\|\widehat a-a(q)\|>C_N/\sqrt N\}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{E9}
\]
The finite shrunken cover has a positive Lebesgue-number margin.  Coefficients
on bounded local alternatives are \(O(N^{-1/2})\) from the base stratum and
thus lie within half the margin for all large \(N\).  Equation
\textup{(E9)} shows that the probability of no valid chart tends to zero
uniformly; use a fixed ordered value on that event.  Otherwise the analytic
maps, \textup{(E6)}, and finite derivative bound give root-\(N\) errors for
every ordered simple root and for \(A,B\).

The separated rates are \(-\log z_j/\Delta\tau\).  For the cluster,
\[
 c=-\frac1{2\Delta\tau}\log B,
 \qquad
 \delta=\frac1{(\Delta\tau)^2}
 \left[\operatorname{arcosh}\left(\frac A{2\sqrt B}\right)\right]^2
\tag{E10}
\]
are real analytic near \(A=2z,B=z^2\), after continuing the square of
\(\operatorname{arcosh}\) through one.  Project the continued
\(\widetilde\delta_N\) onto \([0,\infty)\); projection is nonexpansive.
This proves the two root-\(N\) conclusions in \textup{(3.18)} and all stated
measurability properties.

Finally form \(\widehat c_N\pm\sqrt{\widehat\delta_N}\) and append the
estimated separated rates.  Since
\(|\sqrt{x^+}-\sqrt y|\le\sqrt{|x-y|}\) for \(y\ge0\), the colliding pair
has \(O_P(N^{-1/4})\) error while all other components have root-\(N\) error.
The separation \(c_0\) fixes the optimal matching with probability tending to
one uniformly, proving \textup{(3.13)}.
\end{proof}
