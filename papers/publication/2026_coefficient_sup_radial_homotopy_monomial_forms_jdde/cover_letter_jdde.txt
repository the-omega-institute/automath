Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript "Boundary trace rigidity and cubical stability for coefficient-$L^\infty$ primitives on boxes" for consideration in the Journal of Dynamics and Differential Equations.

The paper proves a sharp anisotropic equality-and-stability theorem for coefficient-$L^\infty$ primitives of the top form on rectangular boxes. It identifies the exact extremal value, the affine minimizer, boundary trace rigidity for exact minimizers, and a linear boundary-trace stability estimate for near-minimizers. It also records the corresponding one-cell cubical and Whitney consequences while explicitly limiting those results to local cell-level statements.

We believe the manuscript is appropriate for JDDE because the results provide local analytic and cubical stability estimates relevant to differential-form evolution, interface balance laws, and discrete analogues of differential-equation structures. The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The authors declare no competing interests and received no specific funding for this work.
