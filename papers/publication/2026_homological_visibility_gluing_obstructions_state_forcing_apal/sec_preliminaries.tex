\section{Preliminaries on the extension chain}\label{sec:preliminaries}

We fix the semantic framework used throughout. In particular, we distinguish conservative extension from the weaker singleton embedding that links the pointwise layer to information-state forcing.

\subsection*{Standing notation}
States are denoted by $p,q,u$, and admitted references by $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ with associated class $a=[r]_p$. The local reference site at a state and sort is $\mathcal C_{p,s}$, the local object functor is $F_{p,s}$, the visible value classes of a reference are $\Vis_{p,s}(r)$, and branch obstruction classes are denoted by $\omega$ or $\omega_v$. Subscripts such as $\mathrm{vis}$, $\mathrm{br}$, and $\mathrm{unif}$ consistently indicate visible, branch-sensitive, and branch-uniform quotients.

\begin{definition}[Logic layer]\label{def:logic-layer}
A \emph{logic layer} is a tuple
\[
\mathbb{L}=(\Sigma,\mathrm{Form},\Mod,\St,\Vdash,\models),
\]
where $\Sigma$ is a signature, $\mathrm{Form}$ is the class of well-formed $\Sigma$-formulas, $\Mod$ is a class of admissible models, $\St$ assigns to every $M\in\Mod$ a nonempty set $\St(M)$ of states, $\Vdash$ is a forcing relation on model-state pairs, and $\models$ is the induced consequence relation defined by
\[
\Gamma\models\varphi
\quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall M\in\Mod\;\forall p\in\St(M)\,
\bigl(M,p\Vdash\Gamma \Rightarrow M,p\Vdash\varphi\bigr).
\]
\end{definition}

Forcing is the primitive notion; consequence is derived from it.

\begin{definition}[Conservative extension]\label{def:conservative-extension}
Let
\[
\mathbb{L}_m=(\Sigma_m,\mathrm{Form}_m,\Mod_m,\St_m,\Vdash_m,\models_m)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathbb{L}_n=(\Sigma_n,\mathrm{Form}_n,\Mod_n,\St_n,\Vdash_n,\models_n)
\]
with $m<n$. We say that $\mathbb{L}_n$ is a \emph{conservative extension} of $\mathbb{L}_m$, written $\mathbb{L}_m\preceq\mathbb{L}_n$, if there exist
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item a language embedding $e_{m,n}:\Sigma_m\hookrightarrow\Sigma_n$,
\item a forgetful functor $U_{n,m}:\Mod_n\to\Mod_m$,
\item for every $M\in\Mod_n$, a state projection
\[
\pi^M_{n,m}:\St_n(M)\to \St_m\bigl(U_{n,m}(M)\bigr),
\]
\end{enumerate}
such that for every $\varphi\in \mathrm{Form}_m$, every $M\in\Mod_n$, and every $p\in\St_n(M)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conservative-extension}
M,p\Vdash_n \varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{n,m}(M),\pi^M_{n,m}(p)\Vdash_m \varphi.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

Thus a higher layer may add coordinates or geometric data, but lower-layer formulas are interpreted unchanged after projection.

\begin{definition}[Singleton semantic embedding]\label{def:singleton-semantic-embedding}
Let $\mathbb{L}_0=(\Sigma_0,\mathrm{Form}_0,\Mod_0,\St_0,\Vdash_0,\models_0)$ and $\mathbb{L}_1=(\Sigma_1,\mathrm{Form}_1,\Mod_1,\St_1,\Vdash_1,\models_1)$. We say that $\mathbb{L}_0$ \emph{embeds into $\mathbb{L}_1$ by a singleton semantic embedding}, written $\mathbb{L}_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb{L}_1$, if there exist
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item a language embedding $e_{0,1}:\Sigma_0\hookrightarrow\Sigma_1$,
\item a forgetful functor $U_{1,0}:\Mod_1\to\Mod_0$,
\item for every $M\in\Mod_1$ and every pointwise state $\rho\in\St_0(U_{1,0}(M))$, a canonical singleton state
\[
\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho)\in\St_1(M)
\]
\end{enumerate}
such that for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:singleton-semantic-embedding}
M,\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho)\Vdash_1\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{1,0}(M),\rho\Vdash_0\varphi.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

A singleton semantic embedding is weaker than a conservative extension: it compares only singleton states, and no projection from arbitrary information states to pointwise states is required.

\begin{proposition}[Composition of conservative extensions]\label{prop:conservative-composition}
If $\mathbb{L}_\ell\preceq\mathbb{L}_m$ and $\mathbb{L}_m\preceq\mathbb{L}_n$, then $\mathbb{L}_\ell\preceq\mathbb{L}_n$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Compose the language embeddings and forgetful functors:
\[
e_{\ell,n}:=e_{m,n}\circ e_{\ell,m},
\qquad
U_{n,\ell}:=U_{m,\ell}\circ U_{n,m}.
\]
For every $M\in\Mod_n$, define
\[
\pi^M_{n,\ell}:=
\pi^{\,U_{n,m}(M)}_{m,\ell}\circ \pi^M_{n,m}.
\]
If $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_\ell$ and $p\in\St_n(M)$, then
\[
M,p\Vdash_n\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{n,m}(M),\pi^M_{n,m}(p)\Vdash_m\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{n,\ell}(M),\pi^M_{n,\ell}(p)\Vdash_\ell\varphi,
\]
by two applications of \Cref{def:conservative-extension}. This is exactly the required conservativity condition.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite-chain invariance]\label{cor:whole-chain-invariance}
If
\[
\mathbb{L}_0\preceq\mathbb{L}_1\preceq\cdots\preceq\mathbb{L}_n,
\]
then every formula belonging to any lower layer keeps the same forcing behavior when interpreted in any later layer and projected back down.
\leanverified{paper_conservative_extension_whole_chain_invariance_apal}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply \Cref{prop:conservative-composition} repeatedly along the chain.
\end{proof}

The semantic layers considered later fit into the chain
\[
\mathbb{L}_0
\xhookrightarrow{\iota}
\mathbb{L}_1
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_2
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_3
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_4,
\]
where the hooked arrow $\xhookrightarrow{\iota}$ denotes a singleton semantic embedding (\Cref{def:singleton-semantic-embedding}) and each $\preceq$ denotes a conservative extension in the sense of \Cref{def:conservative-extension}. Here $\mathbb{L}_0$ is the underlying pointwise semantics, $\mathbb{L}_1$ is the information-state forcing layer with reference sorts, $\mathbb{L}_2$ adds local-object predicates and site structure, $\mathbb{L}_3$ adds structural absence and obstruction data, and $\mathbb{L}_4$ adds refinement dynamics. By \Cref{cor:whole-chain-invariance}, the passage from $\mathbb{L}_1$ upward is cumulative. The first step is weaker: pointwise states embed faithfully as singleton information states, but arbitrary multi-realization states need not project back to single assignments.

The model classes of the higher layers are used below as expansion classes.
Precisely, an expansion from $\mathbb L_m$ to $\mathbb L_n$ is admissible when
the newly adjoined symbols of $\Sigma_n\setminus\Sigma_m$ are interpreted by
the kind of data named for that layer, the reduct under $U_{n,m}$ is the given
lower model, and the lower atomic forcing clauses are read through that
reduct.  No existence theorem for gluing-sensitive data is being smuggled into
this convention: the convention says only that, once such data are explicitly
constructed or assumed and satisfy the stated typing axioms, the resulting
structure is a member of the relevant higher model class.

\begin{lemma}[Flat reduct induction for a displayed layer extension]\label{lem:layer-reduct-induction}
Let $1\le m<n\le 4$. Suppose that $\Sigma_n$ is obtained from $\Sigma_m$ by adjoining a disjoint block of new symbols, that $\Mod_n$ consists of expansions of $\Sigma_m$-reducts through the forgetful functor
\[
U_{n,m}:\Mod_n\to\Mod_m,
\]
and that the state projection
\[
\pi^M_{n,m}:\St_n(M)\to\St_m(U_{n,m}(M))
\]
forgets only the new layer coordinates. Assume also that every atomic forcing clause whose atom belongs to $\Sigma_m$ is interpreted through the reduct:
\[
M,p\Vdash_n \alpha
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{n,m}(M),\pi^M_{n,m}(p)\Vdash_m \alpha
\]
for every $\Sigma_m$-atomic formula $\alpha$. Then the same equivalence holds for every formula $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_m$:
\[
M,p\Vdash_n \varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{n,m}(M),\pi^M_{n,m}(p)\Vdash_m \varphi.
\]
Consequently $\mathbb L_m\preceq\mathbb L_n$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For every retained formula \(\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_m\), forcing is the
flat information-state satisfaction of \Cref{def:state-forcing}: it means
ordinary pointwise satisfaction of \(\varphi\) by every realization in the
state. The reduct functor \(U_{n,m}\) leaves all retained symbols, retained
sorts, and retained assignments unchanged, while the state projection
\(\pi^M_{n,m}\) forgets only the newly adjoined layer coordinates. Hence, for
every retained assignment \(\rho\) occurring in \(p\),
\[
M,\rho\models \varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{n,m}(M),\rho|_{\Sigma_m}\models \varphi .
\]
This is the ordinary first-order reduct induction on the formula
\(\varphi\): atomic formulas are the displayed hypothesis, Boolean
connectives are immediate, and quantifiers range over the same retained
sort-carriers on both sides. Taking the universal quantifier over the same
projected set of realizations in the state gives
\[
M,p\Vdash_n\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{n,m}(M),\pi^M_{n,m}(p)\Vdash_m\varphi .
\]
Persistence and decidedness are not additional recursive forcing clauses in
this paper; persistence is a theorem about the flat forcing relation, and
decidedness is the abbreviation from \Cref{def:decidedness}. The displayed
equivalence is exactly \Cref{eq:conservative-extension}, so
\(\mathbb L_m\preceq\mathbb L_n\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Admissible-expansion model-class interface for the layer chain]\label{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}
Let $i\in\{1,2,3\}$ and let $M_i\in\Mod_i$. Suppose a block of new layer data
$D_{i+1}$ is supplied as follows:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item for $i=1$, $D_2$ may be any finite pullback-based local reference site
attached to the given admitted references, with covering families, refinement
relations, pullbacks, and any contravariant local object functors satisfying the
typing clauses of
\Cref{def:reference-classes,def:local-reference-frame,def:local-object-functor};
\item for $i=2$, $D_3$ consists of structural-absence predicates, visible
branches, realization prestacks, banded stackifications or banded
realization-stack extensions, component-gerbe data, and obstruction classes
satisfying the hypotheses explicitly stated where those data are used;
\item for $i=3$, $D_4$ consists of refinement-dynamic axes, update relations,
support data, and rewrite operations satisfying the typing clauses of
\Cref{def:multi-axis-dynamic-structure,def:value-preserving-rewrite}.
\end{enumerate}
Assume in each case that the interpretations of all symbols already belonging
to $\Sigma_i$ are exactly those of $M_i$, and that every $\Sigma_i$-atomic
forcing clause is evaluated through the reduct. Then the expansion
\[
M_{i+1}:=M_i[D_{i+1}]
\]
is an admissible model in $\Mod_{i+1}$, satisfies
\[
U_{i+1,i}(M_{i+1})=M_i,
\]
and, with the state projection forgetting only the newly added layer
coordinates, preserves forcing of every $\mathbb L_i$-formula:
\[
M_{i+1},p\Vdash_{i+1}\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
M_i,\pi^{M_{i+1}}_{i+1,i}(p)\Vdash_i\varphi
\qquad(\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_i).
\]
Thus arbitrary finite pullback subsites, arbitrary local object functors on
those subsites, and arbitrary supplied banded prestack or stack-extension data
are admissible only in the generated model class defined by this proposition,
once they satisfy the displayed typing and theorem-level hypotheses. This is a
model-class convention for the expanded layers, not a semantic existence or
closure theorem for an independently restricted class of models. The realization
theorems below therefore have two parts: an algebraic construction of typed
site, presheaf, prestack, stackification, and cohomology data, followed by the
admissible-expansion step which declares those well-typed data to be an
\(\mathbb L_{i+1}\)-model without changing lower-layer forcing.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the expansion convention just fixed, $\Mod_{i+1}$ is exactly the class of
$\Sigma_{i+1}$-expansions of admissible $\Sigma_i$-models by the corresponding
well-typed layer data.  The hypotheses on $D_{i+1}$ therefore put
$M_i[D_{i+1}]$ in $\Mod_{i+1}$ and make its reduct under $U_{i+1,i}$ equal to
$M_i$.  The state projection is the projection specified for the adjacent
layer boundary and forgets only the new layer coordinates.  Since lower atomic
forcing clauses are assumed to be interpreted through the reduct, all
hypotheses of \Cref{lem:layer-reduct-induction} hold for the adjacent pair
$\mathbb L_i\preceq\mathbb L_{i+1}$.  Applying that lemma gives the displayed
forcing equivalence for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_i$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Concrete instantiation of the finite chain]\label{prop:chain-instantiation}
The chain
\[
\mathbb L_0\xhookrightarrow{\iota} \mathbb L_1\preceq \mathbb L_2\preceq \mathbb L_3\preceq \mathbb L_4
\]
is instantiated as follows.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=3em]
\item \textbf{$\mathbb L_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb L_1$ (pointwise states to information states: singleton semantic embedding).}
The language embedding
\[
e_{0,1}:\Sigma_0\hookrightarrow \Sigma_1
\]
is the inclusion of the underlying many-sorted symbols. In addition, $\Sigma_1$ contains the reference sorts $\mathsf{Ref}_s$ (one for each referenceable sort $s\in S_{\mathrm{ref}}$) together with their equality; no geometric predicates or site structure is imposed at this step. The forgetful functor
\[
U_{1,0}:\Mod_1\to\Mod_0
\]
forgets the information-state presentation and the reference carrier sets, remembering only the underlying many-sorted structure of $\Sigma_0$. A pointwise state $\rho$ in $\mathbb L_0$ is represented inside $\mathbb L_1$ by the singleton information state
\[
\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho):=(\Gamma,\{\rho\}).
\]
By \Cref{prop:singleton-conservativity},
\[
M,\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho)\Vdash_1\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{1,0}(M),\rho\Vdash_0\varphi
\]
for every $\mathbb L_0$-formula $\varphi$. This is a singleton semantic embedding in the sense of \Cref{def:singleton-semantic-embedding}: it preserves forcing of $\mathbb L_0$-formulas on the canonical singleton image but does not provide a state projection from arbitrary $\mathbb L_1$-states back to $\mathbb L_0$-states.

\item \textbf{$\mathbb L_1\leadsto\mathbb L_2$ (information states to local objects).}
The embedding
\[
e_{1,2}:\Sigma_1\hookrightarrow \Sigma_2
\]
adjoins the admissibility predicates, the local-object predicates $\LocSec_s$, $\CompSec_s$, $\Sec_s$, the site-structure symbols (covers, refinement preorder, pullbacks), and the associated geometric notation. The reference sorts $\mathsf{Ref}_s$ and their equality are already present in $\Sigma_1$; the step from $\mathbb L_1$ to $\mathbb L_2$ equips references with the geometric structure needed to state local-to-global questions. The forgetful functor
\[
U_{2,1}:\Mod_2\to\Mod_1
\]
forgets the local reference sites, covers, and local object functors but retains the reference sorts and their carrier sets. The state projection
\[
\pi^M_{2,1}:\St_2(M)\to \St_1(U_{2,1}(M))
\]
is the identity on the underlying information state $(\Gamma_p,R_p)$. Since the forcing clauses for formulas already in $\mathrm{Form}_1$ never inspect the added local-object data, one has
\[
M,p\Vdash_2\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{2,1}(M),\pi^M_{2,1}(p)\Vdash_1\varphi
\]
for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_1$.

\item \textbf{$\mathbb L_2\leadsto\mathbb L_3$ (local objects to obstruction data).}
The embedding
\[
e_{2,3}:\Sigma_2\hookrightarrow \Sigma_3
\]
adjoins the predicates and symbols used to talk about structural absence, visible branches, component gerbes, and obstruction classes. The forgetful functor
\[
U_{3,2}:\Mod_3\to\Mod_2
\]
drops the realization prestack, band, component-gerbe, and obstruction data. The state projection
\[
\pi^M_{3,2}:\St_3(M)\to \St_2(U_{3,2}(M))
\]
again leaves the underlying state unchanged. Formulas from $\mathrm{Form}_2$ are evaluated exactly as before, so
\[
M,p\Vdash_3\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{3,2}(M),\pi^M_{3,2}(p)\Vdash_2\varphi
\]
for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_2$.

\item \textbf{$\mathbb L_3\leadsto\mathbb L_4$ (obstruction data to refinement dynamics).}
The embedding
\[
e_{3,4}:\Sigma_3\hookrightarrow \Sigma_4
\]
adjoins the update relations, axis indices, support notions, and rewrite language of the refinement layer. The forgetful functor
\[
U_{4,3}:\Mod_4\to\Mod_3
\]
forgets the dynamic axes, update structure, and rewrite operations. The state projection
\[
\pi^M_{4,3}:\St_4(M)\to \St_3(U_{4,3}(M))
\]
forgets only the new dynamic bookkeeping. Because the forcing clauses for $\mathrm{Form}_3$ do not depend on that added bookkeeping, one has
\[
M,p\Vdash_4\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U_{4,3}(M),\pi^M_{4,3}(p)\Vdash_3\varphi
\]
for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_3$.
\end{enumerate}
The base boundary $\mathbb L_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb L_1$ is a singleton semantic embedding: pointwise states embed faithfully into information states, preserving the forcing of all $\mathbb L_0$-formulas on that canonical image. The three higher boundaries $\mathbb L_1\preceq\mathbb L_2\preceq\mathbb L_3\preceq\mathbb L_4$ are conservative extensions in the sense of \Cref{def:conservative-extension}, each with an explicit forgetful functor and state projection.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For the first boundary, the data displayed in \textup{(i)} give the language embedding
\[
e_{0,1}:\Sigma_0\hookrightarrow\Sigma_1,
\]
the forgetful functor $U_{1,0}$, and for each pointwise state $\rho$ the canonical singleton information state
\[
\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho)=(\Gamma,\{\rho\}).
\]
\Cref{prop:singleton-conservativity} gives, for every $\mathbb L_0$-formula $\varphi$,
\[
M,\iota_{0,1}^M(\rho)\Vdash_1\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{1,0}(M),\rho\Vdash_0\varphi.
\]
These are exactly the three pieces of structure and the preservation condition required in \Cref{def:singleton-semantic-embedding}. Notice that the verification uses only singleton information states; it does not assert a projection from arbitrary $\mathbb L_1$-states to pointwise states.

For $\mathbb L_1\preceq\mathbb L_2$, the displayed clauses fix the object language of the extension: $\Sigma_2$ is the disjoint expansion of $\Sigma_1$ by the local-object and site symbols listed in \textup{(ii)}, $\Mod_2$ is the class of $\Sigma_2$-expansions whose $\Sigma_1$-reduct is obtained by $U_{2,1}$, and $\pi^M_{2,1}$ is the identity on the underlying information state. Equivalently, this is the local-object instance of the admissible expansion interface of \Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}. Atomic $\Sigma_1$-formulas use only symbols retained by $U_{2,1}$. Thus \Cref{lem:layer-reduct-induction} gives, for every $\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_1$,
\[
M,p\Vdash_2\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{2,1}(M),\pi^M_{2,1}(p)\Vdash_1\varphi,
\]
which is \Cref{def:conservative-extension}.

The same lemma applies to $\mathbb L_2\preceq\mathbb L_3$. Here $\Sigma_3$ is the disjoint expansion of $\Sigma_2$ by the realization-prestack, band, component-gerbe, structural-absence, and obstruction-class symbols listed in \textup{(iii)}. The admissible models are precisely the corresponding well-typed expansions singled out in \Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}. The functor $U_{3,2}$ forgets precisely those symbols, while $\pi^M_{3,2}$ leaves the underlying state unchanged. Atomic $\Sigma_2$-formulas contain no symbols for the forgotten gerbe or obstruction data, so \Cref{lem:layer-reduct-induction} gives
\[
M,p\Vdash_3\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{3,2}(M),\pi^M_{3,2}(p)\Vdash_2\varphi
\qquad(\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_2).
\]
Thus $\mathbb L_2\preceq\mathbb L_3$.

Finally, $\Sigma_4$ is obtained from $\Sigma_3$ by adjoining only the refinement-dynamic axes, update structure, support notions, and rewrite operations listed in \textup{(iv)}. This is the refinement-dynamic instance of \Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}. The functor $U_{4,3}$ forgets exactly that dynamic structure, and $\pi^M_{4,3}$ forgets only the corresponding state bookkeeping. Atomic $\Sigma_3$-formulas are interpreted through the reduct, so \Cref{lem:layer-reduct-induction} yields
\[
M,p\Vdash_4\varphi
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{4,3}(M),\pi^M_{4,3}(p)\Vdash_3\varphi
\qquad(\varphi\in\mathrm{Form}_3).
\]
Consequently the three upper steps are conservative extensions, and the displayed finite chain has exactly the asserted form.
\end{proof}

Hence the genuinely conservative part of the chain begins at $\mathbb L_1$.
