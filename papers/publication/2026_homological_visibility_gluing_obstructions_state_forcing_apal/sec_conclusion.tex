\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Terminal rigidity shows that strictly pullback-stable lift representatives,
slice separatedness, terminal essential surjectivity, and vanishing
\(H^1\) force the terminal sheafification unit to be bijective and every
strict matching family to have a unique amalgamation.  Its proof separates
identity-valued descent from the subsequent Isom-torsor argument.

On a finite good-cover site with connected overlaps and a coherently
trivialized constant band, prescribed maps
\(H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A\) are realized simultaneously as the evaluations of
component-gerbe classes in one explicitly constructed prestack.  The same
construction fixes its component presheaf, terminal fibre, and neutral
labels.  For the selected open-star covers of
\(\bigvee_{j=1}^{\beta}S^2\), it realizes two complementary nonzero images
in a finite abelian group \(G\) exactly when
\[
 G\ne0,\qquad d(G)\le2\beta,\qquad
 G\text{ is not cyclic of prime-power order}.
\]
These conclusions are relative to the supplied finite site, band, prestack
lift, and component classes.
