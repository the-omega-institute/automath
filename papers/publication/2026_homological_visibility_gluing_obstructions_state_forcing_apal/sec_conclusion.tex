\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Component neutrality shows that strictly pullback-stable lift
representatives and slice separatedness force every terminal component gerbe
to be neutral.  Thus a nonzero component-gerbe class obstructs such a
representative system without any terminal essential-surjectivity or
\(H^1\)-vanishing hypothesis.  With terminal essential surjectivity and
vanishing \(H^1\), the terminal sheafification unit is bijective and every
strict matching family has a unique amalgamation.  The proof separates
identity-valued descent from the subsequent Isom-torsor argument.

On a finite good-cover site with connected overlaps and a coherently
trivialized constant band, prescribed maps
\(H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A\) are realized simultaneously as the evaluations of
component-gerbe classes in one explicitly constructed prestack.  The same
construction fixes its component presheaf, terminal fibre, and neutral
labels.  Without the \(H_1\)-vanishing hypothesis, arbitrary classes in
\(H^2(N,A)\) are still realized simultaneously.  Terminal essential
surjectivity then forces exactly an \(H^1(N,A)\)-torsor of source components
over each neutral label.  Nonzero pure-Ext classes yield matching without
amalgamation while all homological images vanish, so the aggregate quotients
do not detect the gluing obstruction.  For the selected open-star covers of
\(\bigvee_{j=1}^{\beta}S^2\), it realizes two complementary nonzero images
in a finite abelian group \(G\) exactly when
\[
 G\ne0,\qquad d(G)\le2\beta,\qquad
 G\text{ is not cyclic of prime-power order}.
\]
These conclusions are relative to the supplied finite site, band, prestack
lift, and component classes.
