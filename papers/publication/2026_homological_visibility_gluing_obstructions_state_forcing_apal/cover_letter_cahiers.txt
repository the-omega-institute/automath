Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration the manuscript "Finite-Site Component
Gerbes, Terminal Rigidity, and Prescribed Realization" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin
Zhang.

The article studies component gerbes arising from banded prestack lifts on
finite sites and the homological images carried by explicitly supplied Cech
2-cocycles. Its categorical starting point is Giraud's standard result that,
for any stack, the full substack over a global component section is a gerbe
(Cohomologie non abelienne, III, Proposition 2.1.5.3). The associated
neutrality/global-object equivalence and transport under compatible banded
equivalences are treated as standard gerbe theory rather than as new
constructions.

The paper's main constructed results are of two kinds. First, a
representative-rigidity theorem identifies conditions under which strictly
pullback-stable lift representatives and vanishing first cohomology force
terminal effectivity. Second, a self-contained finite-label prestack
construction realizes prescribed evaluation maps from H_2 of a finite nerve
to a finite abelian band, while simultaneously controlling the component
presheaf, terminal fibre, neutral labels, and matching without amalgamation.
The finite-nerve comparison is stated under the connected-overlap and
coherent coefficient-trivialization hypotheses needed to identify Cech
cochains with ordinary nerve cochains. Universal-coefficient naturality,
finite duality, and subgroup arithmetic are consolidated as standard inputs
to the resulting aggregate quotient calculation.

The manuscript's central objects are
finite sites, stacks, banded gerbes, Cech descent, and functorial comparison
of component and cohomological data. The article includes an internal
appendix containing the specified-comparison naturality statement used by
the homological images. A separate technical supplement contains extended
finite-group and Ext examples, a matrix workflow, and a narrow separation
example. The complete proofs of terminal rigidity and prescribed realization
are in the article itself.

The manuscript cites the authors' companion work for the overlapping
conditional gluing criterion.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
been approved by both authors. Thank you for considering it for publication
in Cahiers de Topologie et Geometrie Differentielle Categoriques.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma
Wenlin Zhang
