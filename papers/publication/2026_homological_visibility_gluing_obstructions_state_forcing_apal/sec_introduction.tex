\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Fix an integer $\beta\geq 1$.  Our main classification theorem concerns the
good-cover intersection site obtained from barycentric open-star covers of a
finite model of the wedge $\bigvee_{j=1}^{\beta}S^2$.  A nonzero finite
abelian group $G$ occurs as the kernel of the canonical map
\[
 A/\bigl(K_1\cap K_2\bigr)\longrightarrow A/\bigl(K_1+K_2\bigr)
\]
for two non-neutral component gerbes with $K_1\cap K_2=0$ and
$K_1+K_2=G$ if and only if
\[
 d(G)\leq 2\beta
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 G\text{ is not a cyclic }p\text{-group}.
\]
Here $d(G)$ is the minimal number of generators of $G$.  In the terminology
fixed below, this kernel is the \emph{component-forgetting kernel}; the
displayed special case is a two-component label-loss kernel with complementary
nonzero image subgroups.  The statement is an existence and classification
result for the chosen site, band, and lift data, not an invariant of an
arbitrary empirical model.

The standard input is as follows.  We use universal satisfaction on nonempty
teams of assignments, presheaves and their sheafifications, stackification of
categories fibred in groupoids, the classification of banded gerbes by
Giraud's $H^2$, the \v{C}ech-to-derived comparison on the specified Leray
covers, the universal coefficient theorem, and elementary structure theory
and duality for finite abelian groups
\cite{Giraud1971,Breen2006,Hatcher2002}.  Our contributions are the organization
of these inputs into the stated semantic framework; the comparison between
matching families and component gerbes under explicit lift hypotheses; the
presentation-comparison groupoid and the two aggregate quotients; the
component-forgetting exact sequence; the construction of prescribed
componentwise homological images; and the preceding finite-group
classification.

The relation with the Abramsky--Mansfield--Barbosa obstruction must be stated
at the outset.  Their obstruction is a relative \v{C}ech class
\[
 \gamma_e(s)\in
 \check H^1(\mathcal M,\mathcal F_{e,\overline C_0})
\]
of a presheaf, equivalently a torsor class indexed by a chosen local section
\cite{AbramskyMansfieldSoaresBarbosa2012,Caru2017}.  Our obstruction is the
derived Giraud class
\[
 \omega_v=[\mathfrak G_v]\in H^2(\mathcal C/a,\Band)
\]
of a gerbe banded by a sheaf, indexed by a global component section.  These
classes do not correspond.  What is functorial is only the support-level
comparison: every finite Abramsky--Brandenburger empirical model gives a
canonical split stack whose component gerbes are neutral.  In particular, a
strongly contextual model has no global component section from which our map
could produce its local-section-indexed AMB classes.  Consequently the
condition $d(G)\leq2\beta$ with $G$ not a cyclic $p$-group does not transfer to
bare empirical models; a band and a non-split banded stack lift would have to
be supplied as additional data.  This is proved precisely in
\Cref{thm:empirical-model-stack-interface,thm:unique-branch-contextuality-comparison}.

We now describe the framework.  An information state
$p=(\Gamma_p,R_p)$ is a nonempty team $R_p$ of assignments with common finite
context $\Gamma_p$.  We write $M,p\Vdash\varphi$ when every assignment in
$R_p$ satisfies $\varphi$ in the ordinary Tarskian sense.  Thus $\Vdash$ is
universal team satisfaction, not set-theoretic, Kripke, Beth, or
Kripke--Joyal forcing.  Refinement enlarges the context and may restrict the
team, which immediately gives persistence.  Point assignments enter through
the singleton-state embedding.  The later semantic frameworks add typed data
while preserving the truth of formulas in the reduct; we call these
truth-preserving reduct expansions because consequence conservativity is not asserted without an expansion
theorem for every lower model.

For an admissible parameter $r$ at $p$, the paper specifies a thin
reference-refinement site $\mathcal C_{p,s}$, its base object
$a=[r]_p$, and a presheaf of local sections
$F_{p,s}:\mathcal C_{p,s}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$.  A matching family
without an element of $F_{p,s}(a)$ is the set-valued failure studied here.
The forward implication from a matching family to a section of the
sheafification is formal; the converse holds when
$F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_{p,s}/a}$ is separated
(\Cref{prop:compsec-to-sheafification,thm:sheafification-characterization}).
The predicates expressing a matching family, a global section, and matching
without amalgamation are not definable in the automorphism-invariant,
constant-free, one-variable fragment of the lower reduct
(\Cref{thm:pointwise-irreducibility}).

Gerbe-valued obstructions require extra, noncanonical structure.  A
gluing-sensitive banded lift datum consists of a banded groupoid lift of $F$,
its stackification together with an identification of its component sheaf
with $aF$, essential surjectivity on the terminal fibre, and a
\v{C}ech-to-derived comparison hypothesis.  A section
$v\in\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)(a)\cong(aF)(a)$ determines a full component gerbe
$\mathfrak G_v$ and hence a Giraud class $\omega_v$.  Under the stated
separatedness, representability, and comparison hypotheses, matching without
amalgamation is equivalent to the existence of global component sections with
all corresponding component gerbes non-neutral
(\Cref{thm:component-gerbe-decomposition,thm:gerbe-null-semantics}).  A
strictly pullback-stable representative choice imposes a useful rigidity
condition: together with terminal-fibre essential surjectivity it forces the
sheafification unit to be onto at the terminal object
(\Cref{thm:canonical-lift-rigidity}).

Fix next a global component section, a finite nerve representative, and a
constant finite abelian band $A$.  The universal coefficient map evaluates
the class on second homology,
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\omega}:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow A.
\]
We write $K_\omega=\operatorname{Im}(\operatorname{ev}_\omega)$ for its
homological image and $Q_\omega=\operatorname{coker}(\operatorname{ev}_\omega)
=A/K_\omega$ for its homological cokernel.  These are invariants of an object
of the presentation-comparison groupoid and transport functorially along the
chosen comparison zigzags; no path independence is claimed.  A class with
zero $H_2$ evaluation is precisely a class in the $\operatorname{Ext}^1$
kernel of the universal coefficient sequence.  This separates the
homology-evaluation quotient from the more presentation-dependent quotient by
the subgroup generated by the coordinates of a chosen cocycle.

For finitely many component sections $v$, put $K_v=\operatorname{Im}
(\operatorname{ev}_{\omega_v})$.  Retaining the component label gives the
label-aware quotient $A/(\bigcap_vK_v)$; retaining only characters that work
for every component gives the common-character quotient
$A/(\sum_vK_v)$.  Their canonical comparison has kernel
\[
 \frac{\sum_vK_v}{\bigcap_vK_v}.
\]
The exact sequence and its finite-register interpretation are formal.  The
existence results are not: on the finite intersection site associated to a
connected good-cover nerve with $H_1=0$, we construct typed expansions whose
componentwise evaluation maps have prescribed images, including mixtures of
neutral and non-neutral component gerbes.  If $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\cong
\mathbb Z^\beta$, the attainable image subgroups are exactly those generated
by at most $\beta$ elements.  The main classification follows by asking when
$G$ decomposes as $K_1\oplus K_2$ with both summands nonzero and each generated
by at most $\beta$ elements.

The paper is organized as follows.  Sections~\ref{sec:preliminaries} and
\ref{sec:information-states} define the semantic frameworks and universal
team satisfaction.  Section~\ref{sec:null-decomposition} treats presheaves,
sheafification, and lower-fragment non-definability.  The component-gerbe and
Giraud-class results follow, then the finite-presentation and homological
quotients, their aggregation over component sections, the construction
theorems, the wedge-of-spheres classification, and the precise empirical-model
comparison.  The appendix records supplementary results on indexed
refinements and the GHZ/Mermin example.

\subsection*{Glossary of retained terminology}

The following short names are used only after their standard-language content
has been fixed.

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=2em,labelindent=0pt]
\item[Information state $p=(\Gamma,R)$.]
A nonempty team $R$ of assignments with common finite context $\Gamma$.
\item[Universal team satisfaction.]
$M,p\Vdash\varphi$ means that every assignment in the team of $p$ satisfies
$\varphi$ in ordinary Tarskian semantics.
\item[Singleton-state embedding.]
The map from an assignment to its singleton team, preserving ordinary
satisfaction.
\item[Truth-preserving reduct expansion.]
An expansion whose higher models preserve lower-language satisfaction after
the specified model reduct and state projection; consequence conservativity
is not part of the definition.
\item[Typed expansion class.]
The class of all supplied higher-layer data of the stipulated types over a
fixed lower model.
\item[Admissible parameter $r$.]
An element satisfying the primitive predicate $\Adm_s$; the predicate records
exactly that the site and presheaf data stipulated in the relevant typed model
are attached to $r$.
\item[{Base object $a=[r]_p$.}]
The object of the reference-refinement site associated to the pair $(p,r)$;
the brackets do not denote an equivalence class.
\item[Reference-refinement site $\mathcal C_{p,s}$.]
The thin category of base objects ordered by refinement, equipped with the
specified pretopology and pullbacks.
\item[Presheaf of local sections $F_{p,s}$.]
The functor $\mathcal C_{p,s}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$.
\item[Matching without amalgamation at $a$.]
A matching family exists on a cover of $a$, but $F_{p,s}(a)$ is empty.
\item[Gluing-sensitive banded lift datum.]
A banded groupoid lift, its stackification and component-sheaf identification,
terminal-fibre essential surjectivity, and a \v{C}ech-to-derived comparison
hypothesis.
\item[Pullback-stable representative choice.]
A choice of one representative of each presheaf section that is preserved
strictly by every pullback; ``representative-rigid'' abbreviates this condition.
\item[Global component section $v$.]
An element of $\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)(a)$, identified with a section of
$(aF)(a)$.
\item[Component gerbe $\mathfrak G_v$ and component obstruction $\omega_v$.]
The full banded substack over $v$ and its Giraud class
$[\mathfrak G_v]\in H^2(\mathcal C/a,\Band)$.
\item[Presentation-comparison groupoid.]
The groupoid freely generated by the specified refinements, cocycle
replacements, band retrivializations, and their formal inverses; distinct
zigzags need not give the same comparison.
\item[Coordinate-image subgroup and cocycle-coordinate quotient.]
For a chosen cocycle $\alpha$, the subgroup generated by its coordinate values
and the corresponding quotient $A/H_\alpha$.
\item[Homological image $K_\omega$ and homological cokernel $Q_\omega$.]
$\operatorname{Im}(\operatorname{ev}_\omega)$ and
$\operatorname{coker}(\operatorname{ev}_\omega)=A/K_\omega$.
\item[Label-aware quotient.]
$A/(\bigcap_vK_v)$, equivalently the image of $A$ in the product of the
componentwise homological cokernels.
\item[Common-character quotient.]
$A/(\sum_vK_v)$, detected by the characters that annihilate every $K_v$.
\item[Component-forgetting kernel.]
The kernel of the canonical map from the label-aware quotient to the
common-character quotient, namely $(\sum_vK_v)/(\bigcap_vK_v)$.
\item[Fibre-separating auxiliary register.]
A finite set used in an injective lift of a quotient map; its minimum
cardinality is the largest quotient-fibre cardinality.
\item[Finite intersection site associated to a cover nerve.]
The finite thin pullback site generated by the formal intersections indexed by
the nerve, with an initial object representing empty intersections.
\item[Typed readout.]
The partial output attached to an admissible parameter: inadmissible if no
typed data are attached, uniquely determined if the sheafified global section
is unique, and non-unique-or-absent otherwise.
\item[Indexed refinement system.]
A family of state-refinement relations indexed by information coordinates,
each monotone for the state preorder and the specified time order.
\item[Information-determining axis set.]
A finite set of refinement coordinates whose information values determine any
decided truth value among the states reachable along those coordinates.
\item[Pairwise commuting refinements and common refinement.]
Refinements for which every indicated finite family has a state refining all
its members.
\item[Readout-preserving rewrite.]
A rewrite between partial outputs that preserves both definedness and value.
\item[Banded groupoid lift of a support presheaf.]
A category fibred in groupoids whose presheaf of isomorphism classes is the
given empirical-model support presheaf and whose automorphism sheaves are the
specified band.
\end{description}
