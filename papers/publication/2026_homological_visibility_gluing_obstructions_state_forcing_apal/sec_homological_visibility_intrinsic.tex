\section{Homological images and aggregate quotients}
\label{sec:homological-images}

Fix a finite simplicial complex \(N\), a finite abelian group \(A\), and
\(\omega\in H^2(N,A)\).  Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:homological-image-cokernel}
 K_\omega:=\operatorname{Im}\bigl(
 H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\operatorname{ev}_\omega}A\bigr),
 \qquad Q_\omega:=A/K_\omega.
\end{equation}
For a nonempty finite family \((\omega_v)_{v\in V}\), put
\(K_v=K_{\omega_v}\) and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aggregate-quotients}
 Q_{\mathrm{lab}}:=A\Big/\bigcap_{v\in V}K_v,
 \qquad
 Q_{\mathrm{com}}:=A\Big/\sum_{v\in V}K_v.
\end{equation}
Thus the application chain is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \text{supplied gerbe class}&\longmapsto K_\omega
 \longmapsto Q_{\mathrm{lab}}\longrightarrow Q_{\mathrm{com}}\\
 &\longmapsto\text{rank condition}
 \longmapsto\text{wedge realization}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The definitions are attached to the supplied finite presentation.  Their
naturality under a specified comparison is proved in
Appendix~\ref{sec:presentation-naturality}.

\begin{proposition}[UCT and aggregate subgroup package]
\label{prop:uct-aggregate-package}
With the notation above:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item The universal coefficient sequence is
      \[
      0\longrightarrow
      \operatorname{Ext}^1(H_1(N,\mathbb Z),A)
      \longrightarrow H^2(N,A)
      \xrightarrow{\operatorname{ev}}
      \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(N,\mathbb Z),A)
      \longrightarrow0.
      \]
      Thus \(K_\omega=0\) exactly when \(\omega\) lies in the displayed
      Ext subgroup.  In particular, nonzero classes can have zero
      homological image when \(H_1\) has torsion.
\item The quotient map \(q_\omega:A\to Q_\omega\) is initial among group
      homomorphisms \(q:A\to B\) satisfying
      \(q\circ\operatorname{ev}_\omega=0\).  A character
      \(\chi:A\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) factors through \(Q_\omega\)
      exactly when \(\chi(K_\omega)=0\).  Since finite Pontryagin duality
      separates points,
      \[
      K_\omega=\mathop{\bigcap}_{\chi(K_\omega)=0}\ker\chi.
      \]
\item There is a natural short exact sequence
      \begin{equation}\label{eq:aggregate-exact-sequence}
      0\longrightarrow
      \frac{\sum_vK_v}{\bigcap_vK_v}
      \longrightarrow Q_{\mathrm{lab}}
      \longrightarrow Q_{\mathrm{com}}
      \longrightarrow0.
      \end{equation}
      Moreover,
      \[
      \widehat{Q_{\mathrm{com}}}
      \cong\bigcap_{v\in V}K_v^\perp,
      \qquad
      \widehat{Q_{\mathrm{lab}}}
      \cong\sum_{v\in V}K_v^\perp
      \]
      inside \(\widehat A\).
\item If \(H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0\) and
      \(H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^\beta\), a subgroup
      \(K\le A\) occurs as \(K_\omega\) exactly when \(d(K)\le\beta\).
      Every finite family of such subgroups is simultaneously attained by
      the prestack of
      \Cref{thm:prescribed-component-image-construction}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Clause (i) is the cohomological universal coefficient theorem
\cite[Sec.~3.1]{Hatcher2002}; its right-hand map is evaluation on integral
\(2\)-cycles, so exactness gives the assertion about \(K_\omega\).

For (ii), \(q\circ\operatorname{ev}_\omega=0\) precisely when \(q\)
vanishes on \(K_\omega\), which is the universal property of
\(A/K_\omega\).  The same argument applies to characters.  If
\(a\notin K_\omega\), finite Pontryagin duality supplies a character of
\(A/K_\omega\) nonzero on the class of \(a\); its pullback separates
\(a\) from the displayed intersection \cite{Terras1999}.

For (iii), the inclusion
\(\bigcap_vK_v\subseteq\sum_vK_v\) induces the displayed quotient map,
whose kernel is \((\sum_vK_v)/(\bigcap_vK_v)\) by the third isomorphism
theorem.  Finite Pontryagin duality sends quotients to annihilators and
exchanges finite sums and intersections, giving the two dual formulas.

Under the hypotheses of (iv), UCT gives
\[
 H^2(N,A)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z^\beta,A).
\]
An image of \(\mathbb Z^\beta\) has at most \(\beta\) generators.
Conversely, if \(K=\langle k_1,\dots,k_r\rangle\) with \(r\le\beta\), send
the first \(r\) basis vectors to the displayed generators and the remaining
ones to zero.  Applying
\Cref{thm:prescribed-component-image-construction} to any finite family of
these maps realizes the family simultaneously.
\end{proof}

The Ext term records the precise limit of homological visibility.  For
example, suppose
\[
 H_1(N,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
 \qquad H_2(N,\mathbb Z)=0,
 \qquad A=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z.
\]
Then
\[
 H^2(N,A)\cong
 \operatorname{Ext}^1(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)
 \cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z.
\]
The nonzero class defines a non-neutral banded gerbe, but its evaluation map
has zero domain, so \(K_\omega=0\) and \(Q_\omega=A\).  Evaluation on
\(H_2\) therefore does not detect this gerbe class.
