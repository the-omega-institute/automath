\begin{abstract}
This technical appendix contains material auxiliary to the article
\emph{Finite-Site Component Gerbes and Homological Images}.  It records the
freely generated presentation-comparison groupoid and the corresponding UCT
naturality calculation, gives finite-group and Ext-only examples, and states
a narrow lower-language separation example that motivates treating lift data
as supplied input.  The article itself contains the complete proofs of
terminal rigidity and simultaneous prescribed realization.
\end{abstract}
