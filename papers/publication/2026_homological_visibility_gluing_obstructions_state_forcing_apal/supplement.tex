\begin{abstract}
This document contains the supporting material relocated from the article:
the presheaf, sheafification, banded-lift, terminal-rigidity, typed-readout,
and lower-fragment packages; the cocycle-coordinate strictification results;
the finite-site constructions realizing prescribed and mixed componentwise
homological images; and the refinement-dynamics and GHZ/Mermin appendices.
The article retains the component-gerbe criterion, intrinsic homological
quotients, aggregation exact sequence, wedge-of-spheres classification, and
empirical-model comparison.  Cross-document citations are descriptive and do
not import either document's auxiliary file.
\end{abstract}
