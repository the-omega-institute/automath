\section{Unrestricted realization and terminal multiplicity}
\label{sec:unrestricted-realization}

The homological hypothesis in
\Cref{thm:prescribed-component-image-construction} makes evaluation an
isomorphism and collapses the terminal torsor.  Neither condition is needed
to realize prescribed cohomology classes.  Without that hypothesis, however,
terminal essential surjectivity forces every neutral component of the source
to retain all global torsor twists.  The following theorem gives the resulting
sharp form of the construction.

\begin{theorem}[Unrestricted simultaneous realization and forced terminal
multiplicity]
\label{thm:unrestricted-component-class-realization}
Let \(\mathcal U=\{U_i\}_{i\in I}\) be a finite good cover satisfying
\eqref{eq:connected-overlap-hypothesis}, with connected nerve \(N\), and let
\(A\) be a finite abelian group.  Let \(\Lambda\) be a finite nonempty set,
and prescribe arbitrary classes
\[
 \omega_\lambda\in H^2(N,A)
 \qquad(\lambda\in\Lambda).
\]
Put \(\Lambda_0=\{\lambda:\omega_\lambda=0\}\) and
\(T=H^1(N,A)\).  There are a presheaf \(F\) on
\(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}\) and an
\(A_{\mathcal U}\)-banded prestack lift \(\mathfrak P\), with
stackification \(\mathfrak L\), such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item on objects of the finite intersection site,
      \[
      F(a)=\coprod_{\lambda\in\Lambda_0}T,
      \qquad
      F(W)=\Lambda\quad
      (W\ne a,\varnothing),
      \qquad
      F(\varnothing)=\{*\},
      \]
      and the restriction of \((\lambda,t)\in F(a)\) to a nonempty
      proper intersection is \(\lambda\).  Moreover,
      \[
      (aF)(a)\cong\pi_0(\mathfrak L)(a)\cong\Lambda;
      \]
\item the component gerbe indexed by \(\lambda\) has Giraud class exactly
      \(\omega_\lambda\);
\item \(\mathfrak P(a)\to\mathfrak L(a)\) is essentially surjective, and
      the marking map on terminal source components has fibre
      \[
      H^1(N,A)
      \]
      over every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_0\).  This multiplicity is forced:
      for any terminally essentially surjective
      \(A_{\mathcal U}\)-banded prestack with the same marked component
      classes, the fibre over each neutral label is a principal homogeneous
      set under \(H^1(N,A)\).  Consequently, a finite marked set
      \(m:E\to\Lambda\) occurs as the terminal source-component set of such
      a realization if and only if
      \[
      m^{-1}(\lambda)=\varnothing
      \quad(\lambda\notin\Lambda_0),
      \qquad
      |m^{-1}(\lambda)|=|H^1(N,A)|
      \quad(\lambda\in\Lambda_0);
      \]
\item \(F\) has a strict matching family on \(\mathcal U\), and
      \[
      \operatorname{MWA}(F,a)
      \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
      \Lambda_0=\varnothing;
      \]
\item if \(\Lambda_0\ne\varnothing\), then \(F\) is separated on the slice
      over \(a\) exactly when \(H^1(N,A)=0\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By \Cref{prop:finite-site-comparison}, for every degree \(q\) there are
natural identifications
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unrestricted-site-nerve-comparison}
 H^q(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})
 \cong H^q(N,A).
\end{equation}
Choose a normalized simplicial cocycle
\(\alpha_\lambda\in Z^2(N,A)\) representing each
\(\omega_\lambda\), with \(\alpha_\lambda=0\) when
\(\lambda\in\Lambda_0\).

For \(\lambda\notin\Lambda_0\), use the twisted summand constructed in the
proof of \Cref{thm:prescribed-component-image-construction}: its terminal
fibre is empty, and on a nonempty proper intersection \(W\) its objects are
\(x_i^{\lambda,W}\), \(i\in I(W)\), with arrow coordinate \(b\in A\) and
composition
\[
 (c,j,k)\circ(b,i,j)
 =(b+c+\alpha_{\lambda,ijk},i,k).
\]
The cocycle identity is precisely associativity, restriction preserves the
formula, and the Isom presheaves are restrictions of
\(A_{\mathcal U}\).  Thus this is an
\(A_{\mathcal U}\)-banded prestack whose stackification has component class
\(\omega_\lambda\).  None of these verifications uses
\(H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0\).

For \(\lambda\in\Lambda_0\), let the corresponding summand be the stack
\(\mathfrak T\) of \(A_{\mathcal U}\)-torsors.  Its component gerbe is
neutral and its terminal isomorphism classes are
\[
 \pi_0(\mathfrak T(a))
 \cong H^1(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})
 \cong H^1(N,A)=T.
\]
For a nonempty proper intersection \(W\), the slice acyclicity in
\Cref{prop:finite-site-comparison} gives
\[
 H^1(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}/W,A_{\mathcal U})=0.
\]
Hence \(\mathfrak T(W)\) is connected, while over \(\varnothing\) it is the
terminal groupoid.

On each nonempty object take the coproduct of the summands indexed by the
labels present there, and use one shared terminal groupoid over
\(\varnothing\).  Restriction preserves the label unless its target is
\(\varnothing\), where it is unique.  The Isom presheaf between objects with
the same label is a sheaf by the preceding construction.  Between distinct
labels it is empty on nonempty objects and a singleton on \(\varnothing\),
which is also a sheaf on the relevant slice.  The automorphism sheaves are
\(A_{\mathcal U}\).  The resulting fibred groupoid \(\mathfrak P\) is
therefore an \(A_{\mathcal U}\)-banded prestack.

Its presheaf of fibrewise components is the displayed presheaf \(F\).  A
terminal torsor class becomes trivial on every member of a sufficiently fine
cover, so all members of the copy of \(T\) indexed by one neutral label have
the same image after sheafification.  Distinct labels remain distinct on
every nonempty cover member.  Conversely, a matching family on the vertex
cover is constant along the connected \(1\)-skeleton of \(N\).  It follows
that
\[
 (aF)(a)\cong\Lambda.
\]
Compatibility of stackification with components gives the other isomorphism
in (i).

For a nonzero label, the local objects
\(x_i^{\lambda,U_i}\) and the arrows \((0,i,j)\) have triple-overlap defect
\(\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}\), by the calculation in
\eqref{eq:twisted-composition}.  Its component gerbe therefore has class
\(\omega_\lambda\) under
\eqref{eq:unrestricted-site-nerve-comparison}.  A neutral summand is the
torsor stack itself and has class zero.  This proves (ii).

Stackification restricts on the full subprestack with a fixed label to the
stackification of that summand: descent arrows cannot cross labels, because
distinct labels remain distinct in the component sheaf.  The neutral
summands are already stacks and contain every terminal
\(A_{\mathcal U}\)-torsor, whereas a nonzero component gerbe has no terminal
object.  Every object of \(\mathfrak L(a)\) therefore comes from
\(\mathfrak P(a)\), proving essential surjectivity and the asserted fibre
description for this construction.  For any other terminally essentially
surjective realization, apply
\Cref{thm:marked-terminal-torsor-form} and then
\eqref{eq:unrestricted-site-nerve-comparison}: the source isomorphism classes
over a neutral label form an \(H^1(N,A)\)-torsor.  This proves the sharpness
claim and the necessity of the cardinality criterion in (iii).  Conversely,
if a finite marked set satisfies that criterion, choose bijections from the
terminal component fibres of the constructed prestack to the corresponding
fibres of \(E\).  Using these bijections as the terminal component marking
identifies its restriction map with \(m\), proving sufficiency.

Assigning one label to every vertex gives a strict matching family.  The
terminal set \(F(a)\) is nonempty exactly when \(\Lambda_0\) is nonempty,
which proves (iv).  Finally, all proper slices are separated because their
generated covers contain an identity.  At \(a\), two distinct elements of
the same copy of \(T\) agree after restriction to a cover, so separatedness
fails when \(T\ne0\); when \(T=0\), the map
\(F(a)=\Lambda_0\to\Lambda\) is injective and separatedness holds.  This
proves (v).
\end{proof}

The theorem realizes the complete cohomology class, rather than only its
evaluation on integral \(2\)-cycles.  This yields an exact failure criterion
for the homological images of \Cref{sec:homological-images}.

\begin{corollary}[Pure-Ext gluing obstructions invisible to all homological
images]
\label{cor:pure-ext-unrestricted-realization}
Let \(\mathcal U\), \(N\), and \(A\) satisfy the hypotheses of
\Cref{thm:unrestricted-component-class-realization}.  The following are
equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item \(\operatorname{Ext}^1(H_1(N,\mathbb Z),A)\ne0\);
\item there is a one-label terminally essentially surjective realization for
      which \(F\) has matching without amalgamation, the unique component
      gerbe is non-neutral, and its homological image is zero.
\end{enumerate}
In this realization
\[
 K_\omega=0,
 \qquad Q_{\mathrm{lab}}=Q_{\mathrm{com}}=A,
\]
and the aggregate comparison is the identity, although the component gerbe
has no terminal object.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The universal coefficient sequence identifies
\(\operatorname{Ext}^1(H_1(N,\mathbb Z),A)\) with the kernel of
\[
 H^2(N,A)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(N,\mathbb Z),A).
\]
If this kernel is nonzero, choose a nonzero class \(\omega\) in it and apply
\Cref{thm:unrestricted-component-class-realization} with one label.  Its
homological image is zero, while nonzero class means non-neutrality and makes
\(\Lambda_0\) empty; clause (iv) gives matching without amalgamation.  The
quotient identities follow from \eqref{eq:aggregate-quotients}.  Conversely,
a non-neutral class with zero homological image is a nonzero element of the
displayed kernel, so the Ext group is nonzero.
\end{proof}

Gerbe classification and cocycle realization are standard
\cite[Chap.~IV]{Giraud1971}\cite{Moerdijk2002}.  Pure-Ext classes also occur
as lifting obstructions in a different setting
\cite[Sec.~7]{NeebWagemannWockel2013}.  The result here is the simultaneous
marked finite-site realization, its forced terminal multiplicity, and the
exact blind spot for the aggregate invariants.
