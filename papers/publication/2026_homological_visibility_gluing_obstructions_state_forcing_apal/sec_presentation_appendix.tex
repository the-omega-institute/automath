\section{Naturality for specified presentation comparisons}
\label{sec:presentation-naturality}

Fix a finite abelian group \(A\).  A finite presentation is a pair
\[
 (N,\omega),
 \qquad N\text{ a finite simplicial complex},\quad
 \omega\in H^2(N,A),
\]
with evaluation and image
\[
 e_\omega:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow A,
 \qquad K_\omega=\operatorname{Im}(e_\omega).
\]
The comparison data used in the article are generated by three moves:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item a retrivialization replaces a normalized cocycle \(\alpha\) by
      \(\alpha+\delta b\) on the same nerve;
\item a simplicial isomorphism \(f:N'\to N\) carries
      \((N,\omega)\) to \((N',f^*\omega)\);
\item a coefficient isomorphism \(\theta:A\to A'\) carries
      \((N,\omega)\) to \((N,\theta_*\omega)\).
\end{enumerate}
Let \(\mathscr P\) be the groupoid freely generated by specified invertible
instances of these moves, modulo identities, inverses, and literal
compositions of the underlying maps.  A morphism therefore retains its
chosen comparison word.

\begin{proposition}[Naturality along a specified presentation comparison]
\label{prop:presentation-comparison-naturality}
Let a morphism in \(\mathscr P\) be represented by a simplicial isomorphism
\(f:N'\to N\), a coefficient isomorphism \(\theta:A\to A'\), and any number
of retrivializations, so that
\[
 \omega'=\theta_*f^*\omega\in H^2(N',A').
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:presentation-evaluation-square}
\begin{CD}
H_2(N',\mathbb Z) @>{e_{\omega'}}>> A'\\
@V{f_*}V{\cong}V @A{\theta}A{\cong}A\\
H_2(N,\mathbb Z) @>{e_\omega}>> A
\end{CD}
\end{equation}
commutes.  Consequently
\[
 K_{\omega'}=\theta(K_\omega),
 \qquad A'/K_{\omega'}\cong A/K_\omega.
\]
These identifications are functorial in the chosen comparison word.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The universal coefficient map is natural in the simplicial complex and the
coefficient group.  For \(z\in H_2(N',\mathbb Z)\),
\[
 e_{\omega'}(z)
 =e_{\theta_*f^*\omega}(z)
 =\theta\bigl(e_\omega(f_*z)\bigr),
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:presentation-evaluation-square}.  Since \(f_*\) and
\(\theta\) are isomorphisms, taking images gives
\(K_{\omega'}=\theta(K_\omega)\); the quotient isomorphism sends
\(a+K_\omega\) to \(\theta(a)+K_{\omega'}\).

A retrivialization changes a cocycle by a coboundary and hence fixes its
cohomology class.  The calculation therefore applies to every generator,
and pasting the squares proves functoriality along the recorded word.
\end{proof}

Applying the proposition componentwise transports sums and intersections of
the \(K_\omega\), hence also \(Q_{\mathrm{lab}}\),
\(Q_{\mathrm{com}}\), and the exact sequence
\eqref{eq:aggregate-exact-sequence}.
