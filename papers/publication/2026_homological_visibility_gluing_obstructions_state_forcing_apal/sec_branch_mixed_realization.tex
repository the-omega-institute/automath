\begin{theorem}[Mixed realization of neutral and obstructed branches on a formal \v{C}ech site]\label{thm:mixed-branch-realization}
Let
\[
\mathcal U=\{U_i\to a\}_{i\in I}
\]
be a finite good-cover presentation in a pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$ whose \v{C}ech nerve
\[
N:=N(\mathcal U)
\]
is connected. Equivalently, the realization of the formal cover is a
paracompact space homotopy equivalent to $|N|$ and all nonempty finite
intersections of members of $\mathcal U$ are acyclic for constant
coefficients. Let $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ be the formal \v{C}ech site of
\Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}. Let $A$ be a finite abelian group, and let
$A_{\mathcal U}$ denote the genuine constant sheaf on the formal
\v{C}ech site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ whose value on each nonempty
basic intersection is identified with $A$. View $A_{\mathcal U}$ as the
automorphism band of the realization prestack constructed below, and
assume
\[
H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
Use the Leray comparison isomorphism of
\Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(iv). The model-existence conclusion below is relative to
the deliberately broad well-typed admissible-expansion semantics of
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}. Let $\Lambda$ be a finite nonempty set, and let
\[
\phi_\lambda:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A
\]
be a homomorphism for each $\lambda\in\Lambda$. Define
\[
\Lambda_0:=\{\lambda\in\Lambda\mid \phi_\lambda=0\}.
\]
Then there exist a one-state
admissible $\mathbb L_2/\mathbb L_3$ expansion $M$ with state $p$, a distinguished admitted reference
$r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ with $[r]_p=a$, a local slice site for $r$ equal to
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, a local object functor $F_{p,s}$ on
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and a realization prestack
\[
\mathfrak P_r\to \mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
with band $A_{\mathcal U}$ such that, for $\mathfrak L_r:=a(\mathfrak P_r)$, the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong \Lambda.
\]
\item Under the identification in \textup{(i)}, for each $\lambda\in\Lambda$ the corresponding component gerbe $\mathfrak G_\lambda\subseteq \mathfrak L_r$ has branch obstruction class
\[
\omega_\lambda\in H^2(N,A)
\]
satisfying
\[
\mathrm{ev}_{\omega_\lambda}=\phi_\lambda,
\qquad
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathfrak G_\lambda\ \text{is neutral}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\phi_\lambda=0.
\]
\item $\mathfrak P_r$ is globally conservative at $a$. More precisely, if $\Lambda_0=\varnothing$ this is the vacuous terminal-fibre case
\[
\mathfrak P_r(a)=\varnothing=\mathfrak L_r(a),
\]
while if $\Lambda_0\neq\varnothing$ the terminal objects of $\mathfrak L_r(a)$ lie exactly in the neutral components indexed by $\Lambda_0$ and are in the essential image of $\mathfrak P_r(a)$. In either case
\[
M,p\Vdash \Sec_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_0\neq\varnothing.
\]
\item
\[
M,p\Vdash \CompSec_s(r),
\]
and
\[
M,p\Vdash \Null^{\mathrm{glue}}_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_0=\varnothing.
\]
\item The aggregate visibility quotients satisfy
\[
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{br}}(r)\cong A\Big/\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda),
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{unif}}(r)\cong A\Big/\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since
\[
H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0,
\]
the universal coefficient sequence reduces to an isomorphism
\[
\eta_A:H^2(N,A)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{Hom}(H_2(N,\mathbb Z),A).
\]
For each $\lambda\in\Lambda$, let
\[
\omega_\lambda\in H^2(N,A)
\]
be the unique class satisfying
\[
\eta_A(\omega_\lambda)=\phi_\lambda,
\]
and choose a normalized simplicial cocycle
\[
\alpha_\lambda\in Z^2(N,A)
\]
representing $\omega_\lambda$, with the alternating ordered-triple extension
of \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}. In particular,
\[
\lambda\in\Lambda_0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\omega_\lambda=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\alpha_\lambda=0.
\]

By \Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(i)--(iv), pass to the
formal \v{C}ech site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ appearing in the statement.
The cover $\mathcal U$, its nerve, and the constant-band finite cochain
data are the same, and Leray comparison identifies
$H^2(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})$ with $H^2(N,A)$. The derived branch class
used below is therefore the actual derived class corresponding to the
chosen finite-nerve cocycle.

Define the local object functor
\[
F_{p,s}:\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}^{op}\to\mathsf{Set}
\]
by
\[
F_{p,s}(U):=\Lambda
\qquad
\text{for every nonempty proper intersection object }U\to a,
\]
\[
F_{p,s}(a):=\Lambda_0,
\]
and
\[
F_{p,s}(\emptyset):=\{\ast\}.
\]
Restrictions between nonempty proper intersection objects are identities,
the restriction maps
\[
F_{p,s}(a)\to F_{p,s}(U)
\]
are the inclusions $\Lambda_0\hookrightarrow \Lambda$, and every
restriction to $\emptyset$ is the unique map.

Because $N$ is connected, a matching family for $F_{p,s}$ on the cover $\mathcal U$ is exactly a constant label
\[
\lambda\in\Lambda.
\]
Two such constant families with distinct labels remain distinct after every common refinement, because all restrictions on proper intersections are the identity on the label set $\Lambda$. Therefore
\[
\bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda.
\]
As above, compatibility on nonempty overlaps forces equality of adjacent labels, and connectivity of the nerve makes every matching family constant. Distinct constants remain distinct under every common refinement because the restriction maps on proper intersections preserve the label.

For each $\lambda\in\Lambda\setminus\Lambda_0$, define a category fibred in groupoids
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda\to \mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
exactly as in the proof of \Cref{thm:prescribed-branch-realization}: on the terminal fibre set
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda(a):=\varnothing,
\]
and on each nonempty proper formal intersection object $W$, with
\[
I(W):=\{i\in I\mid W\to U_i\},
\]
let the fibre $\mathfrak P_\lambda(W)$ have object set
\[
\{x_i^{(\lambda,W)}\mid i\in I(W)\},
\]
morphism sets equal to $A$, and composition twisted by the cocycle $\alpha_\lambda$. Over $\emptyset$ take the terminal groupoid, with unique restrictions to it. The argument of \Cref{thm:prescribed-branch-realization} shows that $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is a prestack with genuine constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$, that
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_\lambda)(U)\cong\{ \lambda\}
\]
for every nonempty proper intersection object $U\to a$, that
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_\lambda)(a)=\varnothing,
\]
that the fibre over $\emptyset$ is terminal, and that the \v{C}ech cocycle attached to the branch is exactly $\alpha_\lambda$.

For each $\lambda\in\Lambda_0$, define a stack in groupoids
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda\to \mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
by taking, for every nonempty object $U$ of $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, a one-object groupoid with distinguished object $y_U^{(\lambda)}$ and automorphism group $A_{\mathcal U}(U)$, by taking the terminal groupoid over $\emptyset$, and by taking every restriction between nonempty objects to be the restriction map of the genuine constant sheaf on the distinguished object and on automorphisms. Restrictions to $\emptyset$ are unique. Then $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is already a stack with genuine constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$,
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_\lambda)(U)\cong\{\lambda\}
\]
for every nonempty object $U$ of $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, has terminal component set over $\emptyset$, and the \v{C}ech cocycle on the cover $\mathcal U$ is identically zero.

Now set
\[
\mathfrak P_r:=\coprod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\mathfrak P_\lambda.
\]
As in \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}, this is the fibrewise
coproduct over nonempty objects with the shared terminal fibre over
$\emptyset$.
By \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}, this coproduct is a prestack with genuine constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$: the nonzero summands are the twisted groupoids determined by $\alpha_\lambda$, the zero summands are neutral $A_{\mathcal U}$-gerbes, the restriction maps are strictly functorial, and the Cech cocycle of the $\lambda$-component is $\alpha_\lambda$. Since distinct summands have no morphisms between them, its presheaf of isomorphism classes is
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(U)\cong \Lambda
\qquad
\text{for every nonempty proper intersection object }U\to a,
\]
and
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(a)\cong \Lambda_0.
\]
Over $\emptyset$ the shared terminal fibre gives
$\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(\emptyset)\cong\{\ast\}=F_{p,s}(\emptyset)$.
Hence
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\cong F_{p,s}.
\]
By \Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(v)--(vi), the functor
$F_{p,s}$ and the prestack $\mathfrak P_r$ determine a one-state semantic
model $M$ with admitted reference $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ at its unique state $p$,
with reference class $[r]_p=a$, local slice site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$,
local object functor $F_{p,s}$, and realization prestack $\mathfrak P_r$ with
genuine constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$. This is again the admissible one-state
$\mathbb L_2/\mathbb L_3$ expansion supplied by
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}, not an appeal to an ambient
free-model-existence principle.
The construction has now supplied the same interfaces as in the prescribed case: admitted reference $r$, formal \v{C}ech site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, local object functor $F_{p,s}$, genuine constant sheaf band $A_{\mathcal U}$, the Leray comparison isomorphism for the cover $\mathcal U$, visible classes obtained from sheafified terminal sections, and forcing clauses determined by compatible local sections and actual terminal sections.
Applying \Cref{prop:stackification-components} gives
\[
\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\cong a_{p,s}F_{p,s},
\]
so
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong \bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda.
\]
This proves \textup{(i)}.

For each $\lambda\in\Lambda\setminus\Lambda_0$, the same calculation as in \Cref{thm:prescribed-branch-realization} shows that the component gerbe $\mathfrak G_\lambda$ has branch obstruction class
\[
\omega_\lambda=[\alpha_\lambda]
\]
and therefore
\[
\mathrm{ev}_{\omega_\lambda}=\eta_A(\omega_\lambda)=\phi_\lambda,
\qquad
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
For these nonzero branches the finite-nerve comparison again uses the same good cover $\mathcal U$, the constant band $A$, and the Leray comparison class of the normalized cocycle $\alpha_\lambda$.
For each $\lambda\in\Lambda_0$, the stack $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is already a neutral $A$-banded gerbe on $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and choosing the objects $y_{U_i}^{(\lambda)}$ together with the identity isomorphisms on overlaps yields the zero \v{C}ech $2$-cocycle. Hence
\[
\omega_\lambda=0,
\qquad
\mathrm{ev}_{\omega_\lambda}=0=\phi_\lambda,
\qquad
K_\lambda=0=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
The neutrality criterion in \Cref{prop:derived-branch-classification} therefore gives
\[
\mathfrak G_\lambda\ \text{is neutral}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\omega_\lambda=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\phi_\lambda=0.
\]
This proves \textup{(ii)}.

The identity
\[
F_{p,s}(a)=\Lambda_0
\]
immediately gives
\[
M,p\Vdash \Sec_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_0\neq\varnothing.
\]
It remains to prove global conservativity at $a$. If $\lambda\in\Lambda_0$, then $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is already a stack, so the restriction of the stackification morphism to the fibre over $a$ is an equivalence on that summand. If $\lambda\notin\Lambda_0$, then $\phi_\lambda\neq 0$, hence $\omega_\lambda\neq 0$, and \Cref{prop:derived-branch-classification} implies that the component gerbe $\mathfrak G_\lambda$ is non-neutral. Therefore
\[
\mathfrak G_\lambda(a)=\varnothing
\qquad
(\lambda\notin\Lambda_0)
\]
by \Cref{lem:terminal-fibre-neutrality}. Consequently every object of $\mathfrak L_r(a)$ lies in a zero-branch component and is therefore in the essential image of $\mathfrak P_r(a)$. Thus $\mathfrak P_r$ is globally conservative at $a$. This proves \textup{(iii)}.

Since $\Lambda\neq\varnothing$, the identification
\[
\bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda
\]
shows that
\[
\bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\neq\varnothing.
\]
By \Cref{thm:sheafification-characterization},
\[
M,p\Vdash \CompSec_s(r).
\]
Using \textup{(iii)} and the definition
\[
\Null^{\mathrm{glue}}_s(r):=\Adm_s(r)\land \CompSec_s(r)\land \neg\Sec_s(r),
\]
one gets
\[
M,p\Vdash \Null^{\mathrm{glue}}_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_0=\varnothing.
\]
This proves \textup{(iv)}.

Finally, \textup{(v)} is \Cref{thm:branch-sensitive-visible-quotient,thm:branch-uniform-visible-quotient} applied to the family
\[
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda)
\qquad
(\lambda\in\Lambda).
\qedhere
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact subgroup classification on torsion-free second homology]\label{cor:torsion-free-h2-classification}
Assume the full hypothesis package of \Cref{thm:mixed-branch-realization}: a
fixed finite nerve $N=N(\mathcal U)$ in a formal \v{C}ech good-cover site,
a trivialized constant finite abelian band $A$, the Leray comparison
isomorphism for the finite-nerve classes, the constructed realization
prestack, and global conservativity at the terminal fibre. Assume
in addition that
\[
H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z^\beta
\]
for some integer $\beta\ge 0$. Let $(K_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be a family of subgroups of $A$. Then the following are equivalent.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item There exist a state $p$, an admitted reference $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ on the fixed nerve $N$, and a bijection
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong \Lambda
\]
such that the branch indexed by $\lambda$ has intrinsic hidden subgroup
\[
K_\lambda.
\]
\item Every subgroup $K_\lambda$ is generated by at most $\beta$ elements.
\end{enumerate}
When these conditions hold,
\[
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{br}}(r)\cong A\Big/\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda}K_\lambda,
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{unif}}(r)\cong A\Big/\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}K_\lambda,
\]
and
\[
M,p\Vdash \Sec_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
K_\lambda=0\ \text{for some}\ \lambda\in\Lambda.
\]
In particular, a single branch realizes maximal collapse
\[
K_\omega=A
\]
if and only if $A$ is generated by at most $\beta$ elements. For $\beta=1$, the realizable single-branch intrinsic hidden subgroups are exactly the cyclic subgroups of $A$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Assume \textup{(i)}. For any branch on the fixed finite nerve satisfying the
finite-nerve comparison hypotheses, its intrinsic hidden subgroup is by
definition the image of the homological evaluation map attached to its branch
obstruction class. Since
\[
H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0,
\]
the universal coefficient comparison identifies that evaluation map with a
homomorphism from $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)$ to $A$. Thus, for each $\lambda$, there
exists a homomorphism
\[
\phi_\lambda:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A
\]
with
\[
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
After choosing an identification
\[
H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z^\beta,
\]
the image of $\phi_\lambda$ is generated by the images of the $\beta$ standard basis vectors. Hence $K_\lambda$ is generated by at most $\beta$ elements. Therefore \textup{(ii)} holds.

Assume conversely that \textup{(ii)} holds. For each $\lambda\in\Lambda$, choose generators
\[
k_{\lambda,1},\dots,k_{\lambda,\beta}\in K_\lambda
\]
of $K_\lambda$, allowing repetitions and zeros when fewer than $\beta$ generators are needed. Define
\[
\phi_\lambda:\mathbb Z^\beta\to A,
\qquad
(n_1,\dots,n_\beta)\longmapsto \sum_{j=1}^{\beta} n_j k_{\lambda,j}.
\]
Then
\[
\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda)=K_\lambda.
\]
Transporting these maps across the chosen isomorphism
\[
H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z^\beta
\]
and applying \Cref{thm:mixed-branch-realization} yields the required state $p$, reference $r$, and branch family. Hence \textup{(i)} holds.

The quotient formulas are those of \Cref{thm:mixed-branch-realization}(v). The global-section criterion follows from \Cref{thm:mixed-branch-realization}(iii), because
\[
K_\lambda=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\phi_\lambda=0.
\]
For the maximal-collapse claim, take $\Lambda$ to be a singleton and $K_\omega=A$. Then the equivalence between \textup{(i)} and \textup{(ii)} says exactly that such a branch exists if and only if $A$ is generated by at most $\beta$ elements. The final sentence is the case $\beta=1$.
\end{proof}
