\subsection{Banded groupoid lifts and connected components}

The presheaf $F_{p,s}$ records only isomorphism classes of local lift objects. To recover gerbes and cohomology one needs a groupoid-valued lift whose connected components give back $F_{p,s}$ after sheafification. We use the standard prestack, stackification, and banded-gerbe conventions of \cite{Giraud1971,MacLaneMoerdijk1994,Moerdijk2002,StacksProject}. Once an abelian band has been fixed, split lifts always exist; the substantive issue is whether a chosen lift is gluing-sensitive at the terminal fibre.

\begin{definition}[Banded groupoid lift and stackification]\label{def:banded-groupoid-lift}
Fix an admissible parameter $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ at a state $p$, and write
\[
a=[r]_p,
\qquad
\mathcal C_a:=\mathcal C_{p,s}/a.
\]
A \emph{banded groupoid lift} for $r$ at $p$ over the pullback-based slice site is a category fibred in groupoids
\[
\mathfrak P_r\to \mathcal C_a
\]
such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item the pre-stackification connected-component presheaf is the restriction of the presheaf of local sections,
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\cong F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a};
\]
\item for every object $U$ of $\mathcal C_a$ and every $x\in \mathfrak P_r(U)$, the automorphism sheaf
\[
\underline{\mathrm{Aut}}(x)
\]
on $\mathcal C_a/U$ is functorially identified with the restriction of a fixed abelian sheaf $\Band$ on $\mathcal C_a$.
\end{enumerate}
Write
\[
\mathfrak L_r:=a(\mathfrak P_r)
\]
for the stackification of $\mathfrak P_r$ in groupoids \cite[Tag 02ZP]{StacksProject}.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Separated split banded groupoid lift]\label{prop:split-banded-groupoid-lift}
Let $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ be admissible at $p$, write
\[
a=[r]_p,\qquad \mathcal C_a:=\mathcal C_{p,s}/a,
\]
and let $\Band$ be an abelian sheaf on $\mathcal C_a$. There is a canonical split banded groupoid lift
\[
\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}}\to\mathcal C_a
\]
with band $\Band$ and connected-component presheaf
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}})
\cong F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}.
\]
Explicitly, for every object $U$ of $\mathcal C_a$, the groupoid
$\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}}(U)$ has object set $F_{p,s}(U)$ and
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}}(U)}(\sigma,\tau)=
\begin{cases}
\Band(U),& \sigma=\tau,\\
\varnothing,& \sigma\ne\tau,
\end{cases}
\]
with composition given by addition in $\Band(U)$. For a morphism
$f:V\to U$ in $\mathcal C_a$, restriction sends $\sigma$ to
$\res_V^U(\sigma)$ and sends an automorphism $b\in\Band(U)$ to its
restriction $b|_V\in\Band(V)$.

This split prestack is separated as a prestack in groupoids, in the
sense that every isomorphism presheaf is a sheaf, if and only if the
restricted presheaf of local sections $F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}$ is
separated on the slice in the sense of \Cref{def:separated-restriction}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The displayed rule defines a contravariant pseudofunctor from
$\mathcal C_a$ to groupoids. Since $\mathcal C_a$ is thin, there are no
nontrivial coherence choices to make: functoriality of the object maps is
the functoriality of $F_{p,s}$, and functoriality on automorphisms is the
restriction functoriality of the sheaf $\Band$. The associated category
over $\mathcal C_a$ is therefore fibred in groupoids. Its connected
components over $U$ are exactly the objects $F_{p,s}(U)$, because
distinct objects have no morphisms between them and each automorphism
group is connected to its own object only. Hence
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}})(U)\cong F_{p,s}(U)
\]
naturally in $U$.

For an object $\sigma\in F_{p,s}(U)$, the automorphism presheaf on
$\mathcal C_a/U$ is
\[
V\longmapsto \operatorname{Aut}(\sigma|_V)=\Band(V),
\]
so its sheafification is already the restriction $\Band|_{\mathcal C_a/U}$.
Thus $\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}}$ is a banded groupoid lift with band
$\Band$.

It remains to prove the separatedness criterion. Suppose first that
$F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}$ is separated on the slice. Let $\sigma,\tau\in
F_{p,s}(U)$ and let $\{U_i\to U\}_{i\in I}$ be a cover. A matching
family of local isomorphisms
\[
\alpha_i:\sigma|_{U_i}\xrightarrow{\sim}\tau|_{U_i}
\]
can exist only when
\[
\sigma|_{U_i}=\tau|_{U_i}
\qquad(\forall i).
\]
Separatedness on the slice then gives $\sigma=\tau$. The local isomorphisms
$\alpha_i$ are therefore sections of the sheaf $\Band$ over the $U_i$,
and their matching condition is exactly the sheaf matching condition for
$\Band$. Since $\Band$ is a sheaf, the $\alpha_i$ glue uniquely to a
global element of $\Band(U)$, hence to a unique global isomorphism
$\sigma\to\tau$. Thus every isomorphism presheaf is a sheaf.

Conversely, assume that $\mathfrak P_r^{\mathrm{spl}}$ is separated. Let
$\sigma,\tau\in F_{p,s}(U)$ have equal restrictions on a cover
$\{U_i\to U\}_{i\in I}$. The identity element of $\Band(U_i)$ gives an
isomorphism
\[
\sigma|_{U_i}\xrightarrow{\sim}\tau|_{U_i}
\]
for every $i$, and these identities form a matching family. Separatedness
of the isomorphism presheaf glues them to a global isomorphism
$\sigma\to\tau$ in the split groupoid. Such a global isomorphism exists
only when $\sigma=\tau$. Therefore $F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}$ is separated
at every $U$, which is separatedness on the slice.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Consequences of a chosen gluing-sensitive banded lift datum]\label{prop:gluing-sensitive-lift-consequences}
Let $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ be admissible at a state $p$, and suppose $r$ is equipped with a gluing-sensitive banded lift datum in the sense of \Cref{def:gluing-sensitive-banded-lift}. Then the later component-obstruction theory depends on this extra datum, rather than on the split prestack of \Cref{prop:split-banded-groupoid-lift}, as follows:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item the sheafified global sections of $r$ are identified with $\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)(a)$ via \Cref{thm:visible-value-components};
\item if the cofinal gerbe-adapted-cover hypothesis of
\artresult{Component gerbes and component obstruction classes} is added,
then each global component section determines a component gerbe as in
\artresult{Supporting component gerbes of global component sections};
\item if, in addition, the stackification map is essentially surjective on the terminal fibre and $F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}$ is separated when the matching-family predicate \(\CompSec_s\) is invoked, then the comparison between global sections and neutral component gerbes is exactly the one proved in
\artresult{Global sections versus neutral component gerbes} and
\artresult{Component-gerbe criterion for matching without amalgamation}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Clause \textup{(ii)} of \Cref{def:gluing-sensitive-banded-lift} identifies the connected components of the chosen stack $\mathfrak L_r$ with the sheafification of the presheaf of local sections. Combined with \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}(ii), this is the stack-theoretic input used later to define global component sections and their component gerbes. Essential surjectivity on the terminal fibre supplies the comparison between stack-global sections and actual global lift objects via \Cref{prop:no-new-global-objects}. Finally, the acyclic cover data in \Cref{def:gluing-sensitive-banded-lift} are exactly the hypotheses invoked in \artresult{Component gerbes and component obstruction classes}. Together these assumptions are precisely what is needed for the later gerbe and obstruction arguments.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Components of the stackification]\label{prop:stackification-components}
In the setting of \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift} over the pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$, so without assuming an ambient meet-semilattice presentation, the sheaf of connected components of $\mathfrak L_r$ is canonically identified with the sheafification of the restricted presheaf of local sections $F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By stackification, the morphism presheaves of $\mathfrak P_r$ are sheafified and every object of $\mathfrak L_r$ is locally in the essential image of $\mathfrak P_r\to \mathfrak L_r$ \cite[Tag 02ZP]{StacksProject}. Passing to isomorphism classes therefore shows that $\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)$ is the sheafification of $\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)$. Since stacks in setoids are precisely sheaves of sets \cite[Tag 042Y]{StacksProject}, we obtain
\[
\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\cong a\bigl(\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\bigr).
\]
Using \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}(i), this becomes
\[
\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\cong a_{p,s}F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}.
\qedhere
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Naturality of the stackification component map]\label{prop:stackification-component-unit-naturality}
In the setting of \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}, the map of component
sheaves induced by the stackification morphism
\[
\iota_r:\mathfrak P_r\to a(\mathfrak P_r)=\mathfrak L_r
\]
is, under the identification
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\cong F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\cong a_{p,s}F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a},
\]
exactly the sheafification unit
\[
u:F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}\longrightarrow
a_{p,s}F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}.
\]
Equivalently, for every object \(U\) of the slice and every
\(y\in\mathfrak P_r(U)\),
\[
[\iota_r(y)]=u_U([y])
\]
in \(\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)(U)\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\Cref{prop:stackification-components} identifies
\(\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\) with the sheafification of
\(\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\). This identification is induced by
the universal morphism from a presheaf to its sheafification: applying
\(\pi_0\) to \(\iota_r\) sends a prestack object to the same local
component, now viewed in the sheaf of components. After the defining
identification
\(\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\cong F_{p,s}|_{\mathcal C_a}\), this
universal morphism is precisely \(u\).
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Essential surjectivity on the terminal fibre]\label{def:global-conservativity}
In the setting of \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}, now over a pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$, let
\[
\iota_r:\mathfrak P_r\to \mathfrak L_r:=a(\mathfrak P_r)
\]
be the stackification morphism. We say that $\mathfrak P_r$ is \emph{essentially surjective on the terminal fibre over $a$} if every object of the terminal stack fibre $(\mathfrak L_r)_a$ is isomorphic to the image of some object of the terminal prestack fibre $(\mathfrak P_r)_a$; equivalently, the induced functor
\[
(\iota_r)_a:(\mathfrak P_r)_a\to (\mathfrak L_r)_a
\]
is essentially surjective.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Global representability of component sections]\label{def:terminal-component-effectivity}
In the setting of \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}, the stackification
$\mathfrak L_r$ has \emph{globally represented component sections} if the canonical
map from isomorphism classes of terminal-fibre objects to terminal sections
of the component sheaf,
\[
\pi_0\bigl(\mathfrak L_r(a)\bigr)\longrightarrow \pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)(a),
\]
is surjective. Equivalently, every section of the sheaf of connected
components over $a$ is represented by an actual object of
$\mathfrak L_r(a)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[No new global objects at $a$]\label{prop:no-new-global-objects}
Assume essential surjectivity on the terminal fibre over $a$ in the sense of \Cref{def:global-conservativity} for a banded groupoid lift on the pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$. Then
\[
M,p\Vdash \Sec_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathfrak L_r(a)\neq\varnothing.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By \Cref{def:existence-levels} and \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift}(i),
\[
M,p\Vdash \Sec_s(r)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
F_{p,s}(a)\neq\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(a)\neq\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(\mathfrak P_r)_a\neq\varnothing.
\]
If $(\mathfrak P_r)_a\neq\varnothing$, choose $y\in (\mathfrak P_r)_a$. Then $\iota_r(y)\in \mathfrak L_r(a)$, so $\mathfrak L_r(a)\neq\varnothing$. Conversely, if $x\in \mathfrak L_r(a)$, essential surjectivity of $(\iota_r)_a$ yields $y\in (\mathfrak P_r)_a$ with $\iota_r(y)\cong x$, so $(\mathfrak P_r)_a\neq\varnothing$.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Representative-rigid lift]\label{def:representative-rigid-lift}
In the setting of \Cref{def:banded-groupoid-lift} over the pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$, a banded groupoid lift
\[
\mathfrak P_r\to \mathcal C_a
\]
is called \emph{representative-rigid} if for every object $U$ of $\mathcal C_a$ there is a map
\[
s_U:F_{p,s}(U)\to \Ob(\mathfrak P_r(U))
\]
such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item the image of $s_U(\sigma)$ in
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(U)\cong F_{p,s}(U)
\]
is $\sigma$;
\item for every morphism $f:V\to U$ in $\mathcal C_a$ and every $\sigma\in F_{p,s}(U)$,
\[
f^*s_U(\sigma)=s_V(\res_V^U(\sigma)).
\]
\end{enumerate}
Here \(f^*\) is computed with respect to a fixed split cleavage of the
fibred groupoid \(\mathfrak P_r\), so pullbacks compose on the nose. Thus
the isomorphism classes of lift objects admit a strictly pullback-stable choice
of representatives. This is the condition abbreviated by
\emph{representative-rigid}. Without this split-cleavage convention the same condition
would have to be stated with coherent cartesian isomorphisms, and the proof
of descent below would require the corresponding triple-cocycle coherence
instead of literal equalities of pulled-back representatives.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Where separatedness on the slice enters]\label{lem:unit-equality-on-cover-members}
Let $\mathcal C$ be a site with pullbacks of covering families, let \(F\)
be a presheaf of sets, let \(u:F\to aF\) be the sheafification unit, and
let \(X\) be an object. Assume that \(F\) is separated at every object of
the slice \(\mathcal C/X\). If
\[
\{X_i\to X\}_{i\in I}
\]
is a covering family, \(\sigma\in F(X)\), and
\(\sigma_i\in F(X_i)\) are sections satisfying
\[
u_{X_i}\bigl(\res_{X_i}^{X}\sigma\bigr)=u_{X_i}(\sigma_i)
\qquad(\forall i),
\]
then
\[
\res_{X_i}^{X}\sigma=\sigma_i
\qquad(\forall i).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(i\). Equality in \((aF)(X_i)\) is only sheafified equality, so it
does not by itself give equality in \(F(X_i)\). By
\Cref{lem:sheafification-unit-equality-local}, there is a covering family
\(\{Y_{ik}\to X_i\}_k\) such that
\[
\res_{Y_{ik}}^{X}\sigma=\res_{Y_{ik}}^{X_i}\sigma_i
\qquad(\forall k)
\]
in \(F(Y_{ik})\). The object \(X_i\) lies in the slice \(\mathcal C/X\),
and the hypothesis says that \(F\) is separated at this object. Therefore
these equalities after a cover of \(X_i\) force equality over \(X_i\)
itself:
\[
\res_{X_i}^{X}\sigma=\sigma_i.
\]
This proves the assertion for every \(i\).
\end{proof}
