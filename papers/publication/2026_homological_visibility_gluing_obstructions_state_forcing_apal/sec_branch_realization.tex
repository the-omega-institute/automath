\subsection{Realization of prescribed branch data}

The preceding algebra isolates the branchwise and aggregate quotients attached to a given family of hidden subgroups. The next theorem gives a converse existence statement on connected finite nerves with vanishing first homology. It is internal to the fixed finite-nerve framework of the paper: the nerve, the constant band, and the comparison data are fixed in advance, and the theorem constructs local-object and prestack data realizing the prescribed images.

The realization proofs use the following explicit admissible-expansion
interface. The site is not an arbitrary full subcategory of intersections.
It is the formal \v{C}ech site of a finite good-cover presentation, so that
the cohomological comparison used later is an actual Leray comparison rather
than a declared comparison datum.

\begin{theorem}[Formal \v{C}ech site and one-state admissible expansion]\label{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}
Let
\[
\mathcal U=\{U_i\to a\}_{i\in I}
\]
be a finite cover whose nerve $N=N(\mathcal U)$ is realized by a good cover
of a paracompact space $X\simeq |N|$. Let $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ be the
formal \v{C}ech index site whose objects are $a$ and the symbols
\[
U_J
\qquad
(\varnothing\neq J\subseteq I,\; J\in N),
\]
together with a strict initial object $\emptyset$. The order is generated by
$\emptyset\to U_J\to U_K\to a$ for $K\subseteq J$ and $J,K\in N$. Pullbacks
are computed by
\[
U_J\times_a U_K=
\begin{cases}
U_{J\cup K},& J\cup K\in N,\\
\emptyset,& J\cup K\notin N,
\end{cases}
\]
and by the rule that any pullback involving $\emptyset$ is $\emptyset$. The
pretopology is generated by the cover of $a$ by the $U_i$, its pullbacks, the
identity covers, and the empty cover of $\emptyset$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ is a pullback-based local reference site with terminal object $a$;
\item the cover $\mathcal U$ remains a cover of $a$ in $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and its \v{C}ech nerve is the finite simplicial complex $N$;
\item for every finite abelian band $A$, viewed as the genuine constant
sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$ on $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, the \v{C}ech cochain
complex of $\mathcal U$ with coefficients in $A_{\mathcal U}$ and the finite
nerve presentation of any class in $H^2(N(\mathcal U),A)$ are the same finite
cochain data after replacing $\mathcal C_a$ with $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$;
\item for every constant abelian group $A$, the Leray comparison for the
chosen good cover gives a natural isomorphism
\[
H^n(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})\cong \check H^n(\mathcal U,A_{\mathcal U})\cong H^n(N,A)
\]
for all $n$, and the \v{C}ech cocycle on the vertex cover maps to the
corresponding derived cohomology class;
\item for every contravariant functor
\[
F:\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}^{op}\to\mathsf{Set},
\]
there is a one-state $\mathbb L_2$-model $M_2$ with state $p$ and a
distinguished admitted reference $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ such that $[r]_p=a$, the
local slice site of $r$ is exactly $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and the local
object functor on that slice site is $F$; this model is the admissible
$\mathbb L_2$-expansion supplied by
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface};
\item if, in addition, a category fibred in groupoids
\[
\mathfrak P\to\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
with abelian band $\Band$ satisfies
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P)\cong F
\]
and the automorphism-sheaf condition of
\Cref{def:realization-prestack}, then $M_2$ has an admissible
$\mathbb L_3$-expansion $M_3$ in which $r$ has realization prestack
$\mathfrak P$, band $\Band$, and stackification $a(\mathfrak P)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The formal index category is thin and has terminal object $a$. Pulling
$U_J\to a$ back along $U_K\to a$ gives the formal object $U_{J\cup K}$
when this index set spans a simplex of $N$, and gives the strict initial
object $\emptyset$ otherwise. Pullbacks along morphisms $U_J\to U_K$ are
computed by the same union rule, and pullbacks involving $\emptyset$ are
again $\emptyset$. Thus $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ has the required
pullback-based overlap objects, including empty overlaps, and terminal
object $a$, proving \textup{(i)}.

The covering family $\mathcal U$ is one of the generators of the
pretopology on $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$. A finite set $J\subseteq I$
spans a simplex precisely when the iterated pullback of the corresponding
$U_i$ is a nonempty object $U_J$ rather than $\emptyset$. Hence the
\v{C}ech nerve of the cover is $N$, proving \textup{(ii)}.

For the genuine constant sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$, sections over a nonempty
formal intersection object are $A$, while sections over the strict initial
object are the zero group. Hence the \v{C}ech $n$-cochains on
$\mathcal U$ are exactly functions from the set of nonempty
$(n+1)$-fold intersections to $A$; empty pullback terms contribute only
zero sections. The preceding paragraph identifies that nonempty indexing
set before and after passing to $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and the
alternating coboundary is the same. Hence the \v{C}ech cochain complex, the
simplicial cochain complex of $N(\mathcal U)$, and the
universal-coefficient data used in the finite nerve presentation are
identical. This proves \textup{(iii)}.

For \textup{(iv)}, let $j:X\to\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ denote the
geometric realization comparison induced by the chosen good cover. Since
all nonempty finite intersections in a good cover are contractible, the
higher cohomology of the constant sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$ on each member and
each nonempty finite intersection vanishes; over the strict initial object
there is no additional contribution. Leray's theorem for this acyclic cover identifies
the derived sheaf cohomology of the associated site with the \v{C}ech
cohomology of the cover \cite{Bredon1997,Jardine2015}. The nerve lemma identifies the good-cover
\v{C}ech cohomology with the simplicial cohomology of $N$; the
cochain-level identification is the ordered finite nerve comparison of
\Cref{prop:finite-nerve-comparison}. Naturality in the constant group $A$
is inherited from the functoriality of sheaf cohomology and simplicial
cochains. Therefore a vertex-cover \v{C}ech $2$-cocycle represents exactly
the corresponding derived class in
$H^2(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})$, rather than a separately
declared comparison datum.

For \textup{(v)}, build the lower structure explicitly. Let the state set be
the singleton $\{p\}$. For each object $U$ of $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, take a
reference element $r_U$ of sort $\mathsf{Ref}_s$, interpret $\Adm_s$ at $p$ as
the set of all these elements, and put $[r_U]_p=U$. This gives a surjective
reference-class assignment onto the object set of $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$.
Let $r:=r_a$ be the distinguished terminal reference. The refinement relation
and pretopology on these reference classes are those of
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$; its identity, pullback-stability, transitivity, and
pullback-object clauses were verified in \textup{(i)}. Thus the slice site over
$[r]_p=a$ is exactly $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$. For reference sorts not used in
the construction, take empty reference sets or the inherited background
interpretation. Assign the given functor $F$ as the local object functor on
that slice site. These data are exactly the semantic data required by
\Cref{def:reference-classes,def:local-reference-frame,def:local-object-functor};
the lower $\mathbb L_1$-reduct is the same one-state information model with
the added site and local-object symbols forgotten. Hence they define the
claimed $\mathbb L_2$-model by the local-object expansion case of
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}. Thus the existence
assertions in this subsection are relative to the deliberately broad
well-typed admissible-expansion semantics of that proposition, not to an
independently axiomatized smaller class of semantic models.

For \textup{(vi)}, expand this $\mathbb L_2$-model by the supplied fibred
groupoid $\mathfrak P$, the band $\Band$, and the stackification
$a(\mathfrak P)$. The displayed identification
$\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P)\cong F$ gives clause \textup{(i)} of
\Cref{def:realization-prestack}, and the assumed automorphism-sheaf condition
gives clause \textup{(ii)}. Thus $\mathfrak P$ is a realization prestack for
$r$ at $p$ over the local slice site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and adding it
is precisely the $\mathbb L_2\preceq\mathbb L_3$ expansion described in
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface} and instantiated in
\Cref{prop:chain-instantiation}. This proves \textup{(v)} and \textup{(vi)}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Twisted branch prestack model]\label{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}
Let $\mathcal U=\{U_i\to a\}_{i\in I}$ be a finite cover whose nerve $N$ is connected, let $A$ be a finite abelian group, let $\Lambda$ be a finite nonempty label set, and let
\[
\alpha_\lambda\in Z^2(N,A)
\qquad
(\lambda\in\Lambda)
\]
be normalized simplicial cocycles. We use their alternating extension to all
ordered triples of indices: for pairwise distinct indices, permuting an
ordered simplex multiplies the value by the sign of the permutation, and any
triple with a repeated index has value $0$. Let
\[
\Lambda_0:=\{\lambda\in\Lambda\mid \alpha_\lambda=0\}.
\]
On the formal \v{C}ech site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, define $F$ by
\[
F(W)=
\begin{cases}
\Lambda,& W\to a\text{ a nonempty proper formal intersection object},\\
\Lambda_0,& W=a,\\
\{\ast\},& W=\emptyset,
\end{cases}
\]
with identity restrictions among nonempty proper intersection objects,
inclusions $\Lambda_0\hookrightarrow\Lambda$ from $a$ to nonempty proper
intersections, and the unique restriction map to $F(\emptyset)$. For each
$\lambda\notin\Lambda_0$, define a fibred groupoid $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ by
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda(a)=\varnothing
\]
and, for every nonempty proper object $W$ of $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, put
\[
I(W):=\{i\in I\mid W\to U_i\text{ in }\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}\}.
\]
Thus $I(U_J)=J$ for a formal intersection object. Define objects
\[
x_i^{(\lambda,W)}\qquad (i\in I(W))
\]
and morphism sets
\[
\mathrm{Hom}\bigl(x_i^{(\lambda,W)},x_j^{(\lambda,W)}\bigr)=A,
\]
with composition
\[
(b,j,k)\circ(a,i,j)=(a+b+\alpha_{\lambda,ijk},i,k).
\]
For a morphism $V\to W$, one has $I(W)\subseteq I(V)$, because a map
$W\to U_i$ composes with $V\to W$. Restrictions therefore send
$x_i^{(\lambda,W)}$ to $x_i^{(\lambda,V)}$ and $(a,i,j)$ to the same
coefficient-labelled morphism. Over $\emptyset$ take the terminal groupoid
and let every restriction to $\emptyset$ be the unique functor. For each
$\lambda\in\Lambda_0$, let $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ be the neutral stack with
one object and automorphism group $A$ on every nonempty object, terminal
fibre over $\emptyset$, and the evident restrictions. Its automorphism
sheaf is the genuine constant sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$. Set
\[
\mathfrak P:=\coprod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\mathfrak P_\lambda.
\]
This denotes the fibrewise coproduct over every nonempty object, while over
$\emptyset$ the summands share the single terminal fibre and all
restrictions to $\emptyset$ are the unique functor.
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item each nonzero summand $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is a category fibred in groupoids, and the pullback functors are strictly functorial;
\item the identity, inverse, and associativity laws in each twisted fibre are respectively the normalized identities
\[
\alpha_{\lambda,iij}=\alpha_{\lambda,ijj}=0,
\qquad
\alpha_{\lambda,iji}=0,
\]
and the cocycle identity
\[
\alpha_{\lambda,jkl}-\alpha_{\lambda,ikl}
+\alpha_{\lambda,ijl}-\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}=0;
\]
\item $\mathfrak P$ is a prestack with constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$;
\item its presheaf of connected components is $F$;
\item after stackification, the visible components over $a$ are naturally indexed by $\Lambda$, and the component indexed by $\lambda$ has Cech cocycle $\alpha_\lambda$ on $\mathcal U$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For a nonempty proper formal intersection object $W$ and a fixed
$\lambda\notin\Lambda_0$, the displayed formula makes every morphism
invertible. The identity at $x_i^{(\lambda,W)}$ is $(0,i,i)$ because the
cocycle is normalized on repeated indices. The inverse of $(a,i,j)$ is
$(-a,j,i)$, again because the repeated-index normalization gives
$\alpha_{\lambda,iji}=0$. Associativity of
\[
(c,k,l)\circ\bigl((b,j,k)\circ(a,i,j)\bigr)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\bigl((c,k,l)\circ(b,j,k)\bigr)\circ(a,i,j)
\]
is equivalent, after cancelling $a+b+c$, to
\[
\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}+\alpha_{\lambda,ikl}
=
\alpha_{\lambda,jkl}+\alpha_{\lambda,ijl},
\]
which is the displayed cocycle identity, interpreted under the alternating
ordered-triple convention just fixed. This proves that each fibre is a
groupoid. If $V\to W$, then $I(W)\subseteq I(V)$, so every object and
morphism named over $W$ has the same name over $V$. The restriction maps
therefore preserve labels and coefficients and use the same ordered simplex
indices, so they preserve identities, inverses, and composition. Since
inclusions $I(W)\subseteq I(V)$ are transitive, restrictions compose
strictly. This proves \textup{(i)} and \textup{(ii)}.

For each pair of objects in a twisted fibre, the isomorphism presheaf is the
restriction of the genuine constant sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$ to the locus of
nonempty further intersections where both objects are defined, and is empty
off that locus. On a covering of such an object, compatibility is therefore
the sheaf condition for $A_{\mathcal U}$; on the strict initial object the
empty cover gives the unique section. Hence isomorphism presheaves are
sheaves, so each $\mathfrak P_\lambda$ is a prestack. The zero summands are
already stacks by the same constant-sheaf argument. Taking the finite
coproduct over nonempty objects and the shared terminal fibre over
$\emptyset$ preserves the prestack condition: over nonempty objects this is
the usual finite coproduct of prestacks in groupoids, while the strict
initial object has only the unique matching datum. Every automorphism sheaf
is canonically $A_{\mathcal U}$; this is the stated banding. Thus
\textup{(iii)} holds.

For every nonempty proper intersection object, each twisted summand is
connected and each zero summand is one connected component; there are no
morphisms between distinct summands. Over $\emptyset$ the shared terminal
fibre has the unique empty-local object, matching $F(\emptyset)=\{\ast\}$.
Hence
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P)(U)\cong\Lambda
\]
for every nonempty proper intersection object $U\to a$. Over $a$, the
nonzero summands have empty fibre and the zero summands have one object, so
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P)(a)\cong\Lambda_0.
\]
These identifications are compatible with the restriction maps, giving \textup{(iv)}.

Because $N$ is connected, a matching family for $F$ on $\mathcal U$ is a constant label in $\Lambda$: equality on nonempty pairwise overlaps propagates along paths in the nerve. Therefore the sheafification of $F$ has terminal sections indexed by $\Lambda$. By \Cref{prop:stackification-components}, stackification identifies
\[
\pi_0(a(\mathfrak P))\cong aF,
\]
so the visible components over $a$ are indexed by $\Lambda$.

For a fixed $\lambda$, take on $U_i$ the object $x_i^{(\lambda,U_i)}$ if $\lambda\notin\Lambda_0$ and the distinguished constant object if $\lambda\in\Lambda_0$. On $U_{ij}$ take the morphism $(0,i,j)$ in the nonzero case and the identity in the zero case. The triple defect is
\[
(0,i,k)^{-1}\circ(0,j,k)\circ(0,i,j)
=
(\alpha_{\lambda,ijk},i,i)
\]
in the twisted case and is $0$ in the constant case. Under the band identification this is exactly $\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}$. Thus the Cech obstruction cocycle of the $\lambda$-component is $\alpha_\lambda$, proving \textup{(v)}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Realization of prescribed branchwise hidden subgroups on a formal \v{C}ech site]\label{thm:prescribed-branch-realization}
Let
\[
\mathcal U=\{U_i\to a\}_{i\in I}
\]
be a finite good-cover presentation in a pullback-based slice site $\mathcal C_a$ whose \v{C}ech nerve
\[
N:=N(\mathcal U)
\]
is connected. Equivalently, the realization of the formal cover is a
paracompact space homotopy equivalent to $|N|$ and all nonempty finite
intersections of members of $\mathcal U$ are acyclic for constant
coefficients. Let $A$ be a finite abelian group, viewed on $\mathcal U$ as a
trivialized constant band and as the automorphism band of the realization
prestack constructed below, and assume
\[
H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
Use the Leray comparison isomorphism of
\Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(iv). Let $\Lambda$ be a finite nonempty set, and let
\[
\phi_\lambda:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A
\]
be a nonzero homomorphism for each $\lambda\in\Lambda$. Let
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ be the formal \v{C}ech site of
\Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}. Then, relative to the
admissible-expansion convention of
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}, there exist a one-state
admissible $\mathbb L_2/\mathbb L_3$ expansion $M$ with state $p$, a distinguished admitted reference
$r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ with $[r]_p=a$, a local slice site for $r$ equal to
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, a local object functor $F_{p,s}$ on
$\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$, and a realization prestack
\[
\mathfrak P_r\to \mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
with band $A$ such that, for $\mathfrak L_r:=a(\mathfrak P_r)$, the following hold:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong \Lambda;
\]
\item under the identification in \textup{(i)}, for each $\lambda\in\Lambda$ the corresponding component gerbe $\mathfrak G_\lambda\subseteq \mathfrak L_r$ has branch obstruction class
\[
\omega_\lambda\in H^2(N,A)
\]
satisfying
\[
\mathrm{ev}_{\omega_\lambda}=\phi_\lambda,
\qquad
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda);
\]
\item $\mathfrak P_r$ is globally conservative at $a$ by the vacuous terminal-fibre case
\[
\mathfrak P_r(a)=\varnothing=\mathfrak L_r(a),
\]
and
\[
M,p\Vdash \Null^{\mathrm{glue}}_s(r);
\]
\item the aggregate visibility quotients satisfy
\[
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{br}}(r)\cong A\Big/\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda),
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\mathrm{unif}}(r)\cong A\Big/\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
\end{enumerate}
In particular, within the fixed finite-nerve framework above, every finite family of subgroups of $A$ arising as images of homomorphisms
\[
H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A
\]
is realized as the family of intrinsic hidden subgroups of the visible branches of one admitted reference.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since
\[
H_1(N,\mathbb Z)=0,
\]
the universal coefficient sequence reduces to an isomorphism
\[
\eta_A:H^2(N,A)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{Hom}(H_2(N,\mathbb Z),A).
\]
For each $\lambda\in\Lambda$, choose the unique class
\[
\omega_\lambda\in H^2(N,A)
\]
with
\[
\eta_A(\omega_\lambda)=\phi_\lambda,
\]
and choose a normalized simplicial cocycle
\[
\alpha_\lambda\in Z^2(N,A)
\]
representing $\omega_\lambda$, with the alternating ordered-triple extension
of \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}.

By \Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(i)--(iv), pass to the
formal \v{C}ech site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ appearing in the statement.
The cover $\mathcal U$, its nerve, and the constant-band finite cochain
data are the same, and Leray comparison identifies
$H^2(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})$ with $H^2(N,A)$. The derived branch class
used below is therefore the actual derived class corresponding to the
chosen finite-nerve cocycle.

Define the local object functor
\[
F_{p,s}:\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}^{op}\to\mathsf{Set}
\]
by
\[
F_{p,s}(U):=\Lambda
\qquad
\text{for every nonempty proper intersection object }U\to a,
\]
\[
F_{p,s}(a):=\varnothing,
\]
and
\[
F_{p,s}(\emptyset):=\{\ast\}.
\]
Restrictions between nonempty proper intersection objects are identities,
and every restriction to $\emptyset$ is the unique map. Because $N$ is
connected, a matching family for $F_{p,s}$ on the cover $\mathcal U$ is
exactly a constant label
\[
\lambda\in\Lambda.
\]
Indeed, a matching family assigns labels $\lambda_i\in\Lambda$ to the objects $U_i$, and compatibility on each nonempty overlap $U_{ij}$ forces $\lambda_i=\lambda_j$ because the restriction maps between proper intersections are identities on $\Lambda$. Connectivity of the nerve propagates these equalities along chains of overlaps, so all labels coincide.
Therefore the sheafification satisfies
\[
\bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda.
\]

For each $\lambda\in\Lambda$, define a category fibred in groupoids
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda\to \mathcal C_{\mathcal U}
\]
as follows. On the terminal fibre set
\[
\mathfrak P_\lambda(a):=\varnothing.
\]
If $W$ is a nonempty proper formal intersection object, put
\[
I(W):=\{i\in I\mid W\to U_i\},
\]
and let the fibre $\mathfrak P_\lambda(W)$ have object set
\[
\{x_i^{(\lambda,W)}\mid i\in I(W)\}.
\]
For objects $x_i^{(\lambda,W)}$ and $x_j^{(\lambda,W)}$, define
\[
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathfrak P_\lambda(U)}\!\bigl(x_i^{(\lambda,U)},x_j^{(\lambda,U)}\bigr):=A.
\]
Writing an element $a\in A$ as a morphism
\[
(a,i,j):x_i^{(\lambda,U)}\to x_j^{(\lambda,U)},
\]
define composition by
\[
(b,j,k)\circ (a,i,j):=
\bigl(a+b+\alpha_{\lambda,ijk},\,i,k\bigr).
\]
Since each $\alpha_\lambda$ is normalized, the identity on $x_i^{(\lambda,U)}$ is $(0,i,i)$ and the inverse of $(a,i,j)$ is $(-a,j,i)$. Associativity on quadruples is exactly the simplicial cocycle identity
\[
\alpha_{\lambda,jkl}-\alpha_{\lambda,ikl}+\alpha_{\lambda,ijl}-\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}=0.
\]
If
\[
V\to W
\]
is a morphism between nonempty proper formal intersection objects, then
$I(W)\subseteq I(V)$. Send $x_i^{(\lambda,W)}$ to
$x_i^{(\lambda,V)}$ and send $(a,i,j)$ to the same element $(a,i,j)$.
Over $\emptyset$ take the terminal groupoid and use the unique restriction
functor to it. This defines a prestack because every isomorphism presheaf is
either a restriction of the genuine constant sheaf $A_{\mathcal U}$ or the
empty sheaf.

Let
\[
\mathfrak P_r:=\coprod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}\mathfrak P_\lambda.
\]
As in \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model}, this is the fibrewise
coproduct over nonempty objects with the shared terminal fibre over
$\emptyset$.
The verification that this is a fibred prestack, that its restrictions are strictly functorial, that its automorphism sheaves are the constant band $A_{\mathcal U}$, and that the normalized cocycle identities give the groupoid laws is precisely \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model} with $\Lambda_0=\varnothing$. On every nonempty proper intersection object $U\to a$, each summand $\mathfrak P_\lambda(U)$ is connected, while there are no morphisms between distinct summands. Consequently,
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(U)\cong \Lambda
\qquad
\text{for every nonempty proper }U\to a,
\]
and
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(a)=\varnothing.
\]
Over $\emptyset$ the shared terminal fibre gives
$\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)(\emptyset)\cong\{\ast\}=F_{p,s}(\emptyset)$.
Hence
\[
\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathfrak P_r)\cong F_{p,s}.
\]
By \Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(v)--(vi), the functor
$F_{p,s}$ and the prestack $\mathfrak P_r$ determine a one-state semantic
model $M$ with admitted reference $r:\mathsf{Ref}_s$ at its unique state $p$,
with reference class $[r]_p=a$, local slice site $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$,
local object functor $F_{p,s}$, and realization prestack $\mathfrak P_r$ with
constant band $A$. This admissibility is exactly the expansion convention of
\Cref{prop:admissible-layer-expansion-interface}; no additional semantic
closure axiom for freely adjoining local-object or prestack data is being used.
At this point all semantic interfaces required by the statement have been checked: the admitted reference is $r$; the formal \v{C}ech site is $\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$; the local object functor is the displayed $F_{p,s}$; the prestack band is the trivialized constant finite abelian group $A$; the cover $\mathcal U$ is compared with derived cohomology by the Leray isomorphism of \Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(iv); the visible classes will be the sheafified terminal sections of $F_{p,s}$; and the forcing clauses for $\CompSec_s$, $\Sec_s$, and $\Null_s^{\mathrm{glue}}$ are read from these compatible-section and terminal-section data.
By \Cref{prop:stackification-components},
\[
\pi_0(\mathfrak L_r)\cong a_{p,s}F_{p,s},
\]
so the visible value classes at $a$ are exactly
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong \bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda.
\]
This proves \textup{(i)}.

For each $\lambda\in\Lambda$, let $\mathfrak G_\lambda$ be the full substack of $\mathfrak L_r$ determined by the visible branch $\lambda$. On the cover $\mathcal U$, take the local objects
\[
x_i^{(\lambda)}:=x_i^{(\lambda,U_i)}
\]
and, on each overlap $U_{ij}$, take the distinguished isomorphism
\[
\phi_{ij}^{(\lambda)}:=(0,i,j):x_i^{(\lambda)}|_{U_{ij}}\xrightarrow{\sim}x_j^{(\lambda)}|_{U_{ij}}.
\]
Then on every triple overlap $U_{ijk}$,
\[
\bigl(\phi_{ik}^{(\lambda)}\bigr)^{-1}\circ
\phi_{jk}^{(\lambda)}\circ
\phi_{ij}^{(\lambda)}
=
(\alpha_{\lambda,ijk},i,i),
\]
which identifies with $\alpha_{\lambda,ijk}\in A$ under the automorphism-band identification. Therefore the \v{C}ech cocycle produced by \Cref{thm:cech-bridge-compatible-realizations} is exactly $\alpha_\lambda$, and the branch obstruction class of $\mathfrak G_\lambda$ is
\[
\omega_\lambda=[\alpha_\lambda].
\]
This is the stackification-component compatibility clause of \Cref{lem:twisted-branch-prestack-model} written on the cover $\mathcal U$.
Thus the finite-nerve comparison uses exactly the fixed good cover
$\mathcal U$, the constant band $A$, and the normalized cocycle
$\alpha_\lambda$ supplied by the homomorphism $\phi_\lambda$; the passage
to derived $H^2(\mathcal C_{\mathcal U},A_{\mathcal U})$ is the Leray comparison of
\Cref{lem:generated-subsite-model-interface}(iv).
Since
\[
\mathrm{ev}_{\omega_\lambda}=\eta_A(\omega_\lambda)=\phi_\lambda,
\]
one gets
\[
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda).
\]
This proves \textup{(ii)}.

Because each $\phi_\lambda$ is nonzero and $\eta_A$ is an isomorphism, each class $\omega_\lambda$ is nonzero. By \Cref{prop:derived-branch-classification}, every component gerbe $\mathfrak G_\lambda$ is therefore non-neutral, and \Cref{lem:terminal-fibre-neutrality} gives
\[
\mathfrak G_\lambda(a)=\varnothing
\qquad
(\forall \lambda\in\Lambda).
\]
Hence
\[
\mathfrak L_r(a)=\varnothing.
\]
Since also
\[
\mathfrak P_r(a)=\varnothing,
\]
the stackification functor on the terminal fibre is
\[
(\iota_r)_a:\mathfrak P_r(a)\to \mathfrak L_r(a),
\]
that is, the empty functor $\varnothing\to\varnothing$, and it is essentially surjective. Thus $\mathfrak P_r$ is globally conservative at $a$.

Moreover,
\[
\bigl(a_{p,s}F_{p,s}\bigr)(a)\cong \Lambda\neq\varnothing,
\]
so \Cref{thm:sheafification-characterization} gives
\[
M,p\Vdash \CompSec_s(r).
\]
On the other hand,
\[
F_{p,s}(a)=\varnothing,
\]
so
\[
M,p\nVdash \Sec_s(r).
\]
Since $r$ is admitted by construction, it follows that
\[
M,p\Vdash \Null^{\mathrm{glue}}_s(r).
\]
This proves \textup{(iii)}.

Finally, \textup{(iv)} is \Cref{thm:branch-sensitive-visible-quotient,thm:branch-uniform-visible-quotient} applied to the family
\[
K_\lambda=\operatorname{Im}(\phi_\lambda)
\qquad
(\lambda\in\Lambda).
\]
The last sentence is the same statement rewritten in terms of the images of the chosen homomorphisms.
\end{proof}

The preceding realization theorem forces every branch to be non-neutral. The next theorem removes that restriction and identifies the neutral branches exactly with the zero homomorphisms.
