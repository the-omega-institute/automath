Dear Editors of the Transactions of the American Mathematical Society,

We submit "Brocot Condensation and Critical Fibonacci Renewal" for
consideration as a research article.

The paper proves a total-variation condensation theorem for canonical
continued fractions of fixed digit sum under inverse-denominator weight.
It identifies the limiting left and right context words, the condensate
defect and location laws, and the asymptotic denominator factorization.
The argument also gives the exact scalar denominator-layer coefficient.

That coefficient corrects the leading constant printed in Dushistova's
fixed-digit-sum expansion. The manuscript pinpoints the endpoint overcount:
an empty left context enters a doubled convolution and is counted twice.
The corrected coefficient is 2 R_s^2 rather than R_s + 2 R_s^2. We make
no assertion about the remaining coefficients of that expansion.

At the arithmetic critical exponent, the scalar estimate yields an exact
regularly varying generator-cost tail with context constant 8. Weinstein's
free-generator coordinates then give a weighted renewal identity for the
classical Fibonacci partition function, a spectrally positive stable domain
of attraction, and the noninteger critical correction of order
m^(3-sigma_0).

The scope is intentionally narrow. The continuant weights are
nonmultiplicative, so the total-variation theorem is a structural result for
this arithmetic family rather than a universal condensation mechanism.
Weinstein's generator classification and the classical renewal and stable
limit theorems are cited inputs; the exact context law, corrected arithmetic
constant, critical tail, and Fibonacci finite-size transfer are the article's
contributions.

A companion manuscript, "Finite-Window Zeckendorf Thermodynamics," treats
the logically autonomous finite-window correspondence and large-deviation
problem. No result from that manuscript is needed here.

The manuscript is self-contained, has not been published, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. The authors have no conflicts of interest to
declare.
