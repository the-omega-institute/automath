\begin{abstract}
Fix \(s>2\) and weight the canonical regular continued fractions of digit
sum \(n\) by the inverse \(s\)-th power of their denominators.  We prove that
with probability tending to one a unique partial quotient exceeds \(n/2\),
while the finite words on its two sides converge jointly in total variation
to independent inverse-continuant laws.  The theorem also gives the limiting
defect and location laws and an asymptotic factorization of the denominator.
As a scalar consequence,
\[
 Z_n(s)\sim
 2\left\{\frac{\zeta(s-1)}{\zeta(s)}\right\}^{\!2}n^{-s};
\]
the endpoint calculation corrects the leading coefficient printed by
Dushistova from \(R_s+2R_s^2\) to \(2R_s^2\).  At the arithmetic critical
point \(\zeta(\sigma_0-1)/\zeta(\sigma_0)=2\), Weinstein's generator
coordinates convert this denominator-layer asymptotic into an exact renewal
identity for the classical Fibonacci partition function.  We obtain a cost
tail with context constant \(8\) and the noninteger
\(m^{3-\sigma_0}\) correction to the critical layer sum; as an additional
consequence, centered generator costs belong to the domain of attraction of
a spectrally positive stable law.
\end{abstract}
