Editorial Board\\
\emph{The Fibonacci Quarterly}}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Irredundant
coordinate covers in Fibonacci birth layers''} for
consideration in \emph{The Fibonacci Quarterly}.

For each index $n$, we study the birth layer $B_n$---the set of divisors of
the Fibonacci number $F_n$ whose rank of apparition equals $n$---as an
ordered subset of the divisor lattice of $F_n$. Our main contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item We prove an odd-index primitive-prime realization theorem for
irredundant coordinate covers: every irredundant cover of the prime-power
coordinate set of an odd $n$ is realized by a distinct minimal generator of
$B_n$, and connected covers realize connected minimal generators. This gives
the sharper lower bounds $\#\mathcal M_n\ge I_k$, $\operatorname{width}(B_n)\ge
I_k$, and $M^{\mathrm{conn}}(n)\ge CI_k$, where $I_k$ and $CI_k$ count
irredundant and connected irredundant covers of a $k$-set. The small values
$(\operatorname{Bell}(k),I_k,CI_k)=(1,1,1),(2,2,1),(5,8,4),(15,49,23)$ show
that the result is stronger than the earlier Bell partition subfamily.

\item We establish a connected-block factorization that reduces the global
counts $A(n)=\#B_n$ and $\#\mathcal{M}_n$ to exact partition-lattice formulas
in terms of connected kernels, and then combine the formula with the connected
irredundant-cover realization to obtain explicit arithmetic lower bounds for
the connected kernel contribution (Theorem~1.3 and its corollaries).

\item We supply the exponent-correct infrastructure needed for the realization
theorem: $B_n$ is an upper set obtained by removing covering ideals, and its
minimal generators are classified by pairwise coprime witness covers built from
atomic prime-power birth moduli (Theorems~1.1 and~1.2).

\item We prove a primitive-versus-ladder dichotomy for atomic factors and give
complete structural, finite-rank classifications for layers with
$\omega(n)\le 3$ (Section~4). In the three-coordinate case, each surviving
witness type is controlled by an explicit nonemptiness criterion over
primitive and ladder atom families computed from the prime-power divisors of
$F_n$; the statement is intentionally finite-rank structural rather than a
closed congruence classification of all realizing indices.
\end{enumerate}

These results provide a structural perspective on Fibonacci first-appearance
spectra that complements the existing counting-function approach of
Stroi\'nski and the dynamical studies of Luca--Pomerance and
FitzGibbons--Javaheri--Miller--Verga. The manuscript includes explicit
prime-power apparition formulas, reproducible numerical data through
$n\le30$, a proof of the small irredundant-cover counts, and a corrected
treatment of the first three-coordinate layer \(n=30\), where the five
realized witness covers are separated from the remaining admissible family
shapes.

The manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under
consideration by any other journal. Both authors have read and approved the
final version.
