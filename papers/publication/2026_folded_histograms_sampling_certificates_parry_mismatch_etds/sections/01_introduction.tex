\section{Introduction}

Let an irrational rotation be coded by a finite binary window.  If the
resulting words are then passed through a deterministic map, the empirical law
may differ from a natural reference law for two unrelated reasons: the orbit
has been sampled only for a finite time, and the limiting symbolic law may
occupy a small part of the reference state space.  This paper treats a case in
which both effects can be separated at a fixed block length.

The map considered below is defined as follows.  Given
\(\omega\in\{0,1\}^m\), take its Fibonacci value, write this integer in
Zeckendorf normal form, and retain the first \(m\) digits.  This produces a
map
\[
\Fold_m:\{0,1\}^m\longrightarrow X_m,
\]
where \(X_m\) is the length-\(m\) language of the golden-mean shift.  Although
\(\Fold_m\) has many fibers on the full cube, its restriction to rotation
languages has a sharp window classification.  If
\(\delta=\|\alpha\|=\min\{\alpha,1-\alpha\}\), then the restriction is
injective at every resolution exactly for
\(\beta\in(0,\delta]\cup[1-\delta,1)\).  Outside this range the words \(00\)
and \(11\) both occur with positive mass and collide under \(\Fold_2\).  The
Sturmian coding \(\beta=\alpha\) lies on the boundary of the lossless range;
there the folded sequence is a relabelled \(m\)-block presentation of the
Sturmian subshift.  No entropy, recurrence or complexity is lost by the fold
on the realized language.

This observation is the point at which the sampling question and the Parry
comparison separate.  Star discrepancy controls the empirical mass of each
rotation cylinder, giving total-variation bounds for the microstate law and
for its folded pushforward.  The constants are \(2m\) in the general rotation
window and \(m+1\) in the Sturmian case.  With a lower bound on the smallest
atom of the associated finite rotation partition, the same estimates give
relative-entropy bounds; in the Sturmian slice this lower bound has the usual
continued-fraction and Diophantine forms.  The remaining discrepancy from the
golden-mean Parry law is therefore a property of the limiting folded law, not
of the orbit length.

For the golden-mean shift this limiting comparison is unusually explicit.
Parry cylinder weights collapse to three endpoint classes, and this gives an
exact finite-\(m\) identity for \(\KL(\nu\|\pi_m)\), as well as two-sided
R\'enyi bounds uniform in the order.  The associated support problem is also
finite-dimensional: the largest Parry mass of a \(k\)-point set is obtained by
filling endpoint classes in decreasing weight.  If the Sturmian weights are
kept fixed and only injective placements of the realized words in \(X_m\) are
allowed, then for \(m\ge 6\) the optimum is obtained by placing all words in
the heaviest endpoint class \(X_m^{00}\), and
\[
\inf_{\iota} D_q(\iota_*\mu_m\|\pi_m)
=(m-1)\log\varphi-\log\pi(0)-H_q(\mu_m).
\]
For bounded-type slopes the optimal value satisfies
\[
\inf_{\iota}D_q(\iota_*\mu_m\|\pi_m)
=
m\log\varphi-\log m+O_\alpha(1),
\]
uniformly in \(q\).  The canonical folded law is never more than
\(2\log\varphi\) above this optimum.

All of the separate ingredients are classical.  Sturmian codings, factor
complexity and factor frequencies go back to
\cite{MorseHedlund1940,Slater1950DistributionIntegers,Slater1967GapsSteps} and
are naturally part of symbolic dynamics and substitutions
\cite{LindMarcus1995,QueffelecSubstitution,Fogg2002,
DurandHostSkau1999Substitutional,Durand2000LinearlyRecurrentFactors}. The Parry measure and
the thermodynamic formalism of shifts of finite type are classical
\cite{Parry1964IntrinsicMarkovChains,LindMarcus1995,WaltersErgodicTheory,
ParryPollicott1990Zeta}. Fibonacci and Ostrowski numeration provide the
arithmetic background for the normalization
\cite{Zeckendorf1972,Frougny1991FibonacciFiniteAutomata,
Frougny1992NumbersAutomata,Berthe2001Ostrowski,
BaranwalaSchaefferShallit2021OstrowskiAutomatic}. The discrepancy and
empirical-divergence estimates use
\cite{KuipersNiederreiter1974,Goda2024QuasiUniformKronecker,
WeissmanEtAl2003L1Empirical,SasonVerdu2016fDivergence,
DemboZeitouni2010LargeDeviations}.

The contribution is the finite-resolution comparison obtained when these
standard structures are coupled by the canonical fold: the sharp
all-resolution classification of collision-free rotation windows, the exact Parry block
optimization, and the bounded-type asymptotic window for the canonical folded
law.  The five advertised contributions are represented in the body as
follows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The collision-free window classification is
Theorem~\ref{thm:window-injectivity-classification}.  Its canonical Sturmian
specialization and higher-block conjugacy are
Corollary~\ref{thm:sturmian-no-collision-rigidity},
Corollary~\ref{cor:sturmian-exact-relabeling}, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:sequence-level-transparency}.
\item The closed-form R\'enyi and optimal-injective comparison is
Theorem~\ref{thm:parry-kl-identity},
Proposition~\ref{prop:parry-renyi-window},
Theorem~\ref{thm:parry-support-barrier},
Theorem~\ref{thm:optimal-injective-relabeling},
Proposition~\ref{prop:small-m-endpoint-budget},
Corollary~\ref{cor:injective-relabeling-bounded-type}, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:canonical-golden-slope-gap}.
\item The limited primitive positive-entropy SFT restoration interface is the
support-envelope and injective-placement obstruction of
Proposition~\ref{prop:primitive-sft-support-envelope} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:primitive-sft-placement-barrier}; it is used only to explain
why a low-complexity finite-block Sturmian factor cannot remove an
exponential Parry cylinder budget.
\item The canonicity of the Fibonacci weighting is
Theorem~\ref{thm:local-zeckendorf-normalization} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:local-normalization-church-rosser} together with
Theorem~\ref{thm:contiguous-fibonacci-uniqueness}, with
Proposition~\ref{prop:canonical-fold-basic} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:fold-residue-characterization} giving the resulting
finite-resolution residue interface.
\item The sampling-versus-limit separation is Theorem~\ref{thm:tv-certificate-hist},
Theorem~\ref{thm:general-window-relative-entropy-certificate},
Proposition~\ref{prop:sturmian-microstate-kl-certificate},
Corollary~\ref{cor:rotation-folded-kl-certificate},
Theorem~\ref{thm:multiscale-residual-by-boundary}, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:multiscale-residual-comparison-bound}.
\end{enumerate}

Section~2 sets up the rotation model, the fold and the Parry block law.
Section~3 proves the sampling and relative-entropy estimates.  Section~4
contains the sharp window-rigidity classification and the Sturmian conjugacy
statements, including the transfer to circle maps that are topologically
conjugate to irrational rotations.
Section~5 contains the Parry comparison, including the support minima, the
injective placement problem, and a secondary endpoint-penalty refinement.
Section~6 records brief numerical checks illustrating the theorem chain.
