\begin{letter}{Editors \\ The Fibonacci Quarterly}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit the note \emph{A Two-Letter Criterion for Fibonacci Folding of
Rotation Words} for consideration by \emph{The Fibonacci Quarterly}.

For a binary word of length \(m\), we assign the Fibonacci weights
\(F_2,\ldots,F_{m+1}\), take the Zeckendorf expansion of its value, and
retain the first \(m\) digits.  The note determines exactly when this map is
injective on all finite languages obtained by coding an irrational rotation
with a single interval.

The result is deliberately narrow.  If
\(\delta=\min\{\alpha,1-\alpha\}\), injectivity at every word length holds
exactly for window lengths \(\beta\leq\delta\) or
\(\beta\geq1-\delta\).  Outside that range, failure already occurs at length
two because the realized words \(00\) and \(11\) have the same folded image.
The proof consists of an interval-overlap test and an explicit congruence for
Fibonacci values modulo \(F_{m+2}\).  The Sturmian coding
\(\beta=\alpha\) is a boundary case and is collision-free at every length.

The manuscript is self-contained apart from Zeckendorf's theorem, contains
one principal observation, and has no numerical or supplementary component.
Neither the manuscript nor any substantial part of it is under
consideration elsewhere.  Both authors have approved the submission.

\closing{Sincerely,}
