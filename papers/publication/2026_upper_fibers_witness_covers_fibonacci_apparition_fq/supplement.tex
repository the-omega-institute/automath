\begin{abstract}
We give a fixed-total-weight comparison for the cover polynomial used in the
article and document the finite computations of its rank level sets, minimal
generators, squarefree sector, and support bounds.  The computations are
independent checks of finite instances and are not used in the proofs of the
article's general theorems.
\end{abstract}
