
\begin{abstract}
For each positive integer $n$, let $B_n$ denote the set of divisors of the
Fibonacci number $F_n$ whose rank of apparition under the map $\alpha$ equals
$n$, and let $\mathcal M_n$ be its minimal elements. We classify
$\mathcal M_n$ exactly by irredundant witness covers of the prime coordinates
of $n$. This structural reduction is then weighted by the multiplicities
$a(d)=\#\{p:\alpha(p)=d\}$ of exact-rank primes. On every odd layer we
construct a rank-pure sector with the exact weighted count
\[
 \sum_{\mathcal C\in
 \operatorname{MinCov}(\mathcal P(n))}
 \prod_{S\in\mathcal C}a(n_S)
 \leq\#\mathcal M_n .
\]
Thus any almost-all upper equivalent must control the simultaneous exact-rank
multiplicities in this weighted cover sum. If $k=\omega(n)$ and $R(n)$ is the largest
atomic rank-window multiplicity, then unconditionally
\[
 \frac{\log2}{4}k^2-O(1)
 \leq\log\#\mathcal M_n
 \leq\frac{\log2}{4}k^2+k\log R(n)+O(k).
\]
The requested almost-all equivalent with coefficient $\log2/4$ is therefore
not proved. It would require an explicit $o((\log\log n)^2)$ bound for the
logarithmic excess of the weighted cover sum over its unweighted counterpart
on almost all odd indices, together with upper control of the complementary
non-rank-pure sector. We isolate these as the open arithmetic interface. We
prove that collecting exact ranks into
support windows costs only $O(x\log\log x)$ in the relevant weighted mean, and
a fibotomic argument gives the unconditional pointwise estimate
$a(d)\leq(\log\varphi/2+o(1))\phi_{\rm E}(d)/\log d$, where
$\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$. These estimates do not control the weighted-cover
excess. Connected factorizations, exact support spectra, low-support
classifications, and finite computations are placed in the supplement.
\end{abstract}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Fibonacci numbers, rank of apparition, primitive
divisors, exact-rank primes, minimal covers, divisor lattices.
