
\begin{abstract}
For each positive integer $n$, let $B_n$ denote the set of divisors of the
Fibonacci number $F_n$ whose rank of apparition under the map $\alpha$ equals
$n$. We describe $B_n$ as an upper set in the divisor lattice of $F_n$ and
classify its minimal elements by irredundant witness covers of the prime
coordinates of $n$. The resulting support budget $\omega(m)\leq\omega(n)$ is
best possible as a uniform bound. We determine the complete total and
connected support spectra on every layer: the total spectrum misses its top
value exactly for even squarefree indices, while the connected spectrum has
one additional explicit rank-$6$ obstruction family. We construct a rank-pure exact-rank-prime
sector that embeds every irredundant cover of the prime coordinates except for
at most two explicitly identified exceptional supports, preserving the complete
support hypergraph. If $k=\omega(n)$ and $R(n)$ is the largest number of
atomic prime powers with one fixed essential/full support pair, then, uniformly
as $k\to\infty$,
\[
 \log\#\mathcal M_n^{\rm conn}\geq
 \frac{\log 2}{4}k^2+k\log2-\frac12\log k+O(1),
 \qquad
 \log\#\mathcal M_n
 \leq \frac{\log 2}{4}k^2+k\log R(n)+O(k).
\]
The lower bound has an explicit parity-dependent Jacobi theta constant and is
realized by connected minimal generators. It does not imply that disconnected
generators are negligible in the full arithmetic count. The upper estimate is
not an asymptotic formula without additional control of the unresolved quantity
$R(n)$. We prove that the mean contribution from aggregating several exact-rank
classes in one support window is only $O(x\log\log x)$, reducing the mean
interface to a visible single-rank maximum. We also show that a published
conjecture of Bugeaud--Luca--Mignotte--Siksek would contradict the resulting
mean sparse-window hypothesis, and give a blockwise condition under which
disconnected generators are exponentially negligible. Finally, we classify
the cases $\omega(n)\leq3$ and prove unique connected-block factorizations for
$B_n$ and its minimal elements.
For the remaining single-rank maximum, a fibotomic argument gives the
unconditional pointwise estimate
$a(d)\leq(\log\varphi/2+o(1))\phi_{\rm E}(d)/\log d$, where
$\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ and $\phi_{\rm E}$ is Euler's function, together with
new unconditional upper and lower bounds for its weighted mean contribution.
\end{abstract}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Fibonacci numbers, rank of apparition, divisor
lattices, upper sets, witness covers, atomic decompositions.
