
\begin{abstract}
For $n\geq2$, let $\mathcal M_n$ be the divisibility-minimal part of the
exact fiber $\{q:\alpha(q)=n\}$ of the Fibonacci rank-of-apparition map.
We classify $\mathcal M_n$ by witness covers: pairwise coprime rank-jump
prime powers whose apparition ranks cover the prime coordinates of $n$ and
whose factors each retain a private coordinate after all other factors are
fixed.  Multiplication is a bijection from these covers to $\mathcal M_n$.
The classification incorporates Fibonacci prime-power lifting; for prime
jumps it specializes to Wagner's minimal multiplicative covers, while a
prime-power ladder replaces deletion of a label by lowering it.

Exact-rank primes give a rank-pure sector whose cardinality is an explicit
weighted sum over irredundant set covers, with weights
$a(d)=\#\{p:\alpha(p)=d\}$.  For every squarefree $n\geq3$, this sector is
exactly the squarefree part of $\mathcal M_n$.  Finally, if
$k=\omega(n)$ and $R(n)$ is the largest rank-jump multiplicity with fixed
coordinate data, then
$\log\#\mathcal M_n$ lies between
$(\log 2)k^2/4-O(1)$ and
$(\log 2)k^2/4+k\log R(n)+O(k)$.
\end{abstract}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Fibonacci numbers, rank of apparition, minimal
preimages, witness covers, squarefree fibers, weighted covers.
