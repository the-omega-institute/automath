
\begin{abstract}
For each positive integer $n$, let $B_n=\alpha^{-1}(n)$ be the ordinary level
set of the Fibonacci rank-of-apparition map, and let $\mathcal M_n$ be its
divisibility-minimal elements. We classify
$\mathcal M_n$ exactly by irredundant witness covers of the prime coordinates
of $n$. For prime jumps this is the unordered form of Wagner's minimal
multiplicative cover condition; Fibonacci prime-power ladders replace deletion
of a label by lowering it. We prove the prime-power lifting, prime--ladder
dichotomy, and unique ladder-slot formulas used in the classification and its
upper bounds. The structural reduction is weighted by the multiplicities
$a(d)=\#\{p:\alpha(p)=d\}$ of exact-rank primes. On every odd layer we
construct a rank-pure sector with the exact weighted count
\[
 \sum_{\mathcal C\in
 \operatorname{MinCov}(\mathcal P(n))}
 \prod_{S\in\mathcal C}a(n_S)
 \leq\#\mathcal M_n .
\]
On every squarefree index $n\ge3$ this constructed sector is canonical: it is
exactly the squarefree part of $\mathcal M_n$, equivalently the minimal
squarefree part of $\alpha^{-1}(n)$. For every $n\ge3$ it is also in canonical
bijection with the minimal transversals of a coordinate hypergraph. This
hypergraph and its associated split graph encode the weighted cover sum; no
new theorem about general transversals or split graphs is claimed. Thus any
almost-all upper equivalent must control
the simultaneous exact-rank
multiplicities in this weighted cover sum. If $k=\omega(n)$ and $R(n)$ is the largest
rank-window multiplicity, then unconditionally
\[
 \frac{\log2}{4}k^2-O(1)
 \leq\log\#\mathcal M_n
 \leq\frac{\log2}{4}k^2+k\log R(n)+O(k).
\]
The requested almost-all equivalent with coefficient $\log2/4$ is therefore
not proved. It would require an explicit $o((\log\log n)^2)$ bound for the
logarithmic excess of the weighted cover sum over its unweighted counterpart
on almost all odd indices, together with upper control of the complementary
non-rank-pure sector, which is the nonsquarefree sector on squarefree indices.
We isolate these as the open arithmetic interface. We
prove that collecting exact ranks into
support windows costs only $O(x\log\log x)$ in the relevant weighted mean, and
the standard Fibonacci cyclotomic integer $\Psi_d(1)$ gives the unconditional pointwise estimate
$a(d)\leq(\log\varphi/2+o(1))\phi_{\rm E}(d)/\log d$, where
$\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$. Combining its classical full primitive part with this
count proves a different theorem: for every $\varepsilon>0$ and every
sufficiently large $n$, either $F_n$ has a primitive prime divisor at least
$n^{2-\varepsilon}$, or it has a repeated primitive divisor, necessarily a
Wall--Sun--Sun prime at exact rank $n$. Thus the nonexistence of
Wall--Sun--Sun primes implies $P_{\rm prim}(F_n)\geq n^{2-o(1)}$.
These estimates do not control the weighted-cover
excess. A sharp extremal theorem shows that the cumulative datum
$\omega(F_n)=\sum_{d\mid n}a(d)$ alone cannot do so: at fixed total weight,
concentration on the full support minimizes the weighted cover sum exactly.
As a direct corollary of classical exact-rank prime existence, we answer two
questions of FitzGibbons--Javaheri--Miller--Verga affirmatively in the stronger
prime form; no new prime-existence input is proved here.
Connected factorizations, exact support spectra, low-support
classifications, and finite checks are placed in the supplement.
\end{abstract}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Fibonacci numbers, rank of apparition, primitive
divisors, exact-rank primes, minimal transversals, divisor lattices.
