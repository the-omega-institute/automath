\section{Finite verification}\label{sec:numerical}

Table~\ref{tab:birth-layer-data-generated} gives
$A(n)=\#B_n$ and $\#\mathcal M_n$ for $2\leq n\leq30$.  It is an
illustration of the classification, not an input to its proof.

\begin{proposition}[Finite divisor/rank verification]
\label{prop:effective-birth-layer-computation}
Let $n\geq2$ and suppose the integer factorization of $F_n$ is known.  The
following procedure recovers $B_n$, $A(n)$, and $\mathcal M_n$ exactly.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Enumerate the divisors $D_n$ of $F_n$ and the divisors of $n$.
\item For every $q\in D_n$, compute
\[
 \alpha_n(q):=\min\{d\mid n:q\mid F_d\}.
\]
\item Set $B_n=\{q\in D_n:q\geq2,\ \alpha_n(q)=n\}$ and
$A(n)=\#B_n$.
\item Process $B_n$ in increasing order and retain $m$ precisely when no
previously retained element divides $m$.
\end{enumerate}
The retained set in (d) is $\mathcal M_n$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the rank-divisibility criterion, every $q\mid F_n$ satisfies
$\alpha(q)\mid n$, and
\[
 \alpha(q)=\min\{d\mid n:q\mid F_d\}=\alpha_n(q).
\]
Thus (c) gives the exact fiber $B_n$.  For (d), induct on the processing
order.  Every discarded element has a previously retained divisor, while a
retained element is its own retained divisor.  Hence an element is retained
exactly when it has no smaller divisor in $B_n$, which is minimality in the
divisibility order.
\end{proof}

The reproducibility archive supplies two independent constructions through

The structural route continues through $n=210$ and checks the rank-pure

finite support bounds, and the exact-rank prime counts used in the article.
The stored factorizations are multiplied back to the corresponding Fibonacci
numbers before any check is run.  Separate unit tests cover the arithmetic
routines, archive round trips, minimal-cover enumeration, and the
fixed-total-weight comparison in Proposition~
\ref{prop:sharp-cumulative-data-obstruction}.  All computations use exact
integer arithmetic and report their interpreter and factorization-library
versions.
