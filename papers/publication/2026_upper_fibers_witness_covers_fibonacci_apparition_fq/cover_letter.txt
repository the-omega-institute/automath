Dear Editor,

We submit "Minimal preimages of the Fibonacci rank map: squarefree fibers
and weighted covers" for consideration in The Fibonacci Quarterly.

For each positive integer n, the article studies the exact fiber of the
Fibonacci rank-of-apparition map and its divisibility-minimal elements. Its
main result classifies those elements by irredundant witness covers. Fibonacci
prime-power lifting distinguishes prime jumps from ladder jumps, and the
classification identifies a canonical squarefree sector with an exact
weighted cover count. The article concludes with finite support bounds that
separate the combinatorial quadratic term from the remaining exact-rank prime
multiplicities.

The submission consists of the article, a short computational supplement,
and a reproducibility archive containing exact factorization inputs, tests,
stored reports, and checksums.
