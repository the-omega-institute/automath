## Mathematical Scope

The article classifies the divisibility-minimal exact fibers of the Fibonacci
rank map by witness covers. Its central consequences are the Fibonacci
prime-power realization, the rank-pure weighted cover formula, the exact
squarefree-sector identification, and finite support bounds with residual
exact-rank multiplicity `R(n)`. Wagner's minimal multiplicative covers and
classical exact-rank prime existence are cited inputs.
