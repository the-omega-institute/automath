\begin{corollary}\label{cor:odd-primitive-cover-realization}
Let $n\ge3$ be odd. For each nonempty $S\subseteq\mathcal P(n)$ put
\[
n_S=\prod_{\ell\in S}\ell^{\nu_\ell(n)}.
\]
Choose an exact-rank prime $\pi_S$ with $\alpha(\pi_S)=n_S$, whose existence is
guaranteed by Theorem~\ref{article-thm:primitive-divisors}. Then every set partition
$\Pi$ of $\mathcal P(n)$ gives a minimal generator
\[
m_\Pi:=\prod_{C\in\Pi}\pi_C\in\mathcal M_n.
\]
Thus
\[
\#\mathcal M_n\ge \mathsf B_{\omega(n)},
\]
and, since the minimal generators form an antichain in the divisor lattice,
\[
\widthop(B_n)\ge \#\mathcal M_n\ge \mathsf B_{\omega(n)},
\]
where $\mathsf B_k$ is the $k$th Bell number.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Since $n$ is odd and $n\ge3$, every nonempty $n_S$ is odd and at least $3$;
therefore $n_S\notin\{6,12\}$, and Theorem~\ref{article-thm:primitive-divisors}
supplies the required exact-rank prime $\pi_S$. For each block $C$, the prime
$\pi_C$ is a prime atom of rank $n_C$ and
therefore belongs to $\mathcal M_{n_C}^{\mathrm{conn}}$: its support
hypergraph has the single edge $C$. Distinct blocks have coprime Fibonacci
ambient factors by Lemma~\ref{thm:coprime-embedding}, so the primes
$\pi_C$ are pairwise coprime. Theorem~\ref{thm:mingenerator-connected-factorization}
then gives $m_\Pi\in\mathcal M_n$.

The connected-component partition of $m_\Pi$ is exactly $\Pi$, so different
set partitions give different minimal generators. There are
$\mathsf B_{\omega(n)}$ set partitions of $\mathcal P(n)$.
Finally, no two distinct elements of $\mathcal M_n$ are comparable under
divisibility: if $m,m'\in\mathcal M_n$ and $m\mid m'$ with $m\ne m'$, then
$m'$ is not minimal in the upper set $B_n$. Hence $\mathcal M_n$ is an
antichain in $B_n$, so $\widthop(B_n)\ge\#\mathcal M_n$.
\end{proof}
