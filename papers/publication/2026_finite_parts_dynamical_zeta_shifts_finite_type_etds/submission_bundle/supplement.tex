\begin{abstract}
We record the fixed-primitive-label correction to the Frobenius-class Mertens
constant for finite-group extensions of one-sided shifts of finite type,
together with a quotient-cover refinement and an exact strict-gap
\(S_3\) witness to the erroneous Artin substitution.
\end{abstract}
