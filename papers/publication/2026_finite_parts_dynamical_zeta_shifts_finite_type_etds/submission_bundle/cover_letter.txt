Dear Editors,

We submit "Finite radial determination of Frobenius profiles for elementary
two-group extensions of shifts of finite type" for consideration as a
Research Article in Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems.

The paper proves finite radial determination for elementary two-group
extensions over primitive edge shifts, allowing the two base shifts to be
different. The number of radial locations is independent of the group rank.
The inverse step combines determinant parity with Mahler special-value
theorems, effective rational-coboundary bounds, and finite Pade
reconstruction. The paper also gives an exact boundary collision and locates
the obstruction to extending the finite bound to arbitrary finite groups.

The priority claim is deliberately narrow. Ostrowski treats the linear
multiplicative equation, and Nishioka's 1985 theorem directly supplies
algebraic-solution rationality for the squared functional equation used here.
The claimed increment is parity-compatible algebraic collision lifting,
effective rational-coboundary bounds and reconstruction, and the cross-base
elementary-two-group theorem.

The submission includes separately compiled Supplementary Material containing
the Frobenius-class product-constant companion results, quotient-cover
refinements, and an exact S3 witness. It also includes a reproducibility
archive with all verification scripts, all unit tests, readable archived
outputs, exact certificates, literature audit, checksums, and reproduction
instructions. We ask that both attachments be made available to the referees.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests. No
research datasets were generated or analysed.
