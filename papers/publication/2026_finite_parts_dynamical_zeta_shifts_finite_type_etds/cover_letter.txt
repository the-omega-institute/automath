Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript "Adams--Mobius primitive inversion for finite-group extensions of shifts of finite type" for consideration as a Research Paper in Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems.

The manuscript develops a finite-group primitive determinant calculus for mixing subshifts of finite type. Its main contribution is an Adams--Mobius inversion mechanism in Peter--Weyl coordinates that converts twisted determinant data into primitive class channels, together with Perron-point class Mertens constants under the twisted spectral gap hypothesis. The paper also separates the scalar finite-part and canonical cyclic-lift calculations from the non-abelian primitive profile, clarifying which information is genuinely non-abelian.

We believe the manuscript fits the scope of Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems because it concerns symbolic dynamics, dynamical zeta functions, periodic orbit data, finite-group extensions, and orbit-counting constants.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved this submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
