Dear Editors,

We submit the substantially refocused manuscript "Adams-corrected
Frobenius-class product constants for finite-group extensions of one-sided
shifts of finite type" for consideration as a Research Paper in Ergodic
Theory and Dynamical Systems.

The paper determines the finite-state constant in Frobenius-class Mertens
products. Its central point is that the product repeats the scalar orbit
weight while keeping the primitive Frobenius label fixed. The relevant
coordinate is therefore the fixed-label Euler transform F_rho, recovered from
twisted determinants by Adams--Mobius inversion, rather than the periodic
Artin logarithm L_rho, which powers the label under repetition.

The manuscript states a formal correction of the character-dependent constant
in Mohamed and Noorani's 1999 Frobenius-class product theorem. It identifies
the precise substitution in their proof, retains the classical exponent and
scalar normalization, and replaces the Artin coordinate by F_rho. An exact
strict-gap S_3 extension gives a direct contradiction to the uncorrected
formula: its sign Artin logarithm vanishes, whereas its fixed-label sign
coordinate is strictly negative.

Inverse determinant questions are no longer presented as a novelty claim.
They are confined to a background comparison with conjugacy-class periodic
data, gain-graph G-cospectrality, and switching theory, with explicit
acknowledgment of the relevant literature. The named-edge bouquet
multiplicity is identified as presentation-relative, and the symbol-
permutation example is not claimed to give nonconjugate extensions.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved this submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
