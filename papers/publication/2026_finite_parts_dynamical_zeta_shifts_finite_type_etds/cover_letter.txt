Dear Editors,

We submit "Linear radial determination for unit-Adams-invariant abelian
prime-power extensions of shifts of finite type" for consideration as a
Research Article in Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems.

The paper proves an unconditional linear inverse theorem for one-step
extensions of primitive edge shifts. Let G be a finite abelian ell-group,
where ell is prime, and consider a relatively unit-Adams-invariant pair of
G-cocycles over bases with v and v' vertices. If the primitive data agree
through length L and the full element-profile vectors agree at K distinct
radii in the common open Perron interval, one algebraic, then

    K + L >= max{v,v'}

forces equality of all primitive length-element data. The bases may differ,
and no twisted spectral gap is required. Thus V radial locations suffice for
bases of size at most V, independently of the group rank and exponent. An
explicit common-base binary family gives the complementary linear lower
bound, proving that the universal binary sampling number has exact order
Theta(V).

The arithmetic step uses only verified published inputs. An elementary
coefficient-denominator estimate and Kumiko Nishioka's special-value theorem
first force algebraicity of the critical Mahler product from one algebraic
collision. Logarithmic differentiation then produces an algebraic linear
Mahler function, which is rational by the Nishioka rational-transcendental
dichotomy and its Bell-Coons-Rowland restatement. A divisor congruence under
the power map eliminates residual finite monodromy and proves rationality of
the original critical product. No separate nonlinear rationality hypothesis
is assumed.

The supporting Mahler results include the sharp squarefree estimate
2(p-1) deg rad(AB) <= D, the resulting collision-jet inequality, and an
input-only O_p(D log D) total-degree bound for the multiplicative certificate.
The latter has matching abstract and dynamically realizable lower-bound
families. Explicit height and fixed-base bit-complexity estimates complete
the effective treatment. Bare existence and decidability of rational
certificates remain attributed to published additive and linear Mahler
algorithms, and the finite Pade step is described as rational reconstruction
after a degree cap is known.

The priority claim is deliberately narrow. No novelty is claimed for general
algebraic-solution rationality, Mahler special-value theory,
Frobenius-class Euler products, or the periodic-data dictionary. The new end
result is the unconditional dynamical inverse theorem, its collision-jet
strengthening, and the realizable linear sampling bounds. The paper also
locates the obstruction beyond the prime-primary unit-Adams class: already
for C3, Adams-Mobius inversion couples character determinants at infinitely
many arithmetically distributed powers.

ETDS is the coherent home because the submission is led by a finite-data
rigidity theorem for group extensions of shifts of finite type and by exact
dynamical lower-bound constructions. The Mahler component supplies the
arithmetic mechanism and sharp quantitative control, rather than displacing
the dynamical focus.

The submission includes separately compiled Supplementary Material containing
the Frobenius-class product-constant companion results, quotient-cover
refinements, and an exact S3 witness. It also includes a reproducibility
archive with all verification scripts, unit tests, readable archived outputs,
exact certificates, literature audit, checksums, and reproduction
instructions. We ask that both attachments be made available to the referees.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved the submission. The authors declare no competing interests. No
research datasets were generated or analysed.
