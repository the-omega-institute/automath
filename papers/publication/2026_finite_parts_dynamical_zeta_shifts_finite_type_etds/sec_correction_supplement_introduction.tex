\section{Purpose of the supplement}

This supplement contains the Frobenius-class Mertens correction and its
finite-state consequences that motivated the radial profiles studied in the
main paper.  For a conjugacy class \(C\subset G\), write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-class-product}
 P_C(N):=
 \prod_{\substack{\gamma\in\mathcal P,\ |\gamma|\le N\\g_\gamma\in C}}
 \left(1-\lambda^{-|\gamma|}\right).
\end{equation}
The periodic Artin logarithm
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-artin}
 L_\phi(z)=\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal P}\sum_{r\ge1}
 \frac{\phi(g_\gamma^r)}r z^{r|\gamma|}
\end{equation}
and the fixed-label logarithm
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-fixed}
 F_\phi(z)=\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal P}\sum_{r\ge1}
 \frac{\phi(g_\gamma)}r z^{r|\gamma|}
\end{equation}
coincide in the scalar channel but not for general finite groups.  The
sections below prove the corrected fixed-label constant, identify the
quotient-cover factor missed by the Artin substitution, and give an exact
strict-gap \(S_3\) witness.  Product existence and the exponent
\(|C|/|G|\) are prior work; only the constant coordinate and the stated
finite-state refinements are at issue here.
