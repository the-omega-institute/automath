\section{Exact certificates and reproducibility}\label{sec:exactness}

\subsection{An exact strict-gap test}

Let \(\widetilde A\) be the non-negative integral adjacency matrix of the
regular skew product on \(V\times G\).  Its Peter--Weyl block decomposition
contains \(A_\rho\) with multiplicity \(\dim\rho\).  Thus the strict gap is
decidable from one integer characteristic polynomial, but the field in which
the Perron factor is removed must be stated correctly.

\begin{proposition}[Algebraic-number certificate for the strict gap]
\label{prop:exact-gap-certificate}
Let
\[
  \chi_{\widetilde A}(t)=\det(tI-\widetilde A)\in\ZZ[t],
  \qquad K=\QQ(\lambda).
\]
Division by the Perron factor is performed in \(K[t]\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gap-division-field}
  q(t):=\chi_{\widetilde A}(t)/(t-\lambda)\in K[t].
\end{equation}
Then \(\eta<\lambda\) if and only if every root \(\alpha\) of \(q\) satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exact-root-comparison}
  \alpha\overline\alpha<\lambda^2.
\end{equation}
An exact certificate fixes the following compatible data:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=*]
  \item a squarefree polynomial \(f_\lambda\in\ZZ[t]\) and a rational
    isolating interval selecting \(\lambda\), thereby fixing the embedding
    \(K=\QQ(\lambda)\hookrightarrow\RR\);
  \item a tower from \(K\) to a splitting field \(L\) of \(q\), with defining
    polynomials and isolating rectangles for the successive generators, which
    fixes an embedding \(L\hookrightarrow\CC\) extending the chosen embedding
    of \(K\);
  \item a factorization of \(q\) in \(L[t]\), including multiplicities, and
    pairwise disjoint rational isolating rectangles for every embedded root
    \(\alpha_j\), together with an index \(j^*\) whose rectangle isolates
    \(\overline{\alpha_j}\);
  \item for every \(j\), the real algebraic number
    \[
      \beta_j:=\lambda^2-\alpha_j\overline{\alpha_j}\in L\cap\RR,
    \]
    a defining polynomial and a rational isolating interval contained in
    \((0,\infty)\);
  \item multiplicity data for \(\chi_{\widetilde A}\), its trivial block, and
    \(q\), verifying that exactly one simple trivial Perron root \(\lambda\)
    is removed and that every other root occurrence is represented.
\end{enumerate}
For a machine-readable record, integers are coefficient arrays, rationals are
reduced pairs \([p,q]\) with \(q>0\), real intervals are pairs of rationals,
and complex rectangles are pairs of real intervals.  Tower elements are
coefficient arrays in the declared power bases.  Each root record has six
named fields: \texttt{factor}, \texttt{multiplicity}, \texttt{rectangle},
\texttt{conjugate\_index}, \texttt{beta\_polynomial}, and
\texttt{beta\_interval}.
A verifier checks the tower relations and embeddings, the factorization
identity, disjoint root isolation, conjugate-rectangle pairing, the sum of
multiplicities, and positivity of every \texttt{beta\_interval}.  Sturm or
subresultant sign determination supplies the exact isolations.  Floating-point
root moduli are not certificate data.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The regular representation decomposes as
\(\bigoplus_{\rho\in\Irr(G)}(\dim\rho)\rho\).  Consequently the spectrum of
\(\widetilde A\), with algebraic multiplicity, is the union of the spectra of
the \(A_\rho\), each repeated \(\dim\rho\) times.  The trivial block is \(A\).
Primitivity makes \(\lambda\) a simple root and puts every other root of that
block strictly inside the Perron circle.  Removing this root in
\(\QQ(\lambda)[t]\) therefore leaves all non-trivial twisted roots together
with the harmless non-Perron base roots.  Condition
\eqref{eq:exact-root-comparison} for every remaining root is precisely
\(\max_{\rho\neq\mathbf1}\rad(A_\rho)<\lambda\).

The compatible embeddings make conjugation, multiplication, and sign
determination unambiguous.  Since each
\(\lambda^2-\alpha_j\overline{\alpha_j}\) is real algebraic, its isolating
interval decides its sign.  The multiplicity record prevents the removal of
any non-trivial occurrence of \(\lambda\).
\end{proof}
