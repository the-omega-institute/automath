\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The theorem of Mohamed and Noorani requires two independent corrections.
Primitivity of the base does not imply Frobenius equidistribution: the
trivial \(C_2\)-cocycle shows that an extension-level
transitivity/aperiodicity condition is necessary.  Under the strict twisted
gap used here, the exponent is \(|C|/|G|\).  At the constant level, the
Frobenius-class product fixes the primitive label \(g_\gamma\), whereas the
periodic Artin logarithm replaces it by \(g_\gamma^r\) on the \(r\)-fold
traversal.  The correct coordinate is therefore \(F_\rho\), with the
finite-determinant formula in \eqref{eq:perron-fixed-determinant}; the strict-gap
\(S_3\) sign channel proves that it cannot generally be replaced by
\(L_\rho\).

The discrepancy is universal at the identity class, not confined to the
\(S_3\) witness.  The quotient-cover factor in
\eqref{eq:identity-class-universal-error} is strictly greater than one for
every non-trivial strict-gap extension and records precisely the primitive
orbits whose lifts close only after repeated traversal.  After the constant is
corrected, \eqref{eq:harmonic-renormalized-product} separates the universal
harmonic factor from an exponentially small system-dependent remainder.

The vector of corrected constants is nevertheless not an inverse invariant
of the primitive class profile.  The fixed-graph \(C_2\) pair in
\Cref{thm:c2-boundary-collision} has different length-one data but identical
constants, because the dyadic boundary functional annihilates a non-trivial
determinant coboundary at the Perron point.  This collision is strictly weaker
than full \(G\)-cospectrality.  In the realizable same-base strict-gap
determinant-ratio class, \Cref{thm:determinant-boundary-lifting} identifies this
kernel exactly with the normalized rational coboundaries; its parity hypothesis
prevents an extension of that statement here to arbitrary positive rational
functions.  Within its coprime polynomial input class,
\Cref{thm:effective-rational-mahler-coboundary} decides this rational
coboundary condition by one finite Pad\'e system and supplies explicit output
bounds; \Cref{cor:same-base-effective-rational-mahler} records the compatible
matrix specialization.  The degree bound has a direct inverse consequence:
\Cref{thm:finite-radial-sampling} identifies every real collision radius with
a zero of the certificate numerator-minus-denominator, bounds the algebraic
collision set by \(N-1\), and shows that fewer than \(16v^3\) prescribed
algebraic samples recover the complete profile for a same-base \(C_2\) pair
on \(v\) vertices.  For a general finite group, retaining an analytic radial
profile on a set with an interior accumulation point removes the loss by
\Cref{thm:radial-profile-recovery}; no claim of finite-sample optimality is
attached to that general enhancement.

For a homogeneous union of classes, the downstream constant is
\eqref{eq:homogeneous-union-constant}.  The M\"obius transform used to obtain
it is standard necklace/Witt calculus; the paper-specific point is its use on
the fixed-label coordinate before Adams powering.  The appendices record only
the determinant-derived radial convention and established periodic-data and
gain-graph translations needed to fix the scope of the main result.
