\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The Frobenius-class product fixes the primitive label \(g_\gamma\) while the
scalar logarithm repeats the weight \(\lambda^{-|\gamma|}\).  The periodic
Artin logarithm instead repeats the orbit and consequently replaces that
label by \(g_\gamma^r\).  Distinguishing these operations yields the
fixed-label coordinate \(F_\rho\), its Adams--M\"obius determinant formula,
and the corrected constant \eqref{eq:correct-product-fourier}.  The
\(S_3\) sign channel shows that the correction is necessary, not merely a
change of notation.

The inverse material has a different status.  Twisted-determinant equality is
the represented form of conjugacy-class periodic data and directed
\(G\)-cospectrality; switching is controlled by simultaneous conjugacy of a
finite fundamental-cycle tuple.  Those facts belong to the established
periodic-data and gain-graph framework.  The only multiplicity retained here
is the explicitly presentation-relative fibre cardinality
\(\mu_{\Gamma,G}([\tau])\), and the two-loop permutation example is not a
nonconjugate dynamical collision.

Two extensions of the focused result remain natural.  First, one may develop
the same correction at a non-trivial Perron peripheral boundary, using the
derived radial renormalization of \Cref{prop:derived-boundary}; that material
should be separated from the strict-gap theorem rather than encoded as extra
input data.  Second, an inverse question invariant under base automorphisms
would require a fibre on an automorphism quotient and examples not generated
by symbol permutations.  Neither extension is needed for, or claimed by, the
correction theorem proved here.
