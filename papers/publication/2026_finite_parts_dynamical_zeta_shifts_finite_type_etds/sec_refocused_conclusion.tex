\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The theorem of Mohamed and Noorani requires two independent corrections.
Primitivity of the base does not imply Frobenius equidistribution: the
trivial \(C_2\)-cocycle shows that an extension-level
transitivity/aperiodicity condition is necessary.  Under the strict twisted
gap used here, the exponent is \(|C|/|G|\).  At the constant level, the
Frobenius-class product fixes the primitive label \(g_\gamma\), whereas the
periodic Artin logarithm replaces it by \(g_\gamma^r\) on the \(r\)-fold
traversal.  The correct coordinate is therefore \(F_\rho\), with the
finite-determinant formula in \eqref{eq:perron-fixed-determinant}; the strict-gap
\(S_3\) sign channel proves that it cannot generally be replaced by
\(L_\rho\).

For a homogeneous union of classes, the downstream constant is
\eqref{eq:homogeneous-union-constant}.  The M\"obius transform used to obtain
it is standard necklace/Witt calculus; the paper-specific point is its use on
the fixed-label coordinate before Adams powering.  The appendices record only
the determinant-derived radial convention and established periodic-data and
gain-graph translations needed to fix the scope of the main result.
