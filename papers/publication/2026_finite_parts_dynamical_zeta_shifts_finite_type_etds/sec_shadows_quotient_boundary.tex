\section{Support boundaries: abelian shadows and cyclic observables}
\label{sec:shadows}

This section is a support boundary for the finite-state Perron-boundary
determinant package, specifically the coordinate readout
\Cref{thm:renormalized-coordinate-readout} and the obstruction
\Cref{thm:renormalized-artin-obstruction}.  After the renormalized constants
have already been constructed, Peter--Weyl orthogonality identifies the
abelianised part of the primitive profile and locates the part that no cyclic
quotient can see.  The final \(S_3\) model then exhibits the non-abelian
defect by exact rational windows.

The section is deliberately not a quotient-reconstruction or cyclic-detection
programme.  General quotient functoriality, abelian pullback formulae, and
root-layer comparisons for cyclic reset cocycles are natural sequel material;
here they are not needed as independent theorem inputs.  Cyclic quotients of
the fixed cocycle are kept separate from the canonical cyclic-lift
root-of-unity layer used later in \Cref{sec:finite-part}.  The only role of
the results below is to show where the fixed-label Euler vector, and hence the
Adams obstruction, escapes abelian or cyclic shadow data.

\subsection{Primitive profile and shadow notation}

\begin{definition}[Normalised primitive class profile]
  \label{def:normalised-primitive-class-profile}
  Under the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:class-mertens-explicit}, define the
  class function
  \[
    \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}(C):=\frac{|G|}{|C|}\Delta_C(A,\tau;G),
  \]
  where
  \(\Delta_C(A,\tau;G)=\mathcal M_C(A,\tau;G)-\frac{|C|}{|G|}\mathcal M(A)\).
\end{definition}

By \Cref{prop:class-mertens-deviations},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:normalised-primitive-class-profile}
  \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}(C)
  =
  \sum_{\varrho\neq \mathbf 1}
    \chi_\varrho^\ast(C)\,\Pi_\varrho(\lambda^{-1}).
\end{equation}

\noindent
The abelian shadow is the one-dimensional part of this expansion; the
non-abelian defect is the complementary higher-dimensional part:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:abelian-shadow-defect}
  \begin{aligned}
    \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}^{\mathrm{ab}}(C)
    &:=
    \sum_{\substack{\chi\in\Irr(G)\\ \dim\chi=1,\ \chi\neq \mathbf 1}}
      \chi^\ast(C)\,\Pi_\chi(\lambda^{-1}), \\
    \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}^{\mathrm{na}}(C)
    &:=
    \sum_{\substack{\varrho\in\Irr(G)\\ \dim\varrho>1}}
      \chi_\varrho^\ast(C)\,\Pi_\varrho(\lambda^{-1}).
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:abelian-shadow-defect-sum}
  \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}
  =
  \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}^{\mathrm{ab}}
  +
  \mathcal B_{A,\tau;G}^{\mathrm{na}}.
\end{equation}

\noindent
This notation is the only quotient-shadow infrastructure used below.  The
canonical cyclic lift in \Cref{sec:finite-part} is a fixed-matrix scalar
comparison model and is not a cyclic quotient observable of the fixed
finite-group cocycle.
