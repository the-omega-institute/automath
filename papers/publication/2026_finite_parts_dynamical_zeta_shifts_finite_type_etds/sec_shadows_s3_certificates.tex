\subsection{Strict-gap \texorpdfstring{\(S_3\)}{S3} witness for Artin substitution}
\label{subsec:s3-obstruction-certificate}

This subsection keeps only the finite data needed in the main text.  The
example is a strict-gap specialization of
\Cref{cor:strict-gap-specialization,thm:class-euler-product-mertens}: it proves
that replacing the fixed-label coordinates \(F_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})\) by the
periodic Artin logarithms \(\mathcal L_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})\) can fail.  It is
not an independent proof, test, or source of evidence for the full
Perron-boundary theorem
\Cref{thm:perron-boundary-determinant-product}.  That theorem was proved
earlier from the peripheral determinant package; the finite \(S_3\) arithmetic
below is only a reproducible witness for one downstream obstruction in the
strict-gap chart.

The full two-shift
\[
  A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \lambda=2,
\]
is labelled by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:s3-main-text-edge-labels}
  \tau(0\to0)=(23),\quad \tau(0\to1)=e,\quad
  \tau(1\to0)=(12),\quad \tau(1\to1)=(123)
\end{equation}
in \(S_3\).  In the sign and standard representations,
\Cref{prop:s3-example-channels} gives
\[
  A_\varepsilon=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  A_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}=
  \begin{pmatrix}
    \frac12&\frac32&1&0\\
    \frac12&-\frac12&0&1\\
    -1&0&-\frac12&-\frac32\\
    0&1&\frac12&-\frac12
  \end{pmatrix},
\]
with determinant polynomials
\[
  P_{\mathbf1}(z)=1-2z,\qquad
  P_\varepsilon(z)=1,\qquad
  P_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(z)=1+z .
\]
Thus \(\rho(A_\varepsilon)=0\),
\(\rho(A_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}})=1<2\), and the strict-gap hypotheses of
\Cref{cor:strict-gap-specialization,thm:class-euler-product-mertens} hold for
this model.  The same explicit calculation gives
\[
  \mathcal L_\varepsilon(z)\equiv0,\qquad
  \mathcal L_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(z)=-\log(1+z).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Rational windows for the \(S_3\) obstruction]
  \label{lem:s3-main-text-certificate-summary}
  In the \(S_3\) model \eqref{eq:s3-main-text-edge-labels}, the rational
  windows used in the main text are
  \[
    -\frac{342}{1000}<\Pi_\varepsilon(1/2)<-\frac{341}{1000},
    \qquad
    -\frac{719}{1000}<\Pi_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(1/2)<-\frac{718}{1000},
  \]
  and
  \[
    -\frac{381}{1000}<F_\varepsilon(1/2)<-\frac{380}{1000},
    \qquad
    -\frac{923}{1000}<F_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(1/2)<-\frac{922}{1000}.
  \]
  These are the only numerical certificate inputs used here.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
  The two primitive-channel windows are
  \eqref{eq:s3-example-certified-intervals}, proved in
  \Cref{lem:s3-example-perron-certificates} from the exact rational
  truncation-and-tail estimates in
  \Cref{lem:appendix-s3-sharp-certificates}.  The two product-coordinate
  windows are
  \eqref{eq:s3-f-epsilon-appendix-window} and
  \eqref{eq:s3-f-rho-appendix-window}, proved in
  \Cref{lem:appendix-s3-f-level-certificates}.  The exact reproducibility
  table is \Cref{prop:s3-compact-rational-verification-record}; it prints the
  denominator-cleared positive integers for all eight endpoint comparisons, so
  no separate certificate file is an analytic or publication-level input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strict-gap \(S_3\) specialization and Artin obstruction]
  \label{thm:s3-certified-nonabelian-defect}
  \label{thm:s3-adams-erasure-mechanism}
  \label{prop:s3-two-coordinate-product-obstruction-vector}
  \label{prop:s3-two-coordinate-product-obstruction-factorisation}
  \label{cor:s3-product-constant-obstruction-finite-state}
  For the \(S_3\) model \eqref{eq:s3-main-text-edge-labels}, the strict-gap
  product formula of \Cref{thm:class-euler-product-mertens} specializes as
  follows.  Let \(C_e=\{e\}\), let \(C_t\) be the transposition class, and let
  \(C_3\) be the class of three-cycles.  For
  \[
    H(C):=-\frac{6}{|C|}\log K_C-\bigl(\gamma+\log C(A)\bigr),
    \qquad C\in\{C_e,C_t,C_3\},
  \]
  one has
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:s3-product-obstruction-vector}
    H(C)=
    \chi_\varepsilon(C)F_\varepsilon(1/2)
    +\chi_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(C)F_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(1/2),
  \end{equation}
  with
  \[
    \chi_\varepsilon=(1,-1,1),\qquad
    \chi_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}=(2,0,-1)
    \quad\text{on }(C_e,C_t,C_3).
  \]
  The rational windows of \Cref{lem:s3-main-text-certificate-summary} give
  \[
    F_\varepsilon(1/2)\in\left(-\frac{381}{1000},-\frac{380}{1000}\right),
    \qquad
    F_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(1/2)\in
      \left(-\frac{923}{1000},-\frac{922}{1000}\right).
  \]
  Since \(\mathcal L_\varepsilon(1/2)=0\), the Adams-corrected product
  coordinate \(F_\varepsilon(1/2)\) cannot be replaced by the periodic Artin
  value in the strict-gap class-product constants.  Quantitatively, for at
  least one conjugacy class \(C\subset S_3\),
  \[
    \left|
      \frac{6}{|C|}
      \log\frac{K_C}{K_C^{\mathrm{Art}}}
    \right|
    >\frac{380}{1000}.
  \]
  The standard coordinate is a non-zero higher-dimensional Peter--Weyl
  component, hence is orthogonal to every class observable factoring through
  the abelianisation \(S_3\twoheadrightarrow C_2\).  This proves a concrete
  failure of naive Artin substitution and a concrete non-abelian shadow
  defect; it does not assert any minimal-observation theorem or any general
  finite-certificate theory beyond this model.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The determinant calculation above gives the strict twisted gap
  \(\eta<2\), so \Cref{thm:class-euler-product-mertens} applies with
  \(\lambda=2\).  Its strict-gap product-coordinate formula gives
  \[
    -\frac{6}{|C|}\log K_C-\bigl(\gamma+\log C(A)\bigr)
    =
    \sum_{\varrho\ne\mathbf1}\chi_\varrho^\ast(C)F_\varrho(1/2).
  \]
  The irreducible characters of \(S_3\) are the trivial, sign, and standard
  characters, and the non-trivial character values on
  \((C_e,C_t,C_3)\) are exactly the two displayed rows.  They are real-valued,
  so the Hermitian conjugation in the coefficient has no effect.  This proves
  \eqref{eq:s3-product-obstruction-vector}.

  The two fixed-label intervals are the product-coordinate part of
  \Cref{lem:s3-main-text-certificate-summary}.  In particular
  \(F_\varepsilon(1/2)\ne0\).  Since
  \(\mathcal L_\varepsilon(1/2)=0\), the discrepancy vector
  \(F_\varrho(1/2)-\mathcal L_\varrho(1/2)\) has sign component of absolute
  value \(>380/1000\).  The classwise lower-bound formula
  \eqref{eq:adams-obstruction-bound-lower-bound}, applied with
  \(G=S_3\) and \(\delta=380/1000\), gives the stated lower bound.

  The primitive sign window in \Cref{lem:s3-main-text-certificate-summary}
  records the Adams-erasure mechanism separately: the periodic sign logarithm
  vanishes, but the primitive sign channel is non-zero.  The standard
  primitive and product windows show that the two-dimensional Peter--Weyl
  sector is also present.  A class function on \(S_3\) factors through
  \(S_3\twoheadrightarrow C_2\) exactly when it lies in
  \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1,\varepsilon\}\), so character orthogonality
  puts \(\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}\) in the invisible higher-dimensional sector
  for all cyclic quotient observables.
\end{proof}
