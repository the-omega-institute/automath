\begin{abstract}
Let \(X_\Gamma^+\) be a mixing one-sided edge shift, let \(G\) be finite,
and let a one-step cocycle label each primitive orbit \(\gamma\) by a
conjugacy class \([g_\gamma]\).  We determine the constant in the
Frobenius-class Mertens product by separating two logarithms that coincide in
the scalar theory but not for general finite groups.  The periodic Artin
logarithm repeats both the orbit and its label,
\[
 L_\rho(z)=\sum_{r\geq1}\Pi_{\psi^r\chi_\rho}(z^r)/r,
 \qquad (\psi^r\chi_\rho)(g)=\chi_\rho(g^r),
\]
whereas the class product repeats the scalar weight while keeping the
primitive label fixed.  Its coordinate is therefore
\(F_\rho(z)=\sum_{r\geq1}\Pi_{\chi_\rho}(z^r)/r\).  Under the strict twisted
spectral gap, we prove an Adams--M\"obius determinant formula for
\(F_\rho(\lambda^{-1})\) and hence for every class-product constant.

This gives a formal correction of the Artin-\(L\) constant asserted by
Mohamed and Noorani (1999).  We locate the erroneous step: in passing from
the character expansion of a fixed Frobenius-class product to an Artin
logarithm, their proof replaces \(\chi_\rho(g_\gamma)\) by
\(\chi_\rho(g_\gamma^r)\) in the \(r\)-th logarithmic term.  A strict-gap
\(S_3\) extension makes the contradiction explicit: its sign Artin logarithm
vanishes identically, while its fixed-label coordinate is strictly negative.
The classical exponent \(|C|/|G|\) is unchanged; only the non-trivial
constant coordinates require correction.

For context, we also identify the determinant family with conjugacy-class
periodic data and with the directed gain-graph analogue of
\(G\)-cospectrality.  These equivalences, and the spanning-tree switching
criterion, are recorded as background consequences of established
periodic-data and gain-graph theory.  All inverse statements are explicitly
relative to a fixed marked-edge presentation; the associated determinant
fibre cardinality is not asserted to be an invariant of the underlying
dynamical system.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Guide to the references}
The classical orbit-counting and product scale is taken from
\cite{ParryPollicott1986Chebotarev,NooraniParry1992ChebotarevShifts,
Sharp1991Mertens,MeslizaNoorani1999FrobeniusMertens}; the last of these is the
specific theorem corrected here.  The inverse-data discussion is deliberately
background: it is placed in the framework of Boyle--Schmieding's complete
conjugacy-class periodic data and the gain-graph literature
\cite{BoyleSchmieding2017FiniteGroupExtensions,
CavaleriDAngeliDonno2021BalanceGainGraphs,CavaleriDonno2022Cospectrality,
AbiadBelardoKhramova2024Switching,Zaslavsky1989BiasedGraphs,
GrossTucker1987TopologicalGraphTheory}.
