\title[Adams--Mobius primitive inversion]{Adams--Mobius primitive inversion for finite-group extensions of shifts of finite type}

\begin{abstract}
For a mixing subshift of finite type carrying a finite-group cocycle, we
study how twisted finite-dimensional determinants determine primitive
orbit data by conjugacy class.  Periodic labels record powers of primitive
labels, so passing from periodic determinant logarithms to primitive
label series requires Adams operations \(g\mapsto g^m\) together with
M\"obius inversion.  This gives an explicit Peter--Weyl determinant
formula for the primitive channels.  Under the strict twisted spectral
gap \(\eta<\lambda\), these channels have Perron-point values and hence
class-indexed Mertens constants.  The resulting Frobenius-class Euler
products have the classical exponent \(|C|/|G|\), while their constants
are recovered from the fixed-label Euler transform, not from the periodic
Artin logarithm.  We prove the corresponding determinant rigidity and a
finite \(S_3\) certificate in which the explicit values
\(F_\varepsilon(1/2)\) and \(F_\rho(1/2)\) obstruct naive Artin
substitution at the product-constant level.  The scalar
finite-part and canonical cyclic-lift calculations are used only as
finite-matrix normalisation and comparison tools; they are separate from
the finite-group determinant and product theorems.
\end{abstract}
