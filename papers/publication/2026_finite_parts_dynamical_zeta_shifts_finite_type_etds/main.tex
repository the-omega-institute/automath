\begin{abstract}
Let \(X_\Gamma^+\) be a mixing one-sided edge shift, let \(G\) be finite,
and let a one-step cocycle label each primitive orbit \(\gamma\) by a
conjugacy class \([g_\gamma]\).  We determine the constant in the
Frobenius-class Mertens product by separating two logarithms that coincide in
the scalar theory but not for general finite groups.  The periodic Artin
logarithm repeats both the orbit and its label,
\[
 L_\rho(z)=\sum_{r\geq1}\Pi_{\psi^r\chi_\rho}(z^r)/r,
 \qquad (\psi^r\chi_\rho)(g)=\chi_\rho(g^r),
\]
whereas the class product repeats the scalar weight while keeping the
primitive label fixed.  Its coordinate is therefore
\(F_\rho(z)=\sum_{r\geq1}\Pi_{\chi_\rho}(z^r)/r\).  Under the strict twisted
spectral gap, we prove an Adams--M\"obius determinant formula for
\(F_\rho(\lambda^{-1})\) and hence for every class-product constant.

This corrects two independent defects in the theorem of Mohamed and Noorani
(1999).  First, mixing of the base alone does not imply Frobenius
equidistribution: an extension-level aperiodicity condition, imposed here as
the strict twisted gap, is also necessary.  Second, their constant replaces
\(\chi_\rho(g_\gamma)\) by \(\chi_\rho(g_\gamma^r)\) in the \(r\)-th
logarithmic term.  A trivial \(C_2\)-cocycle disproves the exponent under base
mixing alone, while a strict-gap \(S_3\) extension disproves the asserted
Artin coordinate.  Under the stated gap hypothesis the exponent is
\(|C|/|G|\), and the non-trivial constant coordinates are the fixed-label
values above.  We further identify the exact positive quotient-cover factor
missed by the Artin substitution, proving that it strictly underestimates the
identity-class constant in every non-trivial strict-gap extension.  Harmonic
renormalization gives an exponentially accurate product asymptotic for every
union of Frobenius classes.
\end{abstract}

theorem corrected here are
\cite{Noorani1995ChebotarevFiniteExtensions,

is represented by \cite{StarkTerras1996FiniteGraphsCoverings}.  The short
inverse-data appendix uses Boyle--Schmieding's conjugacy-class periodic data
and the gain-graph literature
\cite{BoyleSchmieding2017FiniteGroupExtensions,
