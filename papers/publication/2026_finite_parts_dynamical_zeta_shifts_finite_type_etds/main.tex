\title[Perron-boundary product coordinates]{Perron-boundary Adams--M\"obius renormalisation and product coordinates for finite-group extensions of shifts of finite type}

\author[Haobo Ma]{Haobo Ma}
\address{CHRONOAI}

\date{}
\renewcommand{\shortauthors}{Haobo Ma}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
For a mixing subshift of finite type carrying a finite-group cocycle, we
study how finite-dimensional twisted determinants determine primitive orbit
data by conjugacy class.  Periodic labels record powers of primitive labels,
so passing from periodic determinant logarithms to primitive label series
requires Adams operations \(g\mapsto g^m\) together with M\"obius
inversion.  This yields an explicit Peter--Weyl determinant formula for the
primitive channels.  The main Perron-boundary theorem keeps the full finite
peripheral spectrum \(\lambda\zeta\) of every twisted block, defines the
non-oscillatory multiplicities \(s_\varrho\), and renormalises the primitive
and fixed-label Euler transforms by subtracting exactly the
\(\zeta=1\) logarithms.  This gives class Mertens exponents
\(\delta_C\), not necessarily \(|C|/|G|\), and a branch-compatible
Adams-corrected determinant formula in which every boundary collision is
evaluated by a reduced determinant finite part.  The strict twisted spectral
gap \(\eta<\lambda\) is recovered as the clean specialization in which the
only Perron collision is the scalar trivial channel.
The Euler-product asymptotic itself is obtained internally from the additive
class estimate and the explicit product-tail reduction; its fixed-label
coordinates are \(F_\varrho^{\mathrm{ren}}\), not periodic Artin logarithms
\(\mathcal L_\varrho^{\mathrm{ren}}\).
We prove determinant coordinate readout for these constants and give a local
finite \(S_3\) witness, with explicit values \(F_\varepsilon(1/2)\) and
\(F_{\varrho_{\mathrm{std}}}(1/2)\), showing that naive Artin substitution fails at the
product-constant level.  Scalar finite parts and the canonical cyclic-lift root
layer serve only as finite-matrix normalisation and comparison devices; the
main result is the internally derived Adams-corrected symbolic-dynamical
determinant formula for the fixed-label constant coordinates, with the
classical Frobenius-class product theorems used only for comparison of
conventions.
\end{abstract}
