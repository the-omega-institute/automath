1. Overall assessment
REJECT in the present form, with encouragement to submit a substantially refocused manuscript.
The Adams-corrected Frobenius-product constant—especially Theorem 3.59 and the distinction between  $F_\rho$  and the periodic Artin logarithm  $L_\rho$ —appears mathematically valuable. I did not find a direct counterexample to that formula.
The principal problems are instead fundamental editorial and scholarly ones:


The advertised inverse-rigidity results substantially overlap with established periodic-data and gain-graph cospectrality theory.


The manuscript does not confront a direct contradiction between its corrected product constant and the Artin- $L$  constant in the cited 1999 theorem.


Its principal counterexamples and abelian-bouquet multiplicity are produced entirely by permutations of named edges; they disappear after quotienting by the corresponding base-shift automorphisms.


The claimed “intrinsic obstruction” is presentation-relative and, as defined, essentially the cardinality of the fibre it purports to explain.


The manuscript is 155 pages long, with extensive repetition and multiple repackagings of the same elementary Fourier/spectral identities.


A publishable paper may exist here, but it should be a much shorter article centred on the corrected Adams-coordinate product constant and the precise correction to the earlier Frobenius-class Mertens formula.

5. Concrete mathematical repairs
B1: Reposition the inverse theorem
The manuscript should state explicitly:

$$\{P_{\rho,\tau}(z)\}_{\rho\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)}
\Longleftrightarrow
\left\{\kappa\!\left(\operatorname{tr}(A_\tau^n)\right)\right\}_{n\ge1}
\Longleftrightarrow
\{N_{n,C}(\tau)\}_{n,C}.$$

The first equivalence is finite character Fourier inversion; the second is the definition of conjugacy-class periodic data. Then

$$N_{n,C}=\sum_{d\mid n}d
 \sum_{\substack{D\\D^{\,n/d}=C}}p_{d,D}$$

is triangular, with leading term  $np_{n,C}$ , hence determines the primitive profile. Presented this way, Theorem 3.82 is a concise corollary of known periodic-data theory plus Adams–Möbius inversion, not a headline rigidity theorem.
B2: State the correction to the classical product formula
For  $g_\gamma=\tau(\gamma)$ , define the truncated primitive channel

$$\Pi_{\chi,N}(z)=
\sum_{\substack{\gamma\in\mathcal P\\|\gamma|\le N}}
\chi(g_\gamma)z^{|\gamma|}.$$

Then the exact product identity is

$$-\log P_C(N)
=
\frac{|C|}{|G|}
\sum_{\rho\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)}
\overline{\chi_\rho(C)}
\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{\Pi_{\chi_\rho,N}(\lambda^{-r})}{r}.$$

Thus the relevant coordinate is

$$F_\rho(z)=\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{\Pi_{\chi_\rho}(z^r)}r.$$

By contrast, the periodic Artin logarithm is

$$L_\rho(z)
=
\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{\Pi_{\psi^r\chi_\rho}(z^r)}r,
\qquad
(\psi^r\chi_\rho)(g)=\chi_\rho(g^r).$$

Consequently,

$$F_\rho(z)-L_\rho(z)
=
\sum_{r\ge2}
\frac{\Pi_{\chi_\rho}(z^r)-\Pi_{\psi^r\chi_\rho}(z^r)}r.$$

This is the exact location of the error in replacing the fixed primitive label by its  $r$ -th power. The cited 1999 paper writes its constant in terms of  $L(e^{-h},\chi)$ , so the present manuscript must explicitly say whether it is correcting that theorem and identify the invalid step. Mohamed–Noorani paper
B3: Correct the rigidity scope
For the two-loop bouquet, let  $s\in S_2$  exchange  $a$  and  $b$ . Then

$$\tau'=\tau\circ s^{-1},$$

and the skew products are conjugate via

$$(x,g)\longmapsto (s x,g).$$

More generally,  $S_m$  acts transitively on all labelings with a fixed multiplicity vector  $(n_g)$ . Therefore

$$\frac{m!}{\prod_g n_g!}$$

measures only the fibre before quotienting by named-loop permutations. Modulo  $S_m$ , that fibre has cardinality one.
The authors must either:


retitle the problem as fixed marked-edge Livšic rigidity, or


define the dynamical fibre on

$$\operatorname{Aut}(X_\Gamma,\sigma)\backslash H^1(\Gamma;G)$$

and produce a collision not induced by a base automorphism.


M1: Repair the multiplicity terminology
Use

$$\mu_{\Gamma,G}([\tau])
=
\#\{[\alpha]\in H^1(\Gamma;G):
S_{\Gamma,G}([\alpha])=S_{\Gamma,G}([\tau])\}.$$

Do not call the implication “determinant rigidity iff  $\mu=1$ ” a classification theorem; it is immediate from the definition. A substantive theorem would instead give a closed formula, a structural criterion, or complexity bounds for  $\mu$  beyond brute-force enumeration.
M2: Remove redundant branch data
Define canonically

$$B_{1,\pi}^{\rm ren}
=
\lim_{t\uparrow1}
\left(
-\Log_0 P_\pi(t/\lambda)+s_\pi\log(1-t)
\right),$$

where  $\Log_0 P_\pi(0)=0$  and continuation is along  $0\le t<1$ . If

$$P_\pi(t/\lambda)
=
\prod_{\zeta\in\Gamma_\pi}(1-t\zeta)^{m_{\pi,\zeta}}
Q_{\partial,\pi}(t),$$

then

$$B_{1,\pi}^{\rm ren}
=
-\sum_{\substack{\zeta\in\Gamma_\pi\\\zeta\ne1}}
m_{\pi,\zeta}\Log_{[0,1]}(1-\zeta)
-\Log_{[0,1]}Q_{\partial,\pi}(1).$$

All terms are determined by  $P_\pi$ ,  $\lambda$ , and the stated path convention. The “branch family” should not be listed as independent cocycle data.
M3: Replace the projector certificate by a finite cycle certificate
Choose a spanning tree  $T$ , normalize both cocycles to identity on  $T$ , and let  $e_1,\ldots,e_r$  be the chords. Denote their normalized fundamental-cycle gains by  $g_i,g_i'$ . Then gauge equivalence is equivalent to the existence of a single  $h\in G$  such that

$$g_i'=h^{-1}g_i h,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,r.$$

This is finite, transparent, and strictly weaker in presentation than assuming a common  $D_\rho(u)$  already conjugates every spectral projector.
M4: Make the exact spectral certificate correct
Let  $\chi_{\widetilde A}(t)\in\mathbb Z[t]$  and let  $K=\mathbb Q(\lambda)$ . Divide

$$q(t)=\frac{\chi_{\widetilde A}(t)}{t-\lambda}$$

in  $K[t]$ , not in  $\mathbb Z[t]$ . For each isolated complex algebraic root  $\alpha$  of  $q$ , certify

$$\lambda^2-\alpha\bar\alpha>0$$

as a sign determination in a compositum containing  $\lambda,\alpha,\bar\alpha$ . The manuscript should specify this exact algebraic-number representation.
M5–M6: Scope and reproducibility
State “one-sided edge shift” in the abstract and main theorems. If two-sided SFTs are intended, supply a separate proof that a finite-memory two-sided transfer between one-step cocycles reduces to a vertex transfer.
For the computational claims, provide a public archive containing:

Rebuild the paper around Theorem 3.59 and the correction  $F_\rho\neq L_\rho$ .


Explicitly resolve the conflict with the 1999 Frobenius-class product theorem.


Add and seriously engage with gain-graph switching and  $G$ -cospectrality literature.


Demote Theorems 3.82–3.84 from “headline” status unless a result survives quotienting by base automorphisms.


Replace “intrinsic” by “fixed-presentation” wherever appropriate.


Remove the redundant boundary-data formalism and present the Abel branch as a canonical derived construction.


Reduce the manuscript drastically—approximately 40–60 pages plus a computational supplement would be more appropriate.


Supply an automorphism-free inverse-spectral collision or explicitly abandon the dynamical-conjugacy interpretation.


Archive the computational verification.


The paper’s strongest publishable contribution is not the advertised inverse-rigidity theorem; it is the Adams-corrected determinant formula for Frobenius-class product constants and the demonstration that the earlier Artin substitution is wrong.
