\subsection{Scalar finite-matrix inputs}

We record the scalar inputs needed for notation and comparison.
They are proved later in \Cref{sec:finite-part}. After removing the
Perron factor one obtains the reduced determinant
\[
  C(A):=\det\nolimits'(I-A/\lambda)^{-1}
\]
and the reduced spectral polynomial
\[
  F_A(t):=\frac{\det(I-tA/\lambda)}{1-t}
  =\prod_{j=2}^d\left(1-\frac{\mu_j}{\lambda}t\right),
\]
where \(\mu_2,\dots,\mu_d\) are the non-Perron eigenvalues. The
Abel-regularised finite-part constant
\[
  \FP(A):=\lim_{r\uparrow 1}\bigl(P_A(r)+\log(1-r)\bigr),
  \qquad
  P_A(r):=\sum_{n\ge 1}p_n(A)\lambda^{-n}r^n,
\]
measures the deviation of the primitive orbit sum from the harmonic
series.

The scalar normalisation used in the class-product theorem chain is
\begin{align*}
  \FP(A)
  &=\log C(A)
    +\sum_{k\ge 2}\frac{\mu(k)}{k}
      \log\det(I-\lambda^{-k}A)^{-1}\\
  &=\log C(A)
    +\sum_{n\ge 1}p_n(A)
      \bigl(\lambda^{-n}+\log(1-\lambda^{-n})\bigr).
\end{align*}
The determinant and primitive-orbit closed formulas for \(\FP(A)\) are proved
in \Cref{lem:finite-part-primitive-closed-form}; the identity
\(\mathcal M(A)=\gamma+\FP(A)\) and the scalar orbit-Mertens asymptotic
\[
  \sum_{n\le N}\frac{p_n(A)}{\lambda^n}
  =\log N+\mathcal M(A)+o(1)
\]
are \Cref{cor:finite-part-mertens-asymptotic}.  These scalar facts normalise
the trivial Peter--Weyl channel; the non-trivial class coordinates and the
eta-gap certificate come from the finite twisted matrices in
\Cref{prop:eta-gap-finite-state-certificate,prop:fixed-label-determinant-perron}.
The scalar projection statement
\Cref{thm:finite-part-scalar-interface-no-descent} identifies exactly the
constant class-function component supplied by fixed-matrix scalar records.

Under the twisted spectral gap, the body records the shadow facts needed to
interpret the \(S_3\) obstruction in
\Cref{thm:abelian-shadow-defect,lem:cyclic-quotients-one-dimensional-characters,thm:cyclic-detection-boundary}.
This support package consists only of the orthogonal projection onto the
abelian shadow and the elementary fact that cyclic quotient pullbacks span the
one-dimensional sector of the fixed cocycle.  The
scalar root-of-unity determinant layers
\(\log\det(I-\omega_q^jzA)^{-1}\) occur only in the fixed-matrix cyclic-lift
comparison model of \Cref{sec:finite-part}, separate from quotient pullbacks
of the fixed cocycle.

The fixed-matrix lift model is adjacent to inverse spectral questions for
SFTs \cite{LindMarcus1995}.  Here it is used only as a finite-dimensional
normalisation and comparison boundary separating scalar cyclic-lift records
from non-abelian class-profile invariants.

\subsection{Plan of the paper}

\Cref{sec:preliminaries} fixes the symbolic, determinant, and finite-group
notation.  \Cref{sec:chebotarev} proves the Adams--M\"obius primitive inversion:
twisted determinants give primitive Peter--Weyl channels, the finite
Perron-boundary certificate gives renormalized Frobenius-class Mertens
constants, and the fixed-label determinant finite parts give the
Adams-corrected product constants.  \Cref{sec:eta-gap} studies the strict
twisted gap \(\eta<\lambda\) as a finite-dimensional specialization criterion
for \Cref{cor:strict-gap-specialization} and the older clean formulas.

support span needed for the non-abelian obstruction example.

\Cref{sec:finite-part} isolates the scalar finite-part normalisation used for
the trivial channel and proves the projection statement for scalar comparison
data.  The appendices give the \(S_3\) model computations
\Cref{app:s3-model-computations}, the rational interval estimates
\Cref{app:s3-log-certificates}, and the fixed-label rational enclosure data
\Cref{app:fixed-label-local-certificates}.  These appendices supply the exact
arithmetic behind the displayed \(S_3\) inequalities used in the
Artin-substitution obstruction.
