\section{Appendix: exact rational enclosure data for the \texorpdfstring{\(S_3\)}{S3} example}
\label{app:fixed-label-local-certificates}

This appendix gives the exact rational enclosure data used only for the local
\(S_3\) determinant calculation and the Main Theorem~III evaluation in that
model.  The main text imports just the four numerical inequalities
\[
  \Pi_\varepsilon(1/2)\in(-342/1000,-341/1000),\quad
  \Pi_\rho(1/2)\in(-719/1000,-718/1000),
\]
and
\[
  F_\varepsilon(1/2)\in(-381/1000,-380/1000),\quad
  F_\rho(1/2)\in(-923/1000,-922/1000),
\]
as recorded in \Cref{cor:s3-displayed-rational-certificate-windows}.  The
finite algebraic model data, branch-tagged logarithm enclosures, rational tail
bounds, and branch--tail enclosure data below explain how those inequalities are
checked by exact arithmetic.  They are finite interval-enclosure data for this
example, not extra analytic input for Main Theorem~III.

\begin{lemma}[Local rational enclosure data for fixed labels]\label{prop:fixed-label-rational-certificate-record}
\label{prop:fixed-label-finite-io-certificate-summary}
  Assume the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:class-mertens-explicit}.  Fix a
  non-trivial \(\varrho\in\Irr(G)\), a tolerance \(\epsilon>0\), and a
  truncation level \(M\) satisfying
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-tail-choice}.  The enclosure obtained from
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm} is reproduced by the following
  finite verification.  This verification consists only of finite algebraic model data and
  rational inequalities of the following four kinds:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=*]
    \item the list of triples \((r,m,\pi)\) with \(rm\le M\), with the scalar
      Perron triple \((1,1,\mathbf1)\) deleted exactly as in
      \Cref{lem:fixed-label-singular-triple-deletion};
    \item the Adams coefficients \(a_{\varrho,
      \pi}^{(m)}=\langle\psi^m\chi_\varrho,\chi_\pi\rangle_G\), written from
      the finite character table and class-power table;
    \item for every listed triple, a branch tag specifying either the Abel
      non-trivial Perron branch \((rm=1,\pi\ne\mathbf1)\) or the ordinary germ
      value inside the Perron disc \((rm\ge2)\), together with a rational
      rectangular interval enclosure for
      \(\Log\det(I-\lambda^{-rm}A_\pi)^{-1}\);
    \item two rational error bounds \(R_M\) and \(R_{\mathrm{num}}\): the
      tail bound \(R_M\) is verified either directly from
      \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-error} or from the
      rational envelope \eqref{eq:fixed-label-rational-tail-envelope}, and
      \(R_{\mathrm{num}}\) is verified from the finite interval-propagation
      bound \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-roundoff}.
  \end{enumerate}
  These four kinds of quantities, verified by exact algebraic comparison
  and rational interval arithmetic, produce a rational rectangular interval
  \(I_M\subset\CC\) enclosing the finite sum \(F_\varrho^{(M)}\) and verifies
  the inclusion
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:fixed-label-certificate-interface-inclusion}
    F_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})
    \in
    I_M+
    \left\{w\in\CC:
      \abs{w}\le R_M+R_{\mathrm{num}}\right\},
  \end{equation}
  where all sides of \(I_M\) and both radii are rational.  Thus the displayed
  finite verification gives an exact rational enclosure for the constant
  used in
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm,cor:class-product-constant-rigidity}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
  The first two entries are finite because the set
  \(\{(r,m):rm\le M\}\) and \(\Irr(G)\) are finite, and because the Adams
  coefficients are finite character-table inner products.  The deleted scalar
  triple is exactly the omitted term in
  \Cref{lem:fixed-label-singular-triple-deletion,prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm};
  its coefficient is zero for \(\varrho\ne\mathbf1\), so no finite enclosure
  assigns a finite value, finite part, or endpoint branch to the singular scalar
  logarithm.

  For each remaining triple, the branch tag is one of the two cases already
  proved in item \textup{(d)} of
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm}: either the non-trivial
  Abel branch at \(rm=1\), made regular by the strict twisted gap, or the
  ordinary logarithmic germ at \(rm\ge2\), where
  \(\rho(\lambda^{-rm}A_\pi)<1\).  Thus every tagged interval encloses a
  uniquely specified logarithm.  Multiplying the rational rectangular
  intervals by the verified coefficients \(\mu(m)a_{\varrho,\pi}^{(m)}/(rm)\),
  enclosing each algebraic product by exact comparison in a rational rectangle,
  and summing those rectangles gives a rational rectangular interval \(I_M\)
  which contains the stated finite truncation.  Its excess radius is at most
  \(R_{\mathrm{num}}\) by the triangle inequality used in
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-roundoff}.  The discarded
  terms with \(rm>M\) have total absolute value at most the rational number
  \(R_M\), either because the enclosure data compare \(R_M\) with the algebraic
  tail in \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-error}, or because it
  records rational \(\theta,Q_\varrho\) satisfying
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-rational-tail-envelope} and
  \(R_M\ge Q_\varrho\theta^{M+1}/(1-\theta)\).  All these checks are finite
  algebraic or rational inequalities.  Adding the two independent radii proves
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-certificate-interface-inclusion}.  Finally,
  \Cref{cor:class-product-constant-rigidity} applies finite Fourier inversion
  to the verified fixed-label Perron vector, so the same finite rational data
  enclose the product constants without introducing a new analytic premise.
\end{proof}


\begin{lemma}[Finite branch--tail enclosure format for fixed labels]\label{prop:fixed-label-certificate-normal-form}
\label{prop:fixed-label-product-certificate-record}
\label{prop:fixed-label-product-enclosure-check}
\label{prop:fixed-label-five-field-rational-record}
  Under the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:class-euler-product-mertens}, every finite
  enclosure produced by the fixed-label procedure and used for the
  Main Theorem~III product constants is described, for each non-trivial
  \(\varrho\in\Irr(G)\), by a branch--tail enclosure record with the following
  finite verification entries:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=*]
    \item the determinant model
      \((D_\pi(z))_{\pi\in\Irr(G)}\), the Perron root \(\lambda\), the twisted
      verified gap inequality \(\eta<\lambda\), the character table, and the
      class-power maps;
    \item rational tail data \(C_\varrho,Q_\varrho,\theta,M\) satisfying the
      majorant hypothesis of \Cref{lem:fixed-label-perron-double-majorant},
      \(\lambda^{-1}<\theta<1\), \(C_\varrho\lambda<Q_\varrho\), and
      \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-rational-tail-choice};
    \item the finite window of all triples \((r,m,\pi)\) with \(rm\le M\), with
      only \((1,1,\mathbf1)\) deleted, and all Adams coefficients
      \(a_{\varrho,\pi}^{(m)}\) in that window;
    \item for every non-deleted listed triple, a branch tag and a rational
      rectangular enclosure for
      \(\Log\det(I-\lambda^{-rm}A_\pi)^{-1}\), using the Abel tag for
      \((rm=1,\pi\ne\mathbf1)\) and the ordinary Perron-disc tag for
      \(rm\ge2\);
    \item the rational finite-propagation radius obtained from
      \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-roundoff}, and the rational
      tail radius \(R_{M,\varrho}=Q_\varrho\theta^{M+1}/(1-\theta)\).
  \end{enumerate}
  These entries determine a disc
  \[
    I_\varrho+
    \{w\in\CC:|w|\le R_{M,\varrho}+R_{\mathrm{num},\varrho}\}
  \]
  containing \(F_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})\), where \(I_\varrho\) is the interval
  evaluation of the finite sum
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-truncation}.  Conversely,
  every finite enclosure produced by
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm} is equivalent, after
  reordering the finite window and splitting the total error into its tail and
  finite-propagation parts, to these entries.  This converse concerns only the stated fixed-label enclosure procedure; it is
  not a claim that no other finite enclosure method could prove the same
  numerical inclusions.  Applying these
  enclosures for all non-trivial \(\varrho\) and then the Fourier formula
  \eqref{eq:class-product-rigidity-forward} gives rational enclosures for all
  Frobenius-class product constants \((K_C)_C\).  Thus the appendix records local finite rational verification data for the displayed \(S_3\) example and not additional analytic hypotheses.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
  The model field is finite because the group has finitely many irreducible
  representations and conjugacy classes, and because each twisted determinant
  is a finite polynomial.  The tail field is finite and checkable by algebraic
  comparison: \(C_\varrho\) is a verified majorant from
  \Cref{lem:fixed-label-perron-double-majorant}, the inequalities for
  \(Q_\varrho\) and \(\theta\) are rational/algebraic comparisons, and
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-rational-tail-choice} is a
  rational inequality.  By \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm},
  this tail field gives the analytic error bound
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-error} through the rational
  envelope \eqref{eq:fixed-label-rational-tail-envelope}.

  For the finite window, the Adams coefficients are recomputed from the finite
  character-table inner product
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-algorithm-adams-coefficients}.  The branch field is
  exactly the two-branch convention of
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm}: the strict twisted gap makes
  every non-trivial \(rm=1\) Abel determinant regular at the boundary, while
  \(rm\ge2\) lies inside the Perron disc.  Signed interval summation over this
  finite window gives \(I_\varrho\).  Enlarging by the finite-propagation radius
  of \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-roundoff} and by
  \(R_{M,\varrho}\) contains the exact value by the triangle inequality and by
  the tail estimate already proved.  This proves the stated enclosure.

  Conversely, the enclosure produced by \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm}
  has no further analytic datum: it uses the same determinant model, the same
  Adams coefficients, the same deleted-triple and branch convention, a finite
  truncation level, finitely many determinant-logarithm enclosures, and the sum
  of one tail error and one finite interval-propagation error.  Recording those
  entries in the five fields above, and replacing an exact tail comparison by
  any rational envelope satisfying
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-rational-tail-choice}, gives
  the same verified disc.  The converse is therefore a sufficiency-and-necessity
  statement for the finite enclosure procedure used here, not a universal
  optimality assertion about all possible finite enclosure methods.  Finally,
  \Cref{cor:class-product-constant-rigidity} expresses the normalized product
  logarithms by the finite character-table Fourier transform
  \eqref{eq:class-product-rigidity-forward}; exponentiating the resulting real
  intervals gives the stated enclosures for the positive constants \(K_C\).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact rational interval replay for fixed labels.}
\label{prop:fixed-label-certificate-replay}
  In the fixed-label rational enclosure format of
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-rational-certificate-record,prop:fixed-label-certificate-normal-form},
  the truth of a recorded enclosure for
  \(F_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})\), and hence of the induced enclosure for every
  Main Theorem~III product constant \(K_C\), is decidable by a finite sequence
  of exact comparisons in \(\QQ\) and in the finite character table of \(G\).
  More precisely, the verification uses no analytic oracle beyond the finite
  branch--tail fields
  \[
    \mathcal D_{\mathrm{alg}},\quad
    \mathcal T_\varrho(M),\quad
    \mathcal L_\varrho(M),\quad
    \mathcal R_\varrho(M),\quad
    \mathcal I_\varrho(M),
  \]
  from \Cref{prop:fixed-label-certificate-normal-form}: if those fields pass
  the branch, Adams-coefficient, logarithm-interval, tail, and interval-sum
  checks listed there, then the resulting rational disc contains the unique
  value \(F_\varrho(\lambda^{-1})\).  Conversely, every enclosure produced by
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm} can be written in this
  finite enclosure format after replacing its analytic tail inequality by a rational
  envelope satisfying
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-rational-tail-choice}.
  The verification begins with the finite algebraic field
  \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{alg}}\).  From the character table and class-power
  table it recomputes each Adams coefficient
  \(a_{\varrho,\pi}^{(m)}\) by the finite inner product
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-algorithm-adams-coefficients}; this is a finite sum of
  algebraic integers, and in the displayed finite-group applications it is an
  exact integer comparison.  It also checks that the only deleted triple is
  \((1,1,\mathbf1)\) and that its coefficient is
  \(\langle\chi_\varrho,\mathbf1\rangle_G=0\), so the scalar Perron logarithm is
  absent before any boundary value is considered.

  For each triple in the finite list \(\mathcal T_\varrho(M)\), one
  checks the branch tag against the dichotomy proved in
  \Cref{lem:fixed-label-singular-triple-deletion,prop:fixed-label-determinant-perron}:
  non-trivial triples with \(rm=1\) use the Abel boundary branch, while all
  triples with \(rm\ge2\) use the ordinary interior-disc logarithm.  The field
  \(\mathcal L_\varrho(M)\) supplies rational rectangular enclosures for those
  finitely many logarithms.  Multiplying each rectangle by the rational weight
  \(\mu(m)a_{\varrho,
  \pi}^{(m)}/(rm)\) and summing intervals is finite rational interval
  arithmetic, so it gives exactly the finite-sum interval
  \(\mathcal I_\varrho(M)\) if and only if all stated interval inclusions hold.

  The only remaining issue is the omitted infinite tail.  The enclosure data
  contains rationals \(Q_\varrho\) and \(\theta_\varrho\) with
  \(\lambda^{-1}<\theta_\varrho<1\) and
  \(C_\varrho\lambda<Q_\varrho\).  These are checked by algebraic or rational
  comparisons from \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{alg}}\).  Then, exactly as in the proof
  of \Cref{prop:fixed-label-branch-tail-record},
  \(\mathrm d(k)\le k\) gives the rational majorisation
  \[
    C_\varrho\sum_{k>M}\frac{\mathrm d(k)}{k}\lambda^{1-k}
    \le
    Q_\varrho\frac{\theta_\varrho^{M+1}}{1-\theta_\varrho}.
  \]
  Thus the enlargement of \(\mathcal I_\varrho(M)\) by the recorded tail radius
  and the recorded finite-propagation radius contains the value represented by
  the absolutely convergent determinant series
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-determinant-perron}.  The uniqueness of that value is
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-determinant-perron}; hence any finite record satisfying
  these checks proves the claimed enclosure.

  Conversely, inspect the construction in
  \Cref{prop:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm}.  It chooses a finite cutoff,
  uses the same Adams coefficients and branch convention, encloses finitely many
  determinant logarithms, and bounds the remaining tail.  By the density of
  \(\QQ\) and the strict inequality \(\lambda^{-1}<1\), its tail comparison can
  be replaced by rationals satisfying
  \eqref{eq:fixed-label-perron-finite-algorithm-rational-tail-choice}.  Recording
  those data in the same branch--tail fields gives a finite enclosure record with the same verified
  disc.  Finally, \Cref{prop:fixed-label-certificate-normal-form} transports the
  fixed-label intervals to the product constants by finite Fourier inversion and
  exponentiation on rational real intervals, so the Main Theorem~III enclosures
  are verified by the same finite rational data.
