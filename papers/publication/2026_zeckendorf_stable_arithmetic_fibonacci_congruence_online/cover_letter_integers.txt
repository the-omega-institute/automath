Dear Editors of Integers: Electronic Journal of Combinatorial Number Theory,

We submit "Zeckendorf Stable Arithmetic: Fibonacci Congruence, Field Phases, and Online Algorithms" for consideration as a research article.

The paper's principal result is the multiplication-delay theorem (Theorem thm:mul-delay-linear-lower-bound): every exact most-significant-digit-first multiplier at effective resolution n has delay at least n-1. This gives a resolution-dependent lower bound for online multiplication in Fibonacci numeration and rules out a resolution-independent bounded-delay multiplier in the stated model. The ring structure developed in the paper supplies the canonical arithmetic setting and notation in which this obstruction is formulated; the delay theorem is the paper's central algorithmic contribution.

The remaining results establish the surrounding structure: local Fibonacci congruence classes have unique Zeckendorf representatives; finite windows transport addition and multiplication to the residue rings Z/(F_{m+2}Z), with field phases at Fibonacci-prime resolutions and Chinese-remainder decompositions at composite resolutions; online addition is realized by finite-state normalization; and the finite-resolution multiplication constructions and Fibonacci-adic inverse-limit ring are described explicitly. The manuscript is written for the intersection of combinatorial number theory, Fibonacci numeration, and automata-theoretic arithmetic.

For transparency, a related manuscript by the same authors is currently with referees at RAIRO ITA: ITA-2026-0032, "Canonical Zeckendorf Normalization and Sharp Iteration Depth of the Berstel Adder." That manuscript studies the Berstel adder and its iteration depth. Section 7 of the present paper introduces an online addition transducer for Fibonacci numeration, so the papers overlap in their common numeration-theoretic setting. The present manuscript cites ITA-2026-0032 explicitly; ITA-2026-0032 was submitted before the present work and does not cite it, and we will inform the RAIRO ITA editors of this submission. The present submission's focus is the multiplication-delay lower bound and the induced finite-resolution arithmetic, not the sibling manuscript's sharp adder-iteration result. ITA-2026-0032 is disclosed here to avoid any ambiguity about overlapping work under review elsewhere.

In the literature search supporting this submission, no prior art for the linear lower bound on multiplication delay was found in an index demonstrably covering the field. We make no stronger priority claim. The manuscript also cites the relevant Zeckendorf-arithmetic and Fibonacci-addition literature, including Labbe and Lepsova, Fenwick, and Dimitrov and Donevsky.

The manuscript is not published and is not under consideration at another venue. The authors have no conflicts of interest to declare.
