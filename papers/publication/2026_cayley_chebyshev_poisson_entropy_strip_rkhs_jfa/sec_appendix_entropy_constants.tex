\section{Finite-moment entropy constant regimes}
\label{app:finite-moment-entropy-constants}

\subsection{Retained-order constants for the finite-moment entropy hierarchy}%
\label{subsubsec:finite-moment-constants-variants}

The finite-moment hierarchy has only one quantitative transfer regime in this
paper.  For coefficients through order \(2N\), the retained density order is
\[
L=2N-2 .
\]
The exact coefficient-identification theorem is
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders}: under the finite centred
\(L\)th absolute moment,
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
=\sum_{m=2}^{N}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}+o(t^{-2N}).
\]
The algebraic lower-moment obstruction for the displayed coefficient
\(A_{2N}\) is recorded next to that theorem in
Proposition~\ref{prop:poisson-kl-moment-threshold-sharpness}.

\paragraph{Positive-margin retained-order estimate.}
\phantomsection\label{thm:finite-moment-entropy-remainder}
\phantomsection\label{thm:positive-margin-retained-order-entropy}
\phantomsection\label{cor:finite-moment-entropy-constant-remainder}
\phantomsection\label{cor:optimized-retained-order-entropy-bridge}
Let \(N\ge2\), \(0<\eta\le1\), and suppose that \(\nu\) has finite centred
\((2N-2+\eta)\)th absolute moment.  Proposition~\ref{prop:density-to-entropy-descent}
applied with \(L=2N-2\) gives constants
\[
C_{\nu,N,\eta},\qquad Q_{\nu,N},\qquad C'_{\nu,N},\qquad T_{\nu,N,\eta}
\]
defined there directly from the retained modes \(u_2,\ldots,u_{2N-2}\), the
density constants in Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-moment-density-remainder}, and
the finite moment jet through order \(2N-2\).  For every
\(t\ge T_{\nu,N,\eta}\),
\[
\left|
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
-\sum_{m=2}^{N}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}
\right|
\le
C_{\nu,N,\eta}t^{-(2N+\eta)}
+Q_{\nu,N}t^{-(2N+1)}
+C'_{\nu,N}t^{-(2N+2)} .
\]
The same proposition records the coarse envelope
\begin{equation}\label{eq:optimized-retained-order-entropy-envelope}
\left|
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
-\sum_{m=2}^{N}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}
\right|
\le
\bigl(C_{\nu,N,\eta}+Q_{\nu,N}+C'_{\nu,N}\bigr)t^{-(2N+\eta)} .
\end{equation}
The equation label
\eqref{eq:positive-margin-retained-order-theorem} is attached to the same
coarse estimate in Proposition~\ref{prop:density-to-entropy-descent}; it is
the estimate used later in the Doob-tail comparison.  No \(2N+\eta\)
density truncation or separate optimized notation is used.

\paragraph{\(N=4,\eta=1\) constants.}
The constants needed for the explicit eighth-order theorem are displayed in
Definition~\ref{def:n4-finite-time-constant-dictionary}, immediately before
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth-seventh-remainder}.  In that specialization
\[
L=6,\qquad p=7,
\]
and \eqref{eq:dictionary-fixed-law-n4-constants} defines
\(B_{\nu,8}\) and \(T_{\nu,8}\) from the law's moments through order seven.
Consequently, for every \(t\ge T_{\nu,8}\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:optimized-n4-seventh-envelope}
\left|
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
-A_4(\nu)t^{-4}
-A_6(\nu)t^{-6}
-A_8(\nu)t^{-8}
\right|
\le B_{\nu,8}t^{-9}.
\end{equation}
The controlled-shell functions \(C(u),B(u),T(u)\) and the dimensional
constants \(C_{\sigma,M}^{(8)},B_{\sigma,M}^{(8)},T_{\sigma,M}^{(8)}\) are
also defined in the same main-text definition, in
\eqref{eq:dictionary-normalized-shell-functions} and
\eqref{eq:dictionary-controlled-shell-c8}.  The fixed-law, conditional
fixed-law, normalized-shell, and dimensional controlled-shell regimes are
consolidated in Table~\ref{tab:n4-constant-regimes}; later appendix
certificate statements use that table rather than introducing additional
constant conventions.

\paragraph{Moment-ball constants.}
\phantomsection\label{cor:uniform-moment-ball-entropy-remainder}
The retained-order moment-ball domination is
Lemma~\ref{lem:moment-ball-uniform-constant-domination}.  It assumes a
centred \(p\)-moment bound with
\[
p=2N-2+\eta,
\]
not \(2N+\eta\), and gives closed finite majorants for
\[
C_{\nu,N,\eta},\qquad Q_{\nu,N},\qquad C'_{\nu,N},\qquad T_{\nu,N,\eta}.
\]
Substituting those majorants in
\eqref{eq:optimized-retained-order-entropy-envelope} gives a uniform
retained-order estimate on the centred \(p\)-moment ball.  These constants
are only quantitative remainders and thresholds; the coefficients remain the
moment-polynomial evaluations of the individual law.
