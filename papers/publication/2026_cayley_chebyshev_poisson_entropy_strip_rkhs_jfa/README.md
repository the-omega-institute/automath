# Stable-Flow Relative Entropy Dissipation

This directory contains the Journal of Functional Analysis resubmission
article *Stable-Flow Relative Entropy Dissipation and Optimal Stable-Kernel
Moment Exponents*.

Compile `main.tex`.  The article contains the two-flow entropy-dissipation and
endpoint theorem, critical stable translate estimates, optimal
first-unmatched-moment asymptotics, the positive tail-jet decomposition and
abstract bounded-score kernel theorem, moving-ball sharpness, and one concise
Poisson specialization.  Every nonstandard lemma essential to this spine is
proved in the article or its integrated appendix.

The Cauchy coefficient, Gaussian-quadrature, higher-defect, RKHS, and lattice
sampling results are in the sibling article directory
`../2026_cauchy_poisson_entropy_coefficients_quadrature_rkhs_lattice`.

Verification commands and mathematical certificate scripts are in
`artifacts/`.
