\section{Fixed-scale proxy conventions}\label{app:stable-proxy-conventions}

We record the convention invariance used after
Corollary~\ref{thm:stable-arbitrary-order-law-by-law-decomposition}.  Let
\(\theta:[0,\infty)\to[0,1]\) be measurable, with \(\theta=0\) on
\([0,1]\) and \(\theta=1\) on \([2,\infty)\), and fix \(c>0\).  For
\(\lambda\in\{\nu,\eta\}\), put
\[
 \chi_s(x)=\theta(|x|/(cs)),\qquad
 \tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda=\int\chi_s(x)\lambda(\dd x),
\]
\[
 V_{s,\chi}^\lambda(y)
 =\int\chi_s(x)F_y^{(\alpha,d)}(x/s)\lambda(\dd x).
\]
The hard convention is obtained by taking
\(\theta=\mathbf1_{(1,\infty)}\).  With the common lower-order jet
\(C_{r-1,s}\) from that corollary, define
\[
 A_{s,\chi}^\lambda
 =1+C_{r-1,s}+V_{s,\chi}^\lambda-	au_{s,\chi}^\lambda,
 \qquad
 \widetilde A_{s,\chi}^\lambda
 =\frac{1+C_{r-1,s}+V_{s,\chi}^\lambda}
        {1+\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Robustness of the fixed-scale tail-jet convention]
\label{prop:stable-proxy-convention-robustness}
Under the hypotheses of
Corollary~\ref{thm:stable-arbitrary-order-law-by-law-decomposition}, both
pairs above are positive probability densities relative to
\(\Omega_{\alpha,d}\) for all sufficiently large \(s\), and their relative
entropies are finite.  For either the hard or a smooth fixed-scale retention
and either additive or multiplicative normalization, write the resulting
proxy entropy as \(\mathcal E_{r,s}^{\rm conv}\).  Then
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(p_s^{(\alpha,d)}*\nu\|p_s^{(\alpha,d)}*\eta)
 =\mathcal E_{r,s}^{\rm conv}
  +\mathcal Q_{\alpha,d,r}(\Delta_r)s^{-2r}+o(s^{-2r}).
\]
Consequently, any two of these fixed-scale conventions have proxy entropies
that differ by \(o(s^{-2r})\), and the condition
\(s^{2r}\mathcal E_{r,s}^{\rm conv}\to0\) is independent of the stated
retention and normalization choices.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write \(F_y=F_y^{(\alpha,d)}\), and let \(\mathsf B_j(y,z)\) denote the
homogeneous order-\(j\) Taylor term used in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:abstract-positive-tail-jet-kernel}.  Direct addition and
subtraction of the Taylor terms gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:convention-exact-tail-split}
 q_s^\lambda=A_{s,\chi}^\lambda+s^{-r}B_r^\lambda
                  +R_{s,\chi}^\lambda,
\end{equation}
where
\begin{align*}
 R_{s,\chi}^\lambda
 &=\int(1-\chi_s(x))
   \left\{F_\cdot(x/s)-\sum_{j=0}^r
                  \mathsf B_j(\cdot,x/s)\right\}\lambda(\dd x)\\
 &\quad-\sum_{j=1}^r\int\chi_s(x)
                  \mathsf B_j(\cdot,x/s)\lambda(\dd x).
\end{align*}
On \(|z|\le2c\), the stable-kernel estimates and the top-quotient
continuity used in \eqref{eq:abstract-tail-local-remainder} give a bounded
modulus \(\omega_c(t)\to0\) such that the Taylor remainder has combined
\(L^\infty\) and \(L^1(\Omega_{\alpha,d})\) norm at most
\(\omega_c(|z|)|z|^r\).  Dominated convergence against \(|x|^r\) controls
the first line.  On the support of \(\chi_s\),
\(|x|\ge cs\), and the mode bounds give, for \(1\le j\le r\),
\[
 \left\|\int\chi_s(x)\mathsf B_j(\cdot,x/s)\lambda(\dd x)\right\|_\infty
 +\left\|\int\chi_s(x)\mathsf B_j(\cdot,x/s)\lambda(\dd x)\right\|_1
 \le C_cs^{-r}\int_{|x|\ge cs}|x|^r\lambda(\dd x).
\]
Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:convention-remainder-smallness}
 \|R_{s,\chi}^\lambda\|_\infty+
 \|R_{s,\chi}^\lambda\|_1=o(s^{-r}),
 \qquad \tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda=o(s^{-r}).
\end{equation}

The retained potential is nonnegative and has mass
\(\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda\).  Since the common jet has zero mean,
\(A_{s,\chi}^\lambda\) has mass one, and
\[
 \inf_yA_{s,\chi}^\lambda(y)
 \ge1-\|C_{r-1,s}\|_\infty-	au_{s,\chi}^\lambda\longrightarrow1.
\]
This proves positivity and normalization.  If the retained mass is nonzero,
convexity of relative entropy and the bounded-score translate estimate yield
\[
 \int \frac{V_{s,\chi}^\lambda}{\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda}
 \log\frac{V_{s,\chi}^\lambda}{\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda}
 \dd\Omega_{\alpha,d}
 \le\frac1{\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda}
 \int\chi_s(x)\{C+(d+\alpha)\log(1+|x|/s)\}\lambda(\dd x)<\infty.
\]
The finite \(r\)-th moment, hence finite first moment, proves the last
inequality.  Adding the bounded common core preserves finite relative
entropy.  The zero retained-mass case is immediate.

Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:two-background-kl-perturbation} to
\eqref{eq:convention-exact-tail-split}.  The two backgrounds tend to one in
\(L^1\), their difference is bounded by the two tail masses and is therefore
\(o(s^{-r})\), and \eqref{eq:convention-remainder-smallness} supplies the
required remainders.  The difference of the order-\(r\) modes is
\(B_r^\nu-B_r^\eta=B_{\Delta_r}\).  The lemma gives the displayed entropy
expansion for every additive hard or smooth convention.  Subtracting two
such expansions proves their equivalence modulo \(o(s^{-2r})\).

For the normalization comparison, set
\[
 X_{s,t}^\lambda=
 \frac{A_{s,\chi}^\lambda+t\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda}
      {1+t\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda},\qquad 0\le t\le1.
\]
The endpoints are the additive and multiplicative proxies.  The preceding
entropy bound and \(\log(1+u)\le C_cu^r\) for \(u\ge c\) imply, uniformly in
\(t\),
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(X_{s,t}^\nu\|X_{s,t}^\eta)=o(s^{-r}),
 \qquad
 \|X_{s,t}^\nu-X_{s,t}^\eta\|_1=o(s^{-r}).
\]
Differentiating the relative entropy along this interpolation, using the
common positive lower bound and
\(\partial_tX_{s,t}^\lambda=
 \tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda(1-X_{s,t}^\lambda)/
 (1+t\tau_{s,\chi}^\lambda)\), shows that its derivative is
\(o(s^{-2r})\) uniformly on \([0,1]\).  Truncation of the retained
potentials justifies differentiation, and the displayed translate-entropy
bound permits passage to the limit.  Integration in \(t\) proves that the
two normalizations differ by \(o(s^{-2r})\), completing the proof.
\end{proof}
