Dear Editors of *Potential Analysis*,

Please consider the manuscript **"Cayley--Poisson Entropy and Optimal
Stable-Kernel Moment Exponents"** for publication in *Potential Analysis*.

The paper studies large-scale relative entropy after smoothing by isotropic
strictly alpha-stable kernels. Suppose two probability laws on
\(\mathbb R^d\) first differ at tensor order \(r\geq 1\). For every
\(0<\alpha<2\), the paper identifies the optimal complete absolute-moment
exponent under which their smoothed KL divergence has an exact
\(s^{-2r}\) leading term. The endpoint exponent is sufficient, and in the
non-Hilbert regime a sparse finite-difference construction proves uniform
sharpness even among pairs whose order-\(r\) difference tensor is finite and
nonzero. A finite-covariance law-by-law decomposition also isolates the
nonnegative nonlinear energy carried by mass beyond the smoothing scale.

The priority boundary is explicit. Chen and Niles-Weed proved the exact
arbitrary first-unmatched-moment KL asymptotic for Gaussian smoothing under
sub-Gaussian tails. Their theorem contains the Gaussian/Hermite instance of
the quadratic mechanism used here. The present contribution is the
heavy-tailed stable range \(0<\alpha<2\), where the paper determines a much
weaker optimal moment condition, proves endpoint sufficiency, and establishes
uniform moment-class sharpness. It does not claim the Gaussian mechanism as
new.

The manuscript fits *Potential Analysis* through its use of fractional
heat-kernel estimates, stable semigroups, weighted translate quotients, and
nonlinear entropy/Bregman methods. These analytic tools, together with the
critical moment transition and stable-kernel counterexamples, are intended
for the journal's readership in nonlocal potential theory and probabilistic
analysis.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all
authors have approved its submission. Supplementary material contains the
supporting coefficient and domain calculations referenced by the article.
