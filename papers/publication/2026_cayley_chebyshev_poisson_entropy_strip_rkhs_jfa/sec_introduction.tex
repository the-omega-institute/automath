\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

The Cayley transform
\[
\iota(x)=\frac{1+\ii x}{1-\ii x}
\]
identifies the one-point compactification $\overline\RR$ of the line with
the circle~$\TT$.  Under this identification normalized Haar measure pulls
back to the Cauchy law
\[
\frac{1}{\pi(1+x^{2})}\dd x=P_1(x)\dd x .
\]
This normalization is the common coordinate system of the paper.  It puts
Poisson smoothing on~$\RR$, circle Fourier analysis, Cauchy-weighted
entropy integrals, and the auxiliary secant observation kernel into one
calculus.

\subsection*{Main results}

The contribution hierarchy is as follows.
\emph{Main result 1} is the finite-moment Poisson entropy expansion,
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders}.  \emph{Main result 2} is the
seventh-moment \(t^{-8}\) entropy jet, Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth}.
The defect coordinates and the moment-defect \(W_2\) theorem are
coefficient-level consequences collected in
Corollaries~\ref{cor:eighth-order-jet-factorization}--%
\ref{thm:poisson-defect-amplification},
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-stability}.  Appendix~\ref{app:finite-time-defect-readouts}
records finite-time readouts obtained by inserting the eighth-order remainder
into that coefficient estimate.  The auxiliary infrastructure is finite phase
sampling, the Doob--Bregman tail identity, the
secant RKHS, and the integer-orbit projection theorem.  These tools provide
coefficient audits, a dissipation-tail representation, and observation
coordinates; they are not the main novelty claims.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\Roman*)},leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{The arbitrary finite-order finite-moment entropy expansion.}
For every $N\ge2$ and $0<\eta\le1$, if a probability law~$\nu$ has mean
$\bar\gamma$ and finite centred $(2N+\eta)$th absolute moment, then
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
=\sum_{m=2}^{N}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}+o(t^{-2N})
\qquad(t\to\infty).
\]
This is Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders}, with finite computable remainder
and uniform moment-ball versions in
Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-moment-entropy-remainder} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-moment-ball-entropy-remainder}.  The result is the
finite-moment passage from the universal Cayley--Chebyshev coefficient algebra
to an actual Poisson relative-entropy asymptotic expansion at the prescribed
order.  It is not a single asymptotic series under one fixed moment
hypothesis; increasing the retained order requires the corresponding higher
finite moment.  Fixed-law and moment-ball remainder constants are included.

\item \emph{The seventh-moment eighth-order entropy jet.}
Under a finite centred seventh absolute moment, the order-$t^{-8}$ entropy
jet is computed directly in
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth}, including the coefficient jet
$(C_4,C_6,C_8^{(7)})$ and a quantitative $O(t^{-9})$ remainder.  This theorem is
then used to define sixth- and eighth-order defect coordinates.  The
downstream consequences are the non-negative defect ladder of
Corollary~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-ladder}, the strict eighth-order
amplification in Corollary~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-amplification}, the
moment-defect $W_2$ theorem
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-stability}.  The finite fourth-moment
polynomial is identified with the genuine entropy coefficient defect by
Lemma~\ref{lem:seventh-moment-bridge} before the \(W_2\) estimate is invoked.
\end{enumerate}

Three supporting tools keep the argument auditable.  Finite root-of-unity
sampling verifies the finite Fourier coefficient arithmetic below the explicit
aliasing barrier.  The KL Doob-tail section re-expresses the same Poisson
comparison quotient through the identity
\(D_{\mathrm{KL}}=\int_t^\infty I_\nu(s)\dd s\).  The secant Gram space and
strip RKHS appendix give the Hilbert-space model for integer-orbit observation
data, including the projection and law-side recovery criterion for that
channel.

\subsection*{Cayley coefficient mechanism}

The comparison kernel
\[
R_\varepsilon(y):=\frac{1+y^2}{1+(y-\varepsilon)^2}
\]
measures the ratio between a translated Poisson profile and the reference
Cauchy density after the rescaling $x=\bar\gamma+ty$.  The Cayley angle
$y=\tan\theta$ converts the Taylor modes of $R_\varepsilon$ into finite
trigonometric expressions governed by Chebyshev polynomials of the second
kind.  Theorem~\ref{thm:cayley-chebyshev-mode} records this mode formula.
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-entropy-coefficients} then defines the formal
coefficient polynomials $A_k^{\rm form}$ by finite Cauchy-weighted mode
integrals and proves the odd-order cancellation.  At this stage the
calculation is algebraic: a law is evaluated only through the moment
interface in
Proposition~\ref{prop:universal-entropy-coefficient-evaluation}.

The analytic content of the first main package is the finite-moment
density-to-entropy passage.  Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders}
shows that, under a finite centred $(2N+\eta)$th absolute moment, the
evaluated even coefficients are the actual coefficients of the large-time
Poisson relative entropy through order~$t^{-2N}$.  The companion remainder
theorem, Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-moment-entropy-remainder}, records fixed-law
constants, while Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-moment-ball-entropy-remainder}
records the corresponding uniform statement on centred moment balls.  The
compact-support analytic theorem
Theorem~\ref{thm:analytic-compact-support-interchange} records the
compact-support convergent-series version of the same coefficients.

Finite phase sampling gives an exactness check for this coefficient calculus.
The main entropy proof uses Laurent constant-coefficient extraction.
Appendix~\ref{sec:finite-phase-sampling} identifies the same circle characters
on root-of-unity grids and records the alias-free recovery formula below the
explicit degree barrier.  The finite-grid moment recovery theorem and the
eighth-order finite-phase verification check the normalized Laurent
constant terms after the analytic asymptotic framework has been established.

\subsection*{Eighth-order defect and moment-defect \(W_2\) estimates}

The second main package specializes the finite coefficient calculus to the
order-$t^{-8}$ jet under a finite centred seventh absolute moment.
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth} is the central result: it gives the
displayed coefficient jet and the $O(t^{-9})$ remainder.  The subsequent
corollaries use that jet.  Corollary~\ref{cor:eighth-order-jet-factorization}
records that the displayed jet factors through centred moments through order
six.  From the sixth- and eighth-order coefficients one obtains non-negative
defect coordinates
(Corollary~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-ladder}) and strict eighth-order
amplification
(Corollary~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-amplification}).  The symmetric two-point
law at fixed variance is the equality case, as stated in
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-two-point-defect-rigidity}.

The \(W_2\) consequence is placed after the seventh-moment bridge.
With only a finite fourth moment the formula
$\Delta_6^{(4)}(\nu)$ is an algebraic polynomial and satisfies the coupling
estimate of Lemma~\ref{lem:fourth-moment-algebraic-coupling}.
Lemma~\ref{lem:seventh-moment-bridge} uses the seventh-moment entropy
expansion to identify that polynomial with the genuine sixth-order entropy
defect~$\Delta_6$.  With this identification,
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-stability} gives the moment-defect
$W_2$ estimate
\[
W_2(\nu_c,\rho_\sigma)
\le
4\,\sigma^{-1/2}\Delta_6(\nu)^{1/4}
+8\,\sigma^{-2}\Delta_6(\nu)^{1/2}.
\]
Appendix~\ref{app:finite-time-defect-readouts} gives finite-time readouts by
displaying the $C_8^{(7)}t^{-2}$ correction and the fixed-law residual in the
renormalized sixth-order scale.  These readouts are consequences of the
coefficient estimate and the eighth-order remainder, not a separate entropy
stability theory.

\subsection*{Observation channel and strip RKHS}

The observation material gives Hilbert coordinates for a Poisson channel.  The
secant family
\[
\Psi_\varepsilon(y):=\frac{R_\varepsilon(y)-1}{\varepsilon}
\]
has Gram kernel
\[
K(a,b)=\frac{2}{4+(a-b)^2}.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:mode-gram-kernel} computes this kernel, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:mode-space-rkhs} identifies the closed secant span with
$L^2_0(\omega)$ and its RKHS completion.  These results are the Hilbert
coordinate system for the appendix projection theorem.

The scalar centred observation functions in
Proposition~\ref{prop:poisson-central-two-channel} recover the centred law
from the central Poisson trace and its derivative by Laplace uniqueness and
characteristic-function inversion.  Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-channel-rkhs}
then places the centred fluctuation profile inside the secant RKHS and
identifies the observation values as evaluation functionals.
Appendix~\ref{sec:strip-lattice} transports this RKHS into the Poisson strip
space
$\sqrt{\pi}\,e^{-|D|}L^2(\RR)$ and records the integer-orbit projection
formula.  The resulting observation theorem is a projection theorem: integer
data recover exactly \(\Pi_{\ZZ}F_t\).  The same theorem translates full
recovery back to the law: for the observation time \(t\), it is equivalent to
\(2\pi\)-periodicity of
\(q_{F_t}(\xi)=e^{2|\xi|}\widehat F_t(\xi)\), equivalently support of the
centred law on \(t\ZZ\).

\subsection*{Theorem-level comparison with adjacent work}

The references in this subsection are positioning references, not proof
inputs.  The classical analytic inputs are the standard Poisson/Fourier,
Cayley, Laplace, characteristic-function, and RKHS facts cited at the point
where they are used.  The table isolates the theorem-level increment of the
paper from four adjacent literatures: entropy CLT and stable-law information,
large-time semigroups, functional-inequality stability and Poisson transport,
and RKHS/shift-invariant sampling.
The closest recent JFA results are of four different kinds: entropy CLT and
Esscher transforms \cite{BobkovGotzeEsscher2025}, geometric heat-semigroup
asymptotics \cite{AnkerPapageorgiouZhang2023HeatSymmetricSpaces}, stability
of entropy inequalities
\cite{BrigatiDolbeaultSimonov2024LogSobolev,Carlen2025LogHLS}, and
Poisson/point-process transport
\cite{GozlanHerryPeccati2021PointMeasureTransport,LopezRiveraShenfeld2025PoissonTransport,ErbarHuesmannJalowyMuller2025StationaryPointTransport}.
None gives a finite-moment coefficient expansion for
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma)).
\]

\subsection*{Organization}

Section~\ref{sec:preliminaries} fixes notation and conventions.
Subsection~\ref{sec:cayley-gate} records the Cayley formulas, and
Subsection~\ref{sec:haar-pullback} records the Haar-pullback normalization.
Section~\ref{sec:finite-phase-checks} points to the finite phase checking
appendix.
Section~\ref{sec:entropy-asymptotics} proves the two main packages:
the finite-moment entropy expansion and the seventh-moment eighth-order
defect layer, followed by coefficient-level moment-defect \(W_2\)
consequences.  Section~\ref{sec:doob-phi-entropy}
records the KL dissipation-tail identity for the same Poisson comparison
quotient.  Appendix~\ref{sec:gram-space} constructs the secant Gram space and
proves the centred Poisson reconstruction statement.  Appendix~\ref{sec:strip-lattice}
identifies the strip RKHS and proves the integer-orbit observation projection
theorem.  Appendix~\ref{app:entropy-integrals}
records the weighted trigonometric integrals and coefficient arithmetic used
in the eighth-order expansion.  Appendix~\ref{sec:finite-phase-sampling}
records the root-of-unity finite check for the same normalized Cayley
coefficients.
Appendix~\ref{app:finite-time-defect-readouts} records the finite-time
translations of the coefficient-level \(W_2\) estimate.
