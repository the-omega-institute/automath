\section{Stable kernels, entropy notation, and elementary quotient estimates}
\label{sec:stable-kernel-preliminaries}

For probability densities \(f,g\) on \(\RR^d\), write
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(f\|g)=\int_{\RR^d}f\log(f/g)\dd x
\]
with the usual extended-value convention.  The scalar Bregman functions used
below are
\[
 \Phi(u)=(1+u)\log(1+u)-u\quad(u\ge-1),
 \qquad
 \Lambda(a,b)=a\log(a/b)-a+b\quad(a,b>0).
\]
Thus, when \(f=(1+u)g\),
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(f\|g)=\int\Phi(u)g\dd x.
\]
Convexity gives \(\Phi,\Lambda\ge0\), and on each compact subinterval of
\((-1,\infty)\) both are controlled quadratically by their increments.

Let \(p_t\) denote the isotropic symmetric \(\alpha\)-stable kernel with
Fourier transform \(e^{-t|\xi|^\alpha}\).  Its spatial-scale version is
\[
 p_s^{(\alpha,d)}(x)=s^{-d}p_1^{(\alpha,d)}(x/s),
 \qquad s=t^{1/\alpha}.
\]
We use the standard density and derivative estimates
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prelim-stable-density-derivatives}
 p_1^{(\alpha,d)}(y)\asymp(1+|y|)^{-d-\alpha},
 \qquad
 |\partial^\gamma p_1^{(\alpha,d)}(y)|
 \lesssim_\gamma(1+|y|)^{-d-\alpha-|\gamma|}.
\end{equation}
They imply bounded normalized derivatives of every fixed order and, in
particular, a bounded score.  Complete statements with the normalization
dependence needed later are collected in
Proposition~\ref{prop:stable-kernel-input-package}.

We repeatedly use the elementary consequence of a bounded score.  If a
strictly positive density \(p\) satisfies
\(\|\nabla\log p\|_\infty\le L\), then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prelim-quotient-exponential-bound}
 e^{-L|z|}\le \frac{p(y-z)}{p(y)}\le e^{L|z|}
 \qquad(y,z\in\RR^d).
\end{equation}
Indeed, integrate \(\nabla\log p\) along the segment from \(y\) to
\(y-z\).  If \(X\) is integrable, Jensen's inequality therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prelim-mixture-lower-bound}
 \int\frac{p(y-X/s)}{p(y)}\dd\mathbb P
 \ge \exp\{-L\EE|X|/s\},
\end{equation}
uniformly in \(y\).  This lower bound is the basic reason a translated
heavy-tailed mixture can serve as the moving denominator in the
two-background entropy expansion.

We also use the bounded-test estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prelim-bounded-test}
 \left|\int h\dd(\rho-\sigma)\right|
 \le \|h\|_\infty\|\rho-\sigma\|_{\rm TV}
\end{equation}
and data processing for relative entropy.  Standard Fourier injectivity,
translation continuity in \(L^1\), and stable semigroup regularity will be
invoked in their classical forms.
