# Submission Metadata

## Target Journal

**Potential Analysis**

## Fit Rationale

The paper's central result is an exact large-scale relative-entropy
asymptotic for convolution with isotropic strictly stable kernels. Its main
analytic inputs are two-sided fractional heat-kernel bounds, derivative
quotients, critical weighted translate estimates, and a nonlinear Bregman
transfer. The endpoint moment theorem and its moving-ball counterexamples
are therefore aimed directly at readers working on stable semigroups,
nonlocal potential theory, fractional diffusion, and probabilistic analytic
inequalities. These subjects are a closer match to *Potential Analysis* than
to a broad functional-analysis venue.

The result is positioned as a strong specialist contribution. Chen and
Niles-Weed already proved the arbitrary first-unmatched-moment KL mechanism
for Gaussian smoothing under sub-Gaussian tails. The paper excludes the
Gaussian endpoint and contributes the heavy-tailed range
\(0<\alpha<2\): the optimal complete-moment exponent, endpoint sufficiency,
and analytic uniform sharpness over the full moment class. In the
non-Hilbert regime the sharpness construction retains a finite nonzero
leading tensor; in the Hilbert regime the lower threshold is only
coefficient-domain minimality.
