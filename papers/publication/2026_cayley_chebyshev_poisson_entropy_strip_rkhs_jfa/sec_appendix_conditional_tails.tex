\section{Optional missing-top-moment tail transfer}%
\label{app:conditional-missing-top-moment-tails}

The missing-top-moment material is optional and is not used in the proof of
Theorems~\ref{thm:main-A}, \ref{thm:main-B}, or~\ref{thm:main-C}.  This
appendix records the scope of the tail claim.  The usable theorem
is the conditional transfer in
Corollary~\ref{cor:main-body-conditional-missing-top-moment-tail}: if the
quotient has the truncated top-mode form, with \(N\ge3\) and \(p=2N-2\),
\[
\Delta_t(y)=
\sum_{n=2}^{p-1}\mu_nu_n(y)t^{-n}
+u_p(y)t^{-p}M_p(t)+e_t(y),
\]
with
\[
\|e_t\|_\infty+\|e_t\|_{L^1(\omega)}=o(t^{-p}M_p(t)),
\qquad
M_p(t)\to\infty,\qquad
M_p(t)=o(t^{p-2}),
\]
then the KL expansion has the corresponding replacement layer
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t)
=
\sum_{m=2}^{N-1}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}
+J(2,p)\mu_2\,t^{-2N}M_p(t)
+o(t^{-2N}M_p(t)),
\qquad p=2N-2.
\]
For an exact power tail this gives the familiar \(t^{-2N}\log t\) layer.
The borderline \(p=2\) infinite-variance case is different: its leading scale
is quadratic in the truncated variance, namely \(t^{-4}(\log t)^2\) for the
model density \(c|x|^{-3}\).  This appendix does not assert that borderline
calculation as a separate theorem.

For completeness, we spell out the conditional transfer proof, because it is
the only tail mechanism used by the optional discussion.  Put
\[
B_t(y)=u_p(y)t^{-p}M_p(t),\qquad
s_t=t^{-2N}M_p(t)=t^{-(p+2)}M_p(t).
\]
The assumptions imply \(B_t=O(s_t t^2)\),
\(\|e_t\|_\infty+\|e_t\|_{L^1(\omega)}=o(t^2s_t)\), and
\(M_p(t)=o(t^{p-2})\), so the moving-scale Taylor transfer
Lemma~\ref{lem:moving-scale-taylor-transfer} applies with
\(\Delta_t=A_t+B_t+e_t\).  Hence
\[
  D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t)
  =\int_{\RR}\Phi(A_t)\,\dd\omega+\mu_2J(2,p)s_t+o(s_t),
  \qquad
  \Phi(s)=(1+s)\log(1+s)-s .
\]
Since \(\|A_t\|_\infty=O(t^{-2})\), Taylor expansion through degree \(N\)
gives
\[
  \int_{\RR}\Phi(A_t)\,\dd\omega
  =
  \sum_{m=2}^{N-1}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}+o(s_t).
\]
The next pure finite-jet layer is \(O(t^{-2N})=o(s_t)\), because
\(M_p(t)\to\infty\).  Finally,
Proposition~\ref{prop:poisson-kl-moment-threshold-sharpness} computes
\[
J(2,p)=(-1)^N(N-1)2^{-2N+2}.
\]
This proves the displayed replacement layer.

The manuscript does not assert separate theorems for perturbative
power tails or slowly varying tails.  Those are standard sources of quotient
inputs, but each requires its own \(L^\infty\cap L^1(\omega)\) tail
verification before the conditional transfer can be used.  Likewise, exact
power tails are mentioned here only as motivation for the scale
\(M_p(t)\sim C\log t\); the theorem used here is the conditional transfer
above, whose hypotheses explicitly include the needed quotient expansion.
