\section{Optional missing-top-moment tail note}%
\label{app:conditional-missing-top-moment-tails}

The missing-top-moment material is optional and is not used in the proof of
Theorems~\ref{thm:main-A}, \ref{thm:main-B}, or~\ref{thm:main-C}.  The main
text already records the usable conditional statement in
Corollary~\ref{cor:main-body-conditional-missing-top-moment-tail}: if the
quotient has the truncated top-mode form
\[
\Delta_t(y)=
\sum_{n=2}^{p-1}\mu_nu_n(y)t^{-n}
+u_p(y)t^{-p}M_p(t)+e_t(y),
\]
with
\[
\|e_t\|_\infty+\|e_t\|_{L^1(\omega)}=o(t^{-p}M_p(t)),
\qquad
M_p(t)\to\infty,\qquad
M_p(t)=o(t^{p-2}),
\]
then the KL expansion has the corresponding replacement layer
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\|P_t)
=
\sum_{m=2}^{N-1}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}
+J(2,p)\mu_2\,t^{-2N}M_p(t)
+o(t^{-2N}M_p(t)),
\qquad p=2N-2.
\]
For an exact power tail this gives the familiar \(t^{-2N}\log t\) layer.
The borderline \(p=2\) infinite-variance case is different: its leading scale
is quadratic in the truncated variance, namely \(t^{-4}(\log t)^2\) for the
model density \(c|x|^{-3}\).

The verification details for exact power tails, perturbative power tails, and
eventually monotone slowly varying tails have been moved to the paper-local
supplementary missing-top-moment note.  Those checks are useful diagnostics
for the sharpness discussion, but the submitted manuscript only needs the
finite-moment coefficient hierarchy and the finite-\(L\)-moment
multidimensional quotient theorem.
