\section{Poisson Smoothing and Entropy Asymptotics}%
\label{sec:entropy-asymptotics}

\subsection*{Entropy theorem-package guide}

\paragraph{Local hypotheses.}
Throughout this section we write
$\Delta:=\gamma-\bar\gamma$ and
$\mu_n:=\int_{\RR}\Delta^n\,\dd\nu(\gamma)$ whenever it exists.  The
assumptions are local:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:poisson-second-order} uses the mean, variance,
and finite centred fourth moment through a direct Taylor expansion of
$R_\varepsilon$.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders} uses a finite centred
$(2N+\eta)$th absolute moment, the density remainder, and Cayley parity.
Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-moment-ball-entropy-remainder} gives the uniform
version on centred $(2N+\eta)$-moment balls.
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:poisson-cauchy-moment-inversion-by-phase-modes}
uses the same finite mode calculus at order four and a finite centred
fifth absolute moment to recover $(\sigma^2,\mu_3,\mu_4)$ from phase
Fourier coefficients.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-grid-poisson-moment-recovery} gives finite-grid,
finite-time recovery from exact phase samples.  It uses
Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-grid-coefficient-recovery} to remove aliasing for
the truncated finite mode polynomial and the uniform order-five remainder to
quantify the residual large-time error.
\item Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-kl-eighth} is proved directly from the
seventh-moment mode remainder.  Its coefficients use the formal mode algebra;
the $N=4$ finite-moment theorem and compact-support analytic theorem give the
same notation only under their stronger hypotheses.
The displayed coefficient arithmetic uses Lemma~\ref{lem:mode-parity-vanish}
and Appendix~\ref{app:entropy-integrals}.
\item Proposition~\ref{prop:eighth-order-finite-phase-verification} uses
the finite Fourier support of the Cayley--Chebyshev modes and the
finite-grid aliasing formula to compute the coefficient integrals
through order~$t^{-8}$ exactly on any phase grid $\mu_N$ with
$N\ge9$.
\item Theorems~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-ladder}
and~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-amplification}, together with
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-two-point-defect-rigidity}, use the same finite
centred seventh absolute moment hypothesis and the explicit coefficients from
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-kl-eighth}.
\item The fourth-moment identity \eqref{eq:defect-sixth-fourth-moment} and
coupling estimate \eqref{eq:fourth-moment-algebraic-coupling} use only the
mean, variance, and finite centred fourth moment; they define the standalone
algebraic defect $\Delta_6^{(4)}(\nu)$.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-stability} is a coefficient-level
seventh-moment theorem for the sixth-order asymptotic defect, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-finite-time-defect-stability} gives the large-time
version using the order-nine remainder in Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-kl-eighth}.
Both use the mean, variance, and finite centred seventh absolute moment; only
then does Lemma~\ref{lem:seventh-moment-bridge} identify
$\Delta_6^{(4)}(\nu)$ with the genuine $t^{-6}$ entropy defect.
Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-finite-time-defect-stability} is the uniform version
on variance-fixed seventh-moment shells.
\end{itemize}
The appendix is used only to evaluate the displayed $t^{-6}$ and $t^{-8}$
coefficients in Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-kl-eighth}.  Below, the
fourth-moment identity is kept as the algebraic polynomial
$\Delta_6^{(4)}$ until the seventh-moment bridge gives it entropy-asymptotic
meaning.  Law-dependent and shell-uniform finite-time constants are separated
in the corresponding corollaries.

\paragraph{Section guide.}
This section first fixes the Cayley--Poisson normalization and the formal
coefficient algebra, then proves the finite-moment entropy asymptotics and the
seventh-moment eighth-order defect formulas, and finally derives
coefficient-level and finite-time stability.  The results are used in the
following order.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Normalization tier.}
Subsection~\ref{subsec:cayley-entropy} fixes the entropy identity,
the low-order Poisson comparison, and the Cayley--Chebyshev mode expansion.
These results depend only on the Poisson kernel identities and finite Taylor
expansions stated there.
\item \emph{Algebraic tier.}
Subsection~\ref{subsec:all-order-coefficient-calculus} is algebraic.
The universal formal polynomials $A_k^{\rm form}$ and the mode integrals
$I(n_1,\ldots,n_r)$ are defined before any law is evaluated or any
asymptotic limit is taken; their odd-order cancellation follows from the
parity of the modes.  Proposition~\ref{prop:universal-entropy-coefficient-evaluation}
then evaluates those polynomials at a law only when the required moments
exist.
\item \emph{Analytic tier.}
Subsection~\ref{subsec:finite-moment-entropy} is the finite-moment passage
step.  Under the finite centred moment hypotheses appearing in
Lemma~\ref{lem:entropy-uniform-remainder} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-moment-entropy-remainder}, the evaluated algebraic
coefficient $A_k(\nu)$ is the actual coefficient of the large-time Poisson
relative entropy expansion.
\item \emph{Defect tier.}
Subsection~\ref{subsec:eighth-order-defects} is a specialization of
the preceding descent.  Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-kl-eighth} supplies the
sixth- and eighth-order entropy jet under a finite centred seventh moment,
using the formal coefficient algebra only for notation and coefficient
identification.  Corollary~\ref{cor:eighth-order-jet-factorization} factors that
jet through the centred moment jet of order six,
Proposition~\ref{prop:eighth-order-finite-phase-verification} checks the
required finite Fourier integrals on phase grids below the aliasing threshold,
and Theorems~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-ladder}
and~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-amplification} use only those explicit
coefficients to prove the defect ladder and the two-point extremal
amplification statement.
\item \emph{Stability tier.}
Subsection~\ref{subsec:fourth-moment-stability} is downstream of the
defect formulas.  The symbols $C_6^{(4)}$ and $\Delta_6^{(4)}$ are only
fourth-moment algebraic polynomials until Lemma~\ref{lem:seventh-moment-bridge}
places them under the seventh-moment entropy expansion.  Only after that
bridge does Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-defect-stability} become a stability
theorem for the genuine sixth-order entropy defect, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:poisson-finite-time-defect-stability} becomes its
finite-time consequence.  Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-finite-time-defect-stability}
is the corresponding controlled-class consequence, obtained by bounding the
same constants uniformly on a fixed variance and seventh-moment shell.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the coefficient algebra, finite-moment remainders, finite-grid
verification, defect identities, and stability consequences have distinct
hypotheses and are used in this order.  In particular, finite grids do not
replace the asymptotic passage, and the fourth-moment polynomial is not treated
as an entropy coefficient before the seventh-moment bridge.
