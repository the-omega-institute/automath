\section{Poisson Smoothing and Entropy Asymptotics}%
\label{sec:entropy-asymptotics}

\subsection*{Entropy proof guide and hypothesis index}\label{sec:entropy-theorem-map}

Throughout this section we write
\[
X_c:=\gamma-\bar\gamma,\qquad
\mu_n:=\int_{\RR}X_c^n\,\dd\nu(\gamma)
\]
whenever the moment exists.  The numbered theorem map is in the introduction,
Theorems~\ref{thm:intro-multidimensional-jet}--\ref{thm:intro-kl-tail-identity}
and Corollary~\ref{cor:intro-defect-certificate}.  This section proves the
entropy part of that map by isolating two analytic steps: a density quotient
expansion and a nonlinear KL transfer.  The paragraphs below are a proof
guide and hypothesis index, not additional theorem statements.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Density quotient input.}
Let \(L\ge2\), and assume \(\EE|X_c|^L<\infty\).  With
\(\omega(\dd y)=\pi^{-1}(1+y^2)^{-1}\dd y\), the Cauchy-scale density
quotient has the coefficient-evaluation threshold expansion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:guide-theorem-a-density-quotient}
\frac{(P_t*\nu)(\bar\gamma+ty)}{P_t(ty)}-1
=
\sum_{n=2}^{L}\mu_nu_n(y)t^{-n}+\rho_{L,t}(y),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:guide-theorem-a-density-remainder}
\|\rho_{L,t}\|_\infty+\|\rho_{L,t}\|_{L^1(\omega)}=o(t^{-L}).
\end{equation}
The quotient identity behind this statement is
\[
\frac{P_t(\bar\gamma+ty-\gamma)}{P_t(ty)}
=\frac{1+y^2}{1+(y-X_c/t)^2}.
\]
Thus the constant mode cancels because the quotient is compared with \(1\),
and the linear mode cancels because \(X_c\) is centred.  In the body this is
proved as Lemma~\ref{lem:exact-moment-density-truncation}; the
Cayley--Chebyshev modes \(u_n\), the formal coefficient algebra, and the
Laurent constant-term rule are developed in
Theorems~\ref{thm:universal-entropy-coefficients} and
\ref{thm:finite-fourier-mode-integral-algorithm}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Nonlinear entropy transfer.}
Suppose that the quotient expansion above holds and put
\[
d_t(y):=\sum_{n=2}^{L}\mu_nu_n(y)t^{-n}.
\]
Then, with
\[
I(n_1,\ldots,n_r):=
\int_{\RR}u_{n_1}(y)\cdots u_{n_r}(y)\,\omega(\dd y),
\]
one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:guide-theorem-b-entropy-transfer}
\begin{split}
D_{\rm KL}(P_t*\nu\|P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
&=
\sum_{m=4}^{L+2}
\sum_{r=2}^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}
\frac{(-1)^r}{r(r-1)}
\sum_{\substack{n_1+\cdots+n_r=m\\ 2\le n_j\le L}}
\mu_{n_1}\cdots\mu_{n_r} \\
&\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad
{}\times I(n_1,\ldots,n_r)t^{-m}
+o(t^{-(L+2)}).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The proof is the finite Taylor expansion of
\(\Phi(s)=(1+s)\log(1+s)-s\) after the change of variables
\(P_t(ty)t\,\dd y=\omega(\dd y)\).  Since \(\Phi(s)\) begins with
\(s^2/2\), a density error \(o(t^{-L})\) first appears in entropy multiplied
by the \(t^{-2}\) density scale, giving \(o(t^{-(L+2)})\).  This transfer is
carried out in
Theorem~\ref{prop:coefficient-identification-poisson-entropy-transfer}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Corollaries and quantitative margins.}
Setting \(L=2N-2\) in Theorems A and B gives the
coefficient-evaluation threshold hierarchy
Theorem~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-even-orders}.  Setting \(L=6\) gives the explicit
eighth-order coefficient layer and the finite seventh-moment remainder,
Theorems~\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth}--\ref{thm:poisson-kl-eighth-seventh-remainder}.
The only quantitative entropy-transfer regime retained in this article uses
the same density order \(L=2N-2\), with a positive moment margin
\(\eta\in(0,1]\); its constants are collected in
Appendix~\ref{subsubsec:finite-moment-constants-variants}.  The finite-time
certificate and controlled-shell estimates are appendix-level bookkeeping
consequences, recorded in
Lemma~\ref{lem:controlled-shell-conditional-certificate}, of the identity
\[
\mathfrak D_t^{(6)}
=\Delta_6-A_8t^{-2}-t^6E(t)
\]
once bounds on \(A_8\) and on the remainder are fixed; if the inserted KL
value is numerical or statistical, the certificate carries a separate
\(t^6\)-scaled error term.

The multidimensional radial Poisson analogue repeats the same two-step
structure only after fixing an integer \(L\ge\max\{2,d+1\}\) and assuming
\(\EE|X_c|^L<\infty\).  Theorem~\ref{thm:multidimensional-density-quotient}
gives the tensor density quotient, Theorem~\ref{thm:multidimensional-poisson-entropy-jets}
transfers it to entropy, and Corollary~\ref{cor:multidim-coefficient-formula}
records the multiindex coefficient formula.  The odd coefficients vanish by
Lemma~\ref{lem:multidim-parity-low-order-display}, which also displays the
leading \(t^{-4}\) layer, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:multidim-leading-positive-defect}
gives the coercive all-dimensional leading covariance defect under the same
dimension-dependent \(L\ge d+1\) tail hypothesis.  In dimensions \(d\ge2\)
the displayed \(\mathcal A_4^{(d)}\) asymptotic is therefore not a
finite-covariance-only theorem.  Proposition~\ref{prop:multidim-large-translation-obstruction}
in the proof section explains the methodological obstruction: the large
translated peak of the quotient has
\(L^\infty\) size \(|z|^{d+1}\), and its \(L^2(\Omega_d)\) size is still
\(|z|^{(d+1)/2}\).
