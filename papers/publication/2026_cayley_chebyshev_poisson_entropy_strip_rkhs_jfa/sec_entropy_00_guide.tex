\subsection*{Theorem map and hypotheses}\label{sec:entropy-theorem-map}

$\Delta:=\gamma-\bar\gamma$ and
$\mu_n:=\int_{\RR}\Delta^n\,\dd\nu(\gamma)$ whenever it exists.  The
following map is the single reference point for the hypotheses used by the
entropy results.
