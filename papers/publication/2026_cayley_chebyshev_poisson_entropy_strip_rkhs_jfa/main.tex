\begin{abstract}
Let two probability measures evolve simultaneously under a symmetric
\(\alpha\)-stable heat semigroup.  Under finite \((d+\alpha)\)-moments, we
prove that their relative entropy is locally absolutely continuous and that
its derivative is the negative logarithmic nonlocal Bregman form.  The result
includes finiteness and local integrability of the dissipation, closure of the
two noncompact Green pairings generated by the singular jump kernel, decay at
infinity, and the endpoint representation of relative entropy as the tail
integral of its dissipation.  A moving stable reference yields the analogous
exact Bregman representation along the symmetric-stable interpolation.

For laws whose tensor moments agree below an integer order \(r\ge1\), we
identify the optimal uniform complete-moment exponent
\[
 p_{r,\alpha,d}=\max\left\{r,
 \frac{2r(d+\alpha)}{d+\alpha+2r}\right\}.
\]
Finiteness at this endpoint implies an \(s^{-2r}\) relative-entropy
asymptotic with an orthogonally invariant positive-definite quadratic form of
the first unmatched tensor.  When \(d+\alpha>2r\), every smaller exponent at
which that tensor is defined fails uniformly; thus the endpoint is both
sufficient and sharp over moment classes.

Under only finite \(r\)-th moments, we further decompose the entropy
law by law into the universal tensor energy and a nonnegative relative
\(\Phi\)-energy between positive proxies that retain the unexpanded tails and
the common lower-order jet.  An abstract bounded-score kernel theorem proves
the same positive tail-jet decomposition from bounded normalized derivatives
and continuity of the top translated derivative quotient.  It applies in
particular to stable, Poisson, and multivariate Student kernels and is stable
under affine images, finite products, and finite positive mixtures.
\end{abstract}
