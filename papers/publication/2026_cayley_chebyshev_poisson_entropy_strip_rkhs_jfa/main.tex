\begin{abstract}
Let two probability measures evolve simultaneously under a symmetric
\(\alpha\)-stable heat semigroup.  Under finite \((d+\alpha)\)-moments, we
prove that their relative entropy is locally absolutely continuous and that
its derivative is the negative logarithmic nonlocal Bregman form.  The result
includes finiteness and local integrability of the dissipation, closure of the
two noncompact Green pairings generated by the singular jump kernel, decay at
infinity, and the endpoint representation of relative entropy as the tail
integral of its dissipation.  Under finite initial relative entropy, the same
local identity and local integrability hold without any moment assumption.  A
moving stable reference yields the analogous
exact Bregman representation along the symmetric-stable interpolation.

For laws whose tensor moments agree below an integer order \(r\ge1\), let
\(m=\lfloor\kappa_{r,\beta}\rfloor\), where \(\beta>d\).  For every strictly
positive \(C^{m+1}\) polynomial-tail density \(p\) satisfying
\(p(y)\asymp(1+|y|)^{-\beta}\) and the bounded normalized derivative condition
\[
 \max_{1\le|\gamma|\le m+1}
 \left\|\frac{\partial^\gamma p}{p}\right\|_\infty<\infty,
\]
we identify the optimal uniform complete-moment exponent:
\[
 \kappa_{r,\beta}=\max\left\{r,
 \frac{2r\beta}{\beta+2r}\right\}.
\]
Finiteness at this endpoint implies an \(s^{-2r}\) relative-entropy
asymptotic with a positive-definite quadratic form of the first unmatched
tensor.  When \(\beta>2r\), every smaller exponent at which that tensor is
defined fails uniformly; thus the endpoint is both sufficient and sharp over
moment classes.  This includes all isotropic strictly stable kernels with
\(\beta=d+\alpha\) and all multivariate Student kernels.

For the power divergences of orders \(1<\theta\le2\), a second sharp
moment phase appears.  The optimal exponent is
\[
 \max\left\{p_{r,\alpha,d},
 \frac{(d+\alpha)(\theta-1)}{\theta}\right\}.
\]
The first term is the relative-entropy asymptotic barrier; the second is
the stable-tail integrability barrier forced by the superlinear divergence.
At and above the endpoint the leading tensor energy is unchanged, whereas
below the two barriers the finite-divergence asymptotic and finiteness
itself, respectively, fail uniformly.

Under only finite \(r\)-th moments, we further decompose the entropy
law by law into the universal tensor energy and a nonnegative relative
\(\Phi\)-energy between positive proxies that retain the unexpanded tails and
the common lower-order jet.  An abstract bounded-score kernel theorem proves
the same positive tail-jet decomposition from bounded normalized derivatives
and continuity of the top translated derivative quotient.  It applies in
particular to stable, Poisson, and multivariate Student kernels and is stable
under affine images, finite products, and finite positive mixtures.
\end{abstract}
