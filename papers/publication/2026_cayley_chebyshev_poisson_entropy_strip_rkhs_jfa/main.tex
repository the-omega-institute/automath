\begin{abstract}
For a probability law \(\nu\) on \(\RR\), the Cayley transform reduces the
large-scale matched Poisson entropy to finite Laurent constant-term algebra.
Writing \(X_c=\gamma-\bar\gamma\), we prove the unconditional threshold
\[
\mathbb E X_c^2<\infty
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\limsup_{t\to\infty}t^4
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\,\|\,P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))<\infty,
\]
with limit \((\mathbb E X_c^2)^2/8\) in the finite-variance case and
\(+\infty\) otherwise.  More generally, finite centred \((2N-2)\)th absolute
moment yields an expansion through \(t^{-2N}\), with all odd coefficients
zero and every even coefficient given by one Cayley--Chebyshev constant-term
rule.  For \(m\ge3\), its top layer is
\(\kappa_m\mathfrak m_2\mathfrak m_{2m-2}\), where
\(\kappa_m=(-1)^m(m-1)2^{-2m+2}\ne0\); hence the \((2m-2)\)nd absolute moment
is the sharp universal polynomial-evaluability threshold.  The resulting
coefficient defects form an explicit two-level ladder.

At a missing top moment, if the centred tails are two-sided regularly varying
of index \(-(2N-2)\), with common slowly varying factor \(L\) and
\(\int^tL(s)\dd s/s\to\infty\), then, without symmetry, a density assumption,
or monotonicity of \(L\),
\[
\frac{t^{2N}}{M_{2N-2}(t)}
\left\{D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\,\|\,P_t)
-\sum_{j=2}^{N-1}A_{2j}(\nu)t^{-2j}\right\}
\longrightarrow \kappa_N\mu_2.
\]
Gaussian-quadrature comparison further gives unconditional all-order moment
thresholds and positive square boundary terms under regular variation.

In \(\RR^d\), finite \(L\)-moment with \(L\ge d+1\) produces the matched
radial quotient jet and tensor KL coefficients through order \(L+2\), with a
sharp two-region remainder criterion.  The sharp all-class
\(L^2(\Omega_d)\) exponent is \(\max\{2,(d+1)/2\}\).  Finite covariance is
equivalent to the leading Poisson KL coefficient for \(d\le3\).  More
generally, for every isotropic strictly \(\alpha\)-stable kernel,
\(0<\alpha<2\), the optimal uniform sufficient absolute-moment exponent for
the covariance-order KL asymptotic is
\[
 p_{\alpha,d}=\max\left\{2,
 \frac{4(d+\alpha)}{d+\alpha+4}\right\}.
\]
The endpoint is sufficient and every smaller exponent fails uniformly over
its moment class.  Under finite covariance alone, the corresponding
law-by-law formula is
\[
 H_{\alpha,d}(s)=\mathcal Q_{\alpha,d}(\Sigma)s^{-4}
 +\int\Phi(V_s^\nu)\dd\Omega_{\alpha,d}+o(s^{-4}),
\]
where \(V_s^\nu\) is the unexpanded translate mixture over
\(\{|X|>s\}\).  Thus the covariance coefficient is attained exactly when
this nonlinear tail energy is \(o(s^{-4})\).  In the Poisson case,
multivariate regular variation alone does not determine the leading entropy;
a covariance-matched proxy isolates the obstruction in an asymptotically
exact nonnegative defect and admits a raw-tail Poisson-energy decomposition.

Finally, the secant modes generate a Poisson-strip RKHS with an explicit Gram
kernel, unitary Poisson image, integer-orbit symbol, and exact lattice
projection theorem for observation channels.  Under finite variance the
moving Cauchy quotient also satisfies the fully justified nonlocal Bregman
tail identity
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\,\|\,P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
=\int_t^\infty\mathcal I_\nu(s)\,\dd s.
\]
\end{abstract}
