\begin{abstract}
For the radial Poisson kernel on \(\RR^d\), the multidimensional
finite-moment entropy jet proved here is stated with an integer truncation
\(L\ge\max\{2,d+1\}\) and a finite centred \(L\)th absolute moment.  Under
that same dimension-dependent tail hypothesis, its leading \(t^{-4}\)
entropy coefficient has an explicit positive isotropic/traceless-covariance
decomposition; for \(d\ge2\) this is not asserted as a finite-covariance-only
asymptotic.  In dimension one, the Cayley transform puts the Cauchy law, the
Poisson semigroup, and circle Fourier analysis in one normalization.  For a
fixed input law~\(\nu\), the large-time entropy comparison
\[
  D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\,\|\,P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
\]
against the matched translated Cauchy profile becomes a finite
Cayley--Chebyshev coefficient problem.  For every \(N\ge2\), a finite centred
\((2N-2)\)th absolute moment gives the expansion
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_t*\nu\,\|\,P_t(\cdot-\bar\gamma))
=\sum_{m=2}^{N}A_{2m}(\nu)t^{-2m}+o(t^{-2N}),
\]
where the coefficients are universal polynomials in the centred moments,
computed by Laurent extraction from Cayley modes.  The explicit \(N=4\) layer
is evaluated under a finite centred sixth absolute moment, giving the
\(t^{-4}\), \(t^{-6}\), and \(t^{-8}\) coefficients and the associated
sixth/eighth-order defect identities; a finite seventh absolute moment
supplies a quantitative \(O(t^{-9})\) remainder.  A final applications and
bookkeeping step combines the deterministic identity
\[
  \mathfrak D_t^{(6)}
  =\Delta_6-A_8t^{-2}-t^6E(t)
\]
with preassigned bounds for \(A_8\) and \(E(t)\) to give conditional
finite-time sixth-defect and \(W_2\) estimates.
\end{abstract}
