\section{Sharpness and one Poisson application}
\label{sec:sharpness-poisson-application}

The finite-difference blocks and moving-ball separation in
Lemmas~\ref{lem:stable-finite-difference-blocks}
and~\ref{lem:stable-moving-ball-probability-separation} prove the sharpness
clauses of Theorems~\ref{thm:high-dimensional-kl-moment-threshold}
and~\ref{thm:all-order-stable-first-unmatched-moment}.  The construction
matches all tensor moments below the prescribed order before the radii and
weights are chosen, so the entropy lower bound does not trade moment matching
for tail separation.

We record one direct application of the abstract tail-jet theorem.  Let
\[
 P_s^{(d)}(x)=c_d\frac{s}{(s^2+|x|^2)^{(d+1)/2}}
\]
be the radial Poisson kernel on \(\RR^d\).

\begin{corollary}[Poisson positive tail-jet decomposition]
\label{cor:poisson-positive-tail-jet-decomposition}
Let \(r\ge1\), and let \(\nu,\eta\) be probability laws on \(\RR^d\) with
finite \(r\)-th absolute moments and matching tensor moments below order
\(r\).  Then the proxies and nonnegative defect of
Corollary~\ref{thm:stable-arbitrary-order-law-by-law-decomposition}, with
\(\alpha=1\), give
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(P_s^{(d)}*\nu\|P_s^{(d)}*\eta)
 =\mathcal E_{r,s}^{\rm Pois}(\nu,\eta)
  +\mathcal Q_{1,d,r}(\Delta_r)s^{-2r}+o(s^{-2r}).
\]
The defect is finite and nonnegative, and the universal coefficient is
attained if and only if
\(s^{2r}\mathcal E_{r,s}^{\rm Pois}(\nu,\eta)\to0\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The Poisson kernel is the isotropic \(1\)-stable kernel in the spatial-scale
normalization used throughout.  Apply
Corollary~\ref{thm:stable-arbitrary-order-law-by-law-decomposition} with
\(\alpha=1\).
\end{proof}
