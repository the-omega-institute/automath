This stable statement is the exact specialization of the numbered abstract
kernel Theorem~\ref{thm:abstract-positive-tail-jet-kernel}.  That theorem
gives a sufficient kernel criterion: bounded normalized derivatives through
order \(r\) and quotient-uniform continuity at the top order.  Its bounded
score already implies finite translate entropy with linear growth, so no
separate entropy hypothesis is imposed.  Proposition
\ref{prop:tail-jet-kernel-closure-and-examples} verifies stable, Poisson, and
multivariate Student kernels and proves closure under invertible affine
images, finite products, and finite positive mixtures.  No tail asymptotic or
regular variation enters the abstract proof.  Proposition
\ref{prop:gaussian-additive-proxy-mechanism-boundary} locates Gaussian
smoothing in a different proxy geometry: this theorem is the
bounded-score/additive branch, whereas the Gaussian requires an
unbounded-score/multiplicative or exponential-score Hermite branch.  The
Gaussian result recalled above is therefore not claimed as coverage by the
positive tail-proxy theorem.
