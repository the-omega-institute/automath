The theorem is a specialization of the unnumbered abstract kernel theorem
that precedes it.  For a strictly positive density \(p\), the proof uses only
bounded normalized derivatives through order \(r\), uniform
translation-continuity of the normalized order-\(r\) derivative, and an
order-\(r\) bound on the entropy of a translate of \(p\).  These conditions
hold for all isotropic strictly stable densities, for the Poisson kernel, and
for every multivariate Student density \(c_{\beta,d}(1+|y|^2)^{-\beta/2}\),
\(\beta>d\).  No tail asymptotic or regular variation enters the abstract
proof.  The Gaussian density does not satisfy the bounded normalized-jet
condition: its normalized derivatives are Hermite polynomials, and an
additive common jet need not remain positive.  Thus the Gaussian
first-unmatched-moment theorem recalled above uses a different weighted
Hermite mechanism; it is not presented as a member of the positive
tail-proxy theorem.
