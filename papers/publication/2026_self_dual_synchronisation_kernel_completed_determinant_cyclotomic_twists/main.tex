\begin{abstract}
This paper is a rigorous computational case study of one explicit
finite-state object: a weighted ten-state synchronisation kernel with a
binary output labelling and a self-duality exchanging the two outputs.
The purpose is not to classify self-dual kernels in general, but to make
this kernel completely reproducible and to show how much information its
completed determinant carries.  Its weighted adjacency matrix is
\(B(u)=B_0+uB_1\), and we write
\(\Delta(z,u)=\det(I-zB(u))\).  The kernel satisfies
\(\Delta(z,u)=\Delta(uz,u^{-1})\), and after the completion
\(u=r^2\), \(s=r+r^{-1}\), \(w=zr\), this yields a polynomial
\(\widehat\Delta(w,s)\in\ZZ[s][w]\) of degree \(6\) in \(w\).  We
determine this polynomial explicitly, prove that the normalization
\(\overline{X}\) of the projective closure of
\(\widehat\Delta(w,s)=0\) has genus \(6\), prove that its quotient by
the involution
\((w,s)\mapsto(-w,-s)\) is a smooth plane quartic of genus \(3\), and
prove that the generic Galois group over \(\QQ(s)\) is \(S_6\).

The computational contributions are explicit and auditable.  The paper
displays the determinant, the self-duality and sign-symmetry
certificates, the quotient quartic, the affine and projective smoothness
checks, the finite-field data certifying the \(S_6\) Galois group, the
root-separation certificates, and the endpoint Taylor jet.  The
calculations are reproduced by the Sage runner
\(\texttt{certificates/verify\_certificates.py}\), whose printed keys
are referenced in the proofs and appendix.

We also identify the cyclic skew-product lifts
\(\widetilde B_q=B_0\otimes I_q+B_1\otimes P_q\), whose Fourier
decomposition produces the unit-circle twists and their real
cyclotomic factors.  For the normalized cyclotomic factors
\[
  R_m(w):=\prod_{\alpha\in A_m}\widehat\Delta(w,\alpha)
\]
and their ordered-resultant representation
\[
  R_m(w)=\Res_s(\Phi_m^+(s),\widehat\Delta(w,s)),
\]
with \(\Phi_m^+\) in the first slot, we prove a degree law and obtain
an asymptotic expansion through order \(m^{-12}\) for the associated
twisted Perron roots \(\rho_m\).  The endpoint-selection argument is
formulated as a reusable lemma for primitive self-dual kernels, but in
this paper it is used only after the stated spectral-gap and separation
hypotheses have been verified for the ten-state kernel above.
\end{abstract}
