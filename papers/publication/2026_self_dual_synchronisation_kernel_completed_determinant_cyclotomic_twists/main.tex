\begin{abstract}
We study one explicit finite-state example: a weighted ten-state
synchronisation kernel
\(B(u)=B_0+uB_1\) whose binary outputs are exchanged by a self-duality.
All claims are made for the displayed transition table, its completed
determinant, and the stated cyclotomic root-separation certificates. No
classification of self-dual kernels and no unconditional statement
about external elementary-function catalogues is asserted.

Writing \(\Delta(z,u)=\det(I-zB(u))\), the self-duality gives
\(\Delta(z,u)=\Delta(uz,u^{-1})\).  With \(u=r^2\), \(w=zr\), and
\(s=r+r^{-1}\), this compresses to an explicit polynomial
\(\widehat\Delta(w,s)\in\ZZ[s][w]\) of degree \(6\).  We record the
main theorem surface: the quotient by
\((w,s)\mapsto(-w,-s)\) is a smooth plane quartic of genus \(3\), the
normalisation of \(\widehat\Delta(w,s)=0\) has genus \(6\), and the
generic Galois group over \(\QQ(s)\) is \(S_6\).

For the cyclic lifts
\(\widetilde B_\ell=B_0\otimes I_\ell+B_1\otimes P_\ell\), Fourier
decomposition gives the primitive real cyclotomic factors
\[
  R_m(w)=\prod_{\alpha\in A_m}\widehat\Delta(w,\alpha)
       =\Res_s(\Phi_m^+(s),\widehat\Delta(w,s)),
\]
with the cyclotomic polynomial in the first resultant slot.  The
associated roots satisfy
\[
  \rho_m
  =3-\frac{11\pi^2}{17m^2}+O(m^{-4}),
\]
with an explicit expansion through \(m^{-12}\). The reproducibility
runner emits the full symbolic transcript; the appendix records the
verification ledger and the key exact polynomials and coefficients used
in the proofs.
\end{abstract}
