# Risk Register: Observer-State Semantics

## Overclaim Risks

- The paper is read as claiming AI is conscious.
- The paper is read as solving phenomenal consciousness.
- The formal rows are treated as sufficient conditions rather than audit
  criteria.
- Existing consciousness literature is cited too thinly.

## Kill Criteria

Do not promote this seed if:

- the title uses "AI consciousness" without "observer-state" or "audit schema";
- non-claims are absent from abstract and introduction;
- exact formal theorem labels are missing;
- the venue requires empirical consciousness evidence rather than conceptual
  analysis.
