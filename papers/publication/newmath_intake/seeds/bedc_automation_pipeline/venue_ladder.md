# Venue Ladder: BEDC Automation Pipeline

1. CICM: mathematical software, formal mathematics, proof-assistant
   infrastructure, and knowledge-management systems.
2. AITP: AI and theorem proving, especially methodology around LLM-assisted
   formalization.
3. CPP / ITP-adjacent workshop routes: certified mathematical libraries,
   proof-assistant workflows, and reusable lessons from formalization.

## Journal Targets

1. Journal of Automated Reasoning.
2. Journal of Formalized Reasoning.
3. Logical Methods in Computer Science, only if the paper is strengthened with
   a clear logical or proof-assistant methodology contribution.
