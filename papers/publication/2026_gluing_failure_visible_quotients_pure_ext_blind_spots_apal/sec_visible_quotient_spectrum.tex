\subsection{Exact realization spectrum of the visible quotient}

The preceding results determine the quotient attached to a fixed branch
class, but they do not determine which quotients can occur or how many
distinct branch classes the quotient identifies. The unrestricted
simultaneous-realization theorem of the sibling paper supplies the missing
existence statement \cite[Theorem~6.1]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026}.
Combined with the character criterion above, it gives the following exact
spectrum.

\begin{theorem}[Exact spectrum and multiplicity of branchwise visible
quotients]\label{thm:exact-visible-quotient-spectrum}
Let $\mathcal U$ be a finite good cover satisfying the connected-overlap and
coherent constant-band hypotheses, let its nerve $N$ be connected, and let
$A$ be a finite abelian group. Write
\[
H_2(N,\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb Z^{\beta}\oplus
\bigoplus_\ell\bigoplus_{j=1}^{r_\ell}\mathbb Z/\ell^{\lambda_{\ell,j}}\mathbb Z,
\qquad
\lambda_{\ell,1}\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_{\ell,r_\ell}\ge1,
\]
and put
\[
E:=\operatorname{Ext}^1(H_1(N,\mathbb Z),A).
\]
For every prime $\ell$, define
\[
\overline\lambda_{\ell,j}:=
\begin{cases}
\infty,&1\le j\le\beta,\\
\lambda_{\ell,j-\beta},&\beta<j\le\beta+r_\ell,\\
0,&j>\beta+r_\ell.
\end{cases}
\]
Let $\Lambda$ be a finite nonempty set, and for each $v\in\Lambda$ fix a
subgroup $K_v\le A$. Write
\[
(K_v)_\ell\cong
\bigoplus_{j=1}^{s_{v,\ell}}\mathbb Z/\ell^{\mu_{v,\ell,j}}\mathbb Z,
\qquad
\mu_{v,\ell,1}\ge\cdots\ge\mu_{v,\ell,s_{v,\ell}}\ge1.
\]
The following are equivalent.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item For every $v\in\Lambda$, every prime $\ell$, and every
$1\le j\le s_{v,\ell}$,
\[
\mu_{v,\ell,j}\le\overline\lambda_{\ell,j},
\]
and, if $K_v=0$ for some $v$, then $E\ne0$.
\item On the finite-intersection reference site associated with $\mathcal U$
there is an admitted reference with a globally conservative stack-banded
realization prestack such that
\[
\Vis_{p,s}(r)\cong\Lambda,
\qquad
M,p\Vdash\Null_s^{\mathrm{glue}}(r),
\]
and the branch indexed by $v$ has a finite nerve presentation
$(\mathcal U,A,\omega_v)$ satisfying
\[
K_{\omega_v}=K_v,
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{vis}}^{\omega_v}=A/K_v.
\]
The realization may be chosen terminally essentially surjective, and its
component gerbes are all non-neutral.
\end{enumerate}

Moreover, for every homomorphism
\[
\phi:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow A,
\]
the set of classes $\omega\in H^2(N,A)$ with
$\mathrm{ev}_{\omega}=\phi$ is a principal homogeneous set under $E$.
Consequently it has cardinality $|E|$, and all of its classes have the same
hidden subgroup $\operatorname{Im}(\phi)$, the same visible quotient
$A/\operatorname{Im}(\phi)$, and the same class-admissible character set.
All of these classes can be realized simultaneously as distinct marked
component gerbes of one terminally essentially surjective prestack. If
$\phi\ne0$, all $|E|$ classes are non-neutral. If $\phi=0$, exactly
$|E|-1$ are non-neutral and one is neutral; in every terminally essentially
surjective realization with these marked classes, the terminal source fibre
is empty over each non-neutral label and is a principal homogeneous set under
$H^1(N,A)$ over the neutral label.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We first classify the possible image subgroups. A subgroup $K\le A$ is the
image of a homomorphism $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\to A$ if and only if it is a
quotient of $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)$: an image map factors as an epimorphism onto
$K$ followed by the inclusion $K\hookrightarrow A$, and the converse is the
same factorization read in the other direction.

It remains to express the quotient condition in invariant factors. Fix a
prime $\ell$. A homomorphism from the free summand to the finite group
$K_\ell$ factors through $(\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z)^\beta$ for any $m$ at
least the exponent of $K_\ell$. For finite abelian $\ell$-groups
\[
D\cong\bigoplus_j\mathbb Z/\ell^{\delta_j}\mathbb Z,
\qquad
K_\ell\cong\bigoplus_j\mathbb Z/\ell^{\mu_j}\mathbb Z,
\]
with both exponent sequences nonincreasing, an epimorphism
$D\twoheadrightarrow K_\ell$ exists exactly when $\mu_j\le\delta_j$ for every
$j$. Sufficiency follows by projecting the $j$th cyclic factor onto the
$j$th target factor. For necessity, an epimorphism induces an epimorphism on
\[
\ell^{t-1}D/\ell^tD\longrightarrow
\ell^{t-1}K_\ell/\ell^tK_\ell
\]
for every $t\ge1$. Comparing dimensions over $\mathbb F_\ell$ shows that the
number of $\delta_j$ at least $t$ is no smaller than the number of $\mu_j$ at
least $t$ for every $t$, which is equivalent to the componentwise
inequalities. Taking $m$ larger than every torsion exponent makes the
$\beta$ free factors the first $\beta$ entries, represented by $\infty$.
Thus $K$ is a quotient of $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)$ exactly when the inequalities in
\textup{(i)} hold for every prime.

Assume \textup{(ii)}. For each $v$, the evaluation map onto its image makes
$K_v$ a quotient of $H_2(N,\mathbb Z)$, so the invariant-factor inequalities
follow. If $K_v=0$, then $\omega_v$ has zero evaluation. The branch gerbe is
non-neutral, hence $\omega_v\ne0$, and exactness of the universal coefficient
sequence gives a nonzero element of $E$. This proves \textup{(i)}.

Conversely, assume \textup{(i)}. For each $v$, the quotient criterion gives
a homomorphism
\[
\phi_v:H_2(N,\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow A
\]
with image $K_v$. The universal coefficient sequence is surjective onto
$\operatorname{Hom}(H_2(N,\mathbb Z),A)$, so choose a lift
$\omega_v\in H^2(N,A)$. If $K_v\ne0$, every such lift is nonzero. If
$K_v=0$, use the last hypothesis in \textup{(i)} to choose a nonzero element
of $E$ as $\omega_v$. Hence every chosen class is nonzero.

Apply the unrestricted simultaneous-realization theorem
\cite[Theorem~6.1]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026} to the family
$(\omega_v)_{v\in\Lambda}$. It gives a terminally essentially surjective
$A$-banded prestack whose sheafified component set is $\Lambda$ and whose
marked component classes are the prescribed $\omega_v$. Take the
one-reference semantic instance whose terminal slice is this finite site and
whose local-object functor is the component presheaf supplied by that
construction. The descended
stackification banding of
\Cref{prop:stackification-band-descends} makes this a stack-banded realization
in the sense used here, while terminal essential surjectivity is global
conservativity at the terminal fibre. The finite-site comparison is
$H^2$-computing, so every nonzero $\omega_v$ gives a non-neutral component
gerbe. The sibling theorem also gives matching without amalgamation because
there are no neutral labels. Equivalently,
\Cref{thm:gerbe-null-semantics} gives
$M,p\Vdash\Null_s^{\mathrm{glue}}(r)$. The identities for the hidden
subgroups and visible quotients follow from their definitions. This proves
\textup{(ii)}.

For the final assertions, exactness of the universal coefficient sequence
shows that the fibre over $\phi$ is a coset of the embedded group $E$, hence a
principal homogeneous $E$-set. Its cardinality is therefore $|E|$.
\Cref{thm:intrinsic-visible-quotient} shows that the class-admissible
characters depend only on $\phi$, and the hidden subgroup and quotient depend
only on $\operatorname{Im}(\phi)$. The same unrestricted theorem realizes
the finite family consisting of every class in the fibre simultaneously.
When $\phi\ne0$, none of those classes is zero. When $\phi=0$, the fibre is
the embedded copy of $E$, so it contains the zero class and exactly $|E|-1$
nonzero classes. Gerbe neutrality is equivalent to vanishing of the class.
The forced terminal-fibre statement is the marked terminal multiplicity
clause of the same realization theorem. This proves all remaining claims.
\end{proof}

The zero-image clause contains the exact pure-$\operatorname{Ext}$ boundary
of \cite[Corollary~6.2]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026}, while
the nonzero-image clauses classify the full branchwise quotient spectrum.
Thus the sibling result subsumes the one-class finite-site existence content
of the worked blind example below, but not the present spectrum or the exact
indistinguishability multiplicity.
