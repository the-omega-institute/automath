\section{Lower-Layer Semantics and Conservative Extension}\label{sec:preliminaries}

The later sections use only one abstract idea: a higher semantic layer may add coordinates or structure without changing the behavior of formulas already present below. The definitions in this section isolate that invariance principle and nothing more.

\begin{definition}[Logic layer]\label{def:logic-layer}
A \emph{logic layer} is a semantic package
\[
\mathbb L=(\Sigma,\Mod,\St,\Frm,\Vdash)
\]
consisting of a signature, a class of admissible models, a state assignment for
each model, a set of formulas, and a forcing relation
\[
M,p\Vdash_{\mathbb L}\varphi
\qquad (M\in\Mod,\ p\in\St(M),\ \varphi\in\Frm).
\]
The formal definition is \cite[Definition~2.1]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026};
the present paper uses only this signature--state--forcing interface.
\end{definition}

The forcing relation is the primitive semantic notion. Consequence is derived from uniform preservation of forcing across all admissible models and states.

\begin{definition}[Conservative extension]\label{def:conservative-extension}
A layer $\mathbb L'\!$ is a \emph{conservative extension} of a layer
$\mathbb L$, written $\mathbb L\preceq\mathbb L'$, if there is an inclusion of
the lower signature and lower formulas into the higher layer, together with a
forgetful map from higher models and states to lower ones, such that every
lower formula has the same forcing value before and after forgetting:
\[
M',p'\Vdash_{\mathbb L'}\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
U(M'),U(p')\Vdash_{\mathbb L}\varphi
\]
for all lower formulas $\varphi$. The formal definition is
\cite[Definition~2.2]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026}.
\end{definition}

Thus a higher layer may add expressive power, state coordinates, or geometric constraints, but it may not alter the forcing behavior of formulas that already belonged to a lower layer.

\begin{proposition}[Composition of conservative extensions]\label{prop:conservative-composition}
If
\[
\mathbb L_0\preceq \mathbb L_1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathbb L_1\preceq \mathbb L_2
\]
are conservative extensions, then the composite inclusion of signatures and
formulas, together with the composite forgetful map on models and states, makes
$\mathbb L_2$ a conservative extension of $\mathbb L_0$. This is
\cite[Proposition~2.3]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026}; the proof
is recalled in the appendix because the finite layer chain below uses exactly
this two-step composition.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Finite-chain invariance]\label{cor:whole-chain-invariance}
For any finite chain
\[
\mathbb L_0\preceq\mathbb L_1\preceq\cdots\preceq\mathbb L_n,
\]
forcing of every $\mathbb L_0$-formula is invariant under passage to
$\mathbb L_n$ and then forgetting back to $\mathbb L_0$. This is
\cite[Corollary~2.4]{HomologicalVisibilityGluingObstructions2026}.
\end{corollary}

The constructions used later fit into the finite chain
\[
\mathbb{L}_0
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_1
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_2
\preceq
\mathbb{L}_3.
\]
Here $\mathbb{L}_0$ is the underlying pointwise semantics, $\mathbb{L}_1$ is the
information-state forcing layer, $\mathbb{L}_2$ adds admitted references and
preorder-site local objects, and $\mathbb{L}_3$ adds structural absence and,
only when a suitable realization prestack has been separately supplied or
constructed, gluing-obstruction data. By \Cref{cor:whole-chain-invariance}, the
passage up the chain is cumulative rather than revisionary: each later layer
preserves the forcing behavior already fixed below.

The conditional clause in the description of $\mathbb{L}_3$ is part of the formal bookkeeping. An $\mathbb{L}_2$-model may contain a local-object presheaf and an intended abelian band without carrying any fibred groupoid of realizations. In that case the structural absence predicates attached to $F_{p,s}$ still make sense, but component gerbes, branch obstruction classes, and visible quotients do not. Those higher objects enter only after the additional stack-banded realization data required in \Cref{def:realization-prestack,def:gluing-sensitive-realization-input} have been supplied, or after the separated split-lift criterion of \Cref{prop:split-realization-prestack} has constructed them.

This three-extension chain is the formal setting used in the present paper.
Refinement-dynamical material is not a layer of this article. Standard
site-theoretic, descent-theoretic, and homological inputs are cited at the
points where they are used.
