\begin{proof}
The algorithm in \textup{(i)} is deterministic because at each step the largest Fibonacci weight not exceeding the current remainder is uniquely defined, and the skip rule is the standard greedy Zeckendorf rule.  The bound in Lemma \ref{lem:audited-binary-fold-certificate-identities} ensures that every legal length-\(m\) word occurs as the prefix of some dyadic vertex, so the enumeration is over finite lists of sizes \(2^6,2^7,2^8\) for the only audited windows used here.

Items \textup{(ii)} and \textup{(iii)} are then obtained by two nested finite sums.  First, for each dyadic vertex, compute its fold value and increment the corresponding fiber count.  Second, in window \(6\), flip each of the six coordinates of every vertex and increment the row-column entry indexed by the original and flipped fold values.  This gives exactly
\[
N_{ij}=\#\{(\omega,k):\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)=x_i,
\ \Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_k)=x_j\},
\]
and hence the matrix entries \(P_6(i,j)=N_{ij}/(6d_i)\).  The displayed hashes are not additional assumptions; they are checksums of the stated canonical streams, so a recomputation agrees with the manuscript precisely when the stream hashes agree.

For \textup{(iv)}, multiplying the rational polynomial \(q_6\) in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate} by \(L_6\) clears all denominators and gives the displayed coefficient list.  Fraction-free Gaussian elimination applied to \(tI-P_6\) over \(\ZZ[t]\), after the same denominator clearing, verifies
\[
Q_6(t)=L_6\,\frac{\det(tI-P_6)}{t-1}.
\]
This is an identity in \(\ZZ[t]\), so no floating-point step enters the characteristic-polynomial certificate.

For \textup{(v)}, form the Sturm sequence
\[
S_0=Q_6,
\qquad
S_1=Q_6',
\qquad
S_{r+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(S_{r-1},S_r)
\]
over \(\QQ[t]\), clearing positive scalar denominators if desired because such clearing does not change signs.  Its degrees are \(20,19,\ldots,0\).  Evaluating this sequence at the four rational endpoints gives the displayed sign strings and variation counts.  Sturm's theorem therefore gives \(1-0=1\) zero in \((4841207858/10^{10},4841207859/10^{10})\) and \(20-19=1\) zero in \((-6030939755/10^{10},-6030939754/10^{10})\), which is the integer Sturm data used in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.

For \textup{(vi)}, every operation in the verbatim block is one of the operations just described: standard-library iteration and SHA--256 hashing for the canonical streams, direct enumeration of the affine map \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\) and the two residual neighbor counts, and SageMath exact rational, integer, matrix, polynomial, characteristic-polynomial, and Sturm-sequence routines for the algebraic certificate.  Since all inputs are printed in the block and every acceptance condition is an assertion against a displayed integer stream, hash, stabilizer identity, or residual count, executing the block under SageMath with Python \(3\) is a complete referee reproduction interface for the finite audit. \qedhere
\end{proof}
