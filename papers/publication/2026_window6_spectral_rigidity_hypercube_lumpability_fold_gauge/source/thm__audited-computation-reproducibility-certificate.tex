\begin{proof}
The algorithm in \textup{(i)} is deterministic because at each step the largest Fibonacci weight not exceeding the current remainder is uniquely defined, and the skip rule is the standard greedy Zeckendorf rule.  The bound in Lemma \ref{lem:audited-binary-fold-certificate-identities} ensures that every legal length-\(m\) word occurs as the prefix of some dyadic vertex, so the enumeration is over finite lists of sizes \(2^6,2^7,2^8\) for the only audited windows used here.

Items \textup{(ii)} and \textup{(iii)} are then obtained by two nested finite sums.  First, for each dyadic vertex, compute its fold value and increment the corresponding fiber count.  Second, in window \(6\), flip each of the six coordinates of every vertex and increment the row-column entry indexed by the original and flipped fold values.  This gives exactly
\[
N_{ij}=\#\{(\omega,k):\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)=x_i,
\ \Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_k)=x_j\},
\]
and hence the matrix entries \(P_6(i,j)=N_{ij}/(6d_i)\).  The displayed digests are not additional assumptions; they identify the stated canonical streams, so a recomputation agrees with the manuscript precisely when the digests agree.

For \textup{(iv)}, multiplying the rational polynomial \(q_6\) in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate} by \(L_6\) clears all denominators and gives the displayed coefficient list.  Fraction-free Gaussian elimination applied to \(tI-P_6\) over \(\ZZ[t]\), after the same denominator clearing, verifies
\[
Q_6(t)=L_6\,\frac{\det(tI-P_6)}{t-1}.
\]
This is an identity in \(\ZZ[t]\), so no floating-point step enters the characteristic-polynomial certificate.

For \textup{(v)}, form the Sturm sequence
\[
S_0=Q_6,
\qquad
S_1=Q_6',
\qquad
S_{r+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(S_{r-1},S_r)
\]
over \(\QQ[t]\), clearing positive scalar denominators if desired because such clearing does not change signs.  Its degrees are \(20,19,\ldots,0\).  Evaluating this sequence at the four rational endpoints gives the displayed sign strings and variation counts.  Sturm's theorem therefore gives \(1-0=1\) zero in \((4841207858/10^{10},4841207859/10^{10})\) and \(20-19=1\) zero in \((-6030939755/10^{10},-6030939754/10^{10})\), which is the integer Sturm data used in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.

For \\textup{(vi)}, every operation in the displayed SageMath check is one of the operations just described: standard-library iteration and SHA--256 hashing for the canonical streams, direct enumeration of the affine map \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\) and the two residual neighbor counts, and SageMath exact rational, integer, matrix, polynomial, characteristic-polynomial, and Sturm-sequence routines for the algebraic certificate.  Since all inputs are printed in the block and every acceptance condition is an assertion against a displayed integer stream, hash, stabilizer identity, or residual count, executing the block under SageMath with Python \(3\) is a complete referee reproduction interface for the finite audit. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Referee guide to the exact window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} audit]\label{prop:referee-guide-exact-window6-audit}
The finite computation used in Theorem \ref{thm:main-fold6-nonlumpability} can be checked by the following four independent exact-arithmetic tests.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \textup{(Fiber and block-indicator check.)}  Recompute the greedy Zeckendorf prefix of each of the \(64\) dyadic vertices.  The resulting fiber table must be exactly Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-canonical-fiber-table}; equivalently, its canonical stream has the fiber digest in item \textup{(ii)} of Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}.
\item \textup{(Edge-count and pushforward check.)}  For each printed source word and each of the six coordinate flips, recompute the folded target.  The resulting integer edge-count matrix must be the matrix \(N\) in Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix}, and the transition matrix is then the rational matrix \(P_6(i,j)=N_{ij}/(6d_i)\).
\item \textup{(Residual check.)}  The two displayed same-fiber words \(000000\) and \(010101\) must fold to \(000000\), and their target-fiber counts into \(000100\) must be \(0\) and \(1\).  This single integer comparison is the nonintertwining witness in Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness}.
\item \textup{(Spectral check.)}  Compute \(\det(tI-P_6)\) over \(\QQ[t]\), divide by the factor \(t-1\), and multiply by \(L_6=3234734993627557134336\).  The resulting integer polynomial must have the coefficient stream in item \textup{(iv)} above.  Applying the Sturm sequence over \(\QQ[t]\) at the four rational endpoints in item \textup{(v)} gives the variation drops \(1-0\) and \(20-19\), hence one real root in each printed interval.
\end{enumerate}
No item in this guide uses floating-point arithmetic.  Decimal strings such as \(0.4841207858\) abbreviate rational endpoints with denominator \(10^{10}\), and the isolating intervals are certified by signs of integer or rational polynomial values, not by numerical eigenvalue routines.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Item \textup{(i)} is exactly the deterministic enumeration in Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}\textup{(i)}--\textup{(ii)} specialized to \(m=6\), and Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-canonical-fiber-table} is the same enumeration printed as a block-indicator table. \end{proof}
