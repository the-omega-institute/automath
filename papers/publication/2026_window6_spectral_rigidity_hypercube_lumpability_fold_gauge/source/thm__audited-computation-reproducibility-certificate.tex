Items \textup{(ii)} and \textup{(iii)} are then obtained by two nested finite sums.  First, for each dyadic vertex, compute its fold value and increment the corresponding fiber count.  Second, in window \(6\), flip each of the six coordinates of every vertex and increment the row-column entry indexed by the original and flipped fold values.  This gives exactly
\[
N_{ij}=\#\{(\omega,k):\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)=x_i,
\ \Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_k)=x_j\},
\]
and hence the matrix entries \(P_6(i,j)=N_{ij}/(6d_i)\).  The displayed digests are not additional assumptions; they identify the stated canonical streams, so a recomputation agrees with the manuscript precisely when the digests agree.

For \textup{(iv)}, multiplying the rational polynomial \(q_6\) in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate} by \(L_6\) clears all denominators and gives the displayed coefficient list. \[
Q_6(t)=L_6\,\frac{\det(tI-P_6)}{t-1}.
\]
This is an identity in \(\ZZ[t]\), so no floating-point step enters the characteristic-polynomial certificate.

For \textup{(v)}, form the Sturm sequence
\[
S_0=Q_6,
\qquad
S_1=Q_6',
\qquad
S_{r+1}=-\operatorname{rem}(S_{r-1},S_r)
\]
over \(\QQ[t]\), clearing positive scalar denominators if desired because such clearing does not change signs.  Its degrees are \(20,19,\ldots,0\). Sturm's theorem therefore gives \(1-0=1\) zero in \((4841207858/10^{10},4841207859/10^{10})\) and \(20-19=1\) zero in \((-6030939755/10^{10},-6030939754/10^{10})\), which is the integer Sturm data used in Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.
