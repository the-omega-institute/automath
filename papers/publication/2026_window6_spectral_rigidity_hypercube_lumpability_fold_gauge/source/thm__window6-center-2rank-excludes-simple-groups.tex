\begin{remark}[Fixed-resolution center-rank consequence]\label{rem:window6-center-2rank-excludes-simple-groups}
In the window-\(6\) dyadic uplift regime, the three fibers in the boundary sector each form a two-sheet cover.  The transposition involution on each such fiber yields a central element of the fixed-resolution fold-gauge group, and the three transpositions give an embedding
\[
(\ZZ_2)^3\hookrightarrow Z\!\left(\mathcal G_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\right)[2],
\qquad
\mathcal G_m^{\mathrm{bin}}:=\Aut(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}).
\]
Thus any external model that tries to retain all three audited boundary sheet flips as central charges inside a compact connected simple Lie-group envelope meets a center-rank obstruction: the center of a compact connected simple Lie group has elementary \(2\)-rank at most \(2\).  Indeed, type \(A_{n-1}\) has cyclic center, types \(B_n\) and \(C_n\) have center \(\ZZ_2\), type \(D_n\) has center \(\ZZ_4\) or \(\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_2\), and the exceptional types have trivial, \(\ZZ_2\), or \(\ZZ_3\) center.  This is only a fixed-resolution comparison with the audited Fold\(_6\) boundary-gauge sector; it is not used in the hypercube lumpability certificate.
\end{remark}
