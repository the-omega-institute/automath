Partition refinement and equitable hypercube partitions are classical topics
\cite{krebs2015universal,hyun2015harmonic}.  The content of the next theorem is
the exact Fold\(_6\)-specific identification of the terminal refinement, not
the general refinement procedure.

\begin{theorem}[Unique minimal hidden repair of the window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} fold]\label{thm:window6-minimal-equitable-refinement}
Let \(H:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow Y\) be a surjection whose fibers form an
equitable partition of \(Q_6\), and suppose that there is a map
\(\rho:Y\twoheadrightarrow X_6\) such that
\[
\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}=\rho\circ H.
\]
Then \(|Y|\ge48\).  This bound is attained by the orbit map
\[
H_{\mathrm{geo}}:\Omega_6\longrightarrow
\Omega_6/\langle\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\rangle,
\]
and, up to a relabeling of \(Y\), it is the unique equitable hidden refinement
with \(|Y|=48\).  Equivalently, the orbit partition of
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\) is the unique coarsest equitable partition refining
the Fold\(_6\) fiber partition.

More precisely, one neighbor-signature refinement of the Fold\(_6\) fibers is
already stable and has \(32\) singleton cells and \(16\) two-element cells.
The non-singleton cells are precisely the two-cycles of
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\).

If \(P_{\mathrm{geo}}\) denotes the transition kernel of this minimal quotient,
then
\[
\Spec(P_{\mathrm{geo}})=
\left\{1,\frac23,\frac13,0,-\frac13,-\frac23,-1\right\}
\]
with multiplicities
\[
(1,5,11,14,11,5,1).
\]
Thus exact Markovian repair requires \(27\) additional states beyond the
\(21\) visible Fold\(_6\) states.  The modes removed by the minimal quotient
are exactly the \(16\)-dimensional antisymmetric carrier \(V^-\), on which
\(A_6\) is isomorphic to \(A_4\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \(\omega\in\Omega_6\), define its Fold-neighbor signature by
\[
s(\omega):=\bigl(c_\omega(y)\bigr)_{y\in X_6},
\qquad
c_\omega(y)=\#\{1\le i\le6:
\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_i)=y\}.
\]
Refine each Fold\(_6\) fiber by equality of \(s(\omega)\), and denote the
resulting partition by \(\mathcal R\).  The complete fiber table in Proposition
\ref{prop:foldbin6-canonical-fiber-table} gives \(\mathcal R\) by an exact
finite calculation: flip each of the six coordinates of each displayed word,
locate the result in the same table, and compare the resulting
\(21\)-component integer vectors.  The equality classes are

In particular, \(\mathcal R\) has \(48\) cells.

\]
recomputes these equality classes directly from the greedy Zeckendorf

Lemma \ref{lem:window6-geometric-stabilizer-audit} gives
\(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}=
\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\), and \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\) is an automorphism of
\(Q_6\).  It therefore maps the six neighbors of \(\omega\) bijectively to
the six neighbors of \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\omega\), preserving their Fold
labels.  Hence \(s(\omega)=s(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\omega)\).  Comparison with
the displayed equality classes shows that \(\mathcal R\) is precisely the
orbit partition of \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\).  An automorphism-orbit partition
is equitable: if two vertices lie in the same orbit, the automorphism gives a
bijection between their neighbors in every target orbit.  Thus \(\mathcal R\)
is equitable and the one-step refinement is already stable.

Let \(\mathcal Q\) be any equitable partition refining the Fold\(_6\) fiber
partition.  If \(\omega,\omega'\) lie in one cell of \(\mathcal Q\), then they
have equal neighbor counts into every cell of \(\mathcal Q\).  Every
Fold\(_6\) fiber is a union of such cells, so summing these equalities over the
cells inside a Fold fiber gives \(s(\omega)=s(\omega')\).  Consequently
\(\mathcal Q\) refines \(\mathcal R\).  Since \(\mathcal R\) is itself
equitable, it is the unique coarsest equitable refinement.  The involution has
\(32\) fixed points, characterized by \(\omega_1\ne\omega_5\), and \(16\)
two-cycles, characterized by \(\omega_1=\omega_5\); hence it has \(48\)
orbits.  This proves the lower bound and the uniqueness assertion.  The factor
map \(\rho([\omega])=\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)\) is well defined by the
stabilizer identity, so the bound is attained.

It remains to identify the quotient spectrum.  Pullback from the orbit space
identifies its function space with the invariant space \(V^+\) of Theorem
\ref{thm:window6-affine-chiral-walsh-decomposition}.  For each
\(J\subseteq\{2,3,4,6\}\), that theorem gives invariant eigenvectors in Walsh
degrees \(|J|\), \(|J|+1\), and \(|J|+2\).  Since a degree-\(k\) Walsh
character has \(T_6\)-eigenvalue \(1-k/3\), the multiplicity sequence is the
coefficient sequence of
\[
(1+q)^4(1+q+q^2)
=1+5q+11q^2+14q^3+11q^4+5q^5+q^6.
\]
This proves the spectral formula.  Finally,
\(\CC^{\Omega_6}=V^+\oplus V^-\), and Theorem
\ref{thm:window6-chiral-compression-hypercube-adjacency} identifies
\(A_6|_{V^-}\) with \(A_4\).  Hence the discarded modes are exactly the
claimed chiral carrier. \qedhere
\end{proof}
