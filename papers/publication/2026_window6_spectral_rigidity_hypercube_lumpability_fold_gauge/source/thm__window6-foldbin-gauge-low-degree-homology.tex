\begin{lemma}[Low-degree homology of finite symmetric groups]\label{lem:symmetric-group-low-degree-homology-background}
For the finite symmetric group \(\mathfrak S_d\), with the convention that \(\mathfrak S_0\) and \(\mathfrak S_1\) are trivial, one has
\[
H_1(\mathfrak S_d;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
0,&d\le 1,\\
\ZZ_2,&d\ge 2,
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
H_2(\mathfrak S_d;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
0,&d\le 3,\\
\ZZ_6,&d=6,\\
\ZZ_2,&d\ge 4,\ d\ne 6.
\end{cases}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first formula is abelianization.  For \(d\le1\) the group is trivial, while for \(d\ge2\) the sign character \(\sgn:\mathfrak S_d\to\{\pm1\}\cong\ZZ_2\) is onto and its kernel contains the commutator subgroup; the standard presentation of \(\mathfrak S_d\) by adjacent transpositions shows that all adjacent transpositions have the same image in the abelianization and that this common image has order two, so the sign map is the full abelianization.

For the second formula, this is the classical Schur-multiplier calculation for symmetric groups, with the small cases and the exceptional case retained explicitly: \(H_2(\mathfrak S_d;\ZZ)\cong M(\mathfrak S_d)\) is trivial for \(d\le3\), is \(\ZZ_2\) for \(d\ge4\) except at \(d=6\), and is \(\ZZ_6\) for \(d=6\).  We use this standard result in the form recorded in Brown's treatment of group cohomology and Nakaoka's stable symmetric-group homology calculation \cite{brown1982cohomology,nakaoka1961homology}. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Low-degree fold-gauge homology with the \texorpdfstring{$\mathfrak S_6$}{S6} Schur-multiplier exception]\label{thm:window6-foldbin-gauge-low-degree-homology}
For each resolution \(m\ge 1\), let
\(
\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m:=\Aut(\Fold^{\mathrm{bin}}_m)
\)
be the discrete fold-gauge group induced by binary interval folding, and write
\(
d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w):=\card{(\Fold^{\mathrm{bin}}_m)^{-1}(w)}
\)
for the fiber multiplicity at \(w\in X_m\).
Define the threshold counts
\[
N_2:=\#\{w\in X_m:\ d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)\ge 2\},
\qquad
N_4:=\#\{w\in X_m:\ d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)\ge 4\}.
\]
Also put
\[
N_6^{=}:=\#\{w\in X_m:\ d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)=6\}.
\]
Then there are natural isomorphisms
\[
H_1(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ)\ \cong\ (\ZZ_2)^{N_2},
\qquad
H_2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ)\ \cong\ (\ZZ_6)^{N_6^{=}}\oplus(\ZZ_2)^{\,N_4-N_6^{=}+\binom{N_2}{2}},
\]
\[
H^2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ_2)\ \cong\ (\ZZ_2)^{\,N_4+\binom{N_2+1}{2}}.
\]
Moreover, for this fixed resolution, if
\[
C(t):=\sum_{w\in X_m}\min\{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w),t\}\qquad(t\ge 0)
\]
is the capacity curve, then its jumps at integer points satisfy
\[
N_2=C(2)-C(1),
\qquad
N_4=C(4)-C(3),
\]
while the integral group \(H_2\) additionally requires the exact count \(N_6^{=}\) of size-six fibers.  Thus \(H_1\), the \(\ZZ_2\)-cohomology group above, and the \(\ZZ_2\)-rank of \(H_2\) are recovered from \(C(1),\dots,C(4)\), whereas the integral torsion type of \(H_2\) is recovered from those values together with \(N_6^{=}\).  Across different values of \(m\), these capacity values and exact size counts are certificate-family statistics; they become stable invariants only after choosing fold-aware descent data as in Theorem \ref{thm:fold-aware-stable-descent}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The fiber decomposition gives
\(
\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m\cong \prod_{w\in X_m}\mathfrak{S}_{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)}
\).
For \(H_1\), we use \(H_1(G;\ZZ)\cong G^{\mathrm{ab}}\) together with compatibility of abelianization with finite products:
\[
H_1(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ)
\cong
\bigoplus_{w\in X_m}\mathfrak{S}_{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)}^{\mathrm{ab}}.
\]
Since \(\mathfrak{S}_d^{\mathrm{ab}}\cong\ZZ_2\) for \(d\ge 2\) and is trivial for \(d\le 1\), we obtain
\(
H_1(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ)\cong (\ZZ_2)^{N_2}
\).
\par
For \(H_2\), the K\"unneth decomposition for group homology over \(\ZZ\) yields
\[
H_2\!\Bigl(\prod_{w}G_w;\ZZ\Bigr)
\cong
\Bigl(\bigoplus_{w}H_2(G_w;\ZZ)\Bigr)\ \oplus\
\Bigl(\bigoplus_{w<w'}H_1(G_w;\ZZ)\otimes H_1(G_{w'};\ZZ)\Bigr),
\]
where \(G_w=\mathfrak{S}_{d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)}\).
By Lemma \ref{lem:symmetric-group-low-degree-homology-background},
\(
H_2(\mathfrak{S}_d;\ZZ)=0
\)
for \(d\le 3\),
\(
H_2(\mathfrak{S}_d;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ_2
\)
for \(d\ge 4\) with \(d\ne6\), and
\(
H_2(\mathfrak{S}_6;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ_6
\).
The same lemma gives
\(
H_1(\mathfrak{S}_d;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ_2
\)
if and only if \(d\ge 2\).
Hence the first direct-sum term contributes one copy of \(\ZZ_6\) for each size-six fiber and one copy of \(\ZZ_2\) for each other fiber of size at least four.  The tensor term contributes one copy of \(\ZZ_2\) for each unordered pair of fibers whose multiplicities satisfy \(d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(\cdot)\ge 2\), namely \(\binom{N_2}{2}\) copies.
Therefore
\(
H_2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ)\cong (\ZZ_6)^{N_6^{=}}\oplus(\ZZ_2)^{N_4-N_6^{=}+\binom{N_2}{2}}
\).
\par
Finally, the universal coefficient theorem gives
\[
H^2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ_2)
\cong
\mathrm{Hom}\!\bigl(H_2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ),\ZZ_2\bigr)\ \oplus\
\mathrm{Ext}\!\bigl(H_1(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ),\ZZ_2\bigr).
\]
Each cyclic summand \(\ZZ_2\) or \(\ZZ_6\) in \(H_2\) contributes one copy of \(\ZZ_2\) to \(\mathrm{Hom}(-,\ZZ_2)\), and each \(\ZZ_2\) summand of \(H_1\) contributes one copy of \(\ZZ_2\) to \(\mathrm{Ext}(-,\ZZ_2)\).
Hence
\[
H^2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ_2)
\cong
(\ZZ_2)^{\,N_4+\binom{N_2}{2}+N_2}
=
(\ZZ_2)^{\,N_4+\binom{N_2}{2}+N_2}
=
(\ZZ_2)^{\,N_4+\binom{N_2+1}{2}}.
\]
\par
For the capacity curve, the identity
\(
\min(d,k)-\min(d,k-1)=\mathbf 1_{\{d\ge k\}}
\)
holds for every integer \(k\ge 1\) and every \(d\in\NN\).
Summing over \(w\in X_m\) gives
\[
C(k)-C(k-1)=\#\{w\in X_m:\ d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)\ge k\}.
\]
Taking \(k=2\) and \(k=4\) yields the formulas for \(N_2\) and \(N_4\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Three natural families of generators in \texorpdfstring{$H^2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ_2)$}{H^2}]\label{rem:window6-foldbin-gauge-h2-generators}
Under the above decomposition, the second cohomology splits naturally as
\[
H^2(\mathcal{G}^{\mathrm{bin}}_m;\ZZ_2)
\cong
(\ZZ_2)^{N_4}\ \oplus\ (\ZZ_2)^{\binom{N_2}{2}}\ \oplus\ (\ZZ_2)^{N_2}.
\]
These three summands correspond respectively to single-fiber Schur-multiplier classes coming from symmetric-group factors with \(d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)\ge 4\), including the mod-\(2\) reduction of the exceptional \(\ZZ_6\) multiplier when \(d^{\mathrm{bin}}_m(w)=6\), cross-fiber pairing classes coming from the K\"unneth term \(H_1\otimes H_1\), and Bockstein-type classes arising from
\(
\mathrm{Ext}(H_1,\ZZ_2)
\).
\end{remark}
