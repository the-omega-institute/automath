\begin{proposition}[Explicit window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} nonintertwining witness]\label{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness}
For the audited fold \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}:\Omega_6\to X_6\), the two vertices
\[
\omega=000000,
\qquad
\omega'=010101
\]
belong to the same fold fiber, namely
\[
\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)=\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega')=000000.
\]
However their target-fiber neighbor counts differ.  For the target folded state \(y=000100\), one has
\[
c_\omega(y)=\#\{1\le i\le6:\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_i)=000100\}=0,
\]
whereas
\[
c_{\omega'}(y)=\#\{1\le i\le6:\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega'+e_i)=000100\}=1.
\]
Consequently the block-indicator matrix \(M\) of \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) admits no intertwining \(T_6M=MP\).  Moreover, for the audited pushforward residual \(R_6:=T_6M-MP_6\), at least one of the two entries \(|R_6(000000,000100)|\) and \(|R_6(010101,000100)|\) is at least \(1/12\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first vertex has integer code \(N(000000)=0\), hence all initial Zeckendorf digits are zero and \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(000000)=000000\).  The second has
\[
N(010101)=16+4+1=21=F_8.
\]
Thus its greedy Zeckendorf expansion has its first nonzero digit in position \(7\,\), and its length-\(6\) prefix is again \(000000\).  Hence the two vertices lie in the same fiber over \(x=000000\).

It remains to compare their one-step folded neighbor counts.  Flipping the six coordinates of \(000000\) gives folded targets
\[
001010,
010000,
101000,
000010,
100000,
001001,
\]
in coordinate order.  None is \(000100\).  Therefore \(c_{000000}(000100)=0\).  Flipping the six coordinates of \(010101\) gives folded targets
\[
000100,
100101,
010101,
010000,
101001,
000010,
\]
in coordinate order.  Exactly the first of these is \(000100\), so \(c_{010101}(000100)=1\).

Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds}, applied with \(m=6\), now rules out any matrix \(P\) satisfying \(T_6M=MP\).  The same theorem gives the residual lower bound with \(\delta=1\): at least one of the two audited residual entries has absolute value at least \(1/(2\cdot6)=1/12\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Single exact certificate for the audited window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} bin-fold]\label{thm:single-exact-fold6-certificate}
The audited window-\(6\) certificate consists of the following finite algebraic-combinatorial data.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item The state set is
\[
X_6=\{x\in\{0,1\}^6:x_i x_{i+1}=0\text{ for }1\le i<6\},
\qquad |X_6|=21,
\]
ordered lexicographically, and the fold is the Zeckendorf-prefix map
\(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow X_6\) from Definition \ref{def:audited-binary-fold-certificate}.
\item The fiber multiplicities in this order are
\[
(4,2,4,4,2,4,2,3,4,2,3,4,2,4,2,3,4,2,4,2,3).
\]
\item The exact edge-count matrix is the integer matrix \(N\) displayed in Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix}, and the audited pushforward kernel is
\[
P_6(i,j)=\frac{N_{ij}}{6d_i}.
\]
\item The same-fiber residual witness is
\[
\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(000000)=\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}(010101)=000000,
\qquad
c_{000000}(000100)=0,
\qquad
c_{010101}(000100)=1.
\]
Hence no matrix \(P\) satisfies \(T_6M=MP\), and the audited residual \(R_6=T_6M-MP_6\) has one of the two corresponding entries of absolute value at least \(1/12\).
\item The nontrivial characteristic factor is the degree-\(20\) rational polynomial \(q_6(t)=\det(tI-P_6)/(t-1)\) of Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.  With
\[
L_6=3234734993627557134336,
\]
the polynomial \(Q_6=L_6q_6\) has the exact integer coefficient stream recorded in Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}\textup{(iv)}.
\item The Sturm signs recorded in Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}\textup{(v)} isolate one root in each interval
\[
(0.4841207858,0.4841207859),
\qquad
(-0.6030939755,-0.6030939754).
\]
Both intervals are disjoint from the window-\(6\) hypercube spectrum \(\{1,2/3,1/3,0,-1/3,-2/3,-1\}\).
\end{enumerate}
Consequently the audited window-\(6\) bin-fold pushforward is not a hidden-state-free strongly lumpable quotient of the simple random walk on \(Q_6\), and the conclusion is certified both by an entrywise residual obstruction and by an off-grid spectral obstruction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Item \textup{(i)} is exactly the specialization to \(m=6\) of Definition \ref{def:audited-binary-fold-certificate}; the cardinality \(|X_6|=21\) is the standard Fibonacci count for no-adjacent-ones words, also enumerated in Lemma \ref{lem:audited-binary-fold-certificate-identities}.  Item \textup{(ii)} is the window-\(6\) multiplicity vector in Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix}.  Item \textup{(iii)} is precisely the matrix formula and edge-count interpretation of that proposition.  Item \textup{(iv)} is Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness}, together with Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds}.  Item \textup{(v)} is Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate} and the integer coefficient stream of Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}\textup{(iv)}.  Item \textup{(vi)} is Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate}, whose proof uses the Sturm data from Proposition \ref{prop:audited-computation-reproducibility-certificate}\textup{(v)}.

The final assertion follows in two independent ways.  The residual witness in \textup{(iv)} violates the row-wise target-fiber count condition of Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds}, so no intertwining \(T_6M=MP\) exists.  Separately, Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity} says that any equitable quotient kernel of \(Q_6\) has spectrum contained in \(\{1-2k/6:0\le k\le6\}\), while \textup{(vi)} gives two eigenvalues of \(P_6\) outside that set. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact interval certificate for the audited window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} eigenvalues]\label{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate}
For the audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\), there are spectral values \(\lambda_+,\lambda_-\in\Spec(P_6)\) satisfying
\[
0.4841207858<\lambda_+<0.4841207859,
\qquad
-0.6030939755<\lambda_-<-0.6030939754.
\]
In particular, \(\lambda_+\), \(\lambda_-\), and \(|\lambda_-|\) are not elements of
\[
\left\{\,1,\ \frac23,\ \frac13,\ 0,\ -\frac13,\ -\frac23,\ -1\,\right\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The existence and the displayed intervals are exactly Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.  The positive interval lies strictly between \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), while the negative interval lies strictly between \(-2/3\) and \(-1/3\).  Neither interval contains an endpoint of the window-\(6\) hypercube grid.  Hence the certified eigenvalues are off the window-\(6\) grid, and so is the absolute value \(|\lambda_-|\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Window-\(6\) is spectrally nonlumpable]\label{cor:terminal-window6-nonlumpable-by-spectrum}
The audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\) associated with \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) is not the transition kernel of an equitable quotient chain on \(Q_6\).  Equivalently, the window-\(6\) certificate is not hidden-state-free strongly lumpable.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity}, every equitable quotient of \(Q_6\) has spectrum contained in
\(
\{1-2k/6:0\le k\le 6\}
\).
Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate} supplies two spectral values of the exact audited pushforward matrix \(P_6\) outside this set.  Therefore \(P_6\) cannot be the transition matrix of an equitable quotient of the hypercube walk. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Boundary of the obstruction: no hidden-refinement exclusion]\label{thm:hidden-refinement-boundary}
The window-\(6\) nonlumpability conclusions in Theorem \ref{thm:single-exact-fold6-certificate} and Corollary \ref{cor:terminal-window6-nonlumpable-by-spectrum} are exactly statements about the audited one-step quotient on the visible state set \(X_6\).  They rule out the existence of a kernel \(P\) on \(X_6\) satisfying
\[
T_6M=MP
\]
for the block-indicator matrix \(M\) of \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\), and they rule out realizing the audited matrix \(P_6\) itself as an equitable quotient kernel of \(Q_6\) on those same visible blocks.  They do not rule out the existence of a different finite state space \(Y\), a surjection \(H:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow Y\), and a factor map \(\rho:Y\twoheadrightarrow X_6\) with \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}=\rho\circ H\), for which the partition defined by \(H\) is equitable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds} applies to a specified surjection \(F:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X\) and the corresponding block-indicator matrix.  In the audited window-\(6\) application, that specified surjection is \(F=\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) and the block-indicator matrix is its visible-fiber matrix \(M\).  Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness} gives two vertices in one visible fiber and one visible target state whose one-step target-fiber counts differ.  Therefore the theorem proves that no kernel on the same visible state set \(X_6\) can satisfy \(T_6M=MP\).

Likewise, the spectral obstruction in Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity} is applied to the audited pushforward matrix \(P_6=D^{-1}M^\top T_6M\) associated with this same visible matrix \(M\).  Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate} shows that \(P_6\) has off-grid eigenvalues, so \(P_6\) cannot be an equitable quotient kernel on those visible blocks.

A hidden refinement replaces \(M\) by another block-indicator matrix \(M_H\) for a different surjection \(H:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow Y\).  The residual counts, the audited pushforward matrix, and the quotient spectrum tested by the above theorems are then those of \(H\), not those of \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\).  Since the hypotheses and computed certificates in this paper contain no data for all such refinements \(H\), the logical conclusion is exactly the stated visible one-step obstruction and not a universal no-hidden-refinement theorem. \qedhere
\end{proof}
