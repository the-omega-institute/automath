\begin{theorem}[Fold\texorpdfstring{$_m^{\mathrm{bin}}$}{m bin} finite certificate package]\label{thm:foldbin-family-certificate-algorithm}
For every \(m\ge1\), the binary Zeckendorf-prefix fold \(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X_m\) has a finite algebraic-combinatorial certificate deciding hidden-state-free strong lumpability of its audited pushforward.  More precisely, let
\[
d_x=\bigl| (\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}})^{-1}(x)\bigr|,
\qquad
N_{xy}=\#\{(\omega,i):\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)=x,
\ \Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_i)=y\},
\]
and set \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(x,y)=N_{xy}/(m d_x)\).  For \(\omega\in\Omega_m\) and \(y\in X_m\), put
\[
c_\omega(y)=\#\{1\le i\le m:\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega+e_i)=y\}.
\]
Then the following effective dichotomy holds.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item If, for every pair \(x,y\in X_m\), the list \(\bigl(c_\omega(y)\bigr)_{\omega\in(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}})^{-1}(x)}\) is constant, then \(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) is an equitable quotient of \(Q_m\), its quotient kernel is exactly \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\), and
\[
\Spec(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}})\subseteq\{1-2k/m:0\le k\le m\}.
\]
\item If one such list is not constant, then any two unequal entries give a two-vertex residual certificate: no matrix \(P\) satisfies \(T_mM_m=M_mP\), where \(M_m\) is the block-indicator matrix of \(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\).  If the two entries differ by \(\delta\), then the audited residual \(R_m=T_mM_m-M_mP_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) has one of the two corresponding entries of absolute value at least \(\delta/(2m)\).
\item Independently, let \(C_m(t)\in\ZZ[t]\) be any nonzero integer multiple of \(\det(tI-P_m^{\mathrm{bin}})\).  If a Sturm sequence for \(C_m\) has a variation drop on a rational interval \((a,b)\) disjoint from \(\{1-2k/m:0\le k\le m\}\), with \(C_m(a)C_m(b)\ne0\), then \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) has an off-grid eigenvalue and therefore is not an equitable quotient kernel of \(Q_m\).
\end{enumerate}
All entries in the certificate are obtained by finite integer enumeration of the block-indicator encoding
\[
\mathcal F_m=\bigl(x:(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}})^{-1}(x)\bigr)_{x\in X_m},
\]
so the window-\(6\) table in Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-canonical-fiber-table}, the matrix of Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix}, and the exact spectral certificate of Theorem \ref{thm:single-exact-fold6-certificate} are the \(m=6\) instance of this family package.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For fixed \(m\), the set \(\Omega_m\) is finite and Definition \ref{def:audited-binary-fold-certificate} gives an explicit value of \(\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}(\omega)\) for each \(\omega\).  Thus the fiber table \(\mathcal F_m\), the cardinalities \(d_x\), the neighbor-count lists \(c_\omega(y)\), and the edge counts \(N_{xy}\) are finite integer sums.  The formula for \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) is exactly the audited pushforward formula \(D^{-1}M_m^\top T_mM_m\) from Section \ref{sec:preliminaries}.

The first two alternatives are Theorem \ref{thm:finite-certificate-dichotomy-hypercube-folds} applied to the particular surjection \(F=\Fold_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\).  In the constant-list case, Lemma \ref{lem:equitable-strong-lumpability-audited-pushforward} identifies the unique strong-lumpability kernel with the audited pushforward, and Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity} gives the hypercube spectral inclusion.  In the nonconstant-list case, Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds} gives the nonintertwining conclusion and the residual bound \(\delta/(2m)\).

For the spectral certificate, multiplying \(\det(tI-P_m^{\mathrm{bin}})\) by a nonzero integer does not change its real zeros.  Since the entries of \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) are rational, such a clearing denominator exists.  Sturm's theorem applied to \(C_m\) counts the zeros in \((a,b)\) when neither endpoint is a zero; a positive variation drop therefore supplies an eigenvalue of \(P_m^{\mathrm{bin}}\) in that interval.  If the interval is disjoint from the hypercube grid, the spectral inclusion in the equitable case is impossible, so the audited pushforward is not an equitable quotient kernel.  The final sentence is obtained by substituting the printed window-\(6\) fiber table, edge-count matrix, and characteristic-polynomial/Sturm data into the same construction. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Explicit window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} nonintertwining witness]\label{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness}
For the audited fold \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}:\Omega_6\to X_6\), the two vertices

\qquad
\omega'=010101

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}

\[
001010,
010000,
101000,
000010,
100000,
001001,
\]
in coordinate order.  None is \(000100\).  Therefore \(c_{000000}(000100)=0\).  Flipping the six coordinates of \(010101\) gives folded targets
\[
000100,
100101,
010101,
010000,
101001,
000010,
\]
in coordinate order.  Exactly the first of these is \(000100\), so \(c_{010101}(000100)=1\).

Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds}, applied with \(m=6\), now rules out any matrix \(P\) satisfying \(T_6M=MP\).  The same theorem gives the residual lower bound with \(\delta=1\): at least one of the two audited residual entries has absolute value at least \(1/(2\cdot6)=1/12\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Boundary of the obstruction: no hidden-refinement exclusion]\label{thm:hidden-refinement-boundary}
The window-\(6\) nonlumpability conclusions in Theorem \ref{thm:single-exact-fold6-certificate} and Corollary \ref{cor:terminal-window6-nonlumpable-by-spectrum} are exactly statements about the audited one-step quotient on the visible state set \(X_6\). \[

\begin{proof}
Theorem \ref{thm:local-residual-certificate-hypercube-folds} applies to a specified surjection \(F:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X\) and the corresponding block-indicator matrix.  In the audited window-\(6\) application, that specified surjection is \(F=\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) and the block-indicator matrix is its visible-fiber matrix \(M\).  Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-explicit-nonintertwining-witness} gives two vertices in one visible fiber and one visible target state whose one-step target-fiber counts differ.  Therefore the theorem proves that no kernel on the same visible state set \(X_6\) can satisfy \(T_6M=MP\).

Likewise, the spectral obstruction in Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity} is applied to the audited pushforward matrix \(P_6=D^{-1}M^\top T_6M\) associated with this same visible matrix \(M\).  Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate} shows that \(P_6\) has off-grid eigenvalues, so \(P_6\) cannot be an equitable quotient kernel on those visible blocks.

A hidden refinement replaces \(M\) by another block-indicator matrix \(M_H\) for a different surjection \(H:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow Y\).  The residual counts, the audited pushforward matrix, and the quotient spectrum tested by the above theorems are then those of \(H\), not those of \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\).  Since the hypotheses and computed certificates in this paper contain no data for all such refinements \(H\), the logical conclusion is exactly the stated visible one-step obstruction and not a universal no-hidden-refinement theorem. \qedhere
\end{proof}
