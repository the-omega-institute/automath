\begin{proof}
By Definition \ref{def:audited-binary-fold-certificate}, the first sixteen fibers of \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) are the sixteen two-point orbits of \(\sigma_{\mathrm{geo}}\), the next four fibers are singletons, and the last fiber contains the remaining twenty-eight vertices.  Hence the displayed vector \((d_i)\) is exactly the list of fiber cardinalities.  The formula for \(P_6(i,j)\) is the averaging convention for the non-equitable audited pushforward kernel introduced in Section \ref{sec:preliminaries}.  Directly enumerating the six bit flips from every vertex in the fiber over \(x_i\) gives the displayed table.  Each row sums to \(6d_i\), so the resulting matrix is row stochastic and is exactly \(D^{-1}M^\top T_6M\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Machine-checkable characteristic certificate]\label{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}
For the exact matrix of Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix}, the characteristic polynomial factors over \(\QQ\) as
\[
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{P_6}(t)=&\ t^2(t-1)(3t-1)^2(3t+1)
(324t^4-54t^2+1)^2 \\
&\cdot(189t^3-117t^2+9t+1)
(324t^4+432t^3+162t^2+12t-1)
/173564379072 .
\end{aligned}
\]
In particular, \(P_6\) has real spectral values \(\lambda_1\in(19/50,39/100)\) and \(\lambda_2\in(1/2,51/100)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The characteristic polynomial is obtained from the explicit rational matrix of Proposition \ref{prop:foldbin6-exact-pushforward-matrix} by fraction-free Gaussian elimination over \(\QQ[t]\); every entry of the input matrix is one of the rational numbers displayed in that proposition, so this is a finite exact certificate.  Multiplying the displayed factorization by the nonzero rational constant \(173564379072\) and expanding gives the monic determinant \(\det(tI-P_6)\), which is a purely rational identity.

It remains to isolate the two positive off-grid roots used below.  Put
\[
q(t):=324t^4-54t^2+1,
\qquad
r(t):=189t^3-117t^2+9t+1.
\]
For \(q\), a Sturm sequence is
\[
q,
\quad 1296t^3-108t,
\quad 27t^2-1,
\quad t,
\quad 1,
\]
after multiplying individual members by positive rational constants.  The signs of this sequence at \(19/50\) are \((- ,+,+,+,+)\), while the signs at \(39/100\) are \((+,+,+,+,+)\).  Thus the variation count drops by one, so the interval contains exactly one root.  Explicitly \(q(19/50)=-65249/1562500<0\) and \(q(39/100)=7053721/25000000>0\).  Hence \(q\) has a root \(\lambda_1\in(19/50,39/100)\), and the factorization shows \(\lambda_1\in\Spec(P_6)\).

For \(r\), a Sturm sequence is
\[
r,
\quad 567t^2-234t+9,
\quad 212t-34,
\quad 1,
\]
again up to positive rational multiples.  The signs at \(1/2\) are \((-,+,+,+)\), while the signs at \(51/100\) are \((+,+,+,+)\), so the variation count again drops by one.  Explicitly \(r(1/2)=-1/8<0\) and \(r(51/100)=229339/1000000>0\).  Hence \(r\) has a root \(\lambda_2\in(1/2,51/100)\), and again the factorization places \(\lambda_2\) in \(\Spec(P_6)\). \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact interval certificate for the audited window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} eigenvalues]\label{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate}
For the audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\), there are spectral values \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\Spec(P_6)\) satisfying
\[
\frac{19}{50}<\lambda_1<\frac{39}{100},
\qquad
\frac12<\lambda_2<\frac{51}{100}.
\]
In particular, \(\lambda_1\) and \(\lambda_2\) are not elements of
\[
\left\{\,1,\ \frac23,\ \frac13,\ 0,\ -\frac13,\ -\frac23,\ -1\,\right\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The existence and the displayed intervals are exactly Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-characteristic-certificate}.  Both intervals lie strictly between \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), and neither contains either endpoint.  The only hypercube grid points in the closed interval \([1/3,2/3]\) are \(1/3\) and \(2/3\).  Thus the two certified values are off the window-\(6\) hypercube grid. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Window-\(6\) is spectrally nonlumpable]\label{cor:terminal-window6-nonlumpable-by-spectrum}
The audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\) associated with \(\Fold_6^{\mathrm{bin}}\) is not the transition kernel of an equitable quotient chain on \(Q_6\).  Equivalently, the window-\(6\) certificate is not hidden-state-free strongly lumpable.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity}, every equitable quotient of \(Q_6\) has spectrum contained in
\(
\{1-2k/6:0\le k\le 6\}
\).
Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate} supplies two spectral values of the exact audited pushforward matrix \(P_6\) outside this set.  Therefore \(P_6\) cannot be the transition matrix of an equitable quotient of the hypercube walk. \qedhere
\end{proof}
