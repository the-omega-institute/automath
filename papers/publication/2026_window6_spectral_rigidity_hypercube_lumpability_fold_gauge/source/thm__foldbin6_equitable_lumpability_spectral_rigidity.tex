\begin{lemma}[Exact interval certificate for the audited window-\texorpdfstring{$6$}{6} eigenvalues]\label{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate}
For the audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\), the finite audit certificate consists of two real spectral values \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2\in\Spec(P_6)\) satisfying the rational enclosures
\[
\frac{2420603929}{5000000000}<\lambda_1<\frac{4841207859}{10000000000},
\qquad
\frac{3015469877}{5000000000}<\lambda_2<\frac{1206187951}{2000000000}.
\]
In particular, \(\lambda_1\) and \(\lambda_2\) are not elements of
\[
\left\{\,1,\ \frac23,\ \frac13,\ 0,\ -\frac13,\ -\frac23,\ -1\,\right\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The displayed rational intervals are part of the finite window-\(6\) audit certificate.  It remains only to check that these certified intervals are disjoint from the hypercube grid.  The first interval satisfies
\[
\frac13<\frac{2420603929}{5000000000}<\lambda_1<\frac{4841207859}{10000000000}<\frac23,
\]
because \(3\cdot2420603929=7261811787>5000000000\) and \(3\cdot4841207859=14523623577<20000000000\).  The second satisfies
\[
\frac13<\frac{3015469877}{5000000000}<\lambda_2<\frac{1206187951}{2000000000}<\frac23,
\]
because \(3\cdot3015469877=9046409631>5000000000\) and \(3\cdot1206187951=3618563853<4000000000\).  Thus both certified values lie strictly between \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), while the only grid points in that closed interval are its endpoints. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Window-\(6\) is spectrally nonlumpable]\label{cor:terminal-window6-nonlumpable-by-spectrum}
Assume that the audited window-\(6\) pushforward kernel \(P_6\) has the certified eigenvalues of Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate}.
If \(m=6\), the only spectral values compatible with an equitable quotient are
\[
\left\{\,1,\ \frac23,\ \frac13,\ 0,\ -\frac13,\ -\frac23,\ -1\,\right\}.
\]
Hence \(\mathrm{spec}(P_6)\) contains eigenvalues outside the allowed set, and the stable-type coarse-graining of window \(6\) cannot arise from a one-step equitable quotient chain on \(Q_6\).
Equivalently, strong lumpability is ruled out directly by the spectral certificate.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem \ref{thm:window6-strong-lumpability-spectral-rigidity}, every equitable quotient of \(Q_6\) has spectrum contained in
\(
\{1-2k/6:0\le k\le 6\}
\).
Lemma \ref{lem:foldbin6-eigenvalue-interval-certificate} supplies two certified spectral values of \(P_6\) outside this set, so \(P_6\) cannot come from an equitable quotient of \(Q_6\). \qedhere
\end{proof}
