\begin{abstract}
We study a concrete partition of the six-dimensional hypercube obtained from a finite
Zeckendorf-prefix fold.  The resulting 21-cell partition is not equitable: two vertices
in one visible cell have different neighbor counts into another cell.  We determine the
exact repair.  The unique coarsest equitable refinement is the orbit partition of an
affine involution, with 48 cells consisting of 32 singletons and 16 pairs.  Consequently
every equitable hidden realization through which the folded partition factors has at
least 48 states, and equality is unique up to relabeling.  We also identify the quotient
spectrum, with multiplicities $(1,5,11,14,11,5,1)$, and show that the discarded
16-dimensional sector carries the adjacency operator of $Q_4$.  All finite data needed
to recompute the claims are supplied in the accompanying supplement.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
Equitable partitions are the exact combinatorial form of lumpability for a random walk:
each vertex in a cell must have the same number of neighbors in every target cell.
They are central in algebraic graph theory, color refinement, and finite Markov-chain
reduction \cite{biggs1993algebraic,kemeny1976finite,buchholz1994exact,krebs2015universal}.
The hypercube is a particularly transparent test graph because its Walsh characters give
the complete spectrum and because every local neighbor count is integral.

This paper gives a complete analysis of one audited six-dimensional example.  Let
\(\Omega_6=\{0,1\}^6\), and map a binary word to the first six digits of the greedy
Zeckendorf expansion of its binary integer.  The image consists of the 21 words with no
adjacent ones.  The visible partition is not equitable, but its failure has an exact
repair: one refinement by equal neighbor signatures is already stable.  The main result
is the following theorem.

\paragraph{Main theorem (Theorem 4.23).}
If \(H:\Omega_6\twoheadrightarrow Y\) has equitable fibers and
\(\Fold_6=\rho\circ H\) for a surjection \(\rho:Y\twoheadrightarrow X_6\), then
\(\card{Y}\ge48\).  Equality is attained uniquely up to relabeling by the orbit map
of \(\sigma_{\rm geo}(a_1,\ldots,a_6)=(1-a_5,a_2,a_3,a_4,1-a_1,a_6)\).
The quotient spectrum has multiplicities \((1,5,11,14,11,5,1)\), and its
16-dimensional orthogonal complement carries the adjacency operator of \(Q_4\).

The proof separates two questions.  Section~\ref{sec:fold6} fixes the finite partition
and records its fibers.  Section~\ref{sec:residual} gives the elementary interval
criterion for approximate intertwinings and the sharp Fold$_6$ residual.  Theorem
\ref{thm:headline} is proved in Section~\ref{sec:repair} by printing all 48
neighbor-signature cells.  Section~\ref{sec:spectrum} then derives the quotient
spectrum and the discarded \(Q_4\) carrier.  The final section describes the independent
finite checks in the supplement.

\section{The Fold$_6$ partition}\label{sec:fold6}
Let \(Q_m\) be the hypercube on \(\Omega_m=\{0,1\}^m\), with adjacency matrix
\(A_m\) and walk matrix \(T_m=A_m/m\).  A surjection \(F:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X\)
has block-indicator matrix \(M\), whose column indexed by \(x\) is the indicator of
\(F^{-1}(x)\).  The partition is equitable exactly when \(A_mM=MB\) for some \(B\),
or equivalently when every vertex in a source fiber has the same number of neighbors
in each target fiber.

\begin{definition}[Audited Fold$_6$]\label{def:fold6}
Set
\[
X_6=\{x\in\{0,1\}^6:x_ix_{i+1}=0\ (1\le i<6)\}.
\]
For \(\omega=(a_1,\ldots,a_6)\), put
\(N(\omega)=\sum_{i=1}^6a_i2^{6-i}\).  If
\(N=\sum_{r\ge1}z_r(N)F_{r+1}\) is its greedy Zeckendorf expansion, define
\(\Fold_6(\omega)=(z_1(N),\ldots,z_6(N))\).  The fibers, in lexicographic order of
\(X_6\), are the following.
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}

000001&001101,101111\\
000010&001000,011101,101010,111111\\

001001&010000,110010\\
001010&001011,100000,101101\\
010000&000010,010111,100100,111001\\
010001&001111,110001\\
010010&001010,011111,101100\\
010100&000111,011100,101001,111110\\
010101&010100,110110\\
100000&000001,010110,100011,111000\\
100001&001110,110000\\

\]
\end{definition}

The fiber-size vector is
\[
(4,2,4,4,2,4,2,3,4,2,3,4,2,4,2,3,4,2,4,2,3).
\]
The count can be obtained without enumerating binary words one by one.  If
\(V(x)=\sum_{r=1}^6x_rF_{r+1}\), let \(\mathcal T_7^{(0)}=\mathcal T_7^{(1)}=\{0\}\)
and, working backwards through the remaining Zeckendorf positions, set
\[
\mathcal T_r^{(1)}=\mathcal T_{r+1}^{(0)},\qquad
\mathcal T_r^{(0)}=\mathcal T_{r+1}^{(0)}\cup
 (F_{r+1}+\mathcal T_{r+1}^{(1)}).
\]
Then
\[
\Fold_6^{-1}(x)=\{\omega:N(\omega)-V(x)\in\mathcal T_7^{(x_6)}\},
\]
intersected with \([0,63]\).  This tail recursion is useful because the same finite sets
also compute every neighbor count used in Section~\ref{sec:residual}.

\begin{proposition}[Finite enumeration of the Fold fibers]\label{prop:fiber-enumeration}
The map in Definition~\ref{def:fold6} is onto \(X_6\), and the displayed 21 rows
partition all 64 vertices.  In particular \(|X_6|=F_8=21\), and the fiber-size vector
is the one displayed above.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The recurrence for admissible words gives \(|X_m|=F_{m+2}\).  Every legal word \(x\)
has value \(V(x)\le F_8-1=20\), so it occurs as the first six digits of the greedy
expansion of some integer in \([0,63]\).  The tail recursion then gives the listed
preimages.  Their rows are disjoint and their sizes sum to 64, proving the partition
claim.
\end{proof}

The involution in Theorem~\ref{thm:headline} preserves every row of this table.
Indeed, direct substitution into the 64-word definition gives
\(\Fold_6\circ\sigma_{\rm geo}=\Fold_6\).  This identity will also explain the
16-dimensional discarded sector.

\section{Visible nonlumpability and the sharp residual}\label{sec:residual}
For \(\omega\in\Omega_m\) and \(y\in X\), write
\[
c_\omega(y)=\#\{1\le i\le m:F(\omega+e_i)=y\}.
\]
The following elementary lemma is the residual calculation used below.

\begin{lemma}[Neighbor-count intervals and residual radius]\label{lem:residual}
For a finite fold \(F:\Omega_m\twoheadrightarrow X\), a matrix \(P\) satisfies
\(\|T_mM-MP\|_\infty\le\varepsilon\) if and only if, for every source fiber
\(F^{-1}(x)\) and target \(y\), the intervals
\[
\bigl[c_\omega(y)/m-\varepsilon,\ c_\omega(y)/m+\varepsilon\bigr]
\qquad(\omega\in F^{-1}(x))
\]
have a common point.  Consequently
\[
\inf_{P\in\RR^{X\times X}}\|T_mM-MP\|_\infty
 =\frac{1}{2m}\max_{x,y}\left(\max_{\omega\in F^{-1}(x)}c_\omega(y)-\min_{\omega\in F^{-1}(x)}c_\omega(y)\right).
\]
The same lower bound holds when \(P\) is required to be row-stochastic.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The \((\omega,y)\)-entry of \(T_mM\) is \(c_\omega(y)/m\), while the corresponding
entry of \(MP\) is the single number \(P(x,y)\) for all \(\omega\in F^{-1}(x)\).
Thus each coordinate is an independent interval intersection problem.  A finite family
of intervals has a common point exactly when its largest left endpoint is no larger than
its smallest right endpoint, giving half the largest diameter.  Restricting the feasible
set to stochastic rows cannot decrease the infimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Fold$_6$ residual certificate]\label{prop:residual-fold6}
For the partition of Definition~\ref{def:fold6}, the largest neighbor-count diameter is
\(\Delta_6=2\).  Among the 441 ordered source-target pairs, the counts of diameters
0, 1, and 2 are respectively \((298,117,26)\).  Hence the sharp real and
row-stochastic residual radii are both \(1/6\).

The two vertices \(000000\) and \(010101\) lie in the fiber \(000000\), but for
the target \(000100\) their neighbor counts are 0 and 1.  A diameter-attaining example
is the fiber \(100000\) and target \(010101\), where the count list is \((0,2,0,0)\);
the averaged pushforward therefore has residual entry \(\frac16(2-\frac12)=\frac14\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Flipping each coordinate in the displayed fiber table gives the finite count lists.
The complete enumeration has maximum 2 and distribution \((298,117,26)\).  The lemma
then gives \(2/(2\cdot6)=1/6\), while the list \((0,2,0,0)\) gives the stated
\(1/4\) entry.  The unequal counts in the same fiber rule out exact intertwining.
\end{proof}

\setcounter{theorem}{22}
\begin{theorem}[Unique minimal hidden repair of Fold$_6$]\label{thm:headline}\label{thm:window6-minimal-equitable-refinement}
The signature refinement in the preceding table has 48 cells, is equitable, and is
exactly the orbit partition of \(\sigma_{\rm geo}\).  Every equitable partition refining
the Fold$_6$ fibers refines this 48-cell partition.  Therefore it is the unique coarsest
equitable refinement, and every equitable hidden realization through which Fold$_6$
factors has at least 48 states.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The identity \(\Fold_6\sigma_{\rm geo}=\Fold_6\) and the fact that \(\sigma_{\rm geo}\)
is a cube automorphism imply \(s(\omega)=s(\sigma_{\rm geo}\omega)\).  Comparing with
the table shows that the signature cells are exactly the orbits: 32 fixed points and
16 two-cycles.  Any orbit partition of a graph automorphism is equitable, since the
automorphism bijects neighbors in each target orbit.

Conversely, let \(\mathcal Q\) be any equitable refinement of the Fold fibers.  Two
vertices in one cell of \(\mathcal Q\) have equal neighbor counts into every cell of
\(\mathcal Q\).  Summing over the cells contained in any Fold fiber gives equal counts
into every visible target, so they have the same signature.  Thus \(\mathcal Q\) refines
the displayed partition.  This proves coarseness, uniqueness, and the lower bound.
\end{proof}

\section{Quotient spectrum and the discarded $Q_4$ carrier}\label{sec:spectrum}
Let \(V=\CC^{\Omega_6}\), and let \(V^\pm\) be the \(\pm1\)-eigenspaces of the
permutation induced by \(\sigma_{\rm geo}\).  The 16 two-cycles give \(\dim V^-=16\).

We first recall the spectral input.  If a partition of \(Q_m\) has block matrix \(M\)
and \(A_mM=MB\), then every eigenvalue of \(B\) is an eigenvalue of \(A_m\): an
eigenvector \(v\) of \(B\) gives the nonzero eigenvector \(Mv\) of \(A_m\).  The Walsh
characters \(\chi_S(\omega)=(-1)^{\sum_{i\in S}\omega_i}\) form an orthogonal basis and
\(A_m\chi_S=(m-2|S|)\chi_S\).  Therefore any equitable quotient walk has eigenvalues
in \(\{1-2k/m:0\le k\le m\}\).  This standard inclusion is used only to interpret
the quotient spectrum below; the minimality argument itself is purely local.

\begin{lemma}[Spectral carrier of the minimal quotient]\label{lem:spectral-carrier}
The antisymmetric space \(V^-\) is invariant under \(A_6\) and
\(A_6|_{V^-}\cong A_4\).  The invariant space \(V^+\), which is the function space of
the 48-cell orbit quotient, has adjacency eigenvalue multiplicities
\[
(1,5,11,14,11,5,1)
\]
at eigenvalues \(6,4,2,0,-2,-4,-6\), respectively.  Hence the quotient walk has
the multiplicities in Theorem~\ref{thm:headline}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each assignment \(u\in\{0,1\}^4\) to coordinates \(2,3,4,6\), let
\(\omega^{00}(u)\) and \(\omega^{11}(u)\) be the vertices with coordinates 1 and 5
equal to 00 and 11.  The 16 differences
\(\mathbf1_{\omega^{00}(u)}-\mathbf1_{\omega^{11}(u)}\) form a basis of \(V^-\).
Flips in coordinates 1 and 5 cancel on each difference; the remaining four flips act
exactly as the four cube directions on \(u\).  This gives the isomorphism with \(A_4\).

For the invariant space, use Walsh characters \(\chi_S(\omega)=(-1)^{\sum_{i\in S}\omega_i}\).
For each \(J\subseteq\{2,3,4,6\}\), the three invariant vectors
\[
\chi_J,\qquad \chi_{J\cup\{1,5\}},\qquad
\chi_{J\cup\{1\}}-\chi_{J\cup\{5\}}
\]
have Walsh degrees \(|J|,|J|+2,|J|+1\).  Thus the multiplicity polynomial is
\[
(1+q)^4(1+q+q^2)=1+5q+11q^2+14q^3+11q^4+5q^5+q^6.
\]
Dividing the adjacency eigenvalues by six gives the quotient-walk spectrum.
\end{proof}

\section{Final remarks and reproducibility}\label{sec:final}
The result is a finite, exact statement about the audited Fold$_6$ partition.  It does
not claim that every folded hypercube or every pushforward construction has the same
repair.  The proof is independently checkable from the fiber table: recompute the six
flips of each of the 64 vertices, group equal signatures, and then check neighbor counts
between the resulting cells.

The supplement contains the complete canonical streams for the Fold$_6$ fibers, the
directed edge-count matrix, the involution and residual witnesses, the exact determinant
certificate for the visible pushforward, and the scripts
\texttt{supplement/verify\_window6\_streams.py} and
\texttt{artifacts/verify\_hidden\_refinement.py}.  The first script checks the printed
finite streams and their hashes; the second recomputes the 48 signature cells, compares
them with the \(\sigma_{\rm geo}\)-orbits, and verifies equitability and the 32/16 orbit
size split.  These checks validate the finite data and the stated partition identities;
they do not replace the proofs of the interval lemma or the spectral-carrier argument.
