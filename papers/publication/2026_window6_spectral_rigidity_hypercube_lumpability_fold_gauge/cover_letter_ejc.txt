Dear Editors,

We submit "The Unique Minimal Equitable Refinement of a Folded Partition of
the 6-Cube" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. Its main result determines the
unique coarsest equitable refinement of the folded partition of Q_6: it is a
48-cell orbit partition, consisting of 32 singletons and 16 pairs, and every
equitable hidden realization retaining the folded labels refines it. The
paper also classifies the dimensions m in {3,6,8,9} at which the relevant
nontrivial equitable hidden mechanism exists.

The paper does not claim that dimension 6 is minimal: the first
non-equitable dimension is 3, while dimension 6 is the instance for which the
complete 48-cell classification is given. Nor does it claim a new proof of
the four-element list of Fibonacci numbers that are sums of two distinct
powers of two; that list is imported as a specialization of the theorem of
Bugeaud, Cipu, and Mignotte. We believe EJC is a suitable venue for this
finite but exact result in equitable partitions, hypercube quotients, and
algebraic combinatorics.

Reproducibility: the scripts in artifacts/ independently recompute the
dimension classification, the 48-cell refinement, equitability, and the
hidden-realization bound.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
