Dear Editors,

We submit "Finite-window Zeckendorf folding: exact decoding and the pair
shift" by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. For every m >= 3, the sliding code formed
from length-m Zeckendorf folds is a topological conjugacy onto its image, and
its causal inverse has exact memory 2 independently of m: three consecutive
fold labels determine the current raw digit, whereas two do not. In contrast,
whole-block reconstruction has the sharp threshold 2m-1, with collisions at
length 2m-2. The contrast between uniform local decoding and the growing
whole-block threshold is the paper's principal result.

The theorem concerns this Fibonacci folding map. We do not claim a general
method or a class-level theorem containing it. The bounded-delay lemma is
included only for orientation; Fibonacci normalization has unbounded carry
cascades and lies outside that lemma's hypotheses. Dynamical Systems is a
natural venue because the result is a sharp, system-specific theorem about
symbolic conjugacy and a sofic pair shift.

Reproducibility: the scripts in artifacts/ check the carry transducer and the
finite symbolic calculations supporting the presentation.

Sincerely,

Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang
