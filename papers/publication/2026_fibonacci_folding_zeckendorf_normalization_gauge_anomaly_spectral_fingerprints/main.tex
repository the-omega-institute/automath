\begin{abstract}
For every \(m\ge3\), the sliding code obtained from length-\(m\) Zeckendorf folds is a topological conjugacy onto its image. Its causal inverse has exact memory \(2\), independently of \(m\): three consecutive fold labels determine the current raw digit, whereas two do not. In contrast, reconstruction of an entire raw block has the sharp threshold \(2m-1\), with collisions still occurring at length \(2m-2\). This uniformly local inverse persists even though Fibonacci normalization admits arbitrarily long carry cascades.

The decoder follows from an arithmetic boundary-cancellation identity. A separate four-state carry transducer presents the associated raw/normalized pair shift, and its discrepancy factor is an irreducible strictly sofic shift with a three-state Fischer cover and entropy \(\log\varphi\).
\end{abstract}
