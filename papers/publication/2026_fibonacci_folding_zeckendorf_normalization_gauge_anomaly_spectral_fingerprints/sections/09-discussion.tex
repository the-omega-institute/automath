\section{Concluding remarks}
\label{sec:discussion}

The main phenomenon is a uniformly local inverse in the presence of unbounded arithmetic propagation. For every \(m\ge3\), the length-\(m\) fold code is a topological conjugacy onto its image, and three consecutive labels determine the current raw digit while two do not. Thus its exact zero-anticipation memory is \(2\), independently of \(m\). The arithmetic contrast is sharp: reconstructing an entire length-\(2m-1\) lift requires \(m\) labels, and collisions persist one digit below that block threshold.

The graph and decoding statements arise from distinct mechanisms. Bounded one-sided influence would give finite pair memory automatically, but Fibonacci normalization does not have bounded influence. Its four-state graph instead follows from the explicit carry imbalance, while the boundary identity \eqref{eq:window-boundary-identity} controls whole-block separation and the shorter causal decoder. Passing from the raw/normalized pair to its discrepancy bit gives a strictly sofic factor with entropy \(\log\varphi\). The graph also yields the discrepancy density \(4/9\), asymptotic variance \(118/243\), and rotation pentagon by standard finite-state calculations. These are checks on the presentation; the distinctive result is the sharp, \(m\)-independent decoder and the conjugacy it induces.
