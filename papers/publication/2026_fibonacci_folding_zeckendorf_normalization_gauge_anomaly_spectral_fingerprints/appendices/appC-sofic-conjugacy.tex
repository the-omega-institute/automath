\section{Graph interpretation of the decoding threshold}
\label{app:sofic}

This appendix records the graph interpretation of the arithmetic separation theorem in Section~\ref{sec:sequence} and the exceptional fiber at $m=2$. The proof of the threshold itself is the boundary cancellation argument in \Cref{thm:block-separation}; it does not require a finite list of short ambiguity cores.

\subsection{The labeled graph presentation}

Proposition~\ref{prop:sofic} constructs a labeled directed graph
\[
G_m=(V_m,E_m,\lambda_m),
\qquad
V_m=\{0,1\}^{m-1},
\]
whose edges are the length-$m$ words $vb$, with
\[
v\longrightarrow \operatorname{suffix}_{m-1}(vb),
\qquad
\lambda_m(vb)=\Fold_m(vb)\in X_m.
\]
The edge shift of $G_m$ is canonically conjugate to the full two-sided shift: the underlying microstate sequence is recovered by reading the appended bit on each edge.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:right-resolving}
For every $m\ge1$, the graph $G_m$ is right-resolving.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For a fixed vertex $v\in V_m$, the values of the two outgoing words $v0$ and $v1$ differ by $\Fib_{m+1}$. As shown in the proof of \Cref{thm:block-separation}, equal fold labels are equivalent to congruent values modulo $\Fib_{m+2}$. Since
\[
0<\Fib_{m+1}<\Fib_{m+2},
\]
the two outgoing labels are distinct.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The fiber product}

Let $\widehat G_m$ be the off-diagonal fiber product of $G_m$ with itself. Its vertices are ordered pairs $(v,v')\in V_m^2$ with $v\ne v'$, and
\[
(v,v')\xrightarrow{y}(w,w')
\]
is an edge when $v\to w$ and $v'\to w'$ both carry the label $y$. A path of $q$ edges in $\widehat G_m$ encodes two distinct binary blocks of length $m+q-1$ with the same sequence of $q$ folded windows, while allowing the two paths to merge would leave the off-diagonal fiber product.

For $m\ge3$, \Cref{thm:block-separation} says that $\widehat G_m$ has no path of $m$ edges. The sharpness blocks in that theorem give a path of $m-1$ edges, so the maximum off-diagonal path length is exactly $m-1$. In particular, $\widehat G_m$ has no bi-infinite path. This is a whole-lift ambiguity bound. The shorter decoder in \Cref{thm:window-decoder} uses the boundary identity to show that all lifts of a three-label block have the same terminal raw digit, even when the rest of the lift is not unique.

\subsection{The exceptional case \texorpdfstring{$m=2$}{m = 2}}

For $m=2$, the edge-label table is
\[
00\mapsto 00,
\qquad
01\mapsto 01,
\qquad
10\mapsto 10,
\qquad
11\mapsto 00.
\]
Thus the only label collision is $00\leftarrow\{00,11\}$.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{thm:m2}]
In the off-diagonal fiber product, the collision above extends bi-infinitely only by repeating itself. It gives the constant label sequence $(00)^\ZZ$, lifted once by $0^\ZZ$ and once by $1^\ZZ$. Every other label sequence contains $01$ or $10$, and each of those labels has a unique preimage. Hence $(00)^\ZZ$ is the unique non-injective fiber, with exactly the two preimages stated in \Cref{thm:m2}.
\end{proof}
