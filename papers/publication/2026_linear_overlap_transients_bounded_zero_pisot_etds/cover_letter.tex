\begin{letter}{Editors\\Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our manuscript ``Linear overlap transients and cyclic rank
recodings in Pisot numeration'' for publication in \emph{Ergodic Theory and
Dynamical Systems}.

The paper proves effective eventual acyclicity for one-position overlap
chains of bounded representations of zero in every fixed strictly increasing
Pisot linear numeration system.  At every sufficiently large aperture $m$,
the zero loop is the only directed cycle and every anchored nonzero path has
at most $m$ edges.  An exact collision quotient makes every cyclic
language-rank recoding eventually injective and gives the sharp universal
bound $\limsup_m\ell_{\rm cau}(U,m)/m\le1$.  A single fixed cubic recurrence
shows that the threshold is necessary: the aperture-two fold is the identity,
aperture three has one constant branch pair, and every aperture from four
onward is injective.  Its exact depth $2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$ at those
apertures attains equality asymptotically.

The source archive includes code for the finite computations discussed in
the article.  Every theorem, including the fixed cubic terminal
classification, is proved in the article itself.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
been approved by both authors.  The authors declare no competing interests.

\closing{Sincerely,\\Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang}
