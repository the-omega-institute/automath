\begin{letter}{Editors\\Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our manuscript ``Linear overlap transients and cyclic rank
recodings in Pisot numeration'' for publication in \emph{Ergodic Theory and
Dynamical Systems}.

The paper proves an effective cycle-or-linear-transient dichotomy for
one-position overlap chains of bounded representations of zero in every
fixed strictly increasing Pisot linear numeration system.  The principal
technical lemma treats an arbitrary bounded coefficient alphabet and assumes
neither Condition~F nor zero preservation.  An exact collision quotient then
transfers the theorem to future-only inverse depth for cyclic language-rank
recodings.  A single fixed cubic recurrence has exact depth
$2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$, proving sharp linear order for both formulations.

The source archive includes code for the finite computations discussed in
the article.  Every theorem, including the fixed cubic terminal
classification, is proved in the article itself.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
been approved by both authors.  The authors declare no competing interests.

\closing{Sincerely,\\Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang}
