\begin{abstract}
We prove an effective linear bound for transients in one-position overlap
graphs of bounded representations of zero in a fixed Pisot linear numeration
system.  If $U=(u_j)$ is strictly increasing, $u_0=1$, and its recurrence
has a nonintegral Pisot characteristic root, then for every coefficient
bound $D$ there is an effective constant $C(U,D)$ such that, whenever no
directed cycle is reachable, every nonzero anchored path at aperture $m$ has
fewer than $C(U,D)m$ edges.  The proof compares adjacent zero
representations under the algebraic embeddings: contraction bounds the
nondominant contributions, and finite separation in the dominant embedding
forces the successive quotients to collapse.

For cyclic recodings obtained by reducing canonical language ranks modulo
$u_m$, the coordinate-difference graph is exactly this bounded-zero overlap
graph, so injectivity implies an aperture-linear future-only inverse depth.
For the fixed binary cubic recurrence with leading root of
$x^3-2x^2+x-1$, the recoding is injective for every $m\ge4$ and has exact
causal length $2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$; the same construction proves that the
linear transient bound is sharp in order.
\end{abstract}
