\section{Auxiliary order-eight Laurent coefficient certificate}%
\label{app:order-eight-laurent-certificate}

This appendix gives the finite certificate for the order-eight rows used to
audit the \(A_8\) substitution.  The main coefficient hierarchy has already
been proved in Section~\ref{sec:entropy-asymptotics}; the material below is
a reproducible check of one explicit example.  The single convention is
\[
q_{n,k}=2^{-n}(-\ii)^n
\operatorname{sgn}(k)^{\,n\bmod2}
\binom{n-1}{|k|-1},
\qquad 0<|k|\le n,
\]
and
\[
J(n_1,\ldots,n_r)=[z^0]\prod_{j=1}^rQ_{n_j}(z).
\]

The Laurent representatives used in the rows through total order eight are
\begin{align}
Q_2(z)
&=-\frac14\bigl(z+z^{-1}+z^2+z^{-2}\bigr),
\label{eq:appendix-d-explicit-q2}\\
Q_3(z)
&=\frac{\ii}{8}\bigl((z-z^{-1})+2(z^2-z^{-2})+(z^3-z^{-3})\bigr),
\label{eq:appendix-d-explicit-q3}\\
Q_4(z)
&=\frac1{16}\bigl((z+z^{-1})+3(z^2+z^{-2})
  +3(z^3+z^{-3})+(z^4+z^{-4})\bigr),
\label{eq:appendix-d-explicit-q4}\\
Q_5(z)
&=-\frac{\ii}{32}\bigl((z-z^{-1})+4(z^2-z^{-2})
  +6(z^3-z^{-3})+4(z^4-z^{-4})+(z^5-z^{-5})\bigr),
\label{eq:appendix-d-explicit-q5}\\
Q_6(z)
&=-\frac1{64}\bigl((z+z^{-1})+5(z^2+z^{-2})
  +10(z^3+z^{-3})+10(z^4+z^{-4}) \nonumber\\
&\hspace{6.7em}
  +5(z^5+z^{-5})+(z^6+z^{-6})\bigr).
\label{eq:appendix-d-explicit-q6}
\end{align}
These are exactly the cases \(2\le n\le6\) of
\eqref{eq:mode-laurent-polynomial-definition}.  They are displayed so that
the signs in the odd negative-exponent rows can be checked directly.

For a Laurent polynomial \(F(z)=\sum_{|k|\le K}a_kz^k\), put
\[
\mathsf G_N(F):=\frac1N\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}F(\zeta_N^\ell),
\qquad \zeta_N:=e^{2\pi\ii/N}.
\]
If \(N>K\), then \(\mathsf G_N(F)=[z^0]F\).  Thus the following exact
root-of-unity checks extract the same constant terms without using the
zero-sum recurrence:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{object}&\text{value}\\
\hline
Q_2(1),Q_2(-1)&-1,\ 0\\
Q_3(1),Q_3(-1)&0,\ 0\\
Q_4(1),Q_4(-1)&1,\ 0\\
Q_5(1),Q_5(-1)&0,\ 0\\
Q_6(1),Q_6(-1)&-1,\ 0\\
\mathsf G_{17}(Q_2Q_6)&3/64\\
\mathsf G_{17}(Q_3Q_5)&-15/128\\
\mathsf G_{17}(Q_2Q_3^2)&-9/128 .
\end{array}
\]
The grid checks are exact finite averages, since
\(17>2+6=3+5=2+3+3\).

\begin{lemma}[Deterministic exact Laurent recurrence]%
\label{lem:appendix-d-deterministic-laurent-recurrence}
Let \(\mathbf n=(n_1,\ldots,n_r)\) be any finite row of positive integers.
Define finitely supported maps \(C_j:\ZZ\to\QQ[\ii]\) by
\[
C_0(s)=\mathbf 1_{\{s=0\}},\qquad
C_j(s)=\sum_{0<|k|\le n_j}
2^{-n_j}(-\ii)^{n_j}\operatorname{sgn}(k)^{\,n_j\bmod2}
\binom{n_j-1}{|k|-1}\,C_{j-1}(s-k).
\]
Then
\[
  C_r(0)=[z^0]\prod_{j=1}^r Q_{n_j}(z)=J(\mathbf n).
\]
Equivalently, with
\[
\mathcal K(\mathbf n)
:=\{(k_1,\ldots,k_r):0<|k_j|\le n_j,\ k_1+\cdots+k_r=0\}
\]
and \(\lambda_n:=2^{-n}(-\ii)^n\),
\[
C_r(0)=
\prod_{j=1}^r\lambda_{n_j}
\sum_{\mathbf k\in\mathcal K(\mathbf n)}
\prod_{j=1}^r
\operatorname{sgn}(k_j)^{\,n_j\bmod2}
\binom{n_j-1}{|k_j|-1}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The coefficient formula \eqref{eq:mode-laurent-coefficients} says that
multiplication by \(Q_{n_j}\) sends a coefficient array \(C_{j-1}\) to the
convolution array displayed above.  Induction on \(j\) gives
\[
  C_j(s)=[z^s]\prod_{a=1}^jQ_{n_a}(z),
\]
and hence \(C_r(0)\) is the desired constant term.  Expanding the last
coefficient and collecting the common factor
\(\prod_j\lambda_{n_j}\) gives the zero-sum formula.
\end{proof}

The row certificate is the following finite table: \(S(\mathbf n)\) is the
integer zero-sum convolution in
Lemma~\ref{lem:appendix-d-deterministic-laurent-recurrence}, and
\(J(\mathbf n)=\Lambda(\mathbf n)S(\mathbf n)\), where
\(\Lambda(\mathbf n)=\prod_j\lambda_{n_j}\).
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|c|c}
\mathbf n&|\mathcal K(\mathbf n)|&S(\mathbf n)&\Lambda(\mathbf n)&J(\mathbf n)\\
\hline
(2,6)&4&12&1/256&3/64\\
(3,5)&6&-30&1/256&-15/128\\
(4,4)&8&40&1/256&5/32\\
(2,2,4)&12&24&1/256&3/32\\
(2,3,3)&14&-18&1/256&-9/128\\
(2,2,2,2)&36&36&1/256&9/64 .
\end{array}
\]
Odd total-order entries through order eight vanish by
\(u_n(-y)=(-1)^nu_n(y)\).  The sign-sensitive rows
\((3,5)\) and \((2,3,3)\) use the same all-sign coefficient rule as the
other rows; no separate conjugation convention is applied.

As a model for the sign-sensitive rows, write
\[
w_n(k):=\operatorname{sgn}(k)^{\,n\bmod 2}
\binom{n-1}{|k|-1},\qquad 0<|k|\le n .
\]
For \((3,5)\), the zero-sum condition forces the second index to be \(-k\).
Hence
\[
S(3,5)
=\sum_{0<|k|\le3}w_3(k)w_5(-k).
\]
Since \(\operatorname{sgn}(-k)=-\operatorname{sgn}(k)\), the two choices
\(k=\pm r\), \(r=1,2,3\), contribute the same negative weight:
\[
S(3,5)
=-2\left[
\binom20\binom40+\binom21\binom41+\binom22\binom42
\right]
=-2(1+8+6)=-30.
\]
The rational prefactor is
\(\Lambda(3,5)=\lambda_3\lambda_5=(\ii/8)(-\ii/32)=1/256\), and therefore
\[
J(3,5)=-\frac{30}{256}=-\frac{15}{128}.
\]
The row \((2,3,3)\) is obtained from the same recurrence with no change of
sign convention.

Finally, the order-eight coefficient substitution is obtained by inserting
the six row values into the universal entropy coefficient:
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_8&=\mu_2\mu_6J(2,6)+\mu_3\mu_5J(3,5)
+\frac12\mu_4^2J(4,4)
-\frac12\mu_2^2\mu_4J(2,2,4)\\
&\hspace{2.5em}
-\frac12\mu_2\mu_3^2J(2,3,3)
+\frac1{12}\mu_2^4J(2,2,2,2).
\end{aligned}
\]
With \(\mu_2=\sigma^2\), this gives
\[
A_8=
\frac{3\sigma^8-12\sigma^4\mu_4+9\sigma^2\mu_3^2
+12\sigma^2\mu_6-30\mu_3\mu_5+20\mu_4^2}{256}.
\]
This appendix is therefore a compact reproducibility certificate for the
finite Laurent rows and the coefficient substitution.  It is not an
additional hypothesis and does not replace the main-text coefficient theorem.
