 1199: \begin{proposition}[Submission artifact role separation]
 1200: \label{prop:submission-artifact-role-separation}
 1201: For the current presentation-only route, the primary artifact, long technical
 1202: supplement, and review bundle have distinct review roles.  The primary source
 1203: \path{submission_abstract.tex} states the presentation claim, \path{main.tex}
 1204: proves the long-form interface theorems and records the case tables, and
 1205: \path{review_bundle/} supplies the supplemental source snapshots, case snapshots,
 1206: and machine-readable manifest rows.  None of these roles converts the support
 1207: bundle into a second primary submission or converts a primary-artifact claim into
 1208: an uncited supplement claim.
 1209: \end{proposition}
 1210: 
 1211: \begin{proof}
 1212: The manifest field \path{submission_artifacts} names the short source, intended
 1213: short PDF, long technical source, long technical PDF, and supplemental bundle as
 1214: separate artifacts.  The checklist above fixes how the short artifact points to
 1215: the supplement: it names the safety theorem, the case rows, the evidence-level
 1216: boundary, and \path{review_bundle/}.  The earlier source-snapshot and
 1217: case-snapshot propositions identify the two support functions of the bundle: a
 1218: pinned discovery-gate source snapshot and bounded case-evidence snapshots.  Since
 1219: the primary note states only the interface and each support claim is tied to a
 1220: specific theorem, table, or manifest field, the artifacts remain role-separated:
 1221: the support materials make the primary note reviewable but do not become hidden
 1222: primary claims.\qed
 1223: \end{proof}
