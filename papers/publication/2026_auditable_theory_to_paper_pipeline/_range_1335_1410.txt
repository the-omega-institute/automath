 1335: \begin{theorem}[Evidence-level overclaim obstruction]
 1336: \label{thm:evidence-level-overclaim-obstruction}
 1337: Fix the certificate schema, dispatch function, and evidence-level vocabulary of
 1338: this paper.  Let $c$ be a load-bearing claim whose accepted certificate has
 1339: level path-verified and whose boundary field $B$ records that no command-run
 1340: assertion is made.  Then every stronger reading of $c$ that asserts any of the
 1341: following additional conclusions is rejected by the finite schema verifier:
 1342: \begin{enumerate}
 1343: \item external gate soundness for an arbitrary implementation of the workflow;
 1344: \item Lean correctness, full-source rebuild success, or axiom-purity beyond the
 1345: recorded formal-source gate fields;
 1346: \item end-to-end external semantic validity of artifact data beyond the finite
 1347: manifest and evaluator surfaces named in $E$ and $R$;
 1348: \item a fresh daemon run, full-source rerun, Rule110 or GroundCompiler rerun, or
 1349: journal or workshop acceptance; or
 1350: \item bundle-backed reviewability when neither \path{review_bundle/} nor an
 1351: archival equivalent carrying the same finite records is in the submitted
 1352: artifact boundary.
 1353: \end{enumerate}
 1354: More precisely, such a stronger reading has an inclusion-minimal bad subrecord of
 1355: one of the five types in
 1356: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton}.  Hence the accepted
 1357: claim is rigid at its declared evidence level: path-verified certificate records
 1358: cannot be promoted by prose into command-run evidence, semantic validation,
 1359: formal-source correctness, or external acceptance.
 1360: \end{theorem}
 1361: 
 1362: \begin{proof}
 1363: Let $c^+$ be one of the stronger readings.  It has the same displayed manuscript
 1364: sentence as $c$ but a larger claim-kind set, a stronger evidence level, or a
 1365: larger submitted-artifact boundary requirement.  The verifier does not inspect
 1366: rhetorical emphasis; by Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-certificate-schema-verifier}
 1367: it inspects only the finite tuple
 1368: $\mathcal C=(K,\ell,E,H,R,D,B)$ and the finite dispatch table.
 1369: 
 1370: If $c^+$ asserts external gate soundness for an arbitrary implementation, then
 1371: it is not merely the conditional theorem-interface claim accepted by
 1372: Theorem~\ref{thm:publication-safety-interface}.  It needs pass records showing
 1373: that the relevant implementation ran every gate in
 1374: $\bigcup_{k\in K}\Gamma(k)$.  Those records are not supplied by the
 1375: path-verified certificate for $c$, so the minimal bad subrecord is a missing
 1376: pass-record obstruction of type~2.  If $c^+$ asserts Lean correctness,
 1377: full-source rebuild success, or axiom-purity, then it is a formal-source or
 1378: command-run claim.  Proposition~\ref{prop:formal-source-gate-instantiation}
 1379: identifies the required build, axiom-purity, and command-run fields; when they
 1380: are absent, the minimal bad subrecord is type~2 for missing formal-source gates
 1381: or type~5 for command-run escalation.
 1382: 
 1383: If $c^+$ asserts end-to-end semantic validity of artifacts beyond the named
 1384: manifest and evaluator surfaces, then it has an artifact component not covered
 1385: by the finite status and limitation-ledger records in $R$.  The current
 1386: acceptance and certificate ledgers display only the bounded artifact row $a_6$:
 1387: its evidence surface is the Rule110 status file, limitation ledgers,
 1388: paper-data reference, and review-bundle manifest, and its boundary field says
 1389: that no fresh artifact rerun is asserted.  A stronger artifact claim therefore
 1390: lacks an assigned artifact pass record, giving type~2,
 1391: or asserts a fresh evaluator run without command-run fields, giving type~5.  The
 1392: same argument covers a fresh daemon run, full-source rerun, and Rule110 or
 1393: GroundCompiler rerun: each is a command-run or artifact-strength escalation and
 1394: is rejected by the missing gate or command-run boundary fields.
 1395: 
 1396: If $c^+$ asserts journal or workshop acceptance, then its acceptance-entry field
 1397: would have to contain an editorial or venue acceptance record.  The operational
 1398: notion of acceptance entry in this paper is internal and submission-local; the
 1399: current ledger contains no external editorial acceptance row.  Thus the stronger
 1400: reading either lacks the required route/submission pass record, giving type~2,
 1401: or lacks the relevant external evidence surface, giving type~1.  Finally, if a
 1402: bundle-backed claim is made while no review bundle or archival equivalent lies
 1403: inside the submitted artifact boundary, the evidence item named by the
 1404: certificate is absent from $E$ at the declared level, so the minimal bad
 1405: subrecord is type~1.  These cases exhaust the displayed stronger readings.
 1406: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton} then gives the stated
 1407: classification and proves rigidity at the declared evidence level.\qed
 1408: \end{proof}
