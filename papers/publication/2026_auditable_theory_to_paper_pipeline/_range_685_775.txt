  685: \begin{theorem}[Conditional publication safety interface]
  686: \label{thm:publication-safety-interface}
  687: Consider a theory-to-paper pipeline with the operational interface above.  Assume
  688: that every load-bearing claim in an active manuscript is either rejected or has an
  689: acceptance entry with an evidence surface; that the pipeline is gate-sound for
  690: acceptance; that every advisory output is demoted unless the deterministic gate
  691: set $\Gamma$ for its claim kind passes; that discovery claims are checked through
  692: the pinned discovery-gate interface certified by
  693: Lemma~\ref{lem:source-interface-extraction-correctness} and
  694: Propositions~\ref{prop:reviewable-source-snapshot-interface} and
  695: \ref{prop:source-interface-record-file-consistency}, in particular its pipeline
  696: safety and main pipeline theorem labels; and that no inactive intake seed is
  697: treated as publication work without human promotion.  Then, conditional on those
  698: workflow assumptions, any accepted load-bearing claim in the manuscript admits a
  699: locally checkable publication certificate $\mathcal C(c)$ accepted by the finite
  700: schema verifier of Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-certificate-schema-verifier}.
  701: Consequently the claim has a recorded evidence surface, a declared evidence
  702: boundary, and a prior pass record for every gate required for its claim kind.
  703: Moreover, accepted discovery claims inherit the bounded pinned source-interface
  704: consequences that statement-only, code-only, mechanical-expansion, and
  705: compression-only outputs are not accepted as discovery without the required
  706: certificate, ledger, and lineage witnesses.  Any purported accepted claim without
  707: such finite certificate data is rejected by one of the five obstruction skeletons
  708: in Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton}.  This theorem is a
  709: conditional safety interface: it does not independently prove that an arbitrary
  710: implementation is gate-sound, that all gates were run, or that path-verified rows
  711: are command-run evidence.
  712: \end{theorem}
  713: 
  714: \begin{proof}
  715: Let $c$ be an accepted load-bearing claim in the manuscript.  The conclusion has
  716: three parts.  The first part constructs the certificate.  The acceptance-entry
  717: hypothesis supplies the claim-kind set $K$, evidence level $\ell$, and evidence
  718: surface $E$, so the decision is attached to a source path, artifact path,
  719: command log, inventory, or witness record rather than to generated prose alone.
  720: Gate-soundness supplies the pass-record field $R$ for every gate assigned by
  721: $\Gamma$.  If accepting $c$ crosses from intake or source material into
  722: daemon-visible publication work, the promotion assumption supplies the human
  723: promotion field $H$; otherwise $H$ records that no such boundary is crossed.  The
  724: evidence-level definition supplies the boundary field $B$: either the command-run
  725: fields are present or the record explicitly remains path-verified.  If $c$ is a
  726: discovery claim, the discovery-routing assumption supplies $D$ from the pinned
  727: source-interface rows; otherwise $D$ is absent as required by the certificate
  728: schema.  Thus $\mathcal C(c)=(K,\ell,E,H,R,D,B)$ is locally checkable whenever its
  729: cited paths and manifest rows lie in the submitted artifact, review bundle, or
  730: explicit historical boundary.
  731: 
  732: Propositions~\ref{prop:claim-kind-dispatch} and
  733: \ref{prop:gate-interface-separation} identify the required finite gate set:
  734: formal-source claims use build and axiom gates, traceability claims use inventory
  735: and marker gates, route claims use promotion, overlap, and submission-state
  736: gates, artifact claims use status and limitation-ledger gates, submission claims
  737: use submission-pack gates, extension-style discovery claims use the
  738: theorem-content delta gate together with the pinned discovery gate,
  739: work-selection claims use the scheduler gate, and mixed claims use the union.
  740: Thus $c$ has a prior pass record for the gates required by its kind.  The
  741: advisory-output assumption prevents an agent draft, review packet, score, or
  742: repair proposal from being promoted by prose alone: without the relevant pass
  743: records it remains demoted.  The promotion assumption similarly prevents an
  744: inactive intake seed from becoming publication work merely because a local source
  745: path exists.
  746: 
  747: If $c$ is a discovery claim, the discovery-routing assumption sends it through
  748: the pinned discovery-gate interface.  Proposition~\ref{prop:reviewable-source-snapshot-interface}
  749: supplies the corresponding reviewer-facing snapshot interface, so this routing
  750: does not depend on a reviewer's access to the author's absolute source path.  The
  751: record-file consistency certificate in
  752: Proposition~\ref{prop:source-interface-record-file-consistency} further shows, at
  753: the path-verified record level, that the manuscript table, JSON mirror, and
  754: bundled source snapshot name the same finite imported interface.  The
  755: source-interface table records the imported statements used here:
  756: statement-only, code-only, mechanical-expansion, and compression-only records do
  757: not become accepted discovery claims without the required certificate, ledger,
  758: and lineage witnesses, and classifier-shift selection remains bounded by the
  759: discovery gate as shown in Proposition~\ref{prop:scheduler-selection-advisory}.
  760: Finally apply Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton}.  If an
  761: allegedly accepted claim lacks the certificate just constructed, its failure has
  762: a minimal bad subrecord of type 1--5: missing evidence surface, missing gate
  763: record, missing promotion field, missing discovery-interface rows, or unsupported
  764: command-run escalation.  Each type contradicts one of the workflow assumptions
  765: above or demotes the claim to a weaker evidence level.  The stated safety
  766: interface follows.\qed
  767: \end{proof}
