  805: \begin{proposition}[Current-package acceptance and certificate ledger]

  807: Let $P^{+}$ be the finite current package consisting of
  808: \path{submission_abstract.tex}, this supplement \path{main.tex},
  809: \path{PIPELINE.md}, \path{SOURCE_MAP.md}, \path{ARTIFACT_INVENTORY.md},
  810: \path{source_interface_record.json}, and
  811: \path{review_bundle/REVIEW_BUNDLE_MANIFEST.json}, together with
  812: \path{review_bundle/certificate_schema.json},
  813: \path{review_bundle/current_package_pass_records.json},
  814: \path{review_bundle/submission_interface_map.json},
  815: \path{review_bundle/primary_claim_inventory.json},
  816: \path{review_bundle/verify_certificate_records.py},
  817: \path{review_bundle/certificate_verification_run.log}, and the explicit
  818: historical paths named in the case catalogue.  Tables~\ref{tab:current-acceptance-ledger}
  819: and~\ref{tab:current-certificate-ledger} form a finite submission-local
  820: acceptance-entry ledger for the load-bearing current-package claims used in
  821: Corollary~\ref{cor:current-package-path-verified-instantiation}.  For each entry
  822: $a_i$, the two tables determine the certificate fields
  823: \[
  824: \mathcal C(a_i)=(K_i,\ell_i,E_i,H_i,R_i,D_i,B_i)
  825: \]
  826: and each such certificate is locally checkable at the path-verified evidence
  827: level.  No load-bearing claim outside these six entries is accepted by the
  828: current-package instantiation.
  829: \end{proposition}
  830: 
  831: \begin{proof}
  832: The acceptance-entry ledger is finite because Table~\ref{tab:current-acceptance-ledger}
  833: has six rows.  Its rows enumerate exactly the assertions on which the current
  834: package corollary depends: the bounded imported source interface, the existence
  835: of the presentation-only package, and the four case-study lessons for intake
  836: isolation, overlap blocking, fake-extension rejection, and artifact limitation.
  837: The third column assigns the claim-kind set $K_i$ for each accepted assertion.
  838: These kinds all lie in the seven-kind domain of the dispatch function $\Gamma$.
  839: 
  840: For each row, Table~\ref{tab:current-certificate-ledger} supplies the remaining
  841: certificate fields.  The declared evidence level $\ell_i$ is path-verified in
  842: every row.  The evidence surface $E_i$ is a finite list of submission-local files,
  843: review-bundle records, pinned source-interface records, or explicit historical
  844: paths.  The promotion field $H_i$ is either the current active-paper marker
  845: \path{main.tex} together with \path{PIPELINE.md}, or the explicit marker that the
  846: case row does not itself cross a new route boundary.  The pass-record field
  847: $R_i$ names the finite gate records used by the row: source-interface extraction
  848: and consistency for $a_1$, package and submission-pack records for $a_2$,
  849: promotion for $a_3$, overlap and submission-state for $a_4$, theorem-content and
  850: discovery gating for $a_5$, and artifact status plus limitation-ledger gating for
  851: $a_6$.
  852: 
  853: The discovery field behaves exactly as required by the certificate schema.  It is
  854: present only for $a_5$, the row whose accepted claim has discovery kind, and it
  855: names the pinned source-interface rows that supply the certificate-and-ledger,
  856: demotion, lineage, pipeline-safety, and main-pipeline witnesses.  For the other
  857: rows the claim-kind set does not contain discovery, so $D_i$ is absent.  Finally,

  865: \(\mathcal C(a_i)\).

  874: acceptance entry in Table~\ref{tab:current-acceptance-ledger} and also violates

  876: Theorems~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton} and
  877: \ref{thm:evidence-level-overclaim-obstruction}, such a reading is therefore
  878: rejected by a finite obstruction subrecord rather than accepted by this
  879: package.\qed

  912: \begin{proof}

  918: interface by pinned commit, JSON mirror, and bundled source snapshot.  Hence the

  922: adds the submission-local acceptance entries for these premises: the imported
  923: source-interface claim is entry $a_1$, and the route/submission package claim is
  924: entry $a_2$.

  927: record naming the source or artifact surface, failed invariant, gate decision,
