 9941: \begin{theorem}[Four-coordinate submission-support skeleton]
 9942: \label{thm:four-coordinate-submission-support-skeleton}
 9943: For the current presentation-only package, every support reading used by the
 9944: publication-safety theorem chain factors through the certificate-calculus spine of
 9945: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-record-certificate-calculus-spine} and then through
 9946: exactly the following four finite coordinates, with no gluing between them unless
 9947: the missing coordinate is explicitly supplied.
 9948: \begin{enumerate}
 9949: \item The theorem-obligation coordinate is the post-inventory byte tuple of
 9950: Corollary~\ref{cor:current-post-inventory-support-tuple-instantiation}, obtained
 9951: only when \path{theorem_inventory.json} and \path{theorem_inventory.md} exist
 9952: before the extractor and retained finite-record verifiers are rerun and before
 9953: the final digest manifest is regenerated.
 9954: \item The external-support coordinate is either exact supplemental upload or the
 9955: archive-equivalent public locator of
 9956: Corollary~\ref{cor:upload-or-archive-instantiation-record}; local digest rows,
 9957: local manifests, and bibliography notes alone do not instantiate it.
 9958: \item The imported BEDC source coordinate is the bounded pinned-snapshot
 9959: interface of Lemma~\ref{lem:source-interface-extraction-correctness} and
 9960: Theorem~\ref{thm:bounded-source-interface-maximality}; stronger readings require
 9961: the exact added coordinate they assert, such as exact source text plus byte
 9962: equality, a public fetch-and-compare record, a Lean/BEDC rebuild record, or a
 9963: semantic-validation record.
 9964: \item The implementation-soundness coordinate for Lean builds, BEDC audits,
 9965: publication-daemon behaviour, and dynamic artifact validators is absent unless a
 9966: row records the command, source state, environment, exit code, and log path for
 9967: that actual gate program.
 9968: \end{enumerate}
 9969: Consequently, after the post-inventory refresh is run, the present manuscript may
 9970: use the refreshed theorem-inventory tuple as local finite-record support for the
 9971: main theorem chain, but it still proves no upload, archive replacement, public
 9972: repository reachability, public byte equality, Lean/BEDC rebuild, daemon rerun,
 9973: or dynamic artifact-validation claim beyond rows explicitly carrying those
 9974: fields.
 9975: \end{theorem}
 9976: 
 9977: \begin{proof}
 9978: The finite-record certificate calculus spine,
 9979: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-record-certificate-calculus-spine}, reduces every support
 9980: reading to projection, obstruction, and positive finite extension.  The four
 9981: coordinates in the statement are the four projections that remain after that
 9982: normal form is applied to the current presentation-only support surface.
 9983: 
 9984: The first coordinate is isolated by
 9985: Theorem~\ref{thm:post-inventory-package-fixed-point}.  Its construction is
 9986: ordered: the inventory bytes must exist, the syntactic extractor and retained
 9987: finite-record verifiers must run on that post-inventory state, and the digest
 9988: manifest must be computed after the resulting logs exist.  Corollary~\ref{cor:current-post-inventory-support-tuple-instantiation}
 9989: identifies the resulting tuple, and
 9990: Corollary~\ref{cor:stable-replay-post-inventory-tuple} shows that later uses of
 9991: the tuple are only byte-level replays unless the sequence is rerun from a new
 9992: source state.  Thus the theorem-obligation coordinate is available precisely at
 9993: the refreshed local finite-record level and not as theorem semantics or as an
 9994: external availability claim.
 9995: 
 9996: The second coordinate is separated by
 9997: Theorem~\ref{thm:support-surface-normal-form},
 9998: Theorem~\ref{thm:upload-or-archive-instantiation-boundary}, and
 9999: Corollary~\ref{cor:upload-or-archive-instantiation-record}.  Those results make
10000: external support a finite field containing either exact venue upload of the
10001: selected support surface or an archive-equivalent locator with matching file
10002: list, path semantics, manifest identity, digest rows, and pinned or same-state
10003: byte equality.  Proposition~\ref{prop:no-external-instantiation-from-local-bundle-records}
10004: proves that local rows in \path{review_bundle/FINAL_DIGESTS_SHA256.md}, local
10005: manifest fields, and bibliography entries do not contain those upload or locator
10006: fields.  Hence this coordinate cannot be glued out of the refreshed theorem
10007: inventory tuple.
10008: 
10009: The third coordinate is the source-interface boundary proved near the start of
10010: the supplement.  Lemma~\ref{lem:source-interface-extraction-correctness} fixes
10011: the imported records as path, pinned commit, line range, label, extraction date,
10012: and bounded summary; Theorem~\ref{thm:bounded-source-interface-maximality} makes
10013: that finite interface maximal for the present package; and
10014: Theorem~\ref{thm:public-source-reachability-non-escalation} rules out upgrading
10015: it to a current public-source reachability or public byte-equality claim without
10016: a new finite fetch-and-compare record.  Therefore a local bundled snapshot and a
10017: source-interface verifier log support only the bounded snapshot reading stated in
10018: the theorem.
10019: 
10020: The fourth coordinate is exactly the implementation-soundness boundary.
10021: Definition~\ref{def:operational-acceptance-interface} requires command, source
10022: state, environment, exit code, and log path for command-result claims;
10023: Theorem~\ref{thm:implementation-soundness-extension-criterion} and
10024: Corollary~\ref{cor:submitted-gate-implementation-contract-dichotomy} state that
10025: actual gate implementation soundness is obtained only by adding such rows for the
10026: gate programs; and Theorem~\ref{thm:external-interface-projection-no-free-upgrade}
10027: prevents external finite records from implying that stronger interface.  The
10028: command-result closure theorem,
10029: Theorem~\ref{thm:current-command-result-closure}, applies only to rows actually
10030: rerun and recorded; it explicitly excludes Lean/BEDC rebuild success, daemon
10031: execution, theorem-proof semantics, and dynamic artifact validation when those
10032: rows are absent.
10033: 
10034: The four coordinates are independent finite fields of the package state.  If a
10035: proposed support reading adds upload, archive replacement, public-source
10036: reachability, public byte equality, source-theory rebuild success, daemon
10037: execution, or artifact-semantic validation, then it changes at least one of the
10038: four fields just listed.  By
10039: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-coordinate-determination-boundary} the missing field is a
10040: finite bad subrecord, so the stronger reading is rejected.  Conversely, the
10041: refreshed post-inventory tuple supplies exactly the first field, and the other
10042: three fields remain at their conditional or absent levels as stated.  This is the
10043: claimed skeleton and connects the closing support boundary back to the finite
10044: publication-safety chain of Theorem~\ref{thm:publication-safety-interface} and
10045: Theorem~\ref{thm:final-local-support-state-reconciliation}.
10046: \end{proof}
