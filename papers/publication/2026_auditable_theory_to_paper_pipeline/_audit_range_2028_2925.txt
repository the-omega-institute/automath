2361: $\Gamma$, and schema verifier $V$ used in

2380: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-certificate-obstruction-skeleton}.  Consequently the
2381: publication-safety theorem is a finite-record certificate invariant under

2384: locators, and source checkouts that leave the finite tuple

2423: Definition~\ref{def:certificate-schema-acceptance-table} did not fail; hence the
2424: required coordinate is present and its assigned gate record passes.  Thus the
2425: normal-form extension coordinates are necessary.

2431: classifies this rejected subrecord and gives inclusion-minimality after removing
