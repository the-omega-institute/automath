  398: \begin{proposition}[Finite certificate schema verifier]
  399: \label{prop:finite-certificate-schema-verifier}
  400: For every load-bearing manuscript claim $c$ whose component kinds lie in the
  401: seven-kind domain, $V(c,\mathcal C)$ is a finite, prose-insensitive
  402: record-schema verifier for the declared publication certificate.  If $V$ accepts,
  403: then $\mathcal C(c)$ is locally checkable at exactly its declared evidence level.
  404: This is certificate-record verification only: it checks finite record shape,
  405: dispatch, evidence pointers, and command-boundary discipline, not end-to-end
  406: external semantic validation of the referenced artifacts or source theory.  If
  407: $V$ rejects, then a reviewer can extract a finite bad subrecord consisting of the
  408: first failed schema check and the fields on which that check depends.
  409: \end{proposition}
  410: 
  411: \begin{proof}
  412: The verifier inspects only the displayed tuple $\mathcal C(c)$, the finite
  413: seven-kind domain, the finite dispatch table $\Gamma$, and the finite list of
  414: paths, rows, commits, or logs cited by the record.  Since $K$ is required to be a
  415: subset of the seven-kind domain, the set
  416: \(\bigcup_{k\in K}\Gamma(k)\) is finite.  The checks over $E$, $R$, and the
  417: command-run fields are therefore finite lookups in named manuscript or bundle
  418: surfaces, not semantic judgments about generated prose.
  419: 
  420: If all six checks pass, the tuple has exactly the fields required in the
  421: definition of a publication certificate: a finite kind set $K$, declared evidence
  422: level $\ell$, evidence surface $E$, route-boundary field $H$, gate-record field
  423: $R$, optional discovery field $D$ with the required presence condition, and
  424: evidence-strength boundary $B$.  Check~(ii) is precisely the local-checkability
  425: condition on cited paths, manifest rows, logs, pinned commits, and historical
  426: boundaries.  Checks~(iii)--(vi) prevent the only possible escalations of the
  427: declared level: missing gate passage, unrecorded route promotion, unsupported
  428: discovery routing, and command-run assertion without command-run fields.  Thus an
  429: accepted record is locally checkable at the level $\ell$ it declares, and no
  430: stronger level is silently inferred.
  431: 
  432: If a check fails, the verifier stops at a finite test.  The bad subrecord is the
  433: finite collection of fields read by that failed test: for example, $K$ and
  434: $\Gamma$ for a gate-domain failure, $E$ for an evidence-surface failure, $K$,
  435: $\Gamma$, $R$, and the cited evidence item for a gate-record failure, $H$ for a
  436: promotion failure, $K$ and $D$ for a discovery-field failure, or $\ell$ and $B$
  437: for an evidence-level failure.  This subrecord is independent of any surrounding
  438: paragraph that may have described the claim.\qed

  511: \begin{proposition}[Deterministic claim-kind dispatch]
  512: \label{prop:claim-kind-dispatch}
  513: For every load-bearing manuscript claim whose kind lies in the seven-kind domain
  514: above, $\Gamma$ assigns a unique finite set of deterministic gates.  In a
  515: pipeline that is gate-sound for acceptance, a mixed load-bearing claim may be
  516: used as accepted work only if its acceptance entry records passage through every
  517: gate in the union assigned to its component kinds.
  518: \end{proposition}
  519: 
  520: \begin{proof}
  521: The seven atomic clauses in the definition of $\Gamma$ assign one explicitly named
  522: finite gate set to each atomic claim kind.  Hence an atomic claim has a unique
  523: assigned gate set.  For a mixed claim, the component-kind set is finite because
  524: it is a subset of the seven-kind domain; taking the union of the corresponding
  525: finite gate sets is deterministic and finite.  Gate-soundness for acceptance
  526: requires the acceptance entry of a load-bearing claim to record passage through
  527: every gate assigned by $\Gamma$.  If a component gate of a mixed claim were
  528: omitted, then the projection of the claim to that component kind would lack its
  529: assigned pass record.  Such an entry would not be gate-sound.  Therefore accepted
  530: mixed claims in a gate-sound pipeline must pass every gate in the union.\qed
  531: \end{proof}
