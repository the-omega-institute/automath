 2982: \begin{theorem}[Bounded software-audit interface under record-gate soundness]
 2983: \label{thm:publication-safety-interface}
 2984: Consider a theory-to-paper pipeline with the operational interface above and a
 2985: finite submitted record surface consisting only of the manuscript source, printed
 2986: tables, submitted manifests, uploaded review-bundle records, recorded logs, and
 2987: explicitly declared historical boundaries.  Assume the following four distinct
 2988: finite-record hypotheses.
 2989: \begin{enumerate}
 2990: \item \emph{Acceptance entries}: every load-bearing claim in an active manuscript
 2991: is either rejected or has an acceptance entry with an evidence surface inside
 2992: this finite submitted record surface.
 2993: \item \emph{Record-gate soundness}: writing $S$ for this finite submitted record
 2994: surface and $L$ for the acceptance ledger, the package satisfies the finite
 2995: predicate $\mathsf{RGS}_{S}(P,L,\Gamma)$ of
 2996: Definitions~\ref{def:operational-acceptance-interface} and
 2997: \ref{def:record-gate-soundness-external-interface} as proved equivalent in
 2998: Proposition~\ref{prop:record-gate-soundness-external-interface-exactness} and as
 2999: delimited by Theorem~\ref{thm:assigned-gate-soundness-audit-boundary}: every recorded
 3000: acceptance entry has the assigned passing rows and submitted evidence pointers,
 3001: every advisory output is demoted unless the deterministic gate set $\Gamma$ for
 3002: its claim kind passes, and no independent implementation-soundness,
 3003: fresh-execution, or semantic-correctness claim about the gates is inferred unless
 3004: the package also contains the corresponding source path, command, source state,
 3005: environment, exit code, and log path.
 3006: \item \emph{Discovery certificate input}: discovery claims are checked through
 3007: the pinned discovery-gate interface certified by
 3008: Lemma~\ref{lem:source-interface-extraction-correctness} and
 3009: Propositions~\ref{prop:reviewable-source-snapshot-interface},
 3010: \ref{prop:source-interface-record-file-consistency}, and
 3011: \ref{prop:source-interface-evidence-level-separation}, in particular the
 3012: separated manual/path-verified and executable finite-record levels for the
 3013: pipeline safety and main pipeline theorem labels.
 3014: \item \emph{Promotion and verifier boundary}: no inactive intake seed is treated
 3015: as publication work without human promotion, and the constructed tuple below is
 3016: submitted to the finite schema verifier rather than being identified with the
 3017: acceptance entry itself.
 3018: \end{enumerate}
 3019: Then, and only under these finite-record hypotheses, the package checker has the
 3020: following bounded software-audit invariant.  The checker input is the submitted surface
 3021: $S$, the ledger $L$, the dispatch table $\Gamma$, and the schema files and bundle
 3022: records named in Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-certificate-schema-verifier}.  Its
 3023: output is the finite accepted set
 3024: \[
 3025: \mathsf{Out}_{\Gamma,S}(L)=
 3026: \{(c,\mathsf{Cert}_{\Gamma}(c)):\ V(c,\mathsf{Cert}_{\Gamma}(c))=1\}
 3027: \]
 3028: together with the finite list of rejected bad subrecords.  For every accepted
 3029: load-bearing claim $c$ it constructs, from the acceptance entry and the submitted
 3030: finite evidence surfaces, a record
 3031: \[
 3032: \mathsf{Cert}_{\Gamma}(c)=\mathcal C(c)=(K,\ell,E,H,R,D,B)
 3033: \]
 3034: accepted by the finite schema verifier of
 3035: Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-certificate-schema-verifier} with the input--predicate--output
 3036: boundary of Theorem~\ref{thm:verifier-input-output-contract}; equivalently, by the
 3037: schema-relative finite-record exactness of Theorem~\ref{thm:record-acceptance-exactness}, the
 3038: constructed record satisfies the schema-relative record-acceptance semantics for
 3039: the dispatch table $\Gamma$.  Consequently, within the submitted finite records
 3040: and under the stated explicit record-gate-soundness hypothesis, $c$ has a recorded evidence
 3041: surface, a declared evidence boundary, and a prior pass record for every gate
 3042: required for its claim kind.  Accepted discovery claims additionally inherit the
 3043: bounded pinned source-interface consequences that statement-only, code-only,
 3044: mechanical-expansion, and compression-only outputs are not accepted as discovery
 3045: without the required certificate, ledger, and lineage witnesses.  Moreover, the
 3046: constructed record is axis-and-level invariant and sharp for the stronger-reading
 3047: preorder: if $c\preceq_{\Gamma}c^{+}$ is a properly stronger reading that moves
 3048: a load-bearing consequence from one architecture axis of
 3049: Definition~\ref{def:architecture-axis-map} to another, raises the evidence level
 3050: from path-verified to command-run strength, adds an implementation, artifact,
 3051: venue, or semantic-correctness assertion, or adds a theorem-obligation
 3052: completeness assertion, then $c^{+}$ is accepted by the same interface if and only
 3053: if the submitted record surface contains the corresponding finite extension
 3054: coordinates and those records pass the assigned gates.  If those coordinates are
 3055: absent, the accepted certificate is maximal below $c^{+}$: it still accepts $c$
 3056: and rejects the stronger reading by a finite bad subrecord.
 3057: 
 3058: The dependency order for this sharpness assertion is made explicit in the
 3059: finite-record certificate calculus spine,
 3060: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-record-certificate-calculus-spine}, and in
 3061: Proposition~\ref{prop:publication-safety-dependency-normal-form}: construction
 3062: and schema-relative exactness first, obstruction classification second,
 3063: extension-budget necessity and sufficiency third, and maximality last.  The
 3064: maximality of this constructed certificate is stated separately in
 3065: Proposition~\ref{prop:publication-safety-maximality}, and the exact finite price
 3066: of any genuine strengthening is classified by
 3067: Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-extension-budget-normal-form}; the current-package
 3068: instantiation is stated separately in
 3069: Corollary~\ref{cor:publication-safety-current-package-instance} and refined by
 3070: Theorem~\ref{thm:current-package-certificate-exactness}.  The concrete boundary
 3071: readings most likely to be mistaken for stronger workflow, script, source,
 3072: artifact, inventory, or support claims are classified uniformly in
 3073: Theorem~\ref{thm:named-boundary-positive-coordinate-classification}, and the
 3074: current five-coordinate skeleton is proved not to glue into implementation,
 3075: semantic, artifact, proof-assistant, daemon, or venue soundness by
 3076: Theorem~\ref{thm:current-boundary-skeleton-non-gluing}.
 3077: 
 3078: The conclusion is therefore an audit-interface theorem rather than an end-to-end
 3079: pipeline-correctness theorem.  It proves only that the finite submitted records
 3080: determine the accepted certificate tuples and the finite bad-subrecord reports
 3081: under the declared record-gate predicate.  It does not prove, unless separately
 3082: added as extension coordinates, gate implementation soundness, Lean/BEDC rebuild
 3083: correctness, artifact semantics, daemon behavior, upload, archive availability,
 3084: venue compliance at upload time, or venue acceptance.  The separate
 3085: implementation-soundness upgrade, if desired, is exactly the extension problem
 3086: classified in Theorem~\ref{thm:gate-implementation-soundness-upgrade-obstruction}.
 3087: \end{theorem}
