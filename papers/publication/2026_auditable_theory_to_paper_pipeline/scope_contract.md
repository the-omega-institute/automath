# Scope Contract

## In Scope

- auditable theory-to-paper workflows for formal mathematics;
- separation of source development, intake packets, active paper tracks, and
  submission packs;
- deterministic source, theorem, artifact, overlap, and publication gates;
- AI-assisted drafting and review as advisory activity;
- four automath/newmath case studies.

## Out Of Scope

- new automated theorem-proving method;
- claim that AI output is proof evidence;
- complete BEDC theory exposition;
- Rule110 universality or full dynamic artifact validation;
- automated journal acceptance.

## Hard Requirements

- Every load-bearing source claim must have a source path.
- Every command-result claim must have a rerun log or be phrased as historical
  or pinned-source evidence.
- Every case study must state the gate, observed issue, and safe lesson.
- The manuscript must preserve the difference between human promotion and
  daemon-visible active paper tracks.
