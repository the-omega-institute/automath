\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

An explicit affine permutation converts each finite-window Zeckendorf fiber
multiplicity into a Fibonacci-lag difference of the indexed partition function.
This identification transfers the full pressure sequence $(p_q)_{q\ge 0}$ to
the collision moments and gives an independent finite-window realization of
each partition constant $\lambda_q$ ($q\ge 2$) as the Perron root of a
nonnegative integer matrix; Sanna had already established the Perron-root and
algebraic-integer conclusions.  The
asynchronous construction has a $k^q$ raw product bound and a
$\binom{k+q-1}{q}$ permutation quotient, where
$k=|Q|(2^{L+1}-1)$; the latter is polynomial in $q$ only for a fixed
transducer.  The same pressure data assemble a closed canonical/microcanonical
formalism: the adjacent pressure
slopes $\Delta_q$ select the canonical thickness bands, the zero-temperature
limit recovers $D_m^{1/m}\to\sqrt{\varphi}$, and the integer-order R\'enyi
entropy rates of the visible law are the $q=1$ projection of the same pressure
geometry.

Section~\ref{sec:chebotarev} extends Sanna's Table~1 beyond $p=8$ with a
finite audited arithmetic window $q=9,\dots,17$, in which the recurrence
polynomials of $S_q(m)$ are computationally certified to be irreducible with
full symmetric Galois groups.
For the four-element block $q\in\{12,13,14,15\}$, the same audit indicates
linear disjointness of the splitting fields and the resulting product
Chebotarev density.  Exact root isolation throughout $q=9,\dots,17$ further
identifies a negative real subdominant root of each displayed polynomial.
Conditional on the audited identification of those polynomials as the minimal
recurrences of the actual sequences, this gives an eventually alternating
second-order law for every collision-moment sequence in that window.
Extension of the arithmetic and archimedean certificates beyond
$q=17$ remains open.

\section*{Reproducibility}
The tables, certificates, and numerical values in this paper are generated by
the scripts in \path{artifacts/}.  The verified scripts are:
\path{artifacts/generate_sequence_data.py}, which regenerates the indexed
sequence data and its hash; \path{artifacts/verify_max_fibre_matches_sibling.py},
which compares the maximum fibre with the sibling sequence; and
\path{artifacts/verify_minimal_polynomial_premise.py}, which checks the two
halves of the minimal-polynomial premise.  The script
\path{artifacts/verify_moments_match_sibling.py} checks the moment/sibling
identities and the displayed cubic premise; \path{artifacts/verify_partition_difference.py}
checks the partition difference, exponential growth limit, and pressure
summary; and \path{artifacts/verify_per_step_rule_defect.py} exhibits the
per-step rule defect on a colliding pair.  The script
\path{artifacts/verify_polynomial_certificates.py} validates the polynomial
certificates, discriminants, and recorded hash; \path{artifacts/verify_sanna_galois_groups.py}
checks the Galois-group computations for the \(q=9,\dots,17\) window and
Sanna's own rows; and
\path{artifacts/verify_sanna_table_continuation.py} checks the continuation of
the recurrence table and its leading-coefficient pattern.  Finally,
\path{artifacts/verify_secondary_spectrum.py} isolates the secondary roots and
reports the certified spectral gap.  These finite algebraic and numerical
checks corroborate, but do not replace, the proofs or the conditional
identification of the audited recurrences with the underlying sequences.
