# Decision record: Fibonacci Quarterly

Date: 2026-05-01

Manuscript:
Finite-window rigidity in Fibonacci numeration

Venue:
The Fibonacci Quarterly

Decision:
Desk rejected.

Editor note summary:
The Editor-in-Chief wrote that the results are more aligned with a journal dealing with dynamical systems or related areas, and that the journal's readers and referees do not work much in this area.

Follow-up with Steven J. Miller:
The author explained that the manuscript had been submitted through the standard FQ portal after the earlier JNT recommendation. Prof. Miller replied that, since it had already been submitted and reviewed in that queue, it was too late for him to intervene further. He said a late Fibonacci conference abstract could be tried by emailing fibconf22@gmail.com and copying him, but he did not have time to look further at the manuscript.

Operational conclusion:
Do not resubmit this manuscript to FQ as a revised version.

Do not place this manuscript back into Stage A for immediate journal retargeting. Its core claims overlap heavily with the DCDS-A submission `2026_sharp_three_window_threshold_fibonacci_conjugacy_dcds`, including the m >= 3 reconstruction threshold, finite-memory conjugacy/recovery, Fischer-cover presentation, and residue-window decoding. While that DCDS-A article is under review, submitting this older manuscript elsewhere would risk violating the no-closely-similar-submission certification.

Recommended handling:
- Treat the FQ/JNT path for this manuscript as closed.
- Keep this folder as a submitted/rejected historical record.
- If an FQ-facing project is desired later, create a new, non-overlapping number-theoretic short note from genuinely distinct material, such as a pure Zeckendorf digit-sum, identity, enumeration, or conference-expository result. Do not base it on the same finite-window rigidity/conjugacy package unless the DCDS-A status and overlap are resolved first.
