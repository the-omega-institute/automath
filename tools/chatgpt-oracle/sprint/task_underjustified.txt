REPAIR 2. papers/publication/2026_brocot_condensation_critical_fibonacci_renewal_tams/sec_finite_size_crossover.tex

Around line 205-212 the proof says aperiodicity and analyticity imply that "uniformly for y near
one, the only dominant pole is the simple root z = z(y) of yP(z) = 1 near one", and that residue
extraction gives the displayed V_j(y) asymptotic "uniformly in that neighborhood". The text then
substitutes y = e^{i lambda / sqrt j}, a complex parameter that moves with j.

For the coefficient asymptotic along a moving complex parameter, what is needed is uniform
separation of the principal root from the other zeros of 1 - y P(z), plus a uniform bound on the
remaining contour contribution. The local expansion of z(y) that the proof gives does not by
itself supply that.

Add the missing step. Aperiodicity gives the strict inequality |P(z)| < P(|z|) for z off the
positive real axis, which is exactly what separates the principal root on the relevant circle;
make that explicit, state the resulting uniform separation on the compact parameter range in
use, and note the uniform bound on the remaining contour. This is a short standard argument, not
a new result, so keep it to a few sentences.
