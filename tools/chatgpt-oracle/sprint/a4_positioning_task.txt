 1. ALBAYRAK AND BELL ALREADY HAVE THE REGULAR INTEGER-BASE SHADOW OF OUR THEOREM. Their paper is
    "Quantitative estimates for the size of an intersection of sparse automatic sets",
    arXiv:2304.09223 (2023). I verified the abstract directly: they give a quantitative
    Cobham-Semenov theorem for sparse automatic sets, showing that the intersection of a sparse
    k-automatic subset of N^d and a sparse l-automatic subset of N^d is finite, WITH A SIZE THAT
    CAN BE EXPLICITLY BOUNDED in terms of automaton data, for multiplicatively independent k and
    l. Taking the two sets equal gives a simultaneous sparse-automatic finiteness statement. A
    slender regular representation language is sparse. So their result contains the regular
    integer-base case of our Theorem 3.2, and it is strictly stronger there, because we conclude
    finiteness without any quantitative bound.
