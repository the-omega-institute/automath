CONTEXT. The referee has now accepted the arbitrary-order law-by-law nonnegative tail-defect
decomposition (Theorem 4.27) as meeting the tier threshold, and the proof has been closed
out. He then listed five things, any ONE of which would be a substantial further
improvement, and said what they have in common:

  "Those would make the mechanism itself, rather than one highly successful implementation
   of it, the central theorem."

His list:
  1. an abstract kernel theorem encompassing stable, Gaussian, Poisson and other
     polynomial-tail kernels;
  2. an extension from KL to a natural class of smooth f-divergences;
  3. a robustness result showing that reasonable changes of cutoff or proxy convention
     alter E_{r,s} only by o(s^{-2r});
  4. a partial uniqueness or minimality characterization of the positive tail-jet proxy;
  5. an optimal Gaussian-endpoint formulation identifying exactly which moment assumption
     makes the proxy entropy finite at each r.

PURSUE 3, AND THEN 4 IF 3 SUCCEEDS. They are the nearest targets and they are linked:
robustness is the weak form of the uniqueness statement, and together they answer the
strongest remaining objection to this construction, namely that the proxy is an arbitrary
convention chosen to make the theorem work.

Note that item 4 is exactly the claim we were just required to DELETE. The manuscript now
states expressly that the proxy is neither unique nor canonical, because we had not proved
it. Item 4 is the way to earn a weaker, honest version of that statement back. If you prove
a partial minimality or uniqueness result, state precisely what it does and does not
establish, and do not reinstate any blanket canonicity language.

TARGET 3, precisely. Identify the conventions the construction currently fixes by choice
rather than by necessity - at least the hard cutoff |x| > s defining tau_s and V_s, the
particular truncation scale, and the form of the proxy
A_s^lambda = 1 + C_{r-1,s} + V_s^lambda - tau_s^lambda. Then prove that replacing them by a
reasonable alternative in a clearly specified admissible class changes E_{r,s} only by
o(s^{-2r}), so that the exact vanishing criterion, and the Hessian constant
Q_{alpha,d,r}(Delta_r), are unchanged. Define the admissible class explicitly; a robustness
theorem whose admissible class is vacuous or tailored to one alternative is worth nothing.
Smooth cutoffs and a scale change s -> c s are the obvious members it must contain.
