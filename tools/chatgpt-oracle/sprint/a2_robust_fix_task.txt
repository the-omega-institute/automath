VERDICT. Both propositions are correct as stated; he sees no false identity, no unjustified

(1) EXTEND TO A PAIR OF PROFILES - free, the proof already works.
    Allow chi_s^nu and chi_s^eta to DIFFER, provided both obey the same fixed constants a, b.
    The referee verified the proof carries: || A_s^nu - A_s^eta ||_1 <= 2(tau^nu + tau^eta) =
    o(s^{-r}), each remainder estimate unchanged, and the profiles need not cancel because the
    retained pieces themselves have o(s^{-r}) total mass. Restate the robustness proposition
    with a pair (chi^nu, chi^eta), both uniformly supported between the same fixed scaled
    radii.

(2) MULTIPLICATIVE NORMALIZATION - the most natural convention a reader will ask about.
    The alternative
        A_s^lambda = (1 + C_{r-1,s} + V_{s,chi}^lambda + s^{-r} H_s) / (1 + tau_{s,chi}^lambda)
    is not covered. The difference from the additive proxy carries a term proportional to
    tau_{s,chi}^lambda V_{s,chi}^lambda, whose L^1 mass is very small, but V need not be
    uniformly bounded, so the present L-infinity-based perturbation lemma does not compare the
    two proxy entropies at order s^{-2r}. He sees no counterexample and expects a scope gap,
    but it needs a separate entropy-level comparison.
    Do ONE of these, honestly:
      (a) prove D_KL(additive) - D_KL(multiplicative) = o(s^{-2r}); or
      (b) state explicitly, in the proposition and in the introduction, that "proxy convention"
          currently means ADDITIVE MASS NORMALIZATION.
    Attempt (a). If it does not close cleanly, do (b) and say plainly that the multiplicative
    case is open. Do not blur the two.

(3) TURN THE ORIGIN RESTRICTION INTO DEMONSTRATED SHARPNESS - this is the most valuable item.
    A retention profile that stays positive at the origin is NOT a harmless exclusion. If a
    smooth profile equals a constant kappa in (0,1] on a neighbourhood of zero and then rises
    to one, then for compactly supported nu, eta matching below order r, for large s every x/s
    lies in that neighbourhood, so tau_{s,chi}^lambda = kappa and V_{s,chi}^lambda = kappa
    q_s^lambda, and the energy changes at order s^{-2r}.
    Put this example in the manuscript, right after the admissible class, as an explicit
    demonstration that the class boundary is mathematically necessary rather than
    proof-engineering. It converts an apparent limitation into a sharpness statement, and it
    is the direct answer to any reader who suspects the class was gerrymandered.

(4) DO NOT OVERCLAIM THE CLASS. It is genuinely broad among positive, fixed-smoothing-scale
    tail/core separations, but it is NOT a theorem about every reasonable proxy normalization.
    Ensure no wording in the abstract, introduction or proposition suggests it exhausts
    reasonable conventions.

(5) DO NOT CALL THE SECOND PROPOSITION ITEM 4 OR A UNIQUENESS THEOREM. The referee is
    explicit: it is materially weaker than uniqueness or entropy minimality, and should be
    described as ASYMPTOTIC COEFFICIENT RIGIDITY INSIDE A PRESCRIBED ANSATZ. He notes the
    manuscript largely observes the distinction already - make it unambiguous everywhere,
    including any introduction or abstract phrasing. The blanket non-uniqueness disclaimer
