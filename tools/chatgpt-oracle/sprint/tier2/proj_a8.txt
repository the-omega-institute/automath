=== THE DEFECT, stated so you do not soften it ===

Proposition A.8 accepts a path only when the current-step outputs c_1, ..., c_q of the q copies are
literally equal. The referee's objection: the commitment boundaries of different copies need not be
synchronized. Two runs can eventually have the same total output while emitting different amounts
of output at the current input step. A bounded terminal-output length bounds the possible LAG; it
does not make that lag ZERO. So one copy may produce a nonempty c_1 while another still holds the
corresponding symbols in its residual or terminal buffer, and the step-wise equality test rejects
runs whose completed visible outputs are in fact equal.

Consequence, as the referee puts it: as written, A.8 does not prove that accepted paths are in
bijection with colliding q-tuples, and therefore Theorem 5.2, the symmetric quotient in Theorem 5.3,
and the claimed polynomial-size matrix realization are NOT established.

Recognize the shape: a condition that is correct for the completed run is imposed at every single
step. This project's recurring error is a bound proved for one step and used for the iteration;
this is its mirror image.
