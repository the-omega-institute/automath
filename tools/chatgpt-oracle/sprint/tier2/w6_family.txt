WHAT TO ADD. I computed the following and each is reproducible by the scripts already in artifacts/
(verify_refinement_family.py, verify_involution_mechanism.py, verify_admissible_dimensions.py).

1. THE SWEEP. For 3 <= m <= 16, the coarsest equitable refinement of the fold partition of Q_m is the
   DISCRETE partition except at m = 3, 6, 8, 9. At those four the cells are singletons and pairs,
   2^{m-1} singletons and 2^{m-2} pairs, and the discarded sector has dimension exactly 2^{m-2} and
   carries exactly the adjacency spectrum of Q_{m-2}. So the paper's m = 6 result is one instance of a
   uniform structure, not an isolated computation.

2. THE INVOLUTION. In all four cases the pairing is the affine involution that swaps two coordinates
   i < j and complements both: on (x_i, x_j) it exchanges 00 with 11 and fixes 01 and 10, which
   yields exactly 2^{m-2} two-element orbits and 2^{m-1} fixed points.

3. WHY THOSE m, AND WHY FINITELY MANY. Such a sigma changes the binary value by plus or minus
   2^{m-i} + 2^{m-j} or leaves it fixed, so it is invisible to the fold precisely when that sum is a
   Fibonacci number lying far enough beyond the window that adding it does not carry into the visible
   Zeckendorf digits. Below F_90 only four Fibonacci numbers are sums of two distinct powers of two:
   F_4 = 3, F_5 = 5, F_9 = 34, F_12 = 144. Each pins the coordinates to i = m - a and j = m - b, which
   bounds m from below by a + 1 so both fit in the window, and from above by the carry condition.
   The result is F_4 none, F_5 gives m = 3, F_9 gives m = 6, F_12 gives m = 8 and 9. Finiteness is a
   consequence of that scarcity, not of how far the search ran. I extended the sweep to m = 22 by
   testing only the candidate pairs and the set is unchanged.

HOW IT SHOULD ENTER. As motivation and a proposition with the computation as evidence, NOT as a
claimed classification. Be exact about what is proved and what is checked:
 - PROVED: the form of sigma, that its weight sum must be Fibonacci for the fold to be preserved, and
   that only four Fibonacci numbers are sums of two distinct powers of two below F_90;
 - CHECKED: that the admissible set is exactly {3, 6, 8, 9} for m up to 22.
State plainly that the upper bound on m is not available in closed form - F_5 works at k - m = 2
while F_9 and F_12 fail there and need k - m at least 3, because a smaller Fibonacci number cannot
carry as far - so the classification is verified rather than proved. Do NOT paper over that.

TWO THINGS NOT TO CLAIM. This is not a minimal-dimension result: the first non-equitable dimension is
3, not 6, so do not suggest m = 6 is minimal. And do not assert that no m beyond 22 qualifies.
