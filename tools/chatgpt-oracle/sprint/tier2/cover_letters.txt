1. single_primitive -> Discrete Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science, as a short note.
   Lead: fixed-degree carry automata for the Zeckendorf fold, the losslessness of the bounded
   signed-Fibonacci carry automaton, the exact recurrence S_2(m) = 2S_2(m-1) + 2S_2(m-2) - 2S_2(m-3),
   and the pairing identity S_2(m) = T_2(m) + 2S_2(m-2).
   DO NOT CLAIM: that losslessness is a novel method. The referee's verdict is that it is substantive
   for correctness but routine as research novelty, and that the contribution is an explicit and
   economical automaton rather than the discovery that a finite automaton exists. Also do not claim
   the ordinary-integer interval-sum recurrence - that is Chow-Jones, cited in the paper.

2. window6 -> The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics.
   Lead: the unique minimal equitable refinement of the folded partition of Q_6, 48 cells, and the
   classification of the dimensions m in {3,6,8,9} at which a nontrivial equitable hidden realization
   exists at all.
   DO NOT CLAIM: that m = 6 is minimal - the first non-equitable dimension is 3. Do not present the
   four-element Fibonacci list as proved here; it is a specialization of Bugeaud-Cipu-Mignotte and the
   paper says so.

3. cubical_stokes -> Results in Mathematics.
   Lead: the sharp box readout theorem, which is what the paper stands on.
   DO NOT CLAIM: novelty for the global patching result. Under the corrected sink-connected network

4. fibonacci_folding -> Dynamical Systems (Taylor and Francis).
   Lead: for every m >= 3 the sliding code is a topological conjugacy onto its image and its causal

   DO NOT CLAIM: a general method or a class-level theorem containing the Fibonacci example. The
   referee is explicit that the paper does not have one. The bounded-delay lemma still excludes
   Fibonacci folding and is orientation only.

5. joukowsky -> a specialist journal in complex analysis or potential theory; propose one and say why.
