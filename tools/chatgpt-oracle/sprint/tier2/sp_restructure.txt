=== WHAT THE REFEREE SAID, so you do not re-derive it weakly ===

Verdict: reject, and explicitly NOT a major-revision problem. The advertised L0/L1/L2 "non-collapse"
is not a separation theorem. The three levels concern different objects, use different primitives,
and impose unrelated structures; there is no common system U carrying L0, L1, L2 structure and no
natural forgetful maps L2 -> L1 -> L0. L0 is only a cited existence result, not proved here. L1 is
decode / do ordinary integer arithmetic / encode, which the manuscript itself admits is
value-transported with no digit-local arithmetic content. Theorem 1.5 is therefore vocabulary, not
mathematics.

  - the fixed-q carry automata;

    (that the construction discards no accepting path) as substantive and NOT implied by the growth
    estimate; it is the real content behind the matrix T_q, and the primitivity argument after it is
    nontrivial too;
  - the exact second-moment recurrence;
  - a restrained discussion of nonuniformity in q.
Target venue: Discrete Mathematics & Theoretical Computer Science.

Must NOT appear in the new paper: the EML / Richardson material, the L0/L1/L2 hierarchy language in
any form, and the arbitrary-cover construction. Theorem 4.27 is to be a concise corollary of the
