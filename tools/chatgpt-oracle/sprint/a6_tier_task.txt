TARGET: Arithmetic critical stable-renewal theorem for Fibonacci partitions.

With alpha := sigma_0 - 1 in (1,2) and the critical letter law
    P{(C,H) = (c(p/q), log q)} = q^{-sigma_0},
so that P(C = 2d+1) = b_{2d+1}(sigma_0), prove:

    (T1)  b_{2d+1}(sigma_0) ~ b_C d^{-sigma_0}   as d -> infinity,
    (T2)  P(C > x) = sum_{q>=2} q^{-sigma_0} #{1 <= p < q : (p,q)=1, c(p/q) > x}
                   ~ K_C x^{1-sigma_0},
    with  K_C = ((2 sigma_0 - 1)/(sigma_0 - 1)) b_C.

The constant b_C MUST be given by an explicitly displayed absolutely convergent "one large
partial quotient" context sum. It may NOT be defined only as the limit in (T1). Take the
exact statement, notation and constants from his report, not from this paraphrase.

THE ANTI-CHEATING CONDITION IS HIS, NOT MINE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE POINT. He states that this
becomes genuinely tier-raising ONLY if the hard arithmetic tail estimate is proved as a

is then applied on top of it. If you find yourself assuming regular variation of the cost
tail anywhere, you have not done the task. The exact renewal identity, the critical law and
the boundary weights are already in the manuscript; what is missing is the arithmetic input,
