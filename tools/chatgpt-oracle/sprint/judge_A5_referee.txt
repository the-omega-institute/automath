An external expert reviewed this paper as an ETDS referee. His report is at artifacts/oracle_referee_A5.md. Verdict: reject and resubmit — not minor revision, and not ordinary major revision either.

He is clear that the open-Perron-interval main theorem is NOT the problem. He traced the dependency chain — fixed label and determinant coordinates, binary Adams-Mobius sparsification, algebraic collision lifting, rational coboundary degree bound, characterwise elimination, Fourier inversion — and found Theorem 3.16's cross-base-graph step closed, with the finite radial determination result likely genuinely new.

Two things must be fixed.

1. A MATHEMATICAL DEFECT, not a presentational one. The paper uses the Perron-ENDPOINT class Euler profile as the object of its main counterexample and of several conclusions, but under the paper's own definitions that quantity is NOT DEFINED AS A FINITE VALUE. His instruction: the endpoint-class profile assertion must be deleted or properly regularised. Work out exactly which statements depend on it, then either give a definition under which it is finite and prove the statements hold, or remove those statements and any conclusion resting on them. Do not paper over this — a referee has now seen it, and it is the kind of defect that sinks a resubmission if it reappears.

2. PRIORITY COMPARISON MISSING. Direct prior literature on Euler products over a fixed Frobenius class, and on rationality of algebraic Mahler solutions, has not been compared. He says this bears on the boundary of our core originality. Find that literature, state exactly what it proves, and rewrite the novelty claim to what survives the comparison. A sibling paper of ours was just rejected for precisely this failure, so treat it as decisive rather than cosmetic.

His summary table: open-interval main theorem — no substantial gap found, provisionally correct. Endpoint-class profile assertion — does not hold as a finite-value assertion under current definitions, must be deleted or regularised. Novelty of the core finite-sampling result — likely new, but the literature comparison needed to support priority is not done. ETDS suitability of the current version — insufficient; reasonable chance after repair, still not a safe acceptance.

Read his full report and act on everything in it. Compile with latexmk -pdfxe -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex until exit 0, and re-run artifacts/verify_a5_results.py. Do NOT git commit. Do not run pytest at the paper root.

Report at most 30 lines: which statements depended on the endpoint-class profile and what you did with each; what the prior literature actually proves and how the novelty claim now reads; anything else he raised; verifier and compile results.
