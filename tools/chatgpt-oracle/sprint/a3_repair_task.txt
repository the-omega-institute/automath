TWO STATEMENT REPAIRS. THE SECOND IS A REAL ERROR IN THE PRINTED TEXT AND IS NOT OPTIONAL.

 1. MAKE THE TERMINAL COEFFICIENT EXPLICIT. In the edge definition the body coefficients lie in
    [-D,D], but the completing coefficient c of u_m is not required by the statement to lie there.
    The proof does establish |c| = |k_t| <= K_{U,D}. So each edge is a bounded U-representation of
    zero over the mixed alphabet [-D,D]^m x [-K_{U,D}, K_{U,D}], not one with every coefficient
    bounded by D. Insert immediately after the displayed edge condition, in his words:

      "The completing coefficient is unique and necessarily satisfies |c| <= K_{U,D}."

    This removes a predictable terminology objection - "bounded representation" must not be
    readable as asserting |c| <= D.

 2. ANCHOR THE "EQUIVALENTLY" CLAUSE. AS PRINTED IT IS FALSE. The theorem passes from paths
    beginning at a vertex with nonzero first coordinate to all overlap chains merely "containing a
    nonzero exposed coefficient". He supplies an explicit counterexample built from our OWN
    nonstandard example: take U = (1,2,4,9,...), D = 2, m = 2. The graph has vertices -2,-1,0,1,2
    with an edge a -> b exactly when a + 2b = 0 mod 4, giving 0 -> 0, 0 -> 2, 2 -> 1, and 1
    terminal. No directed cycle is reachable from a nonzero vertex, since +-2 lead to +-1 and
    those are terminal. Yet for every N the chain

      0 -> 0 -> ... -> 0 -> 2 -> 1     (N zero loops)

    terminates, has arbitrarily large total length, and eventually contains a nonzero exposed
    coefficient. Replace the clause with his corrected formulation:

      "After shifting a chain so that its initial exposed coefficient is nonzero, either it
      reaches a directed cycle, or it has fewer than C(U,D) m further overlaps."

    VERIFY THE COUNTEREXAMPLE YOURSELF before rewriting - recompute the edge set for that U, D and
    m and confirm the chain exists and that no cycle is reachable from a nonzero vertex. Add it to
    the artifact battery as a regression case so the unanchored reading cannot return. If your
    computation disagrees with his, STOP and report the disagreement rather than editing.
