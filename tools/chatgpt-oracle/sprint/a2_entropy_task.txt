An external expert audited which objects OF THE FIELD this paper's machinery can reach. His
conclusion: there is NO ceiling confining this manuscript to its own constructions, and the
strongest reachable field-facing theorem is a full relative-entropy dissipation and integral-
representation theorem for two symmetric stable heat flows. He assigns it 82 percent - the
highest of any target across these papers. It concerns only fractional heat semigroups, the
fractional Laplacian, KL divergence and nonlocal Dirichlet forms, and uses none of our

DO THE PILOT CALCULATION FIRST. He supplies it deliberately as the shortest meaningful
falsification test, and you must run it BEFORE committing to the main theorem.

  Pilot lemma. Let lambda be a probability measure on R^d with finite (d+alpha)-th absolute
  moment, and set

      Q_t^lambda(y) = integral p_1(y - x/t^{1/alpha}) / p_1(y) lambda(dx).

  For every 0 < a < b < infinity prove

      0 < c_{a,b,lambda} <= Q_t^lambda(y) <= C_{a,b,lambda} < infinity,
      |grad log Q_t^lambda(y)| <= 2 ||grad log p_1||_infinity

  for all t in [a,b] and y in R^d. Then take two such measures, put u = Q^mu / Q^nu, and verify
  directly that the double integral of p_1(y) Q^nu(y) Lambda(u(y), u(z)) / |y-z|^{d+alpha} over
  R^d x R^d is finite.
