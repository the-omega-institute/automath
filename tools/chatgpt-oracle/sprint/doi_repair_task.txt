WHAT WAS FOUND. Every DOI in every bibliography under papers/publication was resolved twice - once

There are two classes and they fail differently:

  CLASS A - the DOI resolves, but to a different work. The cited paper is real; the identifier
  attached to it belongs to some unrelated article, so a reader who follows the link lands
  somewhere else. In the JST paper two entries hold each other's DOIs.

  CLASS B - the DOI does not exist at all. Same shape in almost every case: the publisher prefix
  is right and the suffix is close to a real one but wrong.

Neither class is caught by a compile, by a bibliography-parity check, or by anything else this
project currently runs, which is why they survived every review round.

YOUR JOB. For each defective entry in your scope, establish the correct DOI and correct the entry.

The audit lists a proposed replacement for most Class B entries. DO NOT TRUST IT. It came from an
automated bibliographic search and it is a lead, not a result. Verify every replacement yourself
before you write it, and verify it the same way for the entries where the audit proposes nothing:

  curl -A "mailto:alyicabhz@gmail.com" -H "Accept: application/vnd.citationstyles.csl+json" \
       https://doi.org/<candidate>

and read the returned title, author list, container and year against the bibliography entry. A DOI
is acceptable only when all four agree. Resolution alone proves nothing - that is exactly the trap
Class A represents.

Where the metadata disagrees with the bibliography entry, the entry may be the thing that is wrong:
a mis-stated year, volume, page range or journal. In that case correct the entry from the
authoritative metadata rather than hunting for a DOI that matches a wrong entry.

If you cannot establish a correct DOI for an entry, REMOVE THE doi FIELD and leave the rest of the
entry intact, and say so in your report. A citation without a DOI is complete and correct; a
citation with a wrong one is not. Never guess, never construct a DOI from a pattern, and never
leave a defective one in place because no replacement was found.

Report anything the audit got wrong. If an entry it flags is actually fine - the identifier is
