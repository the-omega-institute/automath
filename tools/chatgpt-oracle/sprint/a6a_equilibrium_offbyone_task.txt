In the proof introducing the equilibrium mass and its tail, the paper defines

    p^e_j = \overline F(j-1) / \mu

and then prints

    \overline F^e(j) = \sum_{k>j} p^e_k = (1/\mu) \sum_{r>j} \overline F(r).

Substituting r = k-1 in the middle sum: k ranges over k >= j+1, so r ranges over r >= j. The exact
identity is therefore

    \overline F^e(j) = (1/\mu) \sum_{r >= j} \overline F(r),

with a lower limit of r >= j, not r > j. The omitted term is \overline F(j)/\mu, which is
O(j^{-alpha}) against a sum of order j^{1-alpha}/(alpha-1), so it is lower order and the Karamata
asymptotic and the final constant are unaffected. That is why this is a repair and not a
recalculation.
