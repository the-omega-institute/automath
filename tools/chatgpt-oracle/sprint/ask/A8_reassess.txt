WHAT WAS DONE

Theorems A-G keep their numbering and statements; the equivalence is an ordinary numbered theorem.
For p_0 on the positive integers with mean mu_0 and equilibrium forward-recurrence law
e_p(a) = Pr_p(D > a)/mu(p), the local class keeps exactly the four restrictions you specified: a
common exponential moment, sup_p |mu(p) - mu_0| <= C N^{-1/2}, H^2(p,p_0) <= C/N, and
H^2(e_p, e_{p_0}) <= C/N. The observation is the stationary lattice-renewal indicator on
{0,...,N-1}; for fixed gamma in (1/2,1), m_N = floor(N/mu_0 - N^gamma) and Q_{N,p} = p^{tensor m_N}.
The randomizations may use p_0, N and gamma but not the unknown p, and no global adaptive
equivalence over an unknown compact centre is claimed.

With K_N the number of complete observed interarrivals and r_N = ceil(2 N^gamma), the two
deficiency bounds are delta(E_N, F_N) <= sup_p P_{N,p}{K_N < m_N} = o(1) and
delta(F_N, E_N) <= sup_p Pr_p{L_N > m_N + r_N} + sqrt(C(1+r_N)/N) = o(1), the second via
H^2(e_p tensor p^{tensor r_N}, e_{p_0} tensor p_0^{tensor r_N}) <= H^2(e_p,e_{p_0}) + r_N H^2(p,p_0)
<= C(1+r_N)/N. Hence Delta(E_N, F_N) -> 0 uniformly.

Your boundary-cycle warning was addressed head-on rather than avoided. The extracted cycles are
NOT claimed i.i.d. conditional on fitting inside the window. Instead the record is coupled to an
unconditioned equilibrium construction carrying latent i.i.d. Palm cycles; the kernel's output
equals the first m_N latent cycles on {K_N >= m_N} and may differ only on the complement, and
since K_N = N/mu(p) + O_P(sqrt N) uniformly while the undershoot is N^gamma with gamma > 1/2, that
exceptional probability tends uniformly to zero. Both kernels are Borel on the entire sample
space, including the all-zero record, which the record-to-i.i.d. kernel handles by an independent
p_0-fallback and the i.i.d.-to-record kernel by returning the all-zero indicator when the initial
delay already exceeds the window.

The theorem transfers bounded-loss procedures and local bounded-loss minimax risks, giving
I_cal(theta_0) = mu_0^{-1} I_Palm(theta_0) for admissible uniformly DQM submodels and the
stationary-record efficiency bound mu_0 E_{p_0}{phi(D)^2} under root-N normalization.
