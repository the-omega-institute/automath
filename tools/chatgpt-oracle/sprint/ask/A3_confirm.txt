The theorem has three parts.

Then there is a constant \(C_U<\infty\), effectively computable from the recurrence, the canonical digit set, and the bounded-zero normalization automaton, such that for every \(m\ge2\),

\[
\Phi_{U,m}\text{ injective}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\ell_{\rm cau}(U,m)\le C_Um.
\]

Equivalently, if no first-coordinate-nonzero state of the difference graph \(\Gamma_{U,m}\) reaches a directed cycle, then every directed path from that initial state set has length at most \(C_Um-1\).

(A2) For \(\theta\) the real root of \(x^3-2x^2+x-1\), every \(m\ge4\) is injective and

\[
\ell_{\rm cau}(\theta,m)=2\left\lfloor\frac m2\right\rfloor-1.

(A3) Writing \(\lambda_m=2\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1\), the terminal collision sets are classified exactly by

\[
\mathcal N_{\theta,m,\lambda_m-1}=\{E_m,-E_m\}\ne\varnothing,
