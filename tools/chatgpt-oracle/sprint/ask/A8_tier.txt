Here is the model and the present theorem package, stated so that the target you propose can be judged against the machinery actually available.

The standing hypothesis is a stationary two-state D-MAP with hidden states R and D, positive rates Gamma and kappa_r, and sampling interval Delta tau > 0. Put

  p = exp(-Gamma Delta tau),   s = exp(-kappa_r Delta tau),

  b = integral_0^{Delta tau} kappa_r exp(-kappa_r r) exp[-Gamma(Delta tau-r)] dr,

  a = integral_0^{Delta tau} kappa_r exp(-kappa_r r){1-exp[-Gamma(Delta tau-r)]} dr,

and

  T_0 = [[p,0],[b,s]],   T_1 = [[0,1-p],[0,a]].

The convention is that (T_i)_{XY}=P(A_n=i,H_{n+1}=Y | H_n=X). The structural hypothesis T_1(R,R)=T_1(D,R)=0 means that every click leaves the counter latched in D at the sampling boundary. The paper derives these kernels from that explicit latched protocol, but does not claim that they describe generic D-MAP(2), generic dead-time detectors, or a detector allowing post-click same-bin recovery. If either killed entry is positive, a click need not regenerate at D and the renewal, quotient, and retained-cycle arguments do not follow.

The main results are as follows.

1. Record law (Theorem A). Under the standing hypothesis, the stationary binary output is a full-support renewal process. If G is the Palm number of zeros between consecutive clicks, rho=P(A_0=1), and lambda_hid=p-b, then

  g_k=P(G=k)=e_D^T T_0^k T_1 1,   k>=0.

For Gamma != kappa_r,

  g_k = [kappa_r(1-p)p^k-Gamma(1-s)s^k]/(kappa_r-Gamma),
  S_k=P(G>=k)=[kappa_r p^k-Gamma s^k]/(kappa_r-Gamma).

On the diagonal Gamma=kappa_r, writing x=Gamma Delta tau and p=e^{-x},

  g_k=p^k{(1-p)(1+xk)-xp},   S_k=p^k(1+xk).

The hazard g_k/S_k is strictly increasing. For n>=1,

  Cov(A_0,A_n)=rho(a-rho)lambda_hid^{n-1},

with rho(a-rho)<0. The record is exactly 1-dependent iff lambda_hid=0, is not 0-dependent, and is never a finite-order Markov chain for positive parameters. In dimensionless coordinates x=Gamma Delta tau and y=kappa_r Delta tau,

  lambda_hid=[y e^{-y}-x e^{-x}]/(y-x)

off the diagonal, with derivative value e^{-x}(1-x) on it; its sharp global range is [-e^{-2},1), with the lower endpoint only at x=y=2. The paper also proves, for fixed positive rates as Delta tau=Delta tends to zero,

  Delta E(G+1)=1/Gamma+1/kappa_r+Delta/2
    +Gamma kappa_r(Gamma+kappa_r)Delta^4/720+O(Delta^6),

and the corresponding first-order downward sampling bias in clicks per unit time.

2. Three-inclusion quotient and physical image (Theorem B and its image propositions). Let r_0=P(A_0=1), r_1=P(A_0=1,A_1=1), and r_2=P(A_0=1,A_2=1). On r_0>0 and Delta_inv=r_1-r_0^2 != 0, define

  a(r)=r_1/r_0,
  lambda(r)=(r_2-r_0^2)/(r_1-r_0^2),

  Phi(r)=(1-a(r)+lambda(r),
          r_0[1-lambda(r)]-a(r)+lambda(r)).

For the constrained model, Phi(r)=(p+s,ps). Thus three visible inclusion probabilities determine the unordered pair {p,s}, and, if Delta tau is known, the unordered rate set {Gamma,kappa_r}; they do not determine the physical labels. The exact image is also characterized. If p,s in (0,1) are the roots of z^2-Phi_1(r)z+Phi_2(r)=0, put

  C(p,s)=(s log p-p log s)/(log p-log s) for p!=s,
  C(p,p)=p(1-log p),
  E(r)=r_1/r_0-1+C(p,s).

Then r is generated by positive sampled-counter parameters iff E(r)=0. In a neighbourhood of every compact dimensionless parameter rectangle [epsilon,M]^2, this has a root-free real-analytic continuation e(r), and the positive image is exactly

  e(r)=0,   D(r)=Phi_1(r)^2-4Phi_2(r)>=0,

with rank D(e,D)=2 even on the exchange diagonal. On that compact chart r_0 and |Delta_inv| have explicit uniform positive lower bounds; away from |p-s|=0 the sorted-root and log-rate maps are uniformly stable.

3. Markovian fibres. For the physical two-state K=T_0, q_*=Gamma/kappa_r, and I_*=[min(1,q_*),max(1,q_*)], the complete fibre among two-state substochastic killed-reset kernels with the same visible law is the injective closed arc

  K(q) = [[p-b+bq, b(1-q)(q-q_*)/q],
          [bq,       s+b(1-q)]],    q in I_*.

Its endpoints are the two triangular kernels obtained by exchanging Gamma and kappa_r. Off the exchange diagonal its interior kernels and killing deficits are strictly positive and the physical triangular family meets it only at the endpoints; on the diagonal the whole fibre collapses to a singleton. For a pure serial n-phase kernel exp(Delta tau Q(theta)), with arbitrary positive rates and repetitions allowed, the first 2n Palm-tail coordinates S_0,...,S_{2n-1} recover the minimal confluent-Prony polynomial prod_j(z-exp[-theta_j Delta tau]), including multiplicities, hence the unordered rate multiset. For an unrestricted minimal interior n-state killed-reset kernel K>0 with (I-K)1>0, reset-preserving similarities M1=1 and e_n^T M=e_n^T give a local positivity-preserving visible-fibre manifold of exact dimension (n-1)^2, modulo the finite reset-preserving permutation group.

4. Fixed-setting inference (Theorems C-E). Retaining only cycles fully observed in a stationary length-N record, define the three-coordinate complete-cycle estimator and Palm rewards

  U_j=(1, 1{G_j=0}, 1{G_j=1}+1{G_j=0,G_{j+1}=0})^T,
  L_j=G_j+1,   Y_j=U_j-r L_j.

Then Y_j is centred and 1-dependent and

  sqrt(N)(rhat_N-r) = N^{-1/2} sum_{j=0}^{K_N-1}Y_j+o_P(1)
                    => N(0,Sigma_r),

  Sigma_r=mu^{-1}{Var(Y_0)+Cov(Y_0,Y_1)+Cov(Y_1,Y_0)}.

With b_N=floor(N^{1/3}), plug-in centring, and a prespecified gate M_N>=m_N where m_N tends to infinity and m_N=o(N/b_N), the separated-batch covariance estimator has operator-norm error O_P(N^{-1/3}) on an event of probability tending to one. Delta transport through Phi gives the Gaussian limit for (p+s,ps); if (p-s)^2>0, the manuscript gives the explicit sorted-root Jacobian and, for known Delta tau, the log-rate CLT. No root CLT is claimed on the exchange diagonal.

Theorem D combines (i) a truncated distributional distance from the compact null family, using all binary words up to L_N=max{3,floor[(log_2 N)/8]}, and (ii) a root-free adjacent-gap score. Its null rejection probability converges uniformly to alpha on [epsilon,M]^2, including N^{-1/4} root splittings. It is consistent against any fixed stationary ergodic binary law at positive distributional distance from the null, uniformly for geometrically mixing, uniformly separated classes. At an exchange point it supplies the explicit fixed-marginal Markov-gap alternative

  Q_eta(l|k)=g_l{1+eta h(k)h(l)},
  h(k)=1{k=1}-(g_1/g_2)1{k=2},

which preserves r_0,r_1,r_2 exactly but changes P(10101) by the factor 1+eta. Along eta_N=t/sqrt(N), the stationary-record likelihood is LAN with information I=(E_g h(G)^2)^2/mu, and the score attains the Gaussian-shift power envelope for this one-dimensional subexperiment.

There is also a compact-uniform physical-image test based on e(r)=0 and D(r)>=0. After residualization its statistic is Z_e^2+(Z_d^-)^2. At separated roots its limit is chi-square_1; on the exchange diagonal it is (chi-square_1+chi-square_2)/2 in the chi-bar-square sense. A log-N selector has compact-uniform asymptotic size at most alpha over [epsilon,M]^2, including intermediate sequences where the selector need not settle, and the test is consistent against the stated full-support renewal alternatives with e(r)!=0 or D(r)<0 and positive-definite constraint covariance.

5. Supplementary claims. At an interior exchange point, the manuscript starts from the Bickel-Kwon fixed-marginal Markov interaction space and proves that imposing preservation of r_0,r_1,r_2 removes exactly the atom direction q(0,0):

  T={q in L^2(g x g): E(q|G_0)=E(q|G_1)=0, q(0,0)=0}.

It constructs positive two-sided paths preserving g and all three inclusions exactly, obtains calendar-time information mu^{-1}Pi_T, fixed-dimensional retained-record LAN subject to an explicit endpoint condition, and a weighted infinite score test with pointwise power against every nonzero fixed direction and exchange-local null calibration.

For the equal-rate tail S_j=p_0^j(1+x_0 j), its moving Helmert layer of dimension d_J=(J+1)^2-1 has a uniform Euclidean coupling to N(0,I_{d_J}) over N^{-1/2}-local physical nulls iff

  n_N S_{J_N}^2 -> infinity,   n_N=N/mu.

In the second-order band this is equivalent to

  2x_0 J_N <= log n_N+2 log log n_N-omega_N

for some omega_N->infinity; at a fixed critical correction the corner instead has a compound-Poisson obstruction. Finally, for a deterministic product experiment of m_N(z)=floor(N/mu_z) independent complete Palm gaps and a symmetric split p=z+hN^{-1/4}, s=z-hN^{-1/4}, uniformly for z in a compact subset of (0,1) and bounded h,

  log(dP_{N,z,h}/dP_{N,z,0})
    =h^2 Delta_{N,z}-(h^4/2)I(z)+o_P(1),
  Delta_{N,z}=>N(0,I(z)).
