We have revised the manuscript in response to your correction of the proposed Theorem A. Our earlier no-go observation was that the conjunction of neutral terminal objects, terminal essential surjectivity, and \(\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathcal P)(X)=\Lambda_0\) forces \(H^1(X,A)=0\), even though that hypothesis had been listed as not worth removing. You confirmed that this no-go is correct, that the conjunction originally proposed was too strong, and you strengthened the result to a terminal torsor obstruction: stackification of a prestack is fully faithful, so an essentially surjective functor \(\mathcal P_\lambda(X)\to\mathcal L[\lambda](X)\) is an equivalence. You then supplied two consistent replacements, formulations A and B, with the warning that they must not be mixed.

The manuscript now contains the following.

* The Terminal torsor obstruction appears as a numbered theorem in your stronger form, with the fully faithful stackification argument made explicit.
* We state your qualification that “in the component \(\lambda\)” means all source objects whose images have sheaf-component \(\lambda\).
* We record the coefficient subtlety: \(\operatorname{Hom}(H_1(N,\mathbb Z),A)=0\) is weaker than, and coefficient-dependent in a way that, \(H^1(N,\mathbb Z)=0\) is not. Thus the manuscript's \(H^1=0\) hypothesis can sometimes be removed while retaining the original terminal clauses, but not when \(H^1(X,A)\ne 0\) and at least one neutral label exists. The all-non-neutral case \(\Lambda_0=\varnothing\) is unaffected.
* We adopt formulation A: terminal essential surjectivity is retained, while \(\pi_0^{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathcal P)(X)=\Lambda_0\) is abandoned in favor of a marking map whose fibers are principal homogeneous sets under \(H^1(X,A)\). For neutral \(\lambda\), we state \(\mathcal P_\lambda(X)\simeq\mathcal L[\lambda](X)\simeq\operatorname{Tors}_A(X)\), with \(\operatorname{Aut}(p)\cong H^0(X,A)\); fibers over non-neutral labels are empty.
* We record formulation B in a remark explicitly headed “not adopted,” together with your impossibility observation that, when \(\Lambda_0\ne\varnothing\), there is no third formulation retaining all three conditions.

We have not presented this correction as a tier jump, because you said that it does not materially raise the paper's tier.
