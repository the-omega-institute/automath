1. What the paper's machinery actually is

Please assess the apparatus below as a collection of input-output mechanisms, not as a list of theorems.

* Deterministic marked reset and Palm reduction. The labelled discrete kernels have every marked transition landing in one fixed post-click state. Consuming that structural reset, the paper turns the visible stationary binary record into a full-support renewal process with Palm zero-gap mass g and tail S. It then uses the exact finite-window factorization

  P(record) = mu^{-1} S(A) [product over complete gaps G_j of g(G_j)] S(R),

  where A and R are the initial age and terminal residual and the number K_N of complete gaps is random. This produces an exact likelihood for the observed stationary record rather than an assumed product experiment. If marked leakage into another state is allowed, this reduction, and everything downstream that uses it, is no longer proved.

* Serial holding-time and sampling transform. For serial rates theta_i, the absorption time is W=sum_i X_i with independent exponential X_i, and sampling produces S(k)=P(W>k Delta tau) and g(k)=S(k)-S(k+1). At a collision the first two holding times are compressed, with density factor

  (c^2-delta)e^{-cy} sinh(sqrt(delta)y)/sqrt(delta),

  analytically continued at delta=0. Conditioning complete-data scores on a sampling bin or a tail event consumes polynomial complete-data score bounds and produces relative bounds for derivatives of log g through order three and log S through order two. Log-concavity/IFR, stochastic domination by a common Erlang law, and a polynomial-exponential mass envelope make these bounds compact-uniform. This relative-score step is essential; the proof does not infer it from absolute derivative bounds.

* Random-stopping likelihood control. A uniform renewal-count argument gives K_N=N/mu+O_P(sqrt N), while the largest complete gap and both endpoint variables are O_P(log N). An Anscombe replacement, a stopped score CLT, a stopped Hessian LLN, and a third-order envelope then consume the derivative bounds above and produce compact-uniform LAN per unit calendar time. The two endpoint survival factors and mu^{-1} are o_P(1) in the local log likelihood, while the random number of complete cycles produces the calendar information factor mu^{-1}.

* Pole-order separation. The survival Laplace transform is rational. At one repeated rate lambda, the derivative in the squared half-separation delta has an exact fourth-order pole at -lambda, the centre derivative has order three there, and each separated-rate derivative has order two at its own pole. After inversion and sampling, these yield, respectively, unique k^3 e^{-lambda Delta tau k}, k^2 e^{-lambda Delta tau k}, and k e^{-theta_j Delta tau k} components. Linear independence of exponential-polynomial sequences consumes those unique bases and degrees and produces linear independence of the one-cycle scores; continuity and compactness then produce a uniform positive lower bound on Fisher information.

* Efficient residualization and plug-in. The collision score is projected off the nuisance-rate scores by the Fisher-information Schur complement. Orthogonality makes the expectation of the parameter derivative of the efficient score vanish. Together with the stopped CLT/LLN and a root-N null fit, this produces root-N equicontinuity of the efficient central sequence and consistency of the empirical Schur complement. A total measurable gate handles a fit outside the score chart, a singular empirical nuisance block, or a nonpositive empirical Schur complement. Le Cam's third lemma and the one-dimensional Gaussian Neyman-Pearson argument then transfer this to the one-sided score test and its half-space power envelope.

* Finite-tail recurrence and analytic lifting. The first 2n empirical complete-gap tails are root-N accurate. The paper solves their n by n Hankel recurrence for the annihilating polynomial, using nonsingularity even at repeated poles. A measurable finite analytic atlas, built from argument-principle contours, consumes root-N recurrence-coefficient error and produces root-N estimates of every separated root and of the first two symmetric power sums of the double-root cluster. Those symmetric quantities are lifted analytically to the centre and squared separation; projecting the latter onto [0,infinity) and taking its square root produces N^{-1/4} recovery of the colliding unordered pair while the centre and separated rates remain root-N.

* Two-point transport. The paper compares delta=0 with delta=v_0/sqrt N at a fixed collision base. LAN gives mutual contiguity and a Gaussian testing lower bound; eventual exact separation of the two rate multisets by sqrt(v_0)N^{-1/4} turns estimation success into a two-point test. This produces a positive threshold-risk lower bound and hence optimality of the N^{-1/4} rate in that pointwise two-point sense, not an exact asymptotic minimax constant over a continuum.

* Order-two population algebra. For the constrained two-state kernel, three inclusion probabilities r_0=P(A_0=1), r_1=P(A_0=A_1=1), and r_2=P(A_0=A_2=1) are converted by a rational quotient into the two elementary symmetric functions p+s and ps of the sampled survival factors. A logarithmic divided-difference identity and the discriminant then produce a local analytic image equation and its boundary chart. Reset-preserving similarities, intersected with entrywise Markov constraints, produce the explicit two-state weak-equivalence fibre within this restricted cone. Regenerative adjacent-gap rewards, calendar stopping, separated batches, and delta-method transport produce the associated fixed-setting covariance and quotient inference.

* Specification and tangent constructions. A weighted metric on all finite binary-word probabilities, truncated at a slowly growing word length, consumes empirical word frequencies and produces a complete-visible-law guard against fixed stationary ergodic alternatives (uniformly only under the stated common mixing and separation conditions). A specially constructed dependent Markov chain of Palm gaps preserves the first three inclusion coordinates but changes a length-five word, providing a local nonrenewal score direction. In the supplement, double-centred known-marginal Markov interaction scores are projected off additive nuisance scores, and Helmert/Christoffel calculations control a moving-dimensional Gaussian score. These are auxiliary to, not substitutes for, the stationary likelihood proof above.

If this description misstates what any estimate consumes or produces, please correct it before using it.

2. Separate the manuscript's objects from the field's objects

(a) Principal objects defined, specially named, or specially assembled by this manuscript for its own purposes:

* the latched “sampled-counter” protocol and its killed-reset constrained D-MAP(2) subfamily (D-MAP itself is standard; this restriction and interpretation are the manuscript's object);
* the three-inclusion vector (r_0,r_1,r_2), its quotient map Phi, the log-divided-difference image residual, discriminant chart, and the paper's “physical-image” diagnostic;
* the “hidden mode” parametrization and its correlation-phase description for that triangular kernel;
* the killed-reset weak-equivalence fibre arc and the higher-state reset-preserving similarity orbit inside the manuscript's constrained Markov cone;
* the retained-cycle inclusion estimators, reporting gates, projected plug-in word-law reconstruction, and the particular complete-visible-law test assembled in the paper;
* the collision stratum C_n(epsilon,M,c_0), its squared half-separation coordinate delta, the paper's finite-atlas recurrence estimator, and its optimal-matching rate-multiset loss;
* the coordinate-preserving Markov-gap path with the additional atom constraint used by the paper, and the paper's moving-layer Helmert score/coupling construction.

Some of these are useful constructions, but they are not thereby standard objects of the field. The squared separation is a regular quotient coordinate of a standard root collision, not a newly discovered general statistical object.

* phase-type and matrix-exponential distributions described through rational Laplace transforms, minimal realizations, poles and multiplicities, and nonunique Markovian representations;
* stationary renewal point processes and stationary binary renewal sequences, including Palm interarrival laws, equilibrium age, forward recurrence/residual life, endpoint censoring, and renewal-reward or regenerative inference;

This list deliberately does not promote the detector interpretation, KMS/Parry language, generic hidden-state physics, or periodic dead-time models merely because the manuscript cites them. The priority boundary is already partly settled and must not be reopened in the paper's favour without evidence.
