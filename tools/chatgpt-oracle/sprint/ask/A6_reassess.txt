WHAT WAS DONE

The three estimates you said were already present were each checked before being relied on, and
you were right about all three: the pointwise normalized context limit via
K(u,a,v) = a K(u) K(v) + O_{u,v}(1); the summable majorant n^s K(u,a,v)^{-s} <= 2^s {K(u)K(v)}^{-s}
for a > n/2; and the exhaustive negligibility of noncondensed configurations, split with
h = floor(n^delta) into words with at least two digits >= h, words with all digits < h, and words
with exactly one digit in [h, n/2]. What was missing was only the normalization into a probability
law, and that is what was added.

The theorem is stated entirely in the field order n = a_1 + ... + a_r, as you required. It proves
P_{n,s}{M_n > n/2} -> 1; d_TV(law(U_n,V_n), law(U,V)) -> 0 with U, V independent and masses
K(u)^{-s}/(2 rho_s) and K(v)^{-s}/rho_s; hence n - M_n -> |U|_1 + |V|_1 in total variation and
M_n/n -> 1 in probability; the explicit law of D = |U|_1 + |V|_1; the joint limit
(J_n - 1, r_n - J_n) -> (l(U), l(V)) in total variation; and q_n/(M_n K(U_n) K(V_n)) -> 1 in
probability.

The index bridge is isolated in its own proposition rather than assumed: Z_n(s) = sigma_{s/2}(n)
and b_{2d+1}(s) = sigma_{s/2}(d+1) = Z_{d+1}(s), so n = d+1 and c = 2n-1. No Brocot limit is
stated in the renewal-cost variable.

On the heavy-tailed check you singled out: the theorem is NOT an immediate corollary of
Armendariz-Loulakis conditional product laws or of Stufler-type Gibbs-partition condensation,
because the continuant weight K(a_1,...,a_r)^{-s} is globally coupled and does not factor into
digit or component weights. Dushistova, Moshchevitin-Zhigljavsky and Kessebohmer-Stratmann were
each checked and none contains the denominator-weighted probability law on the finite
fixed-digit-sum family. This is recorded as a negative search result, not as a novelty claim.

On architecture: we agreed with your judgement. The title, abstract, introduction, conclusion,
metadata and cover letter now lead with Brocot condensation; it is Theorem A, its self-contained
proof section comes immediately after the introduction and before the finite-window preliminaries,
and the Fibonacci renewal and finite-window results are presented as downstream arithmetic and
transfer consequences.

The Dushistova correction is preserved and made more explicit: the three left-context cases are
displayed as R_s + R_s + 2(R_s - 1) R_s = 2 R_s^2 against her printed R_s + 2 R_s^2, with the
numerical contrast 8 against 10 at s = sigma_0, and the finite identity

continues to disclaim pointwise local dimensions, finite-type neighbour graphs, net intervals,
