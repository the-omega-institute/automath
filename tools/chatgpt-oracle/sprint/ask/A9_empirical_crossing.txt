You have assessed this manuscript several times. Your last assessment named the target theorem - the
morphism-level descent theorem making the Cech-cocycle construction a biequivalence of 2-groupoids
rather than an equality of connected components - put the probability of proving it at 35%, and then
named the decisive ceiling. That ceiling was priority: even proved exactly as stated, the theorem
would read as a finite-polyhedral, constant-abelian-band specialization of established cocycle and
homotopy classifications of gerbes, and you cited Jardine, Breen, and Nikolaus-Waldorf. You added
the sentence that this question is about: the machinery "does not contain the source-side geometry
needed to produce a new obstruction theory about an independently given lifting problem."

WE THINK THAT VERDICT PRICED THE MANUSCRIPT AGAINST ONE FIELD, AND THAT THERE IS A SECOND FIELD IT
ALREADY TOUCHES WHERE THAT PRIORITY OBJECTION DOES NOT APPLY.

The manuscript contains Section "Boundary with empirical-model obstructions". It is a negative
result, and it is honest: it proves that for every finite empirical model in the
Abramsky-Brandenburger sense, our construction does NOT transfer. Specifically it records that
supports correspond to local sections, strong contextuality is absence of a matching family, the
Abramsky-Mansfield-Barbosa obstruction is a relative Cech class in degree ONE on the free-abelian
presheaf, and that for a strongly contextual model the relevant map has empty domain, so the
wedge-sphere realization corollary cannot reach it. The abstract states the disclaimer explicitly:
"This is a realization corollary for supplied lift data and does not transfer to bare empirical
models."

The section then lists, as its own diagnosis, exactly what a bare empirical model fails to supply:

  (i)   a finite intersection site with connected nonempty overlaps and a connected nerve;
  (ii)  a finite abelian band A;
  (iii) an A-banded prestack whose component presheaf is related to the chosen support data;
  (iv)  normalized Cech 2-cocycles, or prescribed maps H_2(N,Z) -> A, for its component labels;
  (v)   the terminal-fibre behaviour required by the comparison criterion.

and states: "The support presheaf supplies none of (ii)-(iv). It supplies a canonical answer only
after one chooses the split product stack, and that answer sets every degree-two component class to
zero. No construction established here maps bare support presheaves to the non-split data used by
the wedge corollary."

THAT IS THE GAP, AND THIS QUESTION ASKS WHETHER IT CAN BE CROSSED.

Note what is on the other side of it. In the contextuality literature the standard objects are
measurement scenarios, contexts, empirical models, supports, global sections, and the AMB
cohomological obstruction. That obstruction lives in degree one. Jardine, Breen and Nikolaus-Waldorf
have no claim on that field's objects. If the machinery here produced a degree-TWO obstruction with
a contextuality meaning that the degree-one obstruction cannot see, the priority objection you
raised would be against the wrong literature, because the theorem would no longer be a
specialization of gerbe classification - it would be a statement about empirical models.

So:

 1. Is there a construction, CANONICAL and NATURAL in the empirical model, supplying (ii)-(iv)?
    Say which of the three is the real obstacle. Our reading is that (iv) is - a bare support
    presheaf carries no reason to prefer a nonzero degree-two class, and the split choice zeroes it.
    If you agree, say whether the failure is that the data are underdetermined (many choices, none
    canonical) or genuinely absent (no choice of the required type exists). Those have very
    different consequences.

 2. If no canonical construction exists on ALL empirical models, is there a natural SUBCLASS on
    which the data are determined? Candidates we can see: models arising from a fixed measurement
    scenario whose context cover has a prescribed nerve; models carrying a group action or symmetry
    fixing the band; models presented by a specific physical family where the second homology of the
    nerve is computable. Name the subclass if one exists, rather than confirming ours. A theorem
    restricted to an honestly named subclass is worth more to us than a universal statement we
    cannot prove.

 3. IF THE CROSSING IS POSSIBLE, WHAT IS THE THEOREM? State it. It must pass this test: the target
    theorem must be statable USING ONLY TERMS FROM THE CONTEXTUALITY LITERATURE (empirical model,
    measurement scenario, context, support, global section, obstruction class, sheaf of
    distributions) TOGETHER WITH STANDARD ALGEBRAIC TOPOLOGY - and NO TERM THIS MANUSCRIPT
    INVENTED. If you cannot state it without "component gerbe", "homological visibility",
    "terminal essential surjectivity", or "supplied lift data", then the crossing has not happened
    and you should say so.

 4. Would the degree-two class SEE ANYTHING the degree-one AMB obstruction misses? This is the
    question that decides whether the crossing is worth anything. A degree-two invariant that
    vanishes exactly when the degree-one one does is a reformulation. We want to know whether there
    is a phenomenon - some empirical model, or family of them - that is invisible to the AMB
    obstruction and visible to a degree-two one. If you believe there is, name the phenomenon. If
    you believe the degree-one obstruction is already complete for possibilistic contextuality, say
    that plainly, because then this whole direction is closed and we will record it as closed.

 5. Give a number. Probability that the theorem in (3) can be proved correctly, on the same scale
    you have used. Separately, probability that, once proved, it survives a priority check against
    the contextuality literature - Abramsky-Brandenburger, Abramsky-Mansfield-Barbosa, Caru, and
    whatever else you consider the relevant antecedent. We need both, because your last assessment
    showed a case where the proof was easy and the priority was the ceiling. Say honestly whether
    anyone has already put a degree-two or gerbe-level obstruction on empirical models.

 6. If the crossing is NOT possible, is the impossibility itself a theorem? The manuscript currently
    states the boundary as a set of observations about what the support presheaf does not supply. If
    there is a real obstruction - a proof that no functor from empirical models to banded prestacks
    can be natural and produce nonzero degree-two classes - that is a statement about the limits of
    cohomological methods in contextuality, and it would be of interest to that field rather than
    to gerbe theory. Say whether it is provable and at what strength.

One more thing, and please answer it directly rather than folding it into the above. The manuscript
was written for a logic venue and its own title speaks of state forcing. We have priced it against
gerbe classification because you did. Is there a THIRD field whose standard objects this machinery
touches - definability and forcing, sheaf semantics, distributed computing and local consistency,
constraint satisfaction - where the same priority objection would also not apply? If so, name the
field and its standard objects, and say which is the better target: contextuality or that one. If
not, say so, and we will treat the contextuality crossing as the only remaining lever.

Please give a definite answer rather than a survey. If the honest response is that this manuscript's
ceiling is where you said it was and neither crossing is available, that is a useful answer: we will
stop spending on this paper and submit it where it stands. We would rather have a definite
recommendation we can disagree with than a balanced list of possibilities.
