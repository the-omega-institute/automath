Please read main.pdf as a specialist in stacks, gerbes, descent, and cohomology, and answer the following question. The purpose is not to obtain another list of results internal to this manuscript's vocabulary. It is to determine whether the proof machinery already present can be redirected toward an object that the field independently recognizes and studies.

1. The machinery actually present

Please assess the paper from the following account of its technical apparatus, correcting any mistake against main.pdf before drawing conclusions. This is deliberately an account of what the proofs consume and produce, not a list of theorem headlines.

(i) Presheaf/sheafification interface (Proposition 2.1). The input is a small site C with pullbacks of covering families and terminal object a, a set-valued presheaf F, and the sheafification unit u:F -> aF. A strict matching family produces a section of (aF)(a). In the reverse direction, the plus-plus description of sheafification first produces local representatives whose restrictions agree only after further covers of overlaps; separatedness of F on every object of the slice C/a upgrades those locally witnessed equalities to literal equalities in F, hence produces one strict matching family. At a this also makes u_a injective and turns bijectivity of u_a into unique effective gluing for every strict matching family.

(ii) Stackification and component extraction (Proposition 3.1). The supplied further input is an abelian sheaf \mathscr A and a \mathscr A-banded prestack P with a fixed split cleavage and pi_0^pre(P) isomorphic to F. Stackification iota:P -> L=a(P) produces a stack with pi_0(L) isomorphic to aF. For a global component section v in pi_0(L)(a), pullback of L -> pi_0(L) along v produces the full maximal component gerbe G_v=L[v]. This is Giraud's standard construction, not a construction invented here. Its class omega_v in H^2(C,\mathscr A) vanishes exactly when G_v is neutral, equivalently when it has an object over a. Local objects x_i and overlap isomorphisms g_ij produce the usual triple defect c_ijk=g_ik^{-1}g_jk g_ij; the quadruple-overlap identity makes c a Cech 2-cocycle, changes of overlap arrows add a coboundary, and a coboundary defect can be killed before applying effective descent.

(iii) Full faithfulness and torsor transfer at the terminal fibre (Theorems 3.2 and 3.3). Because P is a prestack, its Isom presheaves are already sheaves; stackification therefore identifies them with the Isom sheaves in L. Thus P(a) -> L(a) is fully faithful. On a neutral component, essential surjectivity upgrades this to an equivalence with the neutral gerbe. Choosing a neutralizing object identifies that gerbe with the groupoid of \mathscr A-torsors. The output is therefore an H^1(C,\mathscr A)-torsor of isomorphism classes and H^0(C,\mathscr A) as each stabilizer, not one canonical source class per neutral component.

(iv) Literal-representative rigidity (Theorem 3.7). A paper-specific hypothesis selects s_X(t) in P(X) for every t in F(X) and requires f^*s_X(t)=s_Y(f^*t) as a literal equality for the split cleavage. It consumes a strict matching family and produces identity-valued object descent data: on overlaps the selected objects are literally equal, so no \mathscr A-valued 2-cocycle remains. Stack descent gives an object of L(a), and terminal essential surjectivity then gives a preimage in P(a), proving surjectivity of u_a. Conversely, surjectivity of u_a reduces comparison with an arbitrary x in L(a) to an Isom torsor; H^1(C,\mathscr A)=0 trivializes it. This is the mechanism behind the manuscript's representative-rigidity no-go result.

(v) Finite good-cover comparison (Proposition 4.1). The geometric input is a finite cover U={U_i} whose nonempty finite intersections are connected, together with coherent trivializations identifying sections of a locally constant coefficient sheaf on every such intersection with one fixed abelian group A. The paper forms a finite intersection site C_U with terminal object a, an object for each nonempty intersection, and one strict initial object for all empty intersections. The topology is generated by the vertex cover, pullbacks, identities, and the empty cover of the initial object. Pulling the vertex cover back to a proper nonempty intersection yields a family containing its identity, and generated covers refine such families; evaluation at the terminal object of the slice is then used to obtain slice acyclicity. Connectedness supplies exactly one copy of A per simplex, so the ordered Cech differential is literally the simplicial coboundary of the ordinary nerve N. With acyclic intersections, the Cech-to-derived spectral sequence collapses and gives H^q(C_U,A_U) isomorphic to H^q(N,A). For open-star covers of all barycentric subdivisions of a finite simplicial complex, the small-mesh, basis, and cofinality arguments further identify sheaves on the all-subdivision basis with sheaves on the polyhedron and identify the Cech colimit with ordinary cohomology.

(vi) UCT lifting and twisted groupoids (Theorem 4.2). The realization input is a finite abelian group A, a finite connected nerve N with H_1(N,Z)=0, a finite label set Lambda, and prescribed homomorphisms phi_lambda:H_2(N,Z) -> A. Degree-two UCT then has no Ext ambiguity and selects omega_lambda in H^2(N,A); degree-one UCT also gives H^1(N;A)=0. After choosing normalized cocycles alpha_lambda, the paper builds a groupoid over each proper nonempty intersection with objects x_i and Hom(x_i,x_j)=A, and with composition

    (c,j,k) o (b,i,j) = (b+c+alpha_{lambda,ijk},i,k).

The 2-cocycle identity is exactly the associativity identity. Restriction preserves the object index and coefficient; coherent trivializations make these restrictions strict. Isom sheaves are copies of A_U, and abelianness makes conjugation trivial, producing a genuine A_U-banding. Zero-labelled summands are neutral one-object stacks; nonzero-labelled summands have empty terminal fibre. A fibrewise coproduct over the labels, with a common value at the initial object, produces one prestack whose component presheaf, sheafified component labels, neutral labels, terminal essential surjectivity, and chosen Cech defects are controlled simultaneously. Connectedness of the 1-skeleton is used to force a matching label family to be constant.

(vii) Evaluation and subgroup arithmetic (Proposition 5.1). A cocycle alpha and an integral 2-cycle z are paired by <alpha,z>, giving the UCT evaluation ev_omega:H_2(N,Z) -> A. Boundaries and coboundaries pair to zero. The manuscript then takes the image K_omega=Im(ev_omega), the quotient A/K_omega, intersections and sums of finitely many K_v, the quotient exact sequence between A/(intersection K_v) and A/(sum K_v), and annihilators under finite Pontryagin duality. These steps consume supplied cohomology classes and produce image subgroups and elementary quotients; they do not create the gerbe class. The Ext term also shows the limitation: a nonzero H^2 class can have K_omega=0 when H_1 has suitable torsion.

(viii) Wedge specialization and negative transfer test (Corollary 6.1 and Theorem 7.1). For selected open-star covers of a finite model of a wedge of beta copies of S^2, the nerve has H_1(N,Z)=0 and H_2(N,Z) isomorphic to Z^beta. The paper combines the generator bound for images of Z^beta -> G with finite-abelian direct-sum arithmetic; this is an algebraic specialization of the supplied-map realization. Separately, for an empirical model it sheafifies the support presheaf and forms the standard split stack Disc(aS_e) x B\mathscr A, which has only neutral component gerbes. The Abramsky-Mansfield-Barbosa class is instead a relative Cech H^1 torsor class indexed by a local section. This empty-domain/degree/indexing argument produces a non-transfer result: the finite-site lift data are additional input, not an invariant extracted from a bare empirical model.

There is no analytic estimate, contraction mapping, automaton, or quantitative separation bound in the core argument. Apart from connectedness of the nerve's 1-skeleton, the engine is sheafification, descent, stackification, cocycle identities, UCT, and finite subgroup arithmetic. Please say so if this inventory makes the machinery look stronger or more general than it is.

2. Separate the manuscript's objects from the field's objects

(a) Objects or named packages introduced by this manuscript for its own purposes:

- the predicate "matching without amalgamation" MWA(F,a), meaning existence of a strict matching family on some cover together with F(a)=empty; it is an existence predicate, not the assertion that a particular or every matching family lacks an amalgamation;
- a "\mathscr A-banded prestack lift of F" as the supplied tuple used here, together with the terminal essential-surjectivity condition on its stackification;
- a "representative-rigid lift," meaning the literal pullback-stable choice of one object in each presheaf component;
- the finite marking package Lambda, Lambda_0, the terminal marking map, its "essentially surjective marked terminal form," and the separate "pointed-atlas alternative";
- the manuscript's particular finite intersection site C_U and label presheaf with Lambda on proper nonempty intersections, Lambda_0 at a, and a singleton at the initial object;
- the finite labelled coproduct prestack assembled from the alpha_lambda-twisted groupoids, viewed as a simultaneous-control realization package;
- the names "homological image" K_omega and "homological cokernel" Q_omega for the image and quotient attached to UCT evaluation, plus the named aggregate quotients Q_lab=A/(intersection K_v) and Q_com=A/(sum K_v);
- the selected two-component comparison kernel and the complementary-nonzero-image realization problem on open-star sites of a wedge of 2-spheres.

Several entries in this list are packages or names around classical constructions, not claims of new underlying mathematics. In particular, K_omega is just the image of the standard UCT evaluation map; the aggregate quotients are ordinary subgroup quotients; and the twisted groupoid is the standard Cech-cocycle gerbe construction placed inside a bespoke labelled prestack.

(b) Standard objects or constructions that occur independently in the gerbe/stack/descent literature and in surveys of the field:

- presheaves, sheaves, separated presheaves, matching families, sheafification, and the plus construction;
- categories fibred in groupoids, prestacks, stacks, split cleavages, pseudofunctorial pullback, and stackification;
- descent data, effective descent data, and descent gerbes;
- the sheaf of connected components pi_0 of a stack and Giraud's maximal component gerbe obtained by pulling back along a component section;
- abelian-banded gerbes, neutral gerbes, equivalences of banded gerbes, and Giraud's H^2 classification;
- torsors, Isom torsors, H^1 classification of torsors, and H^0 automorphism groups;
- the classifying stack B\mathscr A;
- Cech cohomology, Leray/good covers, nerves of covers, simplicial cochains, the nerve lemma, refinement, and comparison with derived sheaf cohomology;
- the universal coefficient theorem, including its Ext term and evaluation of H^2 on integral H_2;
- constant sheaves and locally constant sheaves/local systems, including cohomology with local coefficients;
- classifying topoi/toposes and geometric morphisms;
- the cocycle 2-groupoid of 2-cocycles, 1-cochain gauges, and 0-cochain 2-morphisms, together with refinement-localized or 2-stack presentations of gerbes;
- lifting gerbes and the obstruction classes associated with central extensions or crossed modules;
- effective descent morphisms and the standard question of when descent data are effective.

The phrase "component gerbe" belongs in list (b): the notation G_v and its use in the manuscript are local packaging, but the object is Giraud's. Likewise, "homological image" in list (a) must not be promoted into a new standard field object merely because it has been named. Conversely, if any item in list (b) is too remote from the manuscript's actual field, please strike it rather than crediting the paper with relevance it does not have.
