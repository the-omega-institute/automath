THIS IS A VALUATION QUESTION, NOT A REQUEST FOR MORE MATHEMATICS. We are not asking what else to
prove. We are asking what the alternatives are worth, so the authors can choose.

 1. Suppose the paper were split as follows. Paper A: the Brocot condensation theorem, the

    consequences that genuinely depend on the scalar denominator-layer asymptotic - the critical

    What is Paper A worth? Give your estimate of its length, and its probability of clearing the

 2. What is Paper B worth on its own? If it would not clear a respectable specialist venue

 3. Is there a third option we have not considered - for instance keeping one article but moving
    a defined block to a supplement, or restructuring rather than splitting - that would raise the
    significance density more cheaply than a split? If so, name exactly which material moves.

 4. Your own judgement: if these were your results, would you split, restructure, or submit the
    combined article to JNT and accept the predicted outcome? Answer as an author deciding, not as
    a referee describing options.

Please do not hedge across all four. We would rather have a definite recommendation we can
disagree with than a balanced survey. If the honest answer is that splitting gains little and
costs a paper, that is a useful answer and we will act on it.
