I am asking for a tier-raising mathematical assessment of the attached current manuscript, main.pdf. This is not a journal-placement request. We already know where the paper lands at its present strength. I want you to identify a single named, concrete, reachable new theorem that would materially raise the paper's mathematical tier, or to explain rigorously why no such theorem is reachable from its present machinery.

Here is the paper's setup and its principal results, stated with their hypotheses.

Let C be a small site with pullbacks of covering families and terminal object a, let F be a presheaf of sets, and let P be a prestack in groupoids with a fixed split cleavage, banded by an abelian sheaf A-script, such that the presheaf of fibrewise connected components of P is F. Write L = a(P) for stackification and aF for sheafification. A strict matching family is a family of local sections equal on all fibre-product overlaps. MWA(F,a), "matching without amalgamation", means that such a matching family exists on some cover of a while F(a) is empty; it does not mean that every matching family fails to amalgamate. "Separated on the slice over a" means separated on every object X -> a. "Terminal essential surjectivity" means that P(a) -> L(a) is essentially surjective. For v in (aF)(a) = pi_0(L)(a), let G_v be the full component substack L[v].

1. Presheaf/component-gerbe comparison. If F is separated on the slice over a and P is an abelian-banded prestack lift whose stackification is terminally essentially surjective, then

   MWA(F,a) iff (aF)(a) is nonempty and every G_v, v in (aF)(a), is non-neutral,

equivalently, every Giraud class omega_v = [G_v] in H^2(C,A-script) is nonzero. The proof factors through four facts: slice separatedness identifies existence of a strict matching family with nonemptiness of (aF)(a); Giraud's component construction makes L[v] a gerbe; terminal essential surjectivity identifies nonemptiness of F(a) with nonemptiness of L(a); and a component has a terminal global object exactly when its gerbe is neutral.

2. Representative-rigid terminal theorem. Call the lift representative-rigid if one can choose an object s_X(t) in P(X) in every component t in F(X) such that f^*s_X(t) = s_Y(f^*t) as a literal equality for every f:Y -> X, relative to the fixed split cleavage. If F is separated on the slice over a and P is a representative-rigid A-script-banded prestack lift, with sheafification unit u:F -> aF and stackification map iota:P -> L, then:

   (i) terminal essential surjectivity of iota implies surjectivity of u_a:F(a) -> (aF)(a);

   (ii) if H^1(C,A-script)=0, surjectivity of u_a implies terminal essential surjectivity of iota;

   (iii) under H^1(C,A-script)=0, the following are equivalent: iota is terminally essentially surjective; u_a is bijective; and every strict matching family on every cover of a has a unique amalgamation in F(a).

Literal pullback stability is used only in the forward descent step; the converse uses the usual A-script-torsor of isomorphisms and its H^1-class.

3. Finite-cover comparison used by the realization theorem. Let U be a finite cover with ordinary nerve N. Assume every nonempty finite intersection U_J is connected and that coherent trivializations of the locally constant coefficient sheaf identify Gamma(U_J,A-script) with the same abelian group A. Then the ordered Cech complex is naturally C^*(N,A). If the nonempty intersections are also A-script-acyclic, Leray comparison gives H^q(C_U,A_U) = Cech H^q(U,A_U) = H^q(N,A). For a finite simplicial complex K, the site B_K made from all open stars in all barycentric subdivisions of K and their finite intersections is a basis site for |K|, restriction gives Sh(|K|) equivalent to Sh(B_K), the nonempty basic intersections are contractible, the subdivision star covers are filtered and cofinal, and colim_m Cech H^2(U^(m),A) = H^2(|K|,A).

4. Prescribed simultaneous realization. Let U be a finite good cover satisfying the connected-overlap/coherent-trivialization hypothesis, let its nerve N be connected, let A be a finite abelian group, and assume H_1(N,Z)=0. Given a finite nonempty label set Lambda and arbitrary homomorphisms

   phi_lambda:H_2(N,Z) -> A  (lambda in Lambda),

put Lambda_0 = {lambda : phi_lambda=0}. The paper explicitly constructs a presheaf F on the finite intersection site C_U and an A_U-banded prestack P, with stackification L, such that:

   (i) F(a)=Lambda_0, (aF)(a)=Lambda, and pi_0(L)(a)=Lambda;

   (ii) the component gerbe G_lambda has a class omega_lambda in H^2(N,A) with evaluation map ev_(omega_lambda)=phi_lambda and image Im(phi_lambda), and G_lambda is neutral exactly for lambda in Lambda_0;

   (iii) P(a) -> L(a) is essentially surjective, and terminal objects occur exactly in the Lambda_0-components;

   (iv) F has a strict matching family on U, and MWA(F,a) holds exactly when Lambda_0 is empty.

The construction chooses the UCT class, a normalized simplicial 2-cocycle, twisted groupoid fibres with that cocycle in their composition law, and a fibrewise coproduct over the labels. Its claimed contribution is simultaneous control of the component presheaf, terminal fibre, neutrality pattern, and evaluation images, not realization of an H^2-class by a gerbe by itself.

5. Homological images and aggregate quotients. For a finite simplicial complex N, finite abelian A, and omega in H^2(N,A), define

   K_omega = Im(ev_omega:H_2(N,Z) -> A),    Q_omega = A/K_omega.

For a nonempty finite family (omega_v), define

   Q_lab = A/(intersection_v K_v),    Q_com = A/(sum_v K_v).

The paper records the UCT exact sequence

   0 -> Ext^1(H_1(N,Z),A) -> H^2(N,A) -> Hom(H_2(N,Z),A) -> 0,

so K_omega=0 exactly for an Ext-class; the initiality of A -> Q_omega among coefficient maps killing ev_omega; the annihilating-character description of K_omega; and the exact sequence

   0 -> (sum_v K_v)/(intersection_v K_v) -> Q_lab -> Q_com -> 0,

with the corresponding finite Pontryagin-dual formulas. If H_1(N,Z)=0 and H_2(N,Z)=Z^beta, a subgroup K <= A occurs as K_omega exactly when its minimal number of generators d(K) is at most beta, and every finite family of such subgroups is attained simultaneously by the preceding prestack construction.

6. Sharp two-component wedge realization. Fix beta >= 1 and a finite connected simplicial complex K_beta with |K_beta| homotopy equivalent to the wedge of beta copies of S^2. On the selected all-subdivision open-star basis site B_beta, let G be a finite abelian group. The following are equivalent:

   (i) G is nonzero, d(G) <= 2 beta, and G is not cyclic of prime-power order;

   (ii) on one selected finite open-star cover, the prescribed-realization prestack has two non-neutral component gerbes with nonzero homological images K_1,K_2 satisfying K_1 intersection K_2 = 0 and K_1 + K_2 = G.

In that case Q_lab is isomorphic to G, Q_com=0, the kernel of Q_lab -> Q_com is G, and the constructed component presheaf has matching without amalgamation. This is explicitly presentation-relative and is not claimed to classify all gerbes, arbitrary presentations, or finite abelian groups independently of the constructed realization.

7. Boundary with standard empirical models. For a finite empirical model e on a finite measurement set with finite nonempty outcome sets and a finite antichain of contexts covering the measurements, the terminally augmented support presheaf has

   (aS_e)(X) = compatible supported families = globally supported assignments = global sections in the Abramsky-Brandenburger sense.

Thus strong contextuality is absence of a matching family at this set-valued level, not MWA. For every supplied abelian sheaf A-script, Disc(aS_e) x B A-script is a canonical split banded stack lift and all its component gerbes are neutral. The Abramsky-Mansfield-Barbosa obstruction attached to a local section is instead a relative Cech H^1 torsor class; it cannot universally be recovered from a global-component-section map with component sheaf aS_e, because for a strongly contextual model that map has empty domain while local-section-indexed AMB classes remain. The finite-site homological images and wedge criterion therefore apply to an empirical model only after a band and a non-split prestack lift are supplied as extra data; they are not invariants of the bare empirical model.

The imported-versus-original boundary is important here and should be read against our interest. Imported or essentially standard material includes: Giraud III.2.1.5.3, which says that a stack over its component sheaf is a gerbe and that pullback along a component section is the corresponding maximal subgerbe; Giraud's abelian-banded H^2 classification and neutrality criterion; local-object/overlap-arrow/triple-defect Cech cocycles and their change by coboundaries; realization of H^2-classes by abelian-banded gerbes; Leray and ordinary-nerve comparisons under the stated hypotheses; UCT naturality and its Ext kernel; finite Pontryagin duality; quotient universal properties; subgroup sum/intersection arithmetic and fibre counting; the structure theorem for finite abelian groups; and the standard split stack used at the empirical-model boundary.

Our defensible paper-specific remainder is narrower: the exact representative-rigid terminal no-go formulation; the one explicit finite-label prestack that simultaneously controls the component presheaf, terminal essential surjectivity, neutral/non-neutral labels, and prescribed homological images; the exact negative empty-domain comparison with local-section-indexed AMB classes; and, at most, the exact two-label wedge realization criterion as a sharp corollary of that selected construction. The matching/non-neutrality criterion is substantially present in our cited companion manuscript as a meet-semilattice analogue, and quotient initiality also appears there; this paper gives a pullback-site reformulation and does not claim separate priority for those facts. The component-obstruction framework likewise overlaps the companion. The narrow lower-language separation is only in the technical appendix and is not being offered as a general logic theorem.
