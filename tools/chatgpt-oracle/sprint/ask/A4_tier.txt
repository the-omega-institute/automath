Please give a tier-raising mathematical assessment of the attached manuscript, “Prime support and multiple-context-free languages in recurrent numeration.” This is not a request for a journal recommendation. We already know the paper’s present landing range. The purpose is to identify one named, concrete, realistically attainable theorem that would raise the mathematical tier of the paper, or to establish clearly that no such theorem is reachable from the present machinery.

Here is a self-contained account of the paper as it now stands.

Ambient setting and notation

A canonical eventually recurrent numeration system is a triple \((U,A,\mathcal R_U)\) satisfying all of the following:

1. \(U=(U_n)_{n\ge 0}\) is a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers, and \(A\subseteq\mathbb N\) is a finite digit alphabet containing 0.
2. Words are read least-significant digit first and
   \[
   \operatorname{val}_U(x_0\cdots x_{m-1})=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}x_jU_j.
   \]
   The map \(\operatorname{val}_U:\mathcal R_U\to\mathbb N_{\ge1}\) is a bijection.
3. Canonical words are strictly ordered by length: if \(r,s\in\mathcal R_U\) and \(|r|<|s|\), then \(\operatorname{val}_U(r)<\operatorname{val}_U(s)\).
4. For some integers \(J\ge0,d\ge1\) and \(a_0,\ldots,a_{d-1}\), with \(a_0\ne0\),
   \[
   U_{n+d}=a_0U_n+a_1U_{n+1}+\cdots+a_{d-1}U_{n+d-1}\qquad(n\ge J).
   \]

No regularity of \(\mathcal R_U\) is assumed in the structural theorems. “MCFL” means a multiple context-free language of some finite fan-out; an infinite language is MCF-immune if it contains no infinite MCFL sublanguage. For a recurrence witness, put \(S=\{p:p\mid a_0\}\), and let \(\tau_S\) be the topology on the positive integers generated by congruence classes modulo integers coprime to \(a_0\). For the intrinsic statements, let
\[
\mu_U(X)=X^{d_{\rm r}}-c_{d_{\rm r}-1}X^{d_{\rm r}-1}-\cdots-c_1X-c_0
\]
be the minimal recurrence polynomial of a tail of \(U\), with threshold \(J_{\rm r}\), and put \(S_{\rm r}=\{p:p\mid c_0\}\).

Main results, with their hypotheses

1. In the canonical Zeckendorf language \(\mathcal Z\), every context-free \(L\subseteq\mathcal Z\) has a constant \(P_L\) such that every \(w\in L\) with \(|w|\ge P_L\) and \(\operatorname{val}(w)\ge2\) begins a sequence \(w=w_0,w_1,\ldots\in L\) of strictly increasing lengths whose values satisfy
   \[
   N_{i+1}=N_iQ_i,\qquad Q_i>1,\qquad Q_i\equiv1\pmod{N_i},\qquad \gcd(Q_i,N_i)=1.
   \]
   Hence every infinite context-free sublanguage of \(\mathcal Z\) has unbounded \(\omega(\operatorname{val}(w))\); bounded-distinct-prime, bounded-\(\Omega\), finite-prime-support, and prime Zeckendorf languages are CF-immune when infinite.

2. For every system satisfying conditions 1–4 above and every infinite \(k\)-MCFL \(L\subseteq\mathcal R_U\), there is one fixed factorization, chosen independently of any modulus,
   \[
   W(t)=a u_1v_1^tw_1s_1^tu_2\cdots u_kv_k^tw_ks_k^tu_{k+1}\in L\qquad(t\ge0),
   \]
   with \(a\in A^J\) and positive total pumped length, such that \(N(t)=\operatorname{val}_U(W(t))\) is strictly increasing and, for every \(q\ge2\) with \(\gcd(q,a_0)=1\), some \(H(q)\ge1\) satisfies
   \[
   N(t+H(q))\equiv N(t)\pmod q\qquad\text{for all }t\ge0.
   \]
   The proof gives \(H(q)\le |\operatorname{GL}_{d+1}(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)|\le q^{(d+1)^2}\). Any such synchronized ray is itself an infinite linear \(2k\)-MCFL.

3. For the orbit in result 2, \(Y=\{N(t):t\ge0\}\) has no isolated points in \(\tau_S\), is homeomorphic to \(\mathbb Q\), and its closure in \(\prod_{p\notin S}\mathbb Z_p\) is a Cantor set. Consequently, if \(B\subseteq\mathbb N_{\ge1}\) is scattered in \(\tau_S\), then \(\operatorname{Rep}_U(B)\) is MCF-immune whenever infinite. More precisely, for
   \[
   X_{K,E}=\{n\ge1:\omega_{S^c}(n)\le K,\ v_p(n)\le E_p\ (p\in S)\},
   \]
   the intrinsic Cantor–Bendixson derivative satisfies \(D_SX_{K,E}=X_{K-1,E}\) for \(K\ge1\); its height is exactly \(K+1\), while \(X_{0,E}\) has height 1.

4. It follows that every infinite MCFL \(L\subseteq\mathcal R_U\) obeys the nonunit escape dichotomy: either
   \[
   \sup_{w\in L}\omega_{S^c}(\operatorname{val}_U(w))=\infty,
   \]
   or \(\sup_{w\in L}v_p(\operatorname{val}_U(w))=\infty\) for some \(p\mid a_0\). Thus the prime representation language, every bounded-\(\Omega\) language, and every finite-prime-support language with bounded valuations at primes dividing \(a_0\) are MCF-immune when infinite. If \(|a_0|=1\), every bounded-\(\omega\) language is MCF-immune. In particular the prime representation language of every system satisfying conditions 1–4 is MCF-immune, hence also CF- and REG-immune and nonregular.

5. The exceptional primes of a nonminimal recurrence witness are not intrinsic. If \(\mu_U\) is a minimal tail recurrence, then \((X-p)\mu_U(X)\) is also a recurrence witness for every prime \(p\). The paper exhibits strict separation: ordinary base \(p\) has the regular ray \(0^n1\) with values \(p^n\), whereas Zeckendorf equipped with an inflated order-three witness having the same exceptional prime \(p\) has no infinite MCFL with bounded prime support outside \(p\).

6. The central inverse theorem uses the minimal tail recurrence. The following are equivalent for every system satisfying conditions 1–4:

   (i) for some finite \(k\), an infinite \(k\)-MCFL \(L\subseteq\mathcal R_U\) has \(\sup_{w\in L}\omega_{S_{\rm r}^c}(\operatorname{val}_U(w))<\infty\);

   (ii) \(\mathcal R_U\) contains a synchronized scheme of the form in result 2, now starting with a prefix of length \(J_{\rm r}\), whose values are exactly \(cb^t\) for all \(t\ge0\), for integers \(c\ge1\) and \(b\ge2\);

   (iii) such a ray is an infinite linear MCFL of finite fan-out.

   Moreover every prime divisor of the ratio \(b\) divides \(c_0\), equivalently \(\operatorname{rad}(b)\mid\operatorname{rad}(|c_0|)\).

7. On the promised class in which the finite alphabet, recurrence and initial data, a DFA for \(\mathcal R_U\), and a supplied or effectively certified minimal tail recurrence and valid threshold are given, positive instances of result 6(i) are recursively enumerable (a \(\Sigma^0_1\) upper bound). A proposed synchronized scheme is decidable by the finite transition monoid and an exact recurrence identity of order at most \((d_{\rm r}+1)^{2k}\). No recognition procedure for the promise class and no decision procedure for negative instances is claimed. The missing step is a computable bound on the fan-out and word lengths of a smallest geometric synchronized scheme, or an equivalent finite negative certificate; ordinary DFA loop compression does not preserve the affine recurrence matrices. General decidability, undecidability, and hardness remain open.

8. If \(|a_0|=1\), every infinite MCFL \(L\subseteq\mathcal R_U\) has the following stronger structure. Given arbitrary sequences \(M_i,r_i\ge1\), it contains strictly increasing values
   \[
   N_{i+1}=N_iQ_i,\qquad Q_i>1,\qquad Q_i\equiv1\pmod{M_iN_i^{r_i}}.
   \]
   By making \(M_i\) divisible by all primes up to a prescribed threshold, every prime divisor of \(Q_i\) can be forced above that threshold. The same orbit contains an induced copy of the full countably branching tree \(\mathbb N^{<\omega}\) under divisibility: there is an injection \(\Phi:\mathbb N^{<\omega}\to L\) for which \(\operatorname{val}_U(\Phi(\sigma))\mid\operatorname{val}_U(\Phi(\tau))\) if and only if \(\sigma\) is a prefix of \(\tau\); distinct edge quotients can be pairwise coprime and arbitrarily rough.

9. For a standard greedy linear numeration basis with \(U_0=1\), strictly increasing integer place values with bounded quotients, the LSD-first reversal of the usual greedy representation language, and minimal tail polynomial equal to the minimal polynomial of a Perron number \(\beta>1\) whose other conjugates have modulus strictly less than \(\beta\), the prime language is MCF-immune. More sharply, the three properties in result 6 are equivalent to \(\beta\in\mathbb Z_{\ge2}\). Hence, if \(\beta\) is nonintegral, every infinite MCFL has unbounded prime support outside \(S_{\rm r}\); on effective inputs the bounded-outside-support property is decidable by testing whether the minimal polynomial has degree one. This uses strict Perron dominance, not the stronger Pisot condition that all other conjugates lie in the open unit disk.

What is imported and what is claimed as ours

The imported language-theoretic input is Seki–Matsumura–Fujii–Kasami’s 1991 weak pumping lemma for MCFLs, which supplies one synchronized family with \(2k\) pumped factors; the standard MCFL closure operations used to take a fixed left quotient are also imported. Ogden’s lemma supplies the special context-free Zeckendorf pumping. The arithmetic rigidity input is Evertse’s 1984 theorem that prime-ideal support in quotients of a nondegenerate recurrence with at least two characteristic roots becomes unbounded, followed by the classical Schur theorem to eliminate a nonconstant polynomial factor. The \(\mathbb Q\)-space and Cantor-completion classifications are Broughan’s 2003 results. Dirichlet’s theorem, Cayley–Hamilton, elementary finite-group return, standard Perron recurrence asymptotics, and the usual greedy length thresholds are used in their standard forms. Frougny and the Bertrand/substitution literature provide standard regular canonical interfaces in Pisot numeration. Hartmanis–Shank and Schützenberger already proved the fixed-base context-free prime obstruction in 1968.

What the manuscript claims as its own is the placement of a weak MCFL pumping family beyond the recurrence transient; the affine block-action and one fixed numerical orbit returning modulo every admissible modulus; the application of Broughan’s classification to show that every such orbit meets the no-isolated-point hypothesis; the deleted-prime, distinct-support intrinsic derivative formula; the nonunit support/valuation dichotomy and MCF-immunity consequences; the recurrence-witness separation; the extraction of a fixed-support recurrence from the orbit and its recoding as an exact geometric synchronized ray; the resulting minimal-tail equivalence and positive semidecision boundary; the restriction of the geometric ratio to primes dividing the minimal tail constant; the arbitrary-depth quotient chains and induced divisibility tree in the unit case; and the integer-dominant-root classification for standard greedy Perron-recurrence bases. The Evertse–Schur recurrence lemma is not claimed as an independent arithmetic discovery, and Broughan’s ambient topological classifications are not claimed as new.

Priority position, stated conservatively and against our own interest

The fixed-base CF-immunity of primes is old, so the ordinary-base corollary here is only a strengthening from CF-immunity to MCF-immunity. The 2026 Duncan–Elder–Frenkel–Lyu substitution lemma is newer and stronger in an every-sufficiently-marked-word direction; our current understanding is that its switchable-tuple alternative does not produce simultaneous powers of fixed blocks, a fixed affine matrix-power orbit, or one return time valid for every orbit parameter, and therefore does not directly imply the present arithmetic conclusions. That distinction may still be judged too technical to carry high independent priority.

Likewise, the fixed-support recurrence rigidity is a standard use of Evertse rather than a new number-theoretic theorem, and the homeomorphism to \(\mathbb Q\) and Cantor completion belong to Broughan; our topological increment is the orbit application and the deleted-prime intrinsic rank formula. The supplementary fixed-DFA density results are independent of the main chain: Shen gives a qualitative finite-automaton obstruction, while Dubbe gives a growing-state lower bound for exact prime recognition; our fixed-DFA lengthwise error bounds do not improve Dubbe’s growing-state theorem. Negative searches through 15 August 2026 located no paper combining an arbitrary canonical eventually recurrent numeration system, an infinite finite-fan-out MCFL, an all-admissible-modulus synchronized return orbit, the nonunit MCF-immunity consequences, and the minimal-tail geometric-ray equivalence. That is only negative evidence, not a priority proof, and we make no “first” claim.
