The ambient object is a canonical eventually recurrent numeration system. Its retained hypotheses are:

(U1) U=(U_n)_{n>=0} is a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers, and the finite digit alphabet A is a subset of the nonnegative integers and contains 0;

(U2) R_U is an LSD-first language over A for which

    val_U(x_0...x_{m-1}) = sum_{j=0}^{m-1} x_j U_j

is a bijection R_U -> Z_{>=1}; and

(U4) there are integers J>=0, d>=1 and a_0,...,a_{d-1}, with a_0 nonzero, such that

    U_{n+d}=a_0 U_n+a_1 U_{n+1}+...+a_{d-1} U_{n+d-1}    (n>=J).

The old (U3), which ordered represented values strictly by canonical word length, has been deleted. No comparison between the values of canonical words of different lengths is now assumed. The label (U4) is retained only to preserve the established numbering. The strict increase of the place values U_n in (U1) is a distinct hypothesis and remains in force.

The statements now requiring only (U1), (U2), and (U4), without (U3), include the synchronized local-congruence orbit, the no-isolated-point/local-adic fingerprint and scattered-set obstruction, the nonunit prime-support/valuation escape dichotomy and prime MCF-immunity, the minimal-tail geometric-ray characterization and its positive semidecision result, and, in the unit case, the arbitrarily deep increasing quotient chains and induced full divisibility tree. The revised synchronized-orbit proof obtains pairwise distinct words from positive pumped length and then pairwise distinct values from the injectivity in (U2). Where a later construction needs a value larger than the current one, it chooses sufficiently far along an infinite pairwise-distinct orbit, since only finitely many positive integers lie below the current value. The manuscript contains a downstream-use audit asserting that these exhaust the former uses of length monotonicity.

Strict increase of the place values is retained where it is genuinely needed. In particular, it is used in the new weak-Perron residue argument: after obtaining at least one nonzero leading coefficient on a residue class, the inequalities U_n<U_{n+1} propagate positivity cyclically through all residue-class leading coefficients. Thus the removal of (U3) is not being conflated with removal of strict increase from (U1).

The former Corollary 2.25 has been strengthened in place to Theorem 2.25, the following weak-Perron radical classification.

Let U=(U_n)_{n>=0} be a standard greedy linear numeration basis with U_0=1 and strictly increasing integer place values with bounded quotients, and let R_U be the LSD-first reversal of its greedy representation language. Suppose that the minimal tail recurrence polynomial mu_U is the minimal polynomial of a weak Perron number beta>1, meaning that every conjugate beta' satisfies |beta'|<=beta; strict inequality is not assumed. Let S_r be the set of prime divisors of mu_U(0), and let omega_{S_r^c}(N) denote the number of distinct prime divisors of N outside S_r. Then the following are equivalent:

(i) There exist a finite k and an infinite k-MCFL L contained in R_U such that

    sup_{w in L} omega_{S_r^c}(val_U(w)) < infinity.

(ii) R_U contains a geometric synchronized scheme with values c b^t, where c>=1 and b>=2. Explicitly, for some k>=1, a fixed prefix a beyond the minimal-tail threshold, and fixed words u_1,...,u_{k+1}, v_1,...,v_k, w_1,...,w_k, s_1,...,s_k, one has

    W(t)=a u_1 v_1^t w_1 s_1^t u_2 ... u_k v_k^t w_k s_k^t u_{k+1} in R_U

for every t>=0, the total pumped length sum_j(|v_j|+|s_j|) is positive, and val_U(W(t))=c b^t for every t>=0.

(iii) Some positive power of beta is an integer:

    beta^m=B in Z_{>=2}

for some m>=1.

(iv) For some m>=1 and B>=2,

    mu_U(X) divides X^m-B.

(v) For some m>=1, B>=2, and n_0,

    U_{n+m}=B U_n    for every n>=n_0.

In the positive case, R_U already contains the infinite regular ray

    {0^{n_0+mt}1 : t>=0},

with

    val_U(0^{n_0+mt}1)=U_{n_0} B^t.

Consequently, if no positive power of beta is an integer, then every infinite MCFL L contained in R_U satisfies

    sup_{w in L} omega_{S_r^c}(val_U(w))=infinity.

The new technical step is the weak-Perron asymptotic used in (ii)=>(iii). The proof invokes the spectral characterization of weak Perron numbers to choose h>=1 such that rho=beta^h is Perron and every conjugate gamma with |gamma|=beta satisfies gamma^h=beta^h. Because mu_U is irreducible and separable, from the minimal-tail threshold onward the sequence has the expansion

    U_n=sum_gamma A_gamma gamma^n,

over the conjugates gamma of beta. Uniqueness and rationality make the coefficients Galois-conjugate; minimality rules out A_beta=0. For each residue r in {0,...,h-1}, the proof obtains

    U_{hn+r}=C_r rho^n+o(rho^n),
    C_r=sum_{|gamma|=beta} A_gamma gamma^r,

once hn+r is beyond the tail threshold. Positivity gives C_r>=0. Vandermonde independence shows that not all C_r vanish. Strict increase of the place values propagates positivity from one C_r to every residue class, including across r=h-1 to r=0, and hence yields the global limit U_n^{1/n}->beta. If a geometric synchronized scheme has |W(t)|=L+Dt with D>0, the greedy interval property gives

    U_{L+Dt-1} <= c b^t < U_{L+Dt},

so taking t-th roots gives b=beta^D, proving that a positive power of beta is an integer. The remaining equivalences use minimality of mu_U as the tail annihilator, and (v)=>(ii) uses the displayed regular ray.

The nonemptiness condition you made mandatory is met directly, not merely suggested. For every p,q>=2 with B=pq nonsquare, define the alternating-radix system

    U_{2t}=B^t,    U_{2t+1}=p B^t.

Its successive radices are p,q,p,q,...; Euclidean division gives unique greedy expansions, with digits 0,...,p-1 in even positions and 0,...,q-1 in odd positions, so its canonical LSD-first language is regular. It satisfies U_{n+2}=B U_n, and its minimal recurrence polynomial is the irreducible polynomial X^2-B. The positive root sqrt(pq) is nonintegral and weak Perron, while the conjugate -sqrt(pq) has exactly equal modulus. This is precisely the configuration excluded by strict Perron dominance and admitted by the new theorem. The regular ray {0^{2t}1:t>=0} has values B^t. Concrete instances include (p,q)=(2,3), giving 1,2,6,12,36,72,..., and (2,5), giving 1,2,10,20,100,200,.... The paper also gives a genuinely nonintegral, nonunit Pisot system,

    U_0=1, U_1=3, U_{n+2}=2U_{n+1}+2U_n,

whose minimal polynomial is X^2-2X-2, whose dominant root is 1+sqrt(3), and whose trailing coefficient is 2.

The deterministic verifier exercises the alternating-radix weak-Perron family, including the equal-modulus conjugate case, together with the genuinely nonintegral, nonunit Pisot example. Its archived totals are: 6 systems, 2282 affine-action cases, 159 synchronized-orbit cases, 18 weak-Perron radical cases, and 1 length-order-free selection case; every failure counter is zero, and the final result is OVERALL: PASS.
