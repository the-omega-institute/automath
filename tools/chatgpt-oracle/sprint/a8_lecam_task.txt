FIVE CORRECTIONS TO THE MANUSCRIPT THAT HE IDENTIFIED. Apply all of them.

 1. THE ESSENTIAL RESET CONDITION IS RANK-ONE, NOT NECESSARILY DETERMINISTIC. The proof uses that
    the marked kernel has the form T_1 = c beta, so the post-event hidden distribution beta is
    independent of the pre-event state; beta being a point mass at D is a specialization. So
    "allowing leakage into another state destroys renewal" is TOO CATEGORICAL. What is true:
    arbitrary leakage no longer implies renewal; leakage making the normalized post-click
    distribution depend on the pre-click state destroys the proof; but a rank-one marked kernel
    with mass on both states could still regenerate. Fix every place that overstates this.
 2. THE DISPLAYED STATIONARY LIKELIHOOD HAS A DOMAIN QUALIFICATION. The product factorization
    holds for a record containing at least one renewal; the all-zero record has a separate
    probability. The manuscript proves a uniform polynomial-exponential bound making it negligible
    for the local experiment, so Theorems F and G are unaffected - but the formula must not be
    called the exact likelihood on the entire sample space.
 3. THE ENDPOINT NAMES ARE REVERSED RELATIVE TO STANDARD RENEWAL TERMINOLOGY. With our
    definitions, A = C_1 is the distance from the left boundary to the next renewal, which is
    ordinarily the FORWARD recurrence time or residual life; R = N - 1 - C_J is the time from the
    last renewal to the right boundary, ordinarily the BACKWARD recurrence time or age. The
    formulas are unaffected, but a paper rebuilt around stationary-renewal inference must use the
    field-standard names, as Gill and Keiding do. Rename throughout.
 4. THE ENDPOINT FACTORS ARE NOT THEMSELVES o_P(1). What is o_P(1) is the increment of their
    logarithms in a root-N local likelihood ratio. Neither S(A), S(R) nor mu^{-1} is generally
    close to zero or one. State the local-log-ratio assertion, which is what is actually proved.
 5. THE OBSERVATION OBJECT IS A LATTICE RENEWAL INDICATOR, not generic discretely observed event
    data. After the reset-and-boundary construction the observation is a stationary binary renewal
    process on Z with interrenewal mass obtained by binning the absorption time, and the model
    excludes multiple within-bin post-click renewals by construction. Say so where the scope is
    described.
