# Stage-1 anchor for arXiv:2501.13175

This directory contains a Stage-1 computational anchoring artifact for:

> Yeuk Hay Joshua Lam and Daniel Litt. *Algebraicity and integrality of solutions to differential equations*. arXiv:2501.13175, 2025.

This is a neighbor of the arXiv:2601.07933 Litt/Lam-Litt p-curvature target because it belongs to the same program: use arithmetic integrality and p-curvature-style constraints to distinguish algebraic from non-algebraic solutions of differential equations, including Picard-Fuchs and isomonodromy equations. The later arXiv:2601.07933 target focuses on non-abelian p-curvature; this paper supplies the broader algebraicity/integrality conjectural framework and proves major Picard-Fuchs and isomonodromy cases.

## Stage-1 Scope

Stage-1 anchoring here means extracting one explicit, checkable formula from the paper and verifying it independently with exact Python/SymPy arithmetic. It does not prove the global theorem, the non-finitely-generated denominator conclusion, or any p-adic integrality theorem. It only certifies that the displayed coefficient recurrence in the paper is exactly what follows by substituting a Taylor series into the displayed Picard-Fuchs ODE.

## Anchored Claim

Locator: Proposition 4.0.1 in Section 4, following equation (4.1).

Verbatim statement from the PDF text:

```text
Proposition 4.0.1 (Corollary to Theorem 4.1.1 below). Fix a, b0 , b1 ∈ C with a(a2 − 11a −
1) 6= 0. Let bi , i > 1 be the solution to the recurrence relation

(4.2) a(a2 − 11a − 1)(n + 2)(n + 1)bn+2 + (3a2 − 22a − 1)(n + 1)2 bn+1 +
                                                   [(3a − 11)(n + 1)n + a − 3]bn + n2 bn−1 = 0.

  Then the ring Z [b0 , b1 , b2 , · · · ] ⊂ C generated by the bi is not a finitely-generated Z-algebra.

  For example, if a, b0 , b1 ∈ Q, this says that there are infinitely many primes dividing the
denominators of the bi .
  Note that while (4.2) is an order 3 linear recurrence, the value of b2 , b3 , · · · is determined
uniquely by the value of b0 , b1 .
```

The checker anchors the explicit computational part of this claim: equation (4.2). It starts from the paper's Picard-Fuchs ODE

```text
t(t^2 - 11t - 1) f''(t) + (3t^2 - 22t - 1) f'(t) + (t - 3) f(t) = 0
```

sets `x = t - a`, writes `f(t) = sum b_n x^n`, expands the ODE in powers of `x`, and confirms that the coefficient of `x^n` is exactly the displayed recurrence.
